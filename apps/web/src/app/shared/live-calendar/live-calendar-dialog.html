How to add them

Proton Calendar
  1. In Proton Calendar on the web, open Settings, then All settings, then Calendars.
  2. Under Other calendars, select Add calendar from URL.
  3. Paste a calendar’s link (Copy link above), then add it.

It shows in Proton’s phone and desktop apps too, and Proton checks it for changes every 4 to 16 hours. It’s read-only there: change your payments in Simply Sub.

Google Calendar
  1. On a computer, select Add to Google Calendar above, then confirm. Or open Google Calendar, select + beside Other calendars, then From URL, paste the link and select Add calendar.
  2. To give it a tag’s colour, select ⋮ beside it under Other calendars, then a colour, or + for the tag’s own.

It shows in Google Calendar’s phone apps too. Google checks for changes on its own schedule, about once a day.

Apple Calendar
  1. On an iPhone, iPad or Mac, select Add to Apple Calendar above, and Calendar asks to subscribe. Or, on a Mac, in Calendar choose File, then New Calendar Subscription, and paste the link.
  2. Choose its colour and how often it refreshes, and iCloud as its location to see it on your other Apple devices.
