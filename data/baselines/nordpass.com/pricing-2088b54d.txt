Frequently asked questions
How do I switch between the Teams and Business plans?
You can upgrade from the Teams plan to the Business plan at any time. However, you cannot downgrade from the Business plan to Teams.
If you currently have the Teams trial, you can upgrade to Business once you choose a paid plan. If you’re using the paid Teams plan, you can upgrade to Business directly during checkout or by contacting our Sales team at sales@nordpass.com. You will be charged the difference between the plans based on the number of users and the remaining duration of your Teams subscription
What if I need more information about features, plans, or pricing?
What are the main differences between the Teams, Business and Enterprise plans?
Teams plan – built for small startups and tight-knit teams
Simple, affordable password management designed for startups and small teams of up to 10 users. With quick setup, intuitive sharing, and no complex admin tools, it helps teams stay secure and organized from the start, boosting productivity without added overhead.
Business plan – for scaling companies that need more control
Scalable security for growing businesses. The Business plan includes advanced features like breach monitoring, password health reports, and audit logs. Designed for hybrid and cloud-heavy teams, it empowers admins with full control over access levels, groups, and permissions, making it easier to manage security and support compliance at scale.
Enterprise plan – tailored for more complex organizations and compliance-heavy industries
Advanced protection for larger, global teams with complex needs. The Enterprise plan offers robust access to shared credentials and folders in real-time controls, integration with Entra ID and Okta, detailed audit trails, and dedicated support. With deep customization and compliance features, it’s built to support highly regulated industries and demanding IT environments.

What is NordPass Business’s refund policy?
We understand the importance of flexibility and peace of mind for our customers. If you're unsatisfied with NordPass Business, you can request a refund within 14 calendar days of your initial purchase. This policy ensures you can experience our services worry-free. To request a refund, please contact our customer support at support.business@nordpass.com. If you purchased NordPass Business subscription through an authorized partner, please refer to the policies of such authorized partner. For more information, please take a look at the Refunds section of our Terms of Service.
How to cancel NordPass Business subscription?
How does the billing for monthly plans work?
With a monthly NordPass plan, you pay the full price upfront for each month of service. Your subscription renews automatically until you decide to cancel. While rolling monthly plan contracts offer more flexibility, choosing an annual or 2-year plan will provide better value in the long run with lower average costs each month.
Can I change my monthly plan to an annual one?
Why do different plans cost different amounts?
NordPass offers different plans to fit different needs and stages of growth. This way, you only pay for the features you need, and you can upgrade at any time as your needs change.

How can I convert from a Personal to a Business plan?
If you wish to transfer your Personal account to Business you can purchase the new plan using your current account. Once you’ve bought the plan, you will automatically switch to the NordPass Business account. This switch will not remove any items currently stored in your personal vault.
