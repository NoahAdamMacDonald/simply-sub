Can I get a free trial period to experience Qobuz streaming?
Written by Maxime A
August 16, 2022
It is possible to enjoy a free trial of Qobuz?
​Yes! Absolutely. It is possible to enjoy a free trial for 30 days. Please note we can only offer one free trial per customer. If you’ve already had a trial period, you will be charged immediately.
So I can have a free trial on anything?
Hold on, while you can have a 30 day trial on our plans.
What's the catch?
No catch! However, we do need your payment information to begin your trial. You will not be charged until the start of your next billing cycle. You can end your trial period at any time by unsubscribing from your Qobuz account. If you unsubscribe at least 24 hours before your billing date you will not be charged for the next month.
Why do you need my bank information for a free trial?
A valid payment method will be asked of you in order to confirm your country of origin and facilitate your subscription, but you will not be debited before the end of your free trial period.
Ok, How do I do unsubscribe?
We would hate to see you go. You can unsubscribe from our service anytime in your account at this link If you cancel, your account will not be renewed at the end of your current billing cycle. This applies to all our plans, monthly or annual.
