How do I cancel my subscription?
Written by Maxime A
August 16, 2022
By unsubscribing, you will no longer be able to enjoy the Qobuz streaming service. Your account will always be available along with your library. However, streaming will no longer be available at the end of the current period.
To unsubscribe:
Go to your account on the qobuz.com website
In the “My Streaming Plan” tab
Click on the “Unsubscribe” link of your active subscription
Follow the instructions for unsubscription until this has been completed
If you have subscribed using a third-party application (like Apple), cancelling the account must be done directly through their service.
We remain at your disposal should you want to discuss your experience with Qobuz. Do not hesitate to send us your feedback by contacting customer service.
