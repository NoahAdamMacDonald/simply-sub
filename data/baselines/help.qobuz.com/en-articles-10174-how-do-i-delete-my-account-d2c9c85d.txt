How do I delete my account?
Written by Maxime A
August 16, 2022
To delete your Qobuz account, simply follow these few steps:
Go to www.qobuz.com
Click on your name at the top right of the screen
Click on "My account"
In the “My Personal Information” tab
At the bottom of the page, click on the “Delete my Qobuz account permanently” link
You will receive an email to confirm the closing of your account
​
Please note: Once this change is made, you will no longer have access to your library (favourites, playlists) as well as your purchases (except those already downloaded from the platform)
