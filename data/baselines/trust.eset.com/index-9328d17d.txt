ISO 27001 SoA extension - ISO 27017 & 27018
Compliance
We are pleased to announce that we expanded our Information Security Management System by adding the ISO 27017 and ISO 27018 controls to our Statement of Applicability (SoA).
Building on our existing ISO 27001 certification, these additions reflect our ongoing commitment to delivering secure, privacy-focused solutions. ISO 27017 provides cloud-specific security controls, while ISO 27018 focuses on the protection of personally identifiable information (PII) in public cloud environments.
The updated scope was audited by SGS and applies to products and services in scope.
