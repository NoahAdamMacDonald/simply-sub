Deleting your Udio account
March 9, 2025
Table of contents
We'll be sorry to see you go, but you're free to delete your Udio account at any time. Here's how.
If you're able to sign into your account
1) Visit udio.com/account
2) Select Account Deletion and then Delete Account.
Note that...
This will immediately delete your entire account, including all your songs and other data.
This will also cancel any subscription you might have.
You will not be able to recover your account.
If you're not able to sign in to your account...
...please contact us using the messenger on the lower right in this Help Center. We'll ask you one or more questions to verify that you own the account in question (to prevent jerks from requesting deletion of others' accounts) and -- upon your appropriate response -- we'll then delete your account and confirm the deletion.
