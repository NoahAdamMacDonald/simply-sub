Creating a Udio account
March 9, 2025
While anyone may listen to and share music from Udio, a Udio account is required to create music.
The core creation experience on Udio is available free-of-charge.
To create a Udio account...
Visit udio.com
Click "Sign up" in the upper lefthand corner
Choose your preferred sign-up method: Google, Discord, X, Apple, or email
IMPORTANT:
To ensure fair allocation of resources (and per our Terms of Service), you may only register or control one free account, though you are welcome to have more than one paid account.
