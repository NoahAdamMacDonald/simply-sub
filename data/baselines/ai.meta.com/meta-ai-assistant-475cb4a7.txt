FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
What is Meta AI?
Meta AI is a free AI assistant built by Meta, available at meta.ai, in the Meta AI app, and across WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, and Facebook. It can answer questions, help with complex reasoning, generate images, assist with coding, shopping, and more - all powered by Muse Spark from Meta Superintelligence Labs.
Is Meta AI a free AI assistant?
How do I access Meta AI?
You can access Meta AI at meta.ai on the web, by downloading the Meta AI mobile app on iOS or Android, installing the desktop app on Mac, or by chatting with Meta AI directly within WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, or Facebook. No separate account is needed beyond your existing Meta account.
What makes Meta AI different from other AI assistants?
How do I use Meta AI on WhatsApp or Instagram?
On WhatsApp, tap the Meta AI icon or start a new chat with Meta AI. On Instagram, tap the Meta AI icon in DMs or use it in search. You can ask questions, generate images, get recommendations, and more. The same features are available across all Meta apps.
