1Password refund policy
1Password refund policy You can cancel the subscription you manage for your 1Password account at any time if you’re not completely satisfied with 1Password.
BillingLearn more Important
If the subscription for your account is managed by a 1Password Business representative, contact your Account Manager or the 1Password Business team for information about the terms of your subscription.
Subscription services
If you have a 1Password account with a subscription you manage yourself, you can cancel your 1Password subscription at any time.
Free trial: If you cancel during your trial period, you won’t be charged.
Paid subscription: If you cancel an active subscription after you’ve been charged, your account will remain active until the end of your current billing period, then your account will be frozen. To unfreeze your account, reactivate your subscription.
We’re unable to issue refunds for past charges or for any time remaining on your account at the time of cancellation. For more information on the terms of your subscription, see the 1Password Terms of Service.
Learn more about 1Password subscription billing and how to manage your subscription on 1Password.com. If you started your subscription using an in-app purchase, learn how to manage your subscription with Apple or Google.
1Password Gift Cards
All gift card purchases are final and cannot be refunded. If you didn’t receive the gift card you purchased, contact 1Password Support from the email address you used when purchasing the gift card.
