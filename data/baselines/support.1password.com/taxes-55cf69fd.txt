About taxes for 1Password
About taxes for 1Password Learn how sales tax and VAT are assessed, and what to do if your company or non-profit organization is tax-exempt.
BillingLearn more Help
As a result of growth in the US markets, 1Password is now required to register and collect sales taxes in various states across the US.
1Password collects tax based on your address, regardless of which currency you pay in. The prices displayed on 1Password.com don’t include sales tax or value-added tax because each country and region charges a different tax rate.
To check your tax rate, sign in to 1Password.com, then select Billing in the sidebar.
Note
If you started your subscription using an in-app purchase, any applicable taxes are collected by Apple or Google.

Tax exemptions
United States
If your billing address is in the United States, you may be subject to state and local sales taxes. Your tax rate is determined based on your ZIP code and displayed on your Billing page.
Calculate your state and local sales tax rate.
Other countries: Value added tax
If your billing address is in Australia, Canada, the European Union, India, Japan, Liechtenstein, Norway, Singapore, South Africa, Switzerland, or United Kingdom, you may be subject to VAT, GST, PST, QST, RST, or consumption tax. Your tax rate is displayed on your Billing page, and any tax is included as a line item on your invoice.
If you’re tax exempt
If your company or non-profit organization is exempt from sales tax in your country or region, follow these steps to request the removal of tax from future invoices:
Add the name of your business entity to your invoices.
Send us an email with proof of your tax-exempt status.
