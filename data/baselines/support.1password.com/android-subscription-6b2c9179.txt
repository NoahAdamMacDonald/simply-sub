About in-app purchase subscriptions
When you start your subscription to 1Password using an in-app purchase on your Android device, you can manage your subscription with Google. You can:
See when your subscription renews
Cancel your subscription
Get help
Individual and family subscriptions are available with an in-app purchase. You can’t change your account type after you’ve started your subscription.
When you start your subscription using an in-app purchase, you won’t be able to manage your subscription on 1Password.com.
If you don’t see your 1Password subscription in the Google Play Store app, make sure you’re signed in with the correct Google account. If so, you might have started your subscription on 1Password.com and not using an in-app purchase. Learn how to manage your subscription on 1Password.com.
If you want to switch between monthly and annual billing, you must cancel your subscription with Google. After your current billing period ends, you can start a new subscription and choose monthly or annual billing.
If you canceled your 1Password subscription with Google, and you want to remove your information from the 1Password service, you can delete your account on 1Password.com.
