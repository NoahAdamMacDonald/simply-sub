Does NordVPN have a free trial?
Yes - NordVPN offers a free 7-day VPN trial so you can explore every feature of your chosen plan before paying anything. The trial is currently available only in these countries: France, the United States, Canada, Australia, the United Kingdom, Japan, the Netherlands, Germany, Denmark, Finland, Norway, Sweden, Spain, Italy, and Poland. This article explains who qualifies for the free trial, how to start it, what it includes, and how to cancel before your paid subscription begins. It also covers the separate Android (Google Play) trial and the 30-day money-back guarantee.
Before you start
The 7-day free trial is available only to new NordVPN users (no prior NordVPN subscription).
It applies to 1-year and 2-year [Prime / Ultimate Max / Ultra / Complete Max] plans.
It is available only in these countries: France, the United States, Canada, Australia, the United Kingdom, Japan, the Netherlands, Germany, Denmark, Finland, Norway, Sweden, Spain, Italy, and Poland.
You must use an accepted payment method: credit card or PayPal. Other methods (Google Pay, Union Pay, Sofort, cryptocurrencies) will grey out the free-trial option.
You won’t pay anything during the trial, but you must cancel before the 7-day period ends if you don’t want to continue with a paid subscription.
NOTE: Unless you cancel before the 7-day trial ends, your chosen plan will begin automatically, and you’ll pay the price shown when you started the trial.
Here's what to do
Start your free trial
Select a plan. Pick a 1-year or 2-year plan and enter your payment details (credit card or PayPal). You won’t pay anything before the trial period ends.
Download the NordVPN app. Get the NordVPN app for your device. You'll have full access to all features and VPN servers included in the subscription of your choice.
Use NordVPN free for 7 days. Enjoy the trial. If you decide not to subscribe, cancel your trial before it ends.
Cancel before you're charged
You can cancel your free trial anytime before it ends. Here's how:
Log in to your Nord Account.
Click "Billing" in the left-hand menu.
Under "Subscriptions," click "Cancel" next to "Free trial."
Additional tips
One trial per customer. The free trial is available only once to first-time customers with a new Nord Account.
Android (Google Play) trial: A separate 3-day free trial is available to first-time Android users who download the app from Google Play (one per customer). Google Play manages it under its Terms of Service. Cancel it through Google Play and direct any questions to them. It is not available in Bangladesh, India, South Korea, or Sri Lanka.
30-day money-back guarantee (separate from the trial): First-time customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase by contacting customer support. If you downloaded NordVPN from Apple's App Store, contact Apple support for the refund.
After the trial: If you don't cancel within 7 days, your plan begins at the price shown when you started the trial. Before the subscription begins, NordVPN emails you a reminder of the price and conditions.
