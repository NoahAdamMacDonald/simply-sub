How to purchase a NordVPN subscription
What payment methods does NordVPN accept
You can pay with a credit card, cryptocurrency, PayPal, Sofort (Klarna), bank or local bank transfers, prepaid (debit) card, and many other payment methods. You can also make a payment through the Amazon App Store, Apple Pay, Google Pay, the Apple App Store, or Google Play.
NOTE: Some payment methods are not available in certain regions.
If you want to pay in cash, you can purchase a NordVPN subscription in a retail store.
Once you’ve checked out NordVPN’s plans and prices, you can find out which payment methods are available to you at the checkout page.
