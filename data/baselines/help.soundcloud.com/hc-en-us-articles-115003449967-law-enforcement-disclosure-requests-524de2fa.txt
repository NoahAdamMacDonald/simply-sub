Law enforcement disclosure requests
In line with our Privacy Policy, we can only disclose requested information to a competent authority when we are obliged to do so by applicable law. If you are a law enforcement authority seeking information about SoundCloud users and accounts in the course of criminal investigations, please refer to our Law Enforcement Guidelines for details on submitting requests.
