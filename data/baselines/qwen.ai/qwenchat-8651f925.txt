Qwen Overview
Think deeply and solve tough problems.
By spending extra time analyzing and reasoning through complex questions, Qwen excels in reasoning and cognitive tasks, using critical thinking to solve challenging problems in math, science, coding, and more.
Creative writing and exploring ideas together with you
Qwen combines chain-of-thought reasoning with creative idea generation to craft narratives, develop characters, and explore plots systematically. It serves as a writing assistant, suggesting scenarios and synthesizing ideas into cohesive frameworks. With multi-modal capabilities, it integrates visual and textual reasoning, making it versatile for screenwriting, game design, and immersive storytelling, empowering users to balance creativity with logical coherence.
Click Search, link the world
Qwen's web search functionality connects users to a vast digital ecosystem, delivering fast, reliable, and real-time answers enriched with authoritative sources. Whether exploring trending topics, verifying facts, or accessing specialized knowledge, Qwen ensures precision and seamless access to credible insights. Qwen's web search empowers users to navigate the expanding web landscape efficiently, bridging the gap between questions and trusted information.
Read the document and summary.

With the ability to process up to 1 million tokens, Qwen efficiently extracts key insights from lengthy documents, helping you make informed decisions faster.
