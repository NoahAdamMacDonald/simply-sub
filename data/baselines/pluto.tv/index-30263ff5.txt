Frequently Asked Questions
Learn how to watch free full movies, binge-worthy shows, and curated channels free online and across multiple devices.
What is Pluto TV?
Pluto TV is a free, safe, and legal streaming service. You can watch your choice of free live TV, movies, and shows online or on the Pluto TV streaming app anytime. You can create a free Pluto TV profile to sync your Favorite Live TV Channels and Watch List, and resume where you left off on other devices.
How do I stream Pluto TV?
There are multiple ways to stream Pluto TV for free. You can download the Pluto TV app on any Smart TV, such as Roku, Fire TV, LG, tvOS, Sony & more. Or watch Pluto TV on streaming devices, including Chromecast, Playstation, Xbox, and more. You can also download the app from the Apple Store or Google Play to watch free movies and shows on your mobile or desktop devices. Or, start streaming without downloading on your web browser, such as Chrome or Safari.
Is Pluto TV free?
Pluto TV is a completely free streaming service. Our short commercial breaks allow us to offer all live TV channels, shows and movies at no cost to you. Watch free movies and shows online anytime on any device. No credit cards required. Pay never.
What can I watch on Pluto TV?
Pluto TV offers free Live TV Channels, popular movies, and shows to stream for free. You can binge-watch reality TV shows, enjoy documentaries, watch horror movies, and much more from your favorite genre. You can also stream live TV with live local news channels, live sports, or hit movies and shows.
