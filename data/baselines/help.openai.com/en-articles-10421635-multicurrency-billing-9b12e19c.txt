Multi‑currency billing
Learn about which currencies and payment methods you can use to pay for your ChatGPT subscription
Updated: 2 days ago
Note: This article covers subscriptions purchased from chatgpt.com (web). If you subscribe via the ChatGPT app on iOS or Android, your subscription is managed by the Apple App Store or Google Play and charges will appear in your local currency. API pricing can be found here.
Overview
For subscriptions purchased on chatgpt.com, review the price and billing currency shown at checkout before completing your purchase. Localized prices may differ from a conversion of the U.S. price.
This information applies only to standard paid plans. Enterprise and Education plans are managed separately and are outside the scope of this article.

Payment methods
All countries
Credit and debit cards
United States — ChatGPT wallet balance
For eligible accounts and purchases in the United States, you can use your ChatGPT wallet balance toward charges in US dollars (USD) on the ChatGPT website. This does not extend wallet support to every currency listed above, API billing, or subscriptions billed through Apple or Google.
A wallet balance may remain visible even when gift-card redemption or wallet funding is unavailable for your location, billing currency, or purchase. Seeing a USD balance does not confirm that you can use it for a particular checkout.
Before confirming a purchase, check the currency, the wallet amount applied, and the remaining amount due. If wallet funds are not applied as expected, stop before confirming an unexpected charge and contact OpenAI Support. Changing your subscription currency does not by itself make a purchase eligible for wallet funding.
Note that gift card purchases and redemptions are currently available in the US only, and denominated in USD.

FAQ
Can I change the currency of my subscription?
If you already have an active subscription, you’ll continue to be billed in your current currency.
If you cancel a Pro subscription and subscribe again, your included usage may change. For the current rules, see: About ChatGPT Pro tiers.
If you’d like to switch to a newly supported currency, you’ll need to cancel your subscription and re-subscribe once your current billing period ends.
If a renewal in the same currency costs more than expected, compare the plan, billing period, tax and discount on your invoice with the offer you saw. If your previous billing period has ended and you want to pay in a different currency, check whether checkout offers it.
If the renewal price still doesn’t match the offer or checkout rejects a currency it shows, contact Support. For a renewal, include the invoice date and the price you expected. For a checkout error, include the currency and the error message.
What if my payment method requires extra verification?
Customers in the EEA may be prompted for Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) during purchase or renewal. Follow the on‑screen steps to complete verification.
Can I purchase API credits in additional currencies?
No. Purchases for API credits are currently limited to USD.
