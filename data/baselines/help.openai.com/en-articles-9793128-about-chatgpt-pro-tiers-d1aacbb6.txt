About ChatGPT Pro tiers
Information about our paid subscription plan, Pro.
ChatGPT Pro now offers Pro 500, a new $500/month plan that includes GPT-6 Astra Ultrafast and GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast. Pro 200 is also available for new subscriptions again. Pro 100 and Pro 500 now include more Work and Codex usage. All Pro 200 subscriptions now include the same Work and Codex allowance.
Overview
ChatGPT Pro includes Pro models, Codex, deep research, image creation, memory and file uploads.
Pro plans
Choose from three monthly Pro plans:
Plan Monthly price (USD) Ultrafast
Pro 100 $100 Not included
Pro 200 $200 Not included
Pro 500 $500 Included
Pro 200 includes more usage than Pro 100. Pro 500 offers the highest included usage of the three plans.
See the pricing page for a comparison of the features and usage included in each plan.
How do I subscribe?
Sign in to ChatGPT and open the pricing page to select a Pro plan. Use your existing ChatGPT account if you’re changing plans.
Can I subscribe to Pro 200 again?
Yes. New Pro 200 subscriptions are available from the pricing page and include the increased Work and Codex allowance. The monthly price remains $200.
What happens to my existing Pro 200 subscription?
The reduction announced in our September 29 email is canceled. If you already had the higher Work and Codex allowance, you keep it. If you had the lower allowance, it has doubled to match. Your subscription price stays at $200/month.
Does the updated Pro 200 allowance include Ultrafast?
No. Your subscription remains Pro 200. Ultrafast is available only on Pro 500.
What if I cancel and subscribe again?
If you cancel and later resubscribe, you’ll get the same Work and Codex allowance as other Pro 200 subscribers. If your subscription is active, you don’t need to cancel and resubscribe to get the updated allowance.
Scheduling a cancellation does not end your subscription immediately. You keep access until the end of the current billing period and can undo the cancellation before that period ends.
If you believe your subscription ended because of an error, contact Support so we can review it.
Usage and limits
Increased Work and Codex allowance
Pro 100 and Pro 500 now include twice their previous Work and Codex allowance. On Pro 200, only the lower allowance doubled. New subscriptions include the updated allowance, and existing subscribers receive it automatically without waiting for renewal.
Plus usage is unchanged. The amount of work you can complete still depends on the model, task, and settings. A larger allowance does not guarantee twice as many completed tasks.
Does my weekly GPT-6 Pro message limit change?
Pro 100 and Pro 500 keep their current weekly GPT-6 Pro message limits in Chat. On Pro 200, the lower weekly limit now matches the higher one. If you already had the higher limit, it stays the same. These message limits are separate from your Work and Codex allowance.
Which Pro plans include Ultrafast?
Among Pro plans, Ultrafast is available only on Pro 500. You can select it in the model picker. For GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol, it uses your plan’s included usage first at 8× the Standard rate for the selected model, then draws from your credit balance at 6× the Standard credit rate after that allowance is used.
At launch, buying credits on Pro 100 or Pro 200 does not unlock Ultrafast.
Why is a model temporarily unavailable?
Model allowances vary by tier. At a model’s limit, it may be unavailable until its allowance resets. ChatGPT displays the reset time when available.
A usage limit does not end your subscription or mean your account is restricted. Use another available model or wait for the reset. You cannot bypass a limit or increase it through a setting.
Contact Support if the model remains unavailable after its allowance resets.
Can I buy more usage?
For eligible features, you can purchase credits to continue after using your plan’s included allowance. Included usage is used first, followed by your credit balance.
Credits support eligible usage in Codex, ChatGPT Work, ChatGPT for Word, ChatGPT for Excel and ChatGPT for PowerPoint. Availability depends on your plan and account. Credits do not provide additional usage for every ChatGPT model or feature.
If credit purchases are available for your account, you can view your balance and purchase credits in Settings → Usage in ChatGPT, or Usage & Billing in Codex. See Using Credits for Flexible Usage in ChatGPT (Personal plans) for supported features, rates and purchase details.
Can Support reset my usage limits?
Support cannot reset ChatGPT or Codex limits. We can investigate if usage was counted incorrectly or access failed to return after a reset.
Why has my account been restricted?
Pro use is subject to our Terms of Use. Sharing account credentials, reselling access and automatically extracting data are prohibited. We may restrict access for misuse. If you believe your account was restricted in error, contact Support to request a review.
Why can similar tasks use different amounts of my included allowance?
Usage depends on the model, the amount of context processed, reasoning, tool use, information retrieval, caching, and speed mode.
Compared with Standard mode for the same model, Fast mode uses your included allowance at 2.5 times the rate, and Ultrafast for GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol uses it at 8 times the rate. These are allowance consumption rates, not guarantees of faster performance. When using purchased credits, Ultrafast is charged at 6× the Standard credit rate for the selected model.
Managing your subscription
How do I change my Pro plan?
Open Settings → My Plan to view your subscription and available plan changes.
How do I cancel?
Cancel through the platform where you purchased your subscription: ChatGPT, Apple or Google Play. Canceling stops future renewals. You can normally keep using your plan until the end of the current billing period.
Uninstalling the ChatGPT app does not cancel your subscription. See Canceling your ChatGPT subscription for the steps for each platform.
What happens if my renewal payment fails?
A failed renewal payment does not by itself mean your subscription has ended. For web subscriptions, follow your billing email’s payment recovery instructions. For Apple or Google Play, check the store’s payment settings.
Can I request a refund?
Refund eligibility depends on your purchase and applicable rights. Canceling your subscription does not automatically refund earlier charges. If a refund is approved, access covered by the refunded charge normally ends, subject to applicable law.
For purchases on chatgpt.com or through Google Play, contact OpenAI Support while signed in to the account associated with the charge. For Apple App Store purchases, request a refund directly from Apple. See How do I request a refund for my ChatGPT subscription? for eligibility and instructions.
Can I keep Pro if I join a ChatGPT Business workspace?
Yes, if you keep your personal workspace separate. Your Pro subscription continues until you cancel it. Merging your personal workspace into Business changes how your subscription is handled; see Migrating your personal workspace to ChatGPT Business or Enterprise before merging.
Does Pro offer annual billing?
Pro is billed monthly. Annual billing and payment for multiple months in advance are not available.
How do I request a U.S. sales tax exemption?
Eligible organizations can submit exemption documents for review. See U.S. sales tax exemption requests for the required documentation and account details.
