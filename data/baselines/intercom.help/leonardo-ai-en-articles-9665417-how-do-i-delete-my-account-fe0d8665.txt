How do I delete my account?
Instructions on how to delete a Leonardo.Ai account
M
Written by Marc S
February 18, 2026
Before deleting your account, please cancel any paid subscriptions that you have first. You can find the instructions to cancel your subscription here.
To delete your account,
Click on the settings icon in the bottom left menu bar on the Home page.
At the bottom of Your Profile information in the Delete Account section, click Delete account.
Note that deleting your account will remove all of your information from our database, and this cannot be undone.
