How do I subscribe to a paid plan, change or upgrade my subscription?
A guide to starting, changing, or upgrading your Leonardo.Ai plan.
Written by Nick Lee
August 25, 2026
Whether you’re ready to jump onto a paid plan, switch things up, or upgrade for more features, this guide will cover how to manage your Leonardo.Ai subscription.
To subscribe to a paid plan
From the home page, click Plans in the side bar.
2. On the plans page, Review all our plan options and decide which is best for you.
3. Choose to pay a Yearly or Monthly subscription.
4. Click Subscribe on the plan you want to choose.
A confirmation pop up will appear advising you of the cost, and directing you to pay via PayPal, or Card (which will direct you to the Stripe billing page). Whichever you choose, follow the prompts to complete your purchase, and enjoy the unlocked benefits of your new plan!
To Change (Upgrade or Downgrade) your Subscription Plan
If you wish to change to a different tier of plan, simply follow the above steps and select the plan you wish to move to. The pop up will appear advising you of the payment difference. Follow the prompts.
Please don't hesitate to contact support if you have any questions or issues.
To Cancel (Unsubscribe) from your Plan
Follow the simple steps in this guide to cancel your paid Leonardo.Ai subscription.
