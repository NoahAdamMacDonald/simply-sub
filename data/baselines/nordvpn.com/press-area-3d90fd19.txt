What makes NordVPN special
Ultra-fast VPN
NordVPN is one of the fastest VPNs on the market thanks to a large server network and the WireGuard®-based NordLynx protocol.
No activity tracking
NordVPN has a no-logs policy, meaning it doesn’t track user browsing activity. The company’s no-logs policy has been verified by independent reviews six times.
Thousands of VPN servers
NordVPN operates thousands of ultra-fast VPN servers covering 224 locations around the world — you’ll always be able to pick a server close to you for the best online experience.
Multiple devices
NordVPN lets you protect up to 10 devices at the same time. From phones and computers to tablets and even routers — our users can secure them all with just one account.
Next-level cybersecurity
Our next-gen antivirus and Dark Web Monitor Pro™ take digital security even further. These advanced features block malicious sites, ads, and trackers and find your data in data leaks.
Dedicated IP
Our dedicated IP provides you with a personal IP that isn’t shared with others. It helps you access remote resources without interruptions and avoid CAPTCHAs.
Meshnet
The Meshnet feature allows users to create their own VPN servers, access their files, and play online games securely.
Post-quantum encryption
NordVPN incorporates post-quantum encryption as a proactive way to prevent quantum attacks, even after quantum computers become accessible for consumer use.
Leading cybersecurity company
NordVPN is brought to you by Nord Security opens in a new tab, one of the leading cybersecurity companies in the world. Aside from NordVPN, it offers a full range of internet security solutions.
Security bundles
NordVPN offers a bundle plan that includes a secure password manager, encrypted cloud storage, and identity protection benefits to further boost your cybersecurity.
