Does Quora ever sell my personal data to third parties for marketing?
Official Quora Account
May 05, 2021 22:30
Updated
No. Quora does not sell your personal data – such as your name and contact information – to third parties to use for their own marketing purposes. In the cases where we share data with advertisers and other partners, it’s aggregated to protect your privacy.
For more information regarding how Quora uses your personal data, please see our Privacy Policy.
