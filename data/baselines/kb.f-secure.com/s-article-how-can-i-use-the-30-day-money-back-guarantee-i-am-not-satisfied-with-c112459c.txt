How can I use the 30-day-money-back guarantee? I am not satisfied with the F-Secure product and want a refund.
I want to make use of the 30-day refund policy, I am not satisfied with the F-Secure software. How to do it?
RESOLUTION
Help Resolution
If you purchased your product directly with F-Secure within the past 30-days and wish to apply for a refund, please contact our F-Secure customer care and provide the reference number of the order that can be found in the confirmation email from Cleverbridge.
However, F-Secure products purchased through third party (e.g. resellers, retailers, vendors, services, etc.) such as Apple AppStore, are not eligible for this 30-day money-back guarantee from F-Secure. In this case you may contact the service provider and check if they have their own refund policy.
