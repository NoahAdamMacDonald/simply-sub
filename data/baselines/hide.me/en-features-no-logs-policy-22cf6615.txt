Zero personal data
We strictly do not log any personal data to avoid legal liability, and to ensure your online privacy. Furthermore we do not store any log file on our VPN servers, it's not our job to monitor or filter your data.
True browsing privacy
We do not monitor or log your browsing behaviour. The way we have chosen to build hide.me makes this impossible. All of the usage data we receive is completely anonymous, and is not connected to your real IP address or location.
Why is a no-logging policy important?
Rapidly increasing surveillance and data retention laws making it really harder to keep our online identity protected. VPN providers who keep logs of your online activities and payment details are always a threat to your privacy because they can be forced by authorities to hand over your data. However, with hide.me, it's different. So how can someone force us to handover something which we don’t have.
Independent Auditor Confirms Our No-Logs Policy
We value our users' trust above all else, and being a VPN provider, it's crucial to provide transparency. That's why we undergo regular audits to confirm our no-logs policy.
We were among the first VPN providers to be audited in 2015, and we continue this practice to ensure our users' data is secure. This year, Securitum, a respected European security firm, has once again confirmed that our no-logs policy is strictly enforced.

We are not required to store logs
We are based out of Malaysia and local laws impose no obligations on us to store user logs unlike most European, American and Oceanian countries where VPN providers have no choice but to store user logs because the law of the land makes it mandatory for them to keep activity logs.
