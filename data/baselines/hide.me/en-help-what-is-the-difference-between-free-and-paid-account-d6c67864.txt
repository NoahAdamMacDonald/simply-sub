What Is The Difference Between Free and Paid Account?
Last modified: March 13, 2026
We don’t make money on our free VPN service. We believe Internet security and privacy is a basic right; free VPN is our way of reinstating online freedom to the everyday user and there is no compromise on that part between free vs paid plan.
Having said that, we do offer paid services. Here’s how they’re different:
1. SmartGuard
Our Premium plan includes our SmartGuard. SmartGuard feature protects you online by blocking ads, trackers, and malicious websites that are trying to steal your information.
2. Port Forwarding
Premium users have access to port forwarding feature. Our system uses UPnP and NAT-PMP protocols. This feature is not available in free and is only available in paid plan.
3. Bolt “Fast Proxy”
You can further speed up your Windows connection by using our in-house Bolt feature. It is exclusive to our Premium subscribers.
4. Server Locations
With our free service, you get to connect to a choice of 7 server locations (Finland, France, Germany, Netherlands, Switzerland, United Kingdom, United States). Meanwhile, paid users get access to over 89 server locations worldwide.
5. Simultaneous Connections
Our Free VPN users are allowed to connect to a single device at a time. At the same time, our Premium users can connect up to 10 different devices simultaneously.
6. Data Transfer Limit
While free users used to get a monthly limit of 10 GB data transfer, this is no longer the case and both free and paid subscribers have unlimited data transfer.
7. WireGuard Protocol
WireGuard VPN protocol used to be Premium only feature, but is now available in both plans. You are welcome to enjoy blazing fast speed.
Below are answers to some frequently asked questions (FAQ).
Can I use your service to bypass ISP’s data caps?
Our VPN service works on top of your current Internet connection, so we do require that you have a healthy amount of Internet quota while using our service to avoid any additional speed reduction or interruptions to the service caused by your ISP.
Do you log the activity of free and paid users?
No. Our system is not designed to log any activity. It doesn’t matter which plan you use, you always benefit from our strict privacy policy.
Are VPNs legal to use?
Yes, the usage of a VPN is completely legal. It is your right to protect and encrypt your Internet connection and not to leave traces at every corner of the Internet. You use a cover for your letters, so why should everyone be able to track your traffic?
