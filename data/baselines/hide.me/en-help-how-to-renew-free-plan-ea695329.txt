How To Renew Free Plan?
Last modified: March 13, 2026
There are two ways that you can renew your free plan and the instructions can be found below:
1. Renewing via hide.me VPN client app
Launch hide.me VPN client app and click on Renew Plan.
Plan selection will be presented next and you can choose to renew your plan or decide to upgrade.
2. Renewing via hide.me website
In order to renew your free hide.me plan via our website, please open your web browser and navigate to our web page. Once there, use your hide.me account’s credentials and login to your Member’s area. Once there, click on the highlighted button in order to perform the task.
