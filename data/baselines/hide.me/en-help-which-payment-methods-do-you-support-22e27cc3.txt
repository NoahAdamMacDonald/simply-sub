Which Payment Methods Does hide.me Support?
Last modified: August 18, 2026
We support a few payment methods that you can use to subscribe to our premium service and they are as per below:
Card payment
Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Diners Club), PayPal, SEPA and SOFORT.
Mobile payment system
Apple Pay
Google Pay
Cryptocurrencies
Bitcoin, Dogecoin, Ethereum, Litecoin, Solana, KuCoin Pay, *Tether, *USD Coin.
* Tether (USDT) and USD Coin (USDC) are only supported via ERC20 (Ethereum Network).
Wire Transfer
We also accept manual payment via bank transfer. To do this, follow the steps below:
Click here and choose your subscription duration.
Choose to pay with WebMoney, enter your email address and click on Continue.
Now, choose Wire Transfer as your payment options and click on Next.
You will now be given a references number and also our bank account details that you can use to make your payment.
Note: If you’re not getting wire transfer option, please choose Pay with Bitcoin on step#2 and follow rest of the instructions.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any point in time. There are no restrictions or limitations to do so.
Do you have a money-back guarantee?
Absolutely, within 30 days you can get a refund with no questions asked.
Do you log my payment information on your system?
No. All payments are handled by external payment providers, and we do not log any personal information about you when you proceed with payment.
Which payment models do you offer?
We offer a subscription payment model. If you prefer not to subscribe, you can cancel your subscription via payment confirmation email or by contacting our 24/7 support. We recommend a hide.me subscription so you can stay protected and don’t have to worry about your account expiring.
How can I make a one-off payment (without a subscription)?
To make a one-off payment, please follow the steps below:
Purchase your subscription.
Open the payment confirmation email message.
Follow the provided link and cancel the subscription.
How many devices can I connect simultaneously?
With our Premium plan, you can connect up to ten devices at the same time, regardless of the platform.
