About hide.me VPN Company
hide.me VPN is a privacy-focused VPN service founded in 2012 with the goal of providing secure, unrestricted internet access for users worldwide.
The service is operated by eVenture Ltd., a Malaysia-based company dedicated to online privacy, security, and digital freedom.
Since its founding, hide.me VPN has grown into a globally trusted VPN provider with millions of users who rely on it to protect their online activities and bypass internet restrictions.
Company Overview
Service: hide.me VPN
Parent Company: eVenture Ltd.
Founded: 2012
Headquarters: Malaysia
Jurisdiction: Outside 5/9/14-Eyes surveillance alliances
Operating from Malaysia allows hide.me VPN to maintain strong privacy protections and operate independently from international surveillance agreements.
Independent Jurisdiction Outside Surveillance Alliances
hide.me VPN operates under Malaysian jurisdiction, which is not part of the 5-Eyes, 9-Eyes, or 14-Eyes intelligence alliances.
This means the company is not subject to international intelligence-sharing obligations that could compromise user privacy.
Additionally, Malaysia does not impose mandatory data retention requirements on VPN providers. This allows hide.me VPN to operate with a strict no-logs policy and without legal pressure to store user activity.
Strict No-Logs Policy
Privacy is the foundation of the hide.me VPN company philosophy.
hide.me follows a strict no-logs architecture, meaning the company does not record:
Browsing activity
Connection timestamps
User IP addresses
Traffic or bandwidth usage
Because no identifiable activity logs are stored, user browsing behavior remains private and cannot be reconstructed later.
Privacy-First Infrastructure
hide.me prioritizes technical privacy protections alongside policy commitments.
Key privacy measures include:
RAM-only servers that do not store data on physical disks
Infrastructure designed to avoid persistent logging
Security-focused system architecture
RAM-only technology ensures that all server data is automatically wiped whenever a server restarts, further strengthening user privacy.
Since launching in 2012, hide.me VPN has never handed out user data, reinforcing its commitment to privacy-first operations.
Security Record - Never Breached
A strong security history is essential for any privacy service.
Since its founding, hide.me VPN has:
Never experienced a data breach
Never exposed user data
Maintained secure infrastructure across its global network
Security remains the company’s top priority, with ongoing improvements to server infrastructure, encryption standards, and application security.
Transparency & Accountability
Transparency is a core principle of hide.me VPN operations.
The company publishes yearly transparency reports that outline:
Government requests for user data
Legal inquiries
Compliance actions
Because hide.me operates a strict no-logs policy, these reports consistently demonstrate that the company has no identifiable user activity data to provide.
hide.me also supports independent verification of its policies through no-logs audits (1st) (2nd), helping reinforce trust in its privacy commitments.
No Trackers or Hidden Data Collection
hide.me takes a strong stance against invasive tracking technologies.
The company ensures that:
VPN apps do not contain third-party trackers
The hide.me website avoids intrusive tracking systems
User privacy is respected across the entire platform
This approach ensures that privacy protections extend beyond the VPN connection itself.
Commitment to Open Source
hide.me actively contributes to the open-source ecosystem.
Key initiatives include:
An open-source Linux VPN client
Contributions to open-source software development
No restrictions on how users run or configure VPN clients
Supporting open source allows the community to review code, verify security practices, and contribute improvements to the software.
Accessibility & Internet Freedom
hide.me believes that internet privacy should be accessible to everyone.
The company promotes digital freedom through:
Basic VPN features available for free users
Tools designed to bypass internet censorship and restrictions
Simple apps that prioritize usability and performance
hide.me also focuses on delivering high-speed connections so users can enjoy secure browsing without sacrificing performance.
A Reputation Built Since 2012
Over more than a decade of operation, hide.me VPN has built a reputation based on:
Strong privacy protection
Transparent company practices
Secure infrastructure
Reliable global service
By combining privacy-first policies with modern VPN technology, hide.me continues to provide users with a secure and unrestricted internet experience.
