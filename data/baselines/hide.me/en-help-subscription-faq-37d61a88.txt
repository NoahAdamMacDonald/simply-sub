Q: How to change Payment method for an open order? A: After you have submitted an order with a manual payment option, you will receive an order confirmation email. Our payment service provider Paddle includes a link called “Change payment option“. Change your payment option through this link.
If you have not received it, check your spam folder or contact Paddle through their support form on page.
Q: How to change payment method for an existing subscription? A: In order to change the payment method for subscriptions with automatic renewal, please contact Paddle directly. You will find their contact form on this page.
In addition, they can provide you with a link to the page where you can change it by yourself.
Alternatively, you can also contact our Technical support team on this page.
Q: How can I cancel a subscription with automatic renewal/recurring billing? A: You can stop the automatic renewal at any time before the time of renewal. To do so, follow the instructions on how to cancel your hide.me subscription.
Please note that the cancellation only stops the automatic renewal/recurring billing. It does not stop the active membership. It will not automatically result in a refund.
