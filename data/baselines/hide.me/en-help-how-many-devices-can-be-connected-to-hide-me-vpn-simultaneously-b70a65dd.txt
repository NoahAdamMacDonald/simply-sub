How Many Devices Can Be Connected to hide.me VPN Simultaneously?
Last modified: March 12, 2026
This page is for our Premium users who are having trouble connecting multiple devices at the same time.
With our Premium plan, you can connect ten devices at the same time, regardless of the platform. Furthermore, you can connect all of them by using the same username and password!
You can install hide.me VPN on any number of devices, but only ten devices can be connected at the same time.
If you reach the limit and want to connect another device, disconnect the one that you are not using.
You can also do it in your Connection settings area.
How can I use the Premium plan?
For example, you could use your Premium plan on:
Ten computers
Three computers, Android phone, iPhone, tablets and a router
Your computers, tablets, iPhones, Android phones, Windows phones, Chromebooks and Fire TV
or any other combination of ten devices regardless of platform
I need to connect more than 10 devices?
If you would like to use hide.me VPN on more than ten devices simultaneously, you can consider doing the following:
purchase additional Premium plan, or
share your VPN connection from a Windows or Mac computer, or
configure a VPN router and connect as many devices behind it as you wish.
