Notes about subscriptions and billing
You can buy a Dashlane subscription on our website with a credit card. You can also buy a subscription in the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. You can no longer pay for new personal plans with PayPal unless you live in the United Kingdom.
If you see a small charge from Dashlane before your renewal date, we're testing to make sure your credit card is still valid. This charge is temporary and won't be applied to your final bill.
If you bought your subscription on our website and the card you used expires, you'll need to renew your plan by buying a new subscription from our website. We'll send you an email to let you know.
Dashlane plans sold to customers in the EU and the UK may be subject to Value-Added Tax (VAT). VAT is included on your receipt.
If your billing address is in the United States, you may be subject to state and local sales taxes. Your tax rate is determined by your ZIP code and displayed on the checkout page when you purchase a plan.
