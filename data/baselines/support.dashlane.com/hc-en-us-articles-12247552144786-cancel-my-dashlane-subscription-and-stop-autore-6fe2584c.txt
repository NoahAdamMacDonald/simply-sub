Cancel my Dashlane subscription and stop autorenewal
UPDATED OCTOBER 21, 2026
This article is about Dashlane personal plans. If you're the admin of a Dashlane professional plan, use the steps for professional plans.
Cancel your subscription for a professional plan
You can cancel your Dashlane subscription to a personal plan at any time to stop future charges. When you cancel, we turn off auto-renewal, so we won’t charge you again. You keep your plan and your data until the end of your current subscription period.

Cancel if you paid with a credit card on the Dashlane website
You can cancel directly in Dashlane when you use Dashlane on the web.
Important: This option is available only if you paid with a credit card on our website. You see this option only when you use Dashlane in a computer browser, not in the Dashlane mobile apps or macOS app. If you've paid using a mobile app or macOS app, see the steps for Google Play Store and Apple App Store.
Select the Dashlane D in your browser's toolbar, log in with your Master Password, and select Open the web app.
Open the Vault menu in the top-left of the page, and select Subscription.
On the Subscription page, select Cancel your subscription in the right panel.
On the next page, select Continue cancellation.
If you want a refund, start the refund process after you cancel. The refund link appears in the right panel when the cancellation is complete.
Get a refund for my Dashlane subscription
Important: Be sure to request your refund before you leave the page. If you've already canceled your subscription and can't find the page where you select Request refund, select Chat in the bottom right of this page to get help.
