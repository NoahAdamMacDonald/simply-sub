VPN protection with Hotspot Shield
UPDATED OCTOBER 6, 2026
We offer VPN protection with Dashlane Premium and Premium Plus, and Dashlane Team, Omnix® Password Management, and Omnix Enterprise plans. Dashlane Friends & Family plan managers have VPN access, but members invited to the plan don't.
Compare personal plans
Compare professional plans
A virtual private network (VPN) provides an extra layer of protection for your online life. You can browse the internet from an unsecure network and generally increase your privacy online.
We partner with Hotspot Shield to provide their Premium VPN service and 24/7 Tech Support. You can set up your Hotspot Shield Premium account directly from your Dashlane app.
Learn about VPN protection
Note: If your Dashlane plan includes access to the Hotspot Shield VPN, you can use the VPN on up to five devices. If you already have five devices linked to your VPN account, you won't be able to sign in on a new device until you sign out of Hotspot Shield on another device.
Sign out of Hotspot Shield
Who can use the Hotspot Shield VPN?
Use the Hotspot Shield VPN
To use the VPN, make sure you've set up your Hotspot Shield account through Dashlane. Then turn on the VPN and use the internet like you normally do. With the VPN turned on, you've added an extra layer of protection to your online life.

Hotspot Shield Tech Support
With Hotspot Shield Premium, you have access to 24/7 tech support. The Hotspot Shield support team can help with issues related to your connection and internet speed and answer general questions about your VPN.
On the Hotspot Shield website, select Support to access answers to common questions. If you're signed in to Hotspot Shield, you can also select Live Chat or Email Us to connect directly to their support team.
