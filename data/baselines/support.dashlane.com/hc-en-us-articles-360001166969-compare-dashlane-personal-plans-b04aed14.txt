Compare Dashlane personal plans
Updated September 17, 2026 18:22
Dashlane offers two plans to make your online life easier and more secure: Premium and Friends & Family. Review the following features to decide which Dashlane plan meets your needs. When you're ready, sign up for the plan that's right for you.
View plan pricing and sign up on our website
Want to try a complimentary 14-day trial of select Premium features or view options for changing your subscription? Check out these articles:
Try Dashlane with a free Premium trial
View options to change your subscription
Looking for a plan for your business or organization? Check out this article:
Compare Dashlane professional plans
Dashlane no longer offers or sells these plans: Dashlane Free, Premium Plus, Premium Plus Family, Essentials, and Advanced. Dashlane also no longer offers monthly subscriptions for any plan. We're simplifying our plans to focus on the features that are shared by all our customers. Depending on where you purchased your plan, you may be able to continue to renew your current plan with an annual subscription. If your current plan is no longer available, you can purchase an existing plan.
