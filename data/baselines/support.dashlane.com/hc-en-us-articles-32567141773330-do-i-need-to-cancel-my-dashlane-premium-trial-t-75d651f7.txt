Do I need to cancel my Dashlane Premium trial to avoid charges?
UPDATED NOVEMBER 2, 2026
If you signed up for your free trial without payment details, your trial ends automatically at no cost. If you provided payment details, cancel before your trial ends to avoid a charge.
