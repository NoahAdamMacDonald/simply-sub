Dashlane Friends & Family plan
UPDATED OCTOBER 29, 2026
With Dashlane's Friends & Family plan, you and nine members of your close circle can share a Dashlane subscription. Each person's account is independent, which means your data remains private. If you choose, you can share logins and personal information with other members of your Friends & Family plan.
What's included in a Friends & Family plan?
You can buy a Friends & Family plan or get Friends & Family benefits as part of your Dashlane plan at your organization. You can also join someone else's Friends & Family if they invite you.
When you buy a Friends & Family plan or get Friends & Family through your organization, you become the plan manager. As a manager, you'll enjoy the benefits of Dashlane Premium, including a VPN for Wi-Fi protection. Anyone you invite to join will have access to the same Premium features without the VPN.
