How to scan for malware and remove threats on a Windows PC
This is a one-time scan for a computer, not an in-browser file, URL or website checker.
Download ESET Online Scanner
Click the “Download” button to download ESET Online Scanner, a free malware scanner for Windows. No lengthy forms are required, just a quick, secure download.
Launch ESET Online Scanner
Once downloaded, launch ESET Online Scanner. The interface will guide you through each step of the malware scan.
Start a system scan
Start a full malware scan of your Windows PC. The virus cleaner checks for viruses and other threats, with a progress bar and clear indicators showing what is being scanned.
Review any detected items and remove or quarantine them
After the scan, review detected threats in a detailed report. Follow the prompts to quarantine or remove each malware item safely.
Next steps after a one-time malware scan
If the scan finds threats, consider the full protection trial for continuous, real-time malware protection, plus a firewall, powerful scam and data protection, and automatic updates.
