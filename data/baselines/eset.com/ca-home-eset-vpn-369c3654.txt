Versatile Connection Protocols
Tailor your security with our range of VPN protocols. From the robustness of OpenVPN to the speed of WireGuard®, choose the protocol that best fits your needs for secure, fast, and reliable online protection.
WireGuard® A free, open-source communication protocol. It is typically faster than OpenVPN (called UDP and TCP in the applications) and more flexible than IKEv2, making it a great option for securing user’s online activity.
IKEv2 (IPsec) IKEv2 is a tunneling protocol, providing a secure key exchange session that is paired with IPsec to ensure data encryption and authentication. It is usually the fastest, but can be easily blocked.
UDP (OpenVPN) This mode uses OpenVPN protocol. UDP is usually the fastest protocol to run OpenVPN on due to less data verification, but can also be blocked quite easily.
TCP (OpenVPN) This mode uses OpenVPN protocol. Due to multiple data verifications it could be slower but it is much more resilient to bad network conditions and ensures stable connections.
Stealth Encapsulates OpenVPN in a TLS tunnel via Stunnel. It is an OpenVPN tunnel masked to look like HTTPS traffic. Usage of this protocol is recommended if all other methods fail.
Wstunnel Encapsulates OpenVPN in a WebSocket. Usage of this protocol is recommended if all other methods fail.

Frequently Asked Questions
How to download ESET VPN?
Log in to your ESET HOME account.
Click Security features in the Features section.
Click Explore feature in the VPN tile.
Select your ESET HOME Security Ultimate subscription and click Continue.
Select a profile you want to protect and click Continue.
Select the number of devices you want to protect and click Send instructions. In this step, you will receive an email with VPN activation code.
Each VPN activation code is valid for 24 hours and can only be used on one device.
Click Download VPN.
Chose your operating system (Windows, MacOS, Android, or iOS) and click Download VPN. For Windows or MacOS, double-click the installer file to start the installation and activation process.
For Android or iOS, you will be redirected to the Google Play Store or the App Store, respectively. Tap 'Install' to proceed.
How to install and activate ESET VPN?
After downloading ESET VPN on your device, launch the installation file (for Windows or MacOS) or open the application (for Android or iOS).
Click Continue. By clicking Continue, you accept the End User License Agreement and acknowledge the Privacy Policy.
Type or copy/paste the VPN activation code you received in the email from ESET HOME and click Activate. Read more about managing of security features via User profiles in ESET HOME. The Activation code consists of 10 characters, including capital letters and numbers, with a set expiration time of 24 hours. If you try to activate the application after this time, it will return the status 'Activation code has expired.' In such cases, you need to request a new Activation code and activate the application within 24 hours.
Select the appropriate location and change the protocol as needed. Click the power button to turn on the ESET VPN.
Your ESET VPN is now connected.

What is a VPN and why do I need it?
A virtual private network (VPN) is a commonly used method of securing communication over the internet. As opposed to a public network, in which data is transferred unencrypted, a VPN creates a tunnel between your computer and a private server and encrypts all communications that pass through it.
VPNs are a popular method of adding security to otherwise unencrypted communication between your computer and any web property. While VPNs have been in use for many years, their popularity has increased recently due to changes in privacy laws that dictate how Internet service providers (ISPs) and advertisers can use data they collect about your browsing history.
ESET VPN feature is part of ESET Security Ultimate. Learn more about ESET HOME subscription tiers.
How does a VPN work?
When you connect to a location using the ESET VPN app, your device is assigned a new IP address, securing and encrypting your online traffic. This process, detailed in our blog post on how VPNs work, enhances your privacy by making it harder for third parties to track your activities online and steal data. The encryption occurs in real-time, ensuring your information remains protected as you browse.

When should I use ESET VPN?
ESET VPN should be used whenever you're concerned about your online privacy and security, especially in scenarios where your internet connection might not be entirely secure. Here are some common situations when activating ESET VPN is highly recommended:
Public Wi-Fi networks: Using public Wi-Fi, such as at cafes, airports, or hotels, can expose you to security risks. ESET VPN encrypts your connection, protecting your data from hackers and eavesdroppers.
Remote work: If you're accessing your workplace's systems and sensitive data remotely, ESET VPN ensures that your connection to your company's network is secure, safeguarding both personal and company data.
Privacy from ISPs: Your Internet Service Provider (ISP) can see everything you do online. ESET VPN hides your online activity, ensuring your privacy and preventing your ISP from tracking your browsing habits or throttling your internet speed based on usage.
Safe online banking and shopping: When managing your finances or shopping online, ESET VPN adds an extra layer of security, protecting your financial details from potential cyber threats.
Gaming: ESET VPN can improve your gaming experience by reducing ping times and protecting you from DDoS attacks.
