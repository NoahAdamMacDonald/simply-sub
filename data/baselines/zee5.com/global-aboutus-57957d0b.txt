About Zee 5
Zee 5 is India’s largest homegrown video streaming platform and the global window for South Asian content, reaching viewers across 190+ countries. As the digital entertainment destination of Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd., Zee 5 is driven by the philosophy of a language-first platform experience with hyper-local content, language-specific packs and deeply personalised experiences across seven languages; Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Malayalam, Kannada, and Bangla.
The platform is also home to India's boldest stories, bringing together original voices, iconic franchises and distinctive storytelling across languages and genres.
With a vast library spanning Movies, Series, Live Sports, AI-powered storytelling, Animation, Kids' Entertainment and premium International Content, Zee 5 is the destination for diverse, multilingual entertainment and live sporting experiences for Indians everywhere. Through Zee Entertainment’s long-term partnership with FIFA till 2034, the platform is also home to FIFA’s premier football tournaments in India, including the FIFA World Cup 2026™ and 2030, alongside elite action from Bundesliga, Serie A, and thrilling cricket moments.
