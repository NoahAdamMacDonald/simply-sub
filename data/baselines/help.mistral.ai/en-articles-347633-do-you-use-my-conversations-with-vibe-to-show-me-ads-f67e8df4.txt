All Collections Trust, Security, Compliance Ethical and Safe Use Do you use my conversations with Vibe to show me ads? Do you use my conversations with Vibe to show me ads?
August 12, 2026
Mistral does not use the content of your conversations with Vibe for marketing or advertising purposes.
Provided you have enabled such cookies upon your first visit to our website or services, marketing cookies set by our partners may help us deliver more effective marketing to you, by showing relevant ads on other websites.
🔑 These cookies use your browser and device’s unique identifier to display those ads, not the content of your conversations.
