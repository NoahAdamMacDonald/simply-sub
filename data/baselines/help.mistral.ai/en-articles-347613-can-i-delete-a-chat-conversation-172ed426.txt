Can I delete a chat conversation?
August 12, 2026
Input and output are maintained by default so you can see your conversation history in the product.
However, you can delete a chat conversation, in its entirety and at any time by following this simple process:
Reveal the conversation dropdown
On Vibe page, click on the three vertical dots ⋮ on the right-hand side of your conversation title to reveal the conversation dropdown, then select the Delete option.
​
Select the Delete option
​
Confirm your choice
A modal will appear asking for your to confirm your choice.
Click on the Delete button. The chat conversation has now been deleted.
​
Confirm the chat deletion by clicking the Delete button
​
🚨 This operation is definitive on your side. You won’t be able to retrieve your conversation after deletion.
Delay before definitive deletion of the underlying data
Even after your manual deletion on your end, we may retain different types of data in our backend systems for different amounts of time, as specified in our Privacy Policy and/or your Data Protection Agreement with us.
For instance, we may retain your input and output data to enforce our Usage Policy, address Terms of Service or policy violations, or as required by law.
📌 For the Team and Enterprise plans, please note that your Organization may have separate controls in place based on its own data retention policies. Check with your organization for additional guidance.
