Do you sell or share my data for marketing?
Do you sell or share my data for marketing?
August 12, 2026
Mistral does not sell or share your personal data for marketing or advertising purposes.
Provided you have enabled such cookies upon your first visit to our website or services, marketing cookies set by our partners may help us deliver more effective marketing to you, by showing relevant ads on other websites.
🔑 These cookies do not allow our partners to identify you directly.
🔎 For more information on these matters, please refer to our Privacy Policy and our Cookie Policy.
