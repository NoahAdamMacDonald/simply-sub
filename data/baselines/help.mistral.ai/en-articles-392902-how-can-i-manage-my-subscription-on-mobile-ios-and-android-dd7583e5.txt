All Collections Help and Troubleshooting How can I manage my subscription on mobile (iOS and Android)? How can I manage my subscription on mobile (iOS and Android)?
August 12, 2026
Whether you’re using the app for iOS or Android, you can manage or cancel your subscription directly in the application.
How to manage and cancel your subscription
1. Open the settings menu
In the app:
Tap the menu icon at the very-top left of the app to reveal the panel.
Tap on your Organization name at the bottom of the screen.
The Settings menu opens.
2. Go to your subscription details
In the Settings menu:
Locate the Subscription option under the Account section.
If you're subscribed to a Pro plan, click on it to view your subscription details.
🔑 Mobile subscription management is available only for Pro plans purchased in the mobile app. If you purchased Pro on the web, or if you have a Team plan or higher, manage your subscription from the web instead.
​
If your Pro plan is bundled with your mobile plan or billed by your mobile carrier, the subscription is managed by the carrier: use your carrier’s support process.
💡 Alternatively, you can view your subscription directly in your device’s subscription center, as explained below ⬇️.
3. Manage or cancel your Pro subscription
On the subscription details screen:
Tap Manage subscription to open your device’s subscription management center.
You will be redirected to your provider's store where you can:
iOS (App Store):
View your subscription details, including price and next billing date
Switch between the Monthly and Yearly plans by tapping See All Plans and selecting your plan
Cancel your subscription by tapping Cancel Subscription
Android (Google Play):
View your subscription details
Manage your payment method
Cancel your subscription by tapping Cancel Subscription at the bottom of the screen
📌 These store-management steps are provided for guidance only. Apple and Google may update their interfaces or processes at any time, and Mistral may not be notified of those changes.
FAQ
What happens if I cancel my subscription?
Canceling your subscription immediately stops automatic renewal of your Pro plan.
You’ll keep access until the end of your current billing period (monthly or yearly), then revert to Free mode.
If you want to reactivate before the end of your billing period, you can resubscribe. You won’t be charged again until the next cycle.
If you cancel during the 14-day trial period, you may be eligible for a refund. For all refund scenarios (web, App Store, Google Play, carrier, Education plan), see refund policy.
What is the refund policy for mobile subscriptions?
To request a refund, contact the store where you purchased your subscription (Apple support or Google Play support) and follow their process.
Refund requests sent directly to our support team cannot be processed for App Store or Google Play purchases.
Subscribed through a mobile carrier? Free Mobile subscriptions are managed from your Free Mobile customer account. Orange / NJU offers include 6 months free and end automatically after the offer period. Mistral cannot refund carrier-managed subscriptions directly.
For all refund scenarios (web, App Store, Google Play, carrier, Education plan), see refund policy.
I can’t find my subscription in the store. What should I do?
If you don’t see your subscription, you may be signed in with a different Apple or Google account than the one used for purchase.
Switch to the correct account if possible. You can identify which account was used by checking your original purchase receipt email.
If you still can’t find it, contact support.
The app says I’m not Pro, but the store shows I’m subscribed. What should I do?
If you purchased the subscription on Android, first check whether Google Play is waiting for you to confirm the purchase. Open Google Play, go to Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, then tap Open the app.
Until this Google Play confirmation is completed, Pro may not be linked to your Mistral account. If you do not confirm within 3 days, Google cancels and refunds the subscription.
If the purchase is already confirmed, this can happen when the subscription is linked to a different email address than the one currently signed in.
Pro plan purchases are tied to the email address used at purchase. Check which account received the confirmation and sign in with that email.
If you subscribed through Google Play, your purchase might still need confirmation. Open Google Play, go to Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, select your Pro subscription, then tap Open to open the app from that page.
Until you complete this step, Pro is not applied to your account. If the purchase is not confirmed within 3 days, Google Play cancels it and refunds the payment.
If you can’t find it, contact our support team with your order number from Apple or Google.
How do I locate my subscription in the App Store or Google Play?
You can access your subscription directly in the store. Follow the official tutorials below:
From the App Store → Settings > Subscriptions.
From Google Play → Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
