NordVPN Privacy Notice
Last updated: July 19, 2026
The Privacy Policy explains how Nord's websites, applications, and Services process personal data, and these practices also apply to the NordVPN product. However, the provision of services related specifically to NordVPN (“NordVPN Services”) involves the processing of additional personal data. More details are provided below.
GENERAL REMARKS
Nord guarantees a strict no-logs policy for NordVPN Services, meaning that your internet activity while using NordVPN Services is not monitored, recorded, logged, stored, or passed on to any third party. We do not store used bandwidth, traffic logs, IP addresses, or browsing information. From the moment a NordVPN user connects to one of our VPN servers, their online traffic becomes encrypted.
We use certain non-personal technical data (such as statistical information about server load) to ensure optimal service quality and continuity.
ADDITIONAL INFORMATION PROCESSED WHEN PROVIDING NORDVPN SERVICES
In addition to the information provided in the Privacy Policy, we process the following data when you use NordVPN Services:
The last session information. It is used to limit the amount of concurrent active user sessions and is automatically deleted within 15 minutes after a session is terminated.
Connectivity information. We process information on whether a user has used the NordVPN Service within the last 90 days. We only know if the NordVPN Service was used – Nord does not collect any other personal data in this case. This data is required to prevent abuse and to enable us to dispute unfair chargebacks.
NORDVPN APPLICATIONS
NordVPN Services are provided through dedicated applications for mobile, desktop, smart TV, or other supported devices and browser extensions ("Applications"). To ensure that these Applications work correctly, we automatically collect certain technical information required to provide you with the Services, keep them secure, maintain compatibility with a device, apply required settings, and deliver important Service-related notifications.
The Applications only collect device-level information that does not identify the exact device used or the individual NordVPN user, and that cannot be linked between devices. By default, we collect only what is necessary for the operation of the Application and the provision of our Services. The collection of analytics and advertising data is optional and may be managed by you. Analytics data helps us improve the Application and is designed to work without identifying users. Advertising data, if enabled, provides us with a small amount of additional information (such as device-level language preferences or features in use) which is processed to optimize our marketing campaigns, as described in the Privacy Policy.
3.1. Types of Application data collected
We design our systems with privacy in mind. Identifiers used within the Applications are randomly generated on the device and cannot be linked to a specific user, their email address, Account, or online activity. Application information is stored separately and cannot be connected to the online behavior of any individual NordVPN user. The data collected through Applications may include:
Device and system information, including basic device characteristics (such as device's country, city, or time zone), general operating system details, and an anonymized identifier generated by the Application.
Application information, including the Application name, version, and selected settings.
Event and Application usage information, including binary yes/no indicators of success or failure (such as whether a login or registration attempt was completed) and which features were accessed.
Connection and general network information, such as whether you are using Wi-Fi or mobile data, the name of the public internet service provider, whether the Application is connected to a VPN server, and relevant technical connection attributes (like the protocol used and the server connected to by the Application).
Diagnostic and performance information, including crash logs, error events, service availability state, and configuration failures.
Service configuration information, such as the randomly generated ID used to verify successful Application initialization and to deliver remote configuration settings to a specific Application.
Device identifiers provided by the operating system. If you consent to us collecting advertising data for Applications on Android devices, we may access a resettable device identifier made available by the operating system. This identifier is only used to measure the effectiveness of our own marketing campaigns – it is not used for targeted advertising or profiling. It can be reset at any time (Managing your Google Settings) and is not linked to any other data collected through the Application. We do not collect equivalent identifiers on iOS.
3.2. Use of third-party service providers
While we primarily rely on our internal tools to provide Services, some of our Applications may use third-party service providers.
When third-party providers are needed to provide essential Application features or service-related notifications, they act as our data processors and process only the technical data strictly required for a specific purpose. As set out in our Privacy Policy, third-party providers for analytics or advertising purposes are engaged only when you provide your consent.
You can check which specific providers your Application uses in the "Privacy Preferences" section of the Application settings.
SCAM AND PHISHING PROTECTION BY NORDVPN
The data we process about the users of NordVPN's Scam and Phishing Protection service depends on how they use this service, but in general, we only process the information that is necessary for the service.
Scam and Phishing Protection service (included in all NordVPN subscription plans at no additional charge) is a DNS-based service that checks domains against known malicious or unsafe domain lists and blocks ads, trackers, and harmful sites. It does not use any personal data other than what is strictly necessary for the provision of the NordVPN.
Scam, Phishing, and Malware Protection service (the advanced version provided with certain NordVPN plans), is a security service that provides enhanced protection against multiple online threats. It processes only the minimal technical information required to identify harmful activity and does not collect or store your browsing history, the content of the pages you visited, the content of your communications, or any other similar personal data. Scam, Phishing, and Malware Protection has several sub-features:
Advanced browsing protection, which identifies unsafe or suspicious websites before you interact with them. It checks domain names, URLs, publicly available crypto-wallet addresses found online, and limited hashed cookie data against databases of known malicious or fraudulent resources. This allows the feature to block harmful domains, detect fraudulent crypto-wallets, warn about phishing or scam sites, flag potentially risky search results, identify possible session hijacking, and check emails for suspicious links.
All processing takes place in real time and uses only the data strictly necessary to detect threats. Most analysis occurs locally on your device and, in limited cases, minimal non-personal indicators (such as websites “reported as safe”) may be sent to our threat intelligence systems solely to verify suspected threats and improve functionality.
Ad and tracker blocker, which helps reduce unwanted advertisements and limit third-party tracking. It operates locally on your device by comparing domains and URL elements against advertising and tracking blocklists to block ads, restrict tracking scripts, and remove tracking parameters from URLs.
If you have provided your consent to sharing Application-level analytics data, we may in certain limited cases process minimal anonymous indicators related to feature performance (for example, whether a blocking rule was applied) to improve the effectiveness of the feature. Such information cannot be linked to an individual user.
Anti-malware scans newly downloaded files to determine whether they are malicious and, depending on the device's operating system, installed applications for known security vulnerabilities. For this purpose, we only process the scan results (that is, whether a file was identified as malicious and whether the scan was completed successfully). We may also process the URL from which a file was downloaded to help identify and block harmful sources. For vulnerability scanning, which is available on the Windows platform, we only process the application name and version to check for known vulnerabilities.
When the initial file scan cannot tell if a file is malicious, that file may be uploaded to the cloud for a deep scan to detect malware. This option is only available for users that have enabled cloud-based threat scanner functionality. Only executable files may be uploaded for this purpose – other file types, which may contain personal data (such as .doc, .pdf, .jpg), will never be uploaded. Files sent to the cloud are processed in a way that ensures anonymity: we only analyze the technical indicators required for detection (such as file hashes or minimal metadata) and do not access or retain the full contents of the file.
Scam, Phishing, and Malware Protection also displays statistics on how this service operates, such as the number of malicious files identified, items blocked, and similar metrics. This information is stored locally on your device and shown only to you. We may receive only aggregated totals to monitor overall performance, with no link to individual users.
OTHER SERVICES/FEATURES BY NORDVPN
Meshnet. This service enables you to establish a point-to-point connection and route your online traffic through another device belonging to you or another NordVPN user. When you use this service, we process the following information: details on the connections and permissions you've created, the device's OS and its version, the hashed device ID, and the user's email address. If you use file sharing, we also process anonymized information about the number of files transferred, file sizes, and the type of documents (extensions) shared.
Dedicated IP. A dedicated IP address is a unique internet protocol (“IP”) address assigned exclusively to the user. The dedicated IP service is a subscription that gives the user a specific dedicated IP address (this IP address may be assigned to other users afterwards). Please be aware that if you purchase a dedicated IP address, your email and information related to your account will be linked to that IP address.
Dedicated server. A dedicated server is a private virtual server exclusively allocated to a single user. The dedicated server service is a subscription that provides the user with isolated hardware resources and a static dedicated IP address for the subscription period (this server may be reallocated to other users afterwards). Please be aware that if you purchase a dedicated server, your email, IP address, and information related to your account will be linked to that server.
Smart DNS. The Smart DNS service replaces the DNS server address provided by your internet service provider with a different one. Please be aware that this does not provide you with a new IP address. Due to the nature of this service, we have to store your IP address on our systems when you activate the SmartDNS service.
Dark Web Monitor. This service scans whether your email address or any of the other assets you've added to monitoring have appeared in any known data leaks. All generated reports for an asset are wiped as soon as you remove it from monitoring.
Transaction Monitoring. This service allows you to link your bank accounts to enable monitoring of your financial transactions for suspicious or unusual activity. To provide this service, we connect to your bank using a third-party platform, retrieve your transaction history, and use an automated analysis, which may include machine learning, to derive statistical patterns that represent typical account activity. These patterns are used as a reference point to analyze new transactions and alert you to potential fraudulent activity. To ensure ongoing monitoring performance, we check your latest account activity against these statistical patterns.
By enabling this feature, you authorize the retrieval and real-time processing of your transaction data solely for the purpose of detecting unusual activity and generating security alerts.
To provide the service, we process transaction amounts, counterparties, timestamps, and geographic or currency-related indicators. We do not store individual transactions: once the relevant statistical patterns have been derived, the underlying details are discarded and are not retained on our infrastructure. The only data we retain is linked bank account information, including institution name, account type, account display name, and the last four digits of the account number, together with the derived statistical patterns necessary to continue providing the monitoring service.
When you remove a linked account from monitoring, we immediately lose access to your account data through the third-party platform, and we delete the last four digits of the associated account number, as well as generated alerts, and all derived statistical patterns related to that account.
Call Protection. This service identifies and flags incoming phone calls, such as potential scams or other potentially unwanted call types. We only process the incoming call data that is strictly necessary to provide this service. We never access or process the actual content of the call - we only process metadata such as the caller's phone number, its assigned reputation flag, and, if available, the caller ID. This information is handled based on our legitimate interest to protect users from the widely recognized risks associated with scam and fraudulent calls. Where necessary, we engage trusted data processors to support the caller reputation and flagging functionality.
Message Protection. This feature helps identify potentially harmful SMS messages - such as scams or other suspicious content - in real time. When you receive an SMS from an unknown sender, the message content, any included URLs, and the sender's phone number are analyzed by our backend to assess the potential risk of the message. We do not store SMS message content on our servers. Message content is used only for real-time threat assessment and is not retained after threat analysis. Limited information is stored only on your device for functionality and usage insights, such as the time a message was checked and the resulting threat classification.
