SUBSCRIPTION PERIOD AND PRICES
All our paid services are provided on a recurring Subscription basis. We may offer one or more Subscription plans for purchase, each with a different Subscription length. You can choose one of the offered Subscription periods when you purchase the Services ("Initial Subscription Period"). Your designated Initial Subscription Period will also be stated clearly in the purchase confirmation email you receive from us.
Please note that after your chosen Initial Subscription Period, your Subscription to the Services will be automatically renewed for an additional period as described below (each such period referred to as a "Renewal Period").
The renewal prices displayed at our Websites at the time of your purchase of the Initial Subscription Period are subject to change. If the renewal price has changed, you will be charged the price valid at the time of the automatic renewal of your Subscription.
When you purchase the Services on a Subscription basis (e.g., monthly, annually, or otherwise), you agree that (i) the Services will auto-renew until you or we cancel the auto-renewal; (ii) you are authorizing recurring payments; (iii) the payments will be made to Nord using your provided payment method and at the recurring intervals you have agreed to.
AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
Auto-renewal. Unless canceled, your Subscription will automatically renew at the end of the Initial Subscription Period for additional Renewal Periods as follows:
Subscriptions of one (1) year or longer will renew annually for one (1) year Renewal Periods.
Subscriptions shorter than one (1) year will renew for the same length as the Initial Subscription Period each time.
Zero authorization. By purchasing a Subscription, you authorize Nord to initiate an authorization transaction equal to zero (or a nominal amount not exceeding 1 USD/1 EUR or an equivalent in another currency, which is then instantaneously reverted) up to forty (40) days prior to the Subscription renewal date. Zero authorization is used to ensure that your payment method is still valid and to prevent a disruption in your Subscription. Zero authorization does not represent an actual charge of your account and will not be used if you choose to cancel the automatic renewal of your Subscription before the authorization is to take place.
Reminder. We will send you a notification reminding you about the upcoming Renewal Period and specifying the then-current renewal price thirty (30) days before we are due to charge you (except for users of monthly and shorter Subscriptions).
If you do not wish to continue using our Services, you have the right to cancel the automatic renewal of your Subscription for the upcoming Service renewal period (as described below).
RECURRING PAYMENTS
Subscription charges. Charges for the upcoming Renewal Period will be applied using your provided payment method based on your Subscription plan:
Monthly plans: Charged on the day of the auto-renewal (the start of the Renewal Period).
All other plans: Charged fourteen (14) days prior to the start of the Renewal Period.
Sometimes, Nord is unable to charge the user by the Subscription renewal date because the transaction fails on the user's side (for example, due to an expired card or insufficient funds). In such cases, Nord reserves the right to initiate automatic payment retries for any failed transactions within fourteen (14) days of the billing date. By purchasing the Services, you acknowledge and accept Nord's right to perform such payment retries in an effort to process the necessary charges and ensure the provision of the Services. If our retries are unsuccessful and your payment cannot be processed, we may, at our discretion, suspend or terminate your access to the Services.
CANCELLATION AND REFUND
Auto-renewal cancellation. You have the right to cancel the automatic renewal of your Subscription for the upcoming Renewal Period; however, the method by which you can cancel depends on the channel through which the Subscription was purchased.
If you have purchased directly from us: To prevent your Subscription's auto-renewal and the related charge, please cancel the auto-renewal before the billing date. This can be done from your Nord Account by following these steps:
Log in to your Account.
Open the "Billing" section.
Choose "Cancel Auto-renewal."
Confirm the cancellation by clicking "Cancel Auto-renewal."
Please note that cancelling auto-renewal will stop future charges, but it does not automatically entitle you to a refund of (i) any fees that you have already paid or (ii) any remaining period of your current Subscription, unless the applicable law provides otherwise.
If you have purchased from a third party: Your cancellation options are subject to the terms of the third party you purchased from.
