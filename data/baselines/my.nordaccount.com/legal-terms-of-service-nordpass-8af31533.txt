File Storage and Documents. You may attach (store) files to items in your NordPass vault. Files are encrypted on your device before upload or store encrypted documents in your NordPass vault. Email Masking. Email Masking is therefore not covered by the zero-knowledge architecture applicable to the contents of your NordPass vault. Identity Masking. This feature allows you to generate substitute identity details for use with third-party services. Once deleted, the NordPass Account and any related NordPass Services will no longer be accessible.
Prior to deletion, NordPass will provide notice, typically to the email address associated with your NordPass Account. To prevent deletion due to dormancy, the NordPass Account must reflect at least one of the following activities within each thirty-six (36) month period.
Accounts with an active Premium or Family Version will not be classified as dormant and will not be subject to deletion under this section.
