FAQ
What is OpenCode?
OpenCode is an open source agent that helps you write and run code with any AI model. It's available as a desktop app, a terminal interface, a mobile app and an SDK.
How do I use OpenCode?
Do I need extra AI subscriptions to use OpenCode?
Not necessarily. OpenCode comes with a set of free models that you can use without creating an account. You can even connect your local models. OpenCode also works with all popular providers, including OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI. OpenCode Models give you access to popular coding models that we've tested and benchmarked for coding agents.
Can I use my existing AI subscriptions with OpenCode?
Can I only use OpenCode in the terminal?
No. OpenCode is also available as a desktop app and a mobile app, and the SDK lets you control it from your own code.
How much does OpenCode cost?
What about data and privacy?
Your data and information is only stored when you use our free models. Learn more about our models.
