Questions about Console or its integrations, a problem to report, or a request about your data: email us. You don't need an account.

Your data
To ask for a copy of your data or for it to be deleted, email help@anoma.ly from the address on your OpenCode account or, without one, from the address of the account your request is about, such as your Slack or Zoom account, so we can confirm it's you. The privacy policy explains how Anomaly handles personal data.
To end an integration's access yourself, open it in Console's Extensions tab and click Disconnect. Each integration's guide says what that deletes.
Security
To report a security issue in Console or one of its integrations, email security@anoma.ly with what you found and how to reproduce it. For the open source OpenCode client, use Report a vulnerability on GitHub.
