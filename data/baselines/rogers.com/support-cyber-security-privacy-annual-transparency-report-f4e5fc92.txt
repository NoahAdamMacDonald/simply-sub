Rogers' annual Transparency Report
At Rogers, we deal with sensitive customer data every day and we do our utmost to protect this information.
Since 2013, we have published the details of the number of requests for customer personal information received from law enforcement and government agencies in our annual Transparency Report. This report is an important part of our commitment to protecting our customers’ information.
For the full details, view page 22 of our 2023 Data Supplement.
