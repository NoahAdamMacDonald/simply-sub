Cancelling your Acorn subscription on Google Play
❗ Deleting the Acorn TV app or channel from your device does not cancel your subscription. You must follow the correct cancel instructions in order to stop being billed.
If you signed up via Google Play through the Acorn TV Android app, you can cancel your subscription by following these steps:
Cancel on a Computer Go to play.google.com/store/account
In the left menu, click My Subscriptions (⚠️)
Next to Acorn TV, select Cancel Subscription
Select Cancel Subscription again to confirm
Once you've completed the steps above, you will see a message stating, “You have successfully cancelled your subscription,” and you will receive an email from Google Play confirming the cancellation.
Direct on Google Wallet Go to https://wallet.google.com and sign in with the Google account that you purchased the subscription with (in most cases, this is the Google account registered to your Android device).
Click on "Bills And Accounts" on the left navigation panel.
You will see a list of all Google Wallet transactions. Locate your Acorn TV Subscription Purchase, click on it, and then on the right, click on "Manage your subscription".
Click on "Cancel subscription."
A pop-up window will ask for a confirmation on the subscription cancellation. After that, your subscription will be cancelled.
Cancel on an Android Phone Open the Google Play Store
Tap the Menu icon
Select Subscriptions
Select Acorn TV (⚠️)
Select Cancel Subscription.
Select Cancel Subscription again to confirm.
Once you've completed the above steps, you will see a brief pop-up stating “Subscription will be canceled,” and you will receive an email from Google Play confirming the cancellation.
Your subscription will stop at the end of the current billing cycle.
⚠️ If you don't see Acorn TV listed as one of your subscriptions, then the Google Play account that you are signed in to is not the one paying for your subscription.
Please repeat the steps above while signed in to the Google Play account that is paying for the Acorn TV subscription.
If you can't locate the Google Play account attached to your Acorn TV subscription please contact Google Play for support below.
