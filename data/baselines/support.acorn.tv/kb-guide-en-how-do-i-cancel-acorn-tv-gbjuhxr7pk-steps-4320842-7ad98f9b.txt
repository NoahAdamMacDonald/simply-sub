Amazon Prime Channel Add-On
❗ Deleting the Acorn TV app or channel from your device does not cancel your subscription. You must follow the correct cancel instructions in order to stop being billed.
If you purchased the Acorn TV add-on channel via Amazon Prime, you will need to go into the Video Subscription settings in your Amazon Prime account to cancel your account.
To cancel your subscription on Amazon Prime, follow the instructions below:
Go to Manage Your Prime Video Channels
Look under 'Prime Video Channels' to find Acorn TV
Select the 'Prime Video Channels' option and confirm
Once you cancel, the renewal date in your Acorn TV Channel details becomes the end date, and you'll no longer be charged for it each month, however, you can continue to access the Acorn TV Channel through Prime Video up until the end date.
If you still need help, please contact the Amazon support Team below.
