Cancelling Directly at Acorn.tv
❗ Deleting the Acorn TV app or channel from your device does not cancel your subscription. You must follow the correct cancel instructions in order to stop being billed.
If you signed up with a credit card via our website, you must cancel through a web browser on your computer, tablet, or via the mobile web on your smartphone.
At the moment, you cannot cancel your account in our apps for iOS and Android.
Click on My Acorn TV in the top right-hand corner of the site
Click on to the Manage Account section
Click the option to Cancel Membership on the left side of the screen
Confirm the cancel by clicking cancel Acorn TV
If you don't see a "Cancel Button", you are likely subscribed through a third party. Please contact them to manage or cancel your account.
