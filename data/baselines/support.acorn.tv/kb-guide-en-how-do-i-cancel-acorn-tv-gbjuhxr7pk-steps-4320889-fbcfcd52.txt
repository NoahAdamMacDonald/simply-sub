Cancelling your Acorn subscription on iTunes
❗ Deleting the Acorn TV app or channel from your device does not cancel your subscription. You must follow the correct cancel instructions in order to stop being billed.
If you signed up via the Acorn TV app via iTunes / Apple, you can cancel your subscription by following these steps:
Cancel on a Computer Download or open iTunes
If you are not already signed in, you will need to sign in with your Apple ID
To sign in, select Account from the menu bar at the top of your screen or at the top of the iTunes window, then select Sign In. You'll be asked to enter your Apple ID and password.
Select Account from the menu bar at the top of your screen or at the top of the iTunes window, then select View My Account. You may be asked to sign in
On the Account Information screen, scroll to the Settings section
To the right of Subscriptions, select Manage
To the right of the Acorn TV subscription, select Edit (⚠️)
Select Cancel Subscription from the bottom of the page
Select Confirm
Cancel on an iPhone or iPad On the Home screen, tap Settings
Tap your name
Tap iTunes & App Store
Tap your Apple ID, then tap View Apple ID. You might need to sign in or use Touch ID.
Tap Subscriptions
Select Acorn TV (⚠️)
Select Cancel Subscription from the bottom of the page.
Select Confirm
Your subscription will stop at the end of the current billing cycle.
⚠️ If you don't see an Acorn TV subscription, make sure that you're signed in with the correct Apple ID. If you are still unable to find your subscription, see below to contact Apple support for help locating and cancelling your subscription.
If you don't see an Acorn TV subscription and only have one Apple ID, you might have subscribed directly through Acorn TV and not through iTunes. In this case, please see the Cancelling through Acorn.tv article below.
Contact Apple Support
