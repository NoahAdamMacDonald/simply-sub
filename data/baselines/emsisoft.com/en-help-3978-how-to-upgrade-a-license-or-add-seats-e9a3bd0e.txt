How to upgrade a license or add seats
December 29, 2022
min read
When you need to protect more devices than your license allows, you can upgrade your current subscription through your Management Console at MyEmsisoft portal on https://my.emsisoft.com , click on your workspace and then on “settings”.
From there you can choose the new total amount of seats or the new plan.
You can then click on “renew now / upgrade now” to be forwarded to a shopping cart. The next page will pro-rate the unused time of your subscription and reduce its final cost before you pay.
Your 1, 2 or 3 years subscription will start from the moment the payment is made, which will become your new billing and renewal date.
Please note that If you are deployed with an alternative RMM such as Atera, Kabuto or Syncro, it’s advised to contact your distributor for further assistance.
