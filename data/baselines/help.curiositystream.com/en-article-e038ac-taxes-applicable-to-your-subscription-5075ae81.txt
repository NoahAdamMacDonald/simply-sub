Taxes Applicable to Your Subscription
Last updated Fri, Jan 23, 2026
Certain jurisdictions require Curiosity Stream to charge a sales tax, value-added tax (VAT), or similar such tax to subscribers who are located within a taxing jurisdiction. Starting in February 2016, Curiosity Stream will add the applicable tax, if any, to the subscription plan price for only those subscribers located in a taxing jurisdiction. Subscriber location is determined based upon the billing information provided by the subscriber. For those subscribers located in a jurisdiction that does not charge an applicable tax, no amount will be charged beyond our subscription plan price.
Some states such as Florida have a communications services tax. You can learn more about it here.
For subscribers located outside of the United States, the amounts billed may vary over billing periods due to fluctuating foreign currency rates and currency conversion fees. International customers are subject to taxes applicable to their country.
