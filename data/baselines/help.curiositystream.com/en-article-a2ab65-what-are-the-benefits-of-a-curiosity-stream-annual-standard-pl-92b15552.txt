What are the benefits of a Curiosity Stream annual standard plan?
Last updated Fri, Jan 23, 2026
You can save more than 30% by subscribing to an annual Standard plan versus a monthly Standard plan. The price per month for an annual plan is $3.34 versus $4.99 for a monthly plan. That's 33% savings! You will only be charged $39.99 plus applicable tax for the whole year (some streaming services charge close to that for only a month of service)! Can I get a refund on my Curiosity Stream annual plan? Our annual plans function similarly to our monthly plans; both plans can be canceled at any time and users continue to have access to our content through their respective prepaid billing periods. For monthly accounts, that billing period is 30 days, but for our annual plans, the billing period is a full calendar year.
The annual plans offer a better value and a substantial discount in exchange for paying upfront for a full year of service. Monthly plan holders pay higher rates for the flexibility to discontinue their plan on a month-to-month basis or to manage within their budget. By opting to save with an annual plan, the charge is non-refundable after the payment has been processed.
