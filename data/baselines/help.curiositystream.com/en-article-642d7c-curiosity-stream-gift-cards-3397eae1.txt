Curiosity Stream Gift Cards
Last updated Fri, Jan 23, 2026
How to purchase:
Follow the steps on the gift card page to purchase a digital gift card that will be emailed to the recipient on the date of your choice.
How to Redeem?
Existing Users: Visit your Curiosity Stream account page. Enter your gift code in the gift code box and click on “Apply Gift Code” to finish applying.
New Users: During the sign-up process, on the payment details page, instead of entering credit card information, you will select "Gift Card" and follow the prompts.
If you have a physical gift card, scratch off the gray area on the back to reveal your Gift Code.
Tips:
To check the details of the Gift Card, you can view under Plan Details:
The type of Gift Plan
Date plan expires
If you have any additional questions or issues with a gift card purchase or redemption, please contact us directly at 1-844-778-8999, Monday-Friday 11AM-7PM Eastern Time or anytime at help@curiositystream.com.
