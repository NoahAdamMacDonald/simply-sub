Bulk Purchase FAQ (Business, Education & Libraries)
Last updated Fri, Jan 23, 2026
For Business
How do I buy for a group?
We offer discounted bulk subscriptions for organizations looking to purchase 10 or more subscriptions. Please place your order here: https://curiositystream.com/bulk. If you are looking to buy subscriptions for a group under 10, you can purchase individual gift cards at curiositystream.com/gift.
Can I screen the documentaries in front of a small group from my organization?
A subscription to Curiosity Stream allows you to watch a Curiosity Stream program with friends and colleagues in a small, private setting, but not to screen programs for a general audience.
I’d like to buy the gift of Curiosity Stream for my employees and/or several clients. How can they access Curiosity Stream?
Once you have purchased Curiosity Stream subscriptions through www.curiositystream.com/bulk, you will receive an email with access codes that you can provide your clients or employees. They will then be able to create login credentials by entering their unique access code on www.curiositystream.com/redeem. Once they create their login, they can begin streaming immediately on www.curiositystream.com and our Curiosity Stream app on compatible devices!
How long is the subscription period for bulk subscriptions?
We offer annual subscriptions at this time. For additional questions, please contact us at corporatepartnership@curiositystream.com.
Can I buy bulk subscriptions and then be refunded for the ones that have not been activated?
No, unfortunately, bulk subscriptions are non-refundable.
For Education
How do school subscriptions work? Do I need one subscription/license per teacher, or per student?
For In-person Learning, CS requires that schools purchase one (1) subscription/license per class or educator.
For Hybrid Learning that will require signing into CS remotely outside of the classroom, we require a subscription/license per student, as individuals cannot share credentials.
Bulk pricing begins at 10 subscriptions, so we recommend that schools purchase subscriptions for multiple classrooms. If you have additional questions, please contact us at corporatepartnership@curiositystream.com. We would be happy to discuss further and answer any questions you may have.
Are supplemental guides or lesson plans available?
No, however, we are a supplemental tool for schools and institutions. If you are interested in programming support, please reach out to us at corporatepartnership@curiositystream.com.
What SSO do you support for schools?
We currently do not support SSO for educational institutions at this time, but we do provide custom access sign-in and URL. Restrictions may apply. Please contact us at corporatepartnership@curiositystream.com.
For Libraries
How can my library purchase Curiosity Stream for our patrons?
Please contact us at corporatepartnership@curiositystream.com.
What kind of integrations do you support for libraries?
We support library card number validation in the Polaris integrated library system and customized solutions available upon request.
Do you charge per download?
No, we do not; we offer the full Curiosity Stream experience for all library patrons!
