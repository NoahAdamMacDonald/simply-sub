Cancel TIDAL Subscription or Trial
Updated May 22, 2024 11:57
You can cancel your subscription or trial at any time. If you are paying for TIDAL through a third-party, such as Apple or Google Play, you will need to cancel through them directly.
Cancel subscription via Web:
Visit account.tidal.com
Log in with your username and password
Navigate to Subscription
Click Cancel Subscription and confirm cancellation
Cancel subscription via TIDAL Android app:
Click the heart icon
Click the gear icon at the top right-hand corner
Click Edit then Manage Subscription
Scroll down and click Subscription > Cancel Subscription
Cancel subscription via Apple:
For instructions on how to cancel an Apple subscription, visit this article: Apple iTunes Cancellation & In-App Purchases.
If you do not see an Active subscription within Apple Wallet, this means you're being billed through another payment method.
You can confirm your Apple account status by contacting Apple Support directly.
If you’ve downloaded TIDAL on an Apple device through the App Store, you have the option to delete your account within the TIDAL app. Learn more here.
Cancel subscription via Google Play:
On your Android device, go to subscriptions in Google Play.
Select the subscription you want to cancel.
Tap Cancel subscription.
Follow the instructions.
If your Google Play account does not show an Active Subscription, this means your account was registered using an alternate email address. For more help, you will need to contact Google Play support as TIDAL does not have access to these details.
Delete your TIDAL account
Learn more about TIDAL account deletion here.
