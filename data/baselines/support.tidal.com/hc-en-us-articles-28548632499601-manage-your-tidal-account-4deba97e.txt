Updated January 09, 2026 16:02
Navigating Your Account on Account.Tidal.com
Your Tidal account has several options that allow you to easily manage your subscription, payment details, devices, and more through account.tidal.com. Here's a quick overview of each section and its functionality.
Profile
View and update your personal details, such as your full name, email address, and date of birth. You can also manage your password, delete your account, and add your Nostr Public Key.
Adding your Nostr Public Key allows you to authenticate using your Nostr credentials. This is optional, and you can leave it blank if you prefer.
Subscription
Provides detailed information about your current Tidal subscription, including its features, and renewal date. From here, you can change your plan to one of our available Subscription Types, enable DJ Extension, or Cancel your subscription or trial.
Family (for Family Plan users)
If you're the primary account holder of a Family Plan, this is where you can manage your plan. This includes the option to invite up to five family members to join your plan. Once a family member has accepted the invite, their account details, such as email address and log in name will be visible here. You also have the option to remove family members from the plan if necessary.
Payment
Easily add or update your payment method and review your billing history for past payments.
Offline Devices
Manage the devices registered to your account for offline listening. You can download and listen offline on up to five devices. You can view which devices are registered, when they were last logged in, and how many are available for offline use.
You can also remove any devices you no longer use.
Third-Party Apps
Manage the apps you've granted permissions to and adjust those permissions anytime. If an app isn’t listed, it means no permissions have been granted for it.
Redeem
Enter your voucher or gift card code to redeem your Tidal offer.
