Subscription Types
Updated August 03, 2026 15:05
Tidal Subscription Types:
Tidal Individual: $11.99 USD/month
Tidal Family: $19.99 USD/month (includes up to 6 family members)
Tidal Student: $6.99 USD/month (available to eligible students)
Tidal DJ Individual: $19.99 USD/month
Tidal DJ Student: $14.49 USD/month (available to eligible students)
Tidal is available in supported countries and regions. Prices above are shown in USD, and subscription types and costs may vary depending on your location and applicable taxes. You can modify your subscription anytime at account.tidal.com.
Eligible U.S. customers on the Tidal Individual or Tidal DJ Individual plan can receive 25% off for 12 months by signing up through this Cash App offer. Please note that if you are a new customer and choose this offer, you will not receive a free trial and will be charged immediately. The Cash App discount and a free trial cannot be combined.
