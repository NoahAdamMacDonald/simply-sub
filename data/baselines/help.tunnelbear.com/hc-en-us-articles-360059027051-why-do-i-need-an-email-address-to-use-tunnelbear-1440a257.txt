Why do I need an email address to use TunnelBear?
Updated
June 21, 2021 18:49
To create a TunnelBear account, we require that you have an active email address. There are a few reasons for this:
1. All TunnelBear accounts need to be verified by their owners, this involves confirming the account through a verification email.
2. We need to know where to send your data allowance each month.
3. If you purchase a TunnelBear subscription, we need to know where to send the receipt.
At TunnelBear, we do our 'beary' best to collect as little information as possible. If you'd like to learn more about why we need your email address, check out our Privacy Policy.
