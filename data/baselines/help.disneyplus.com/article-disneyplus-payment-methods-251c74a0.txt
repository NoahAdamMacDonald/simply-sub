Payment methods for Disney+
There are several different ways to purchase your Disney+ subscription.
Credit and debit cards
We accept the following credit or debit cards*:
JCB
Visa
Mastercard
American Express
Diners Club
*International cards are not accepted at this time. Your debit or credit card billing address must match the country/region where you are signing up.
Digital payments
The following digital payment method can be used to pay for Disney+:
PayPal
Third-party billing partners
You can also choose to purchase a Disney+ subscription through a third-party billing partner (Docomo, J:COM, Apple, Google, Amazon, Hulu (Hulu | Disney+ Set Plan), or any other platform). To manage your payment method, please visit the partner’s site or contact them directly.
Having trouble?
If your payment method is not working and you are unable to update your payment information, try fixing these potential billing issues. If the problem persists, please contact Disney+ Support for assistance.
