What Payment Methods Can I Use For Trend Micro Online Purchase?
Views:
The Trend Micro Online Store accept several payment options including:
Visa®
MasterCard®
EuroCard
Discover®
American Express®
Diner’s Club®
JCB
PayPal
Google Pay
You can also use check cards and ATM cards if they are from one of these major credit card companies.
Note: We encrypt all information you send to us, so your credit card details are safe and secure.
How Should I Enter My Credit Card Number?
Enter your credit card number as a continuous string of numbers. Do not use spaces or special characters.
What Address Should I Enter for Billing Information?
For credit card verification, enter the address exactly as it appears on your credit card statement.
Why Is My Credit Card Not Being Accepted?
There are several reasons why a credit card transaction may fail:
Your card may have expired.
Your card may have reached its limit.
There may be a technical error at our end or your bank's end.
When Will My Credit Card Be Charged?
If you buy downloadable products, your credit card will be charged the day after you place your order.
Ready to renew your subscription?
For step-by-step guidance on how to renew your subscription, please refer to this helpful article: How to Renew Trend Micro Subscription.
