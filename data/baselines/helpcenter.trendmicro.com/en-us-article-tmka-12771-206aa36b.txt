Is Auto-Renewal Enabled When Purchasing a Trend Micro Subscription?
Views:
Have you noticed that Auto Renewal is turned on when you buy a Trend Micro subscription? Here’s what you need to know.
What is Auto Renewal?
Auto Renewal is a helpful service that automatically renews your subscription for another year before it runs out. This means you don’t have to worry about renewing it yourself every year.
Why is Auto Renewal Enabled?
When you purchase a subscription through our online store, Auto Renewal is set to be ON by default. This is to make it easier for you to keep your protection up to date without any hassle.
If you prefer not to keep Auto Renewal, you can cancel it anytime. You can use this link for step-by-step instructions to help you cancel Auto Renewal: How to Cancel Trend Micro Auto Renew Subscription
When Will You Be Charged?
If you are subscribed to Auto Renewal, you will see a charge on your payment method up to 15 days before your subscription expires.
