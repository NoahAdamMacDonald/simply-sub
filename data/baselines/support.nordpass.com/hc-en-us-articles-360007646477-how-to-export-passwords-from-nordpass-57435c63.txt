Introduction
This article will show you how to export your Vault items from NordPass.
Before you start
Organizations can manage Data export in the NordPass Business Admin Panel "Policies" section by adjusting the "Item exporting" settings.
You can export items using the Desktop application (Windows, macOS, Apple Silicon macOS, Linux) or WebVault.
Here's what to do
Log in to NordPass.
Open "Settings" by clicking the "Cog" icon in the upper-left corner
Scroll down to the "Import and Export" section, and select the “Export” button.
Enter your Master Password, then click the "Continue" button.
Save the .CSV file on your device.
Additional tips
Never send your exported password file over email or untrusted channels.
