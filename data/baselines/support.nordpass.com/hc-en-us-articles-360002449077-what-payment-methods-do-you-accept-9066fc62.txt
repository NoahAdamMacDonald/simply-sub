What payment methods do you accept?
You can purchase a NordPass subscription with a credit card, debit card, PayPal, Sofort (Klarna), Google Pay, Apple Pay, or cryptocurrencies. In addition, you can make a payment through the Apple Store or the Google Play Store.
Note: Some payment methods are not available in certain regions.
Once you've checked out NordPass's plan and prices on our pricing page, you can find which payment methods are available to you at the checkout page, listed under the “Select a payment method” section.
