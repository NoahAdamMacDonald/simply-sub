Does NordPass save passwords on their servers?
Our service is backed by the zero-knowledge architecture, meaning that your data is encrypted and decrypted at your device level.
All data that you store in your vault is already encrypted, and only you know the details.
Your Master Password, encryption, and decryption keys never reach our servers.
This is the key reason why we do not provide an automated Master Password recovery via email. If you need help with recovering your Master Password, check out our article about what happens if you have forgotten your Master Password.
NordPass has successfully finished an independent security audit by Cure53, a well-respected third-party auditor.
