FAQs: ZoneAlarm Web Secure extension
ZoneAlarm Web Secure is an advanced browser extension for Chrome, Firefox, Brave and Edge that provides you with essential browsing security tools to keep you safe from Phishing attempts, Blocks unsafe websites, Blocks websites based on their content and prevents you from downloading malicious documents.
The extension includes:
ZoneAlarm Anti-Phishing
ZoneAlarm Safe Browsing
ZoneAlarm Content Filtering
ZoneAlarm Threat Emulation (Sandbox)
ZoneAlarm Threat Extraction
FAQs
How do I install Web Secure on my browsers?
ZoneAlarm Web Secure browser extension only comes with:
ZoneAlarm Extreme Security NextGen
ZoneAlarm Pro Antivirus + Firewall NextGen
When installing ZoneAlarm Extreme Security NextGen and ZoneAlarm Pro Antivirus + Firewall NextGen , ZoneAlarm Web Secure will be installed automatically in your Chrome, Firefox, Brave and Edge browsers.
Which browsers Web Secure is compatible with?
The Web Secure browser extension is currently only available for Chrome, Firefox, Brave and Edge browsers.
What is ZoneAlarm Anti-Phishing?
ZoneAlarm Anti-Phishing is a major feature designed to protect your privacy from online phishing attacks and provides maximum security with great browsing experience. The Anti-Phishing mechanism is a heuristic engine that will scan all websites you reach and examine their characteristics before letting you insert any personal information. It will alert you whether it is a safe website or a phishing attempt.
What is Phishing?
Phishing is a deceptive tactic used by cyber criminals to collect sensitive information online. An email from what appears to be a familiar source, such as Apple or PayPal, will often encourage you to click on a link, directing you to a bogus site with the attempt to steal your personal information. It will often require you to fill in personal details such as bank account or credit card information, usernames and passwords, or other sensitive information, posing a real risk that may compromise your privacy, potentially leading to identity theft.
What is Safe Browsing?
Safe Browsing will determine whether the site is trustworthy or not using Check Point’s ThreatCloud™, the world’s biggest threat database. If the site is determined to be fraudulent, you will be immediately alerted and blocked from accessing it.
What is Content Filtering?
