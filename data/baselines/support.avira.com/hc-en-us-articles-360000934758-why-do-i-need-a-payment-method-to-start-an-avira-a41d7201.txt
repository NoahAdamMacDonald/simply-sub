Why do I need a payment method to start an Avira trial?
The payment method ensures that no security gap arises between the free trial period and the subscription.
Further, authorization requests will be sent to your financial institution to verify that your payment method works. These requests are not fees, but in some cases, they may temporarily affect your available account balance.
An Avira trial can be used only if an Avira trial for the same product has not been used within the past 12 months.
Note
You will NOT be charged during your Avira trial period.
To avoid getting charged for the next billing period, you need to cancel the subscription at least one day before the billing date listed here.
