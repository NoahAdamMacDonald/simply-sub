Canceling an Avira plan in-app purchase on the Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or Microsoft Store
If you subscribed to an Avira plan through an in-app purchase, you can cancel or change your subscription settings only from the Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or Microsoft Store.
Uninstalling the Avira app from your device will not cancel your subscription. You must cancel or change your subscription settings on the respective store.
Avira trial:
The trial from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store requires a payment method and the activation of an annual paid subscription through an in-app purchase. A charge for the monthly or annual paid subscription is initiated at the end of your trial period.
To cancel the trial before being charged, you must cancel the subscription before the trial period ends. If your monthly or annual paid subscription has already started and been charged, you must cancel the subscription through the respective store (Apple App Store or Google Play Store).
CANCELING YOUR AVIRA SUBSCRIPTION
To cancel your Avira subscription that you purchased through an in-app purchase, refer to the following support articles:
Apple App Store
Google Play Store
Microsoft Store
