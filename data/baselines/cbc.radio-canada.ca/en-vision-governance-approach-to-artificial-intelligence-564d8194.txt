Our Approach to Artificial Intelligence
Our vision of how artificial intelligence (AI) tools should be used is consistent with our mission of better serving and representing all Canadians. We are committed to a responsible approach that puts the interests of Canadians first and that values the talent and creativity of the people who showcase our culture.
The emergence of generative AI, which can produce text, images, audio, video and other content by learning from existing data, marks a turning point in the media industry.
With care and transparency
AI comes with its challenges, including ethical ones (proliferation of disinformation, threatening copyright, undermining democratic processes such as elections). At the same time, it provides many opportunities to innovate and improve our workflows (creating new experiences, enhancing accessibility of content, etc.). Overall, we intend to seize the opportunities to stay relevant in a rapidly changing media landscape.
Under no circumstances must the use of AI compromise our credibility or our mandate to inform, enlighten and entertain. As such, we are approaching AI with a critical eye and a determination to continuously learn. It goes without saying that we will continue to rigorously apply CBC/Radio-Canada's Journalistic Standards and Practices across our news organization.
With AI developing so rapidly, it is vital that we adopt clear guidelines for its use, even as these directives will likely evolve over time.
The following principles will guide us in making responsible use of AI in all our operations:
Responsible human oversight: AI is not a substitute for human judgment. We will never publish content created with the help of AI without human oversight or verification and approval.
Transparency: We are open and transparent about how we use AI. For example, when we develop a new feature that makes use of AI technology, we mention this fact wherever it is used.
Ethics: We follow the highest standards when it comes to the design, development and deployment of AI technologies, including rigorous verification of content and algorithms. To reduce the risks of bias, we are taking steps to ensure that the data driving our AI tools reflects a diversity of voices and opinions.
Respect for copyright and authors' rights: We respect creators' rights and ensure fair compensation. The tools now available to us allow us to adapt and learn from experimentation across our creative sectors. We will do our utmost to guarantee the adoption of best practices so as to respect and protect creators' rights.
Security and Privacy: We will always strive to ensure data integrity and security. We value your privacy and are transparent about how we handle and use data.
Optimized experience: While AI does make some tasks easier, our goal is to focus on finding ways to enrich the audience experience across all our platforms. Moreover, we are continually optimizing our practices to make sure our content is as accessible as possible.
Collaboration: We will continue to work closely with key players in media, education, research and technology to share our AI expertise, and participate in initiatives to protect trusted news and combat disinformation. These include international organizations such as the Trusted News Initiative (TNI), the Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity (C2PA), the Partnership on AI, and IVADO. By uniting our efforts, we will be better equipped to understand the new technological realities we face. We will also encourage conversations with the public to make sure our use of AI responds to your needs.
Looking to the future
Despite the challenges it poses, AI provides fertile ground for exploring novel solutions, which we are keen to pursue to offer better experiences for our audiences. The tools newly available to us allow us to adapt and to learn from our efforts.
We are therefore proceeding with caution, but in a spirit of open-mindedness and transparency. As Canada's public broadcaster, we are dedicated to leading the way in the media industry and to accompanying you through this new, complex and evolving environment. We hope you will continue to follow developments in the field of AI and reach out to us if you have any questions or concerns.
Publication date: May 15, 2024
