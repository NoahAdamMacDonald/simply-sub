How to sign up on DAZN?
Join our ever-growing family of subscribers around the world to enjoy unlimited live sports, documentaries, pay-per-view events and more.
Once you become a DAZN member, you can choose any plan that suits you or change your plan at any time.
Just follow these steps to sign up.
Website
Visit https://www.dazn.com/signup
Choose the plan that's right for you.
Create an account by entering your email address and password.
Choose a payment method and enter your details.
That's it. Stream on!
TV or TV streaming device
Open the DAZN App on your TV.
Click 'Explore' – a QR code will appear on the screen.
Scan the QR Code using your mobile phone.
Follow the signup process in your mobile browser.
Start watching DAZN on your TV after completing the signup.
iPhone or iPad
Download the DAZN app on your device.
Click on 'Sign Up' and create your DAZN account
Choose a plan and your preferred payment method.
Enter your payment details and complete the subscription process.
That's it. You can start watching DAZN content now.
Android
Download the DAZN app from Google Play store.
Once you start the sign-up process from the app, you will be redirected to www.dazn.com/signup to finish the sign-up.
Create an account by entering your email address and password.
Note: If using the DAZN Android app, you'll receive an email from DAZN with a link to finish sign-up using a mobile or computer browser.
Choose the plan that's right for you. You can downgrade or upgrade at any time.
Choose a payment method and enter your details.
That's it. You can start watching DAZN content now.
Gaming consoles
Watch DAZN on Xbox or PlayStation. To set up, download the DAZN app from the Microsoft Store (Xbox) or PlayStation Store, install it, sign up, and start streaming.
Note: Some of the older versions of gaming consoles may not be compatible.
