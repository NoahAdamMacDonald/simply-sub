Export and share chat history safely, with limits and tips
Export and share chat history safely, with limits and tips
December 2, 2025
Web app:
Click on the gear icon at the bottom left corner of the window. You may need to extend the width of the screen to see the gear icon on the bottom left corner.
Alternatively hover over the the PiTM icon on the top left corner of the page until the icon changes into the navigation bar icon, which looks like two rectangles side by side. Click on the navigation bar icon.
Click on Account on the left hand navigation pane
Click on Download Chat history link to initiate the full chat retrieval. You’ll see the message “Retrieval initiated: Retrieval in progress – your Pi history will be ready to download here on <today’s date>”
Check back to this page later. The time it takes to retrieve and package your chat history depends on how many chat threads and how long each chat thread is.
Once packaged, click on download link and a file named <pi-user-history.json> will be in your Download folder
Android app:
Click on the person icon on the top left corner of the page. This slides out the navigation bar
Select “Manage history”
Click on Download Chat history link to initiate the full chat retrieval. You’ll see the message “Retrieval initiated: Retrieval in progress – your Pi history will be ready to download here on <date>”
Check back to this page later. The time it takes to retrieve and package your chat history depends on how many chat threads and how long each chat thread is.
Once packaged, click on download link and a file named <pi-user-history.json> will be in your Download folder
iOS app:
Click on the icon that looks like a long and short horizontal lines, at the top left corner of the page. A navigation bar should slide out
Click on Settings icon (looks like a gear) at the bottom of the navigation bar
Click on Account
Click on Download your Pi history. You’ll see the message “Retrieval initiated: Retrieval in progress – your Pi history will be ready to download here on <date>”
Check back to this page later. The time it takes to retrieve and package your chat history depends on how many chat threads and how long each chat thread is.
Once packaged, click on download link and a file named <pi-user-history.json> will be in your Download folder.
