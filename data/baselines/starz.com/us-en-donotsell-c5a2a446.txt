Global Privacy Control (GPC)/Universal Opt-out
Under certain US State privacy laws, businesses must respect opt-out signals received through a user selected web browser control indicating their choice to opt out of the sale, sharing, or use of personal information for targeted advertising. Starz has implemented methods to recognize and respond to the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal on our service. If you access the STARZ service via starz.com through a web browser sending a recognized GPC signal, we will opt you out of setting Advertising Cookies on that browser/device.
Note: Your choices related to cookies, device identifiers and GPC signal will apply to devices accessed with your logged in account if you opt out (or opt back in) when you are logged in. If you are not logged in, your choice will only apply to this browser, and you will need to renew this choice on each browser or device where you want your choice to apply or if you clear your browser cookies.
