Request a refund for YouTube TV
If features included with your YouTube TV membership are defective, or do not perform as stated, click the button below to request a refund. If you’re granted a refund, you’ll lose access to YouTube TV immediately, and you will get your money back.
REQUEST A REFUND
If you see a charge you didn’t make, you can report an unauthorized charge instead.
Learn more about refunds for YouTube TV
Credit card refunds typically take 3-5 business days.
Processing times can be affected by your card issuer. Sometimes, refunds can take up to 10 business days to process.
If your credit card is no longer active, your refund will go to the bank that issued your card. To recover funds, get in touch with the bank.
If you subscribe to YouTube TV through your mobile carrier or internet provider:
YouTube cannot grant a refund.
Your provider’s refund policies will apply. Get in touch with them directly to request a refund, or for any questions related to your bill.
You can cancel or pause your membership at any time.
We do not give refunds or credits for partial billing periods.
When you cancel your membership, your access will continue until the end of your billing period.
