Update billing & payments for YouTube TV
You can update your billing and payment info for your YouTube TV subscription and any add-ons you’ve purchased. As a YouTube TV member, you’ll automatically be charged at the start of each new billing cycle until you cancel. Your payment gives you access to YouTube TV for the next month.
Notes:
If you signed up for YouTube TV through a mobile carrier or internet provider, you’ll be billed by them. Learn more about how integrated billing works.
To review your payment history, follow these instructions to find your purchases, reservations & subscriptions.
Learn more about NFL Sunday Ticket pricing, billing, and purchase options.
About YouTube TV Payments
A YouTube TV plan costs $82.99 + tax/month.
Your charge date is the same within each month. If that date doesn’t occur in a given month (for example, the 31st), you'll be charged on the last date of that month.
Add-on networks are prorated for the period before your next charge date, and then added to your monthly charge. Learn more about add-on networks.
Learn more about how taxes are calculated on YouTube TV.
Troubleshoot a YouTube charge
Click the button below to troubleshoot or learn about your YouTube bill.
TROUBLESHOOT A YOUTUBE CHARGE
Add or update payment method
Go to tv.youtube.com.
Select your profile picture .
Select Settings Billing .
Select Update next to your form of payment details.
Add a backup payment method
If your payment method expires, your charge won’t go through. Keep your payment method up to date, or add a backup payment method, to ensure your viewing experience isn’t interrupted.
Go to tv.youtube.com.
Select your profile picture .
Select Settings Billing .
Select Update next to your form of payment details.
Under “YouTube TV,” select Manage.
Under “How you pay,” select Change payment method Continue Add backup.
Select an available payment method or add a new one.
Need a break? Pause your membership and keep your DVR library. Learn how to pause your membership.
