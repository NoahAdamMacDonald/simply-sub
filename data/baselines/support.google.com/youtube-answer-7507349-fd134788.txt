Sign up for & manage a YouTube family plan
Get a YouTube family plan to share a YouTube paid membership, or Primetime Channels (US, Germany, France, Australia, and UK only), with up to 5 other members of your household.
How to Create Family Groups on YouTube and YouTube TV
How the family plan works
YouTube family plans allow you to share membership benefits with up to 5 family members who live within the same residential address.
The family manager:
Is the primary account holder.
Creates a Google family group and can invite family members to the group.
Family members:
Use their own Google account to access a shared membership.
Can view Primetime Channels content the family manager purchases. Members can also purchase their own individual Primetime Channel, which won’t be shared with the family manager.
Have their own personal library, subscriptions, and recommendations – we won’t share viewing preferences or watch history across family member accounts.
Age restrictions apply for family members <18.
Family groups share access to:
YouTube Premium
YouTube Music Premium
YouTube Premium Lite (randomly selected group of users in Canada, Japan, Taiwan, Chile, and Mexico)
YouTube Primetime Channels (US, Germany, France, Australia, and UK only)
And other Google services. Learn more about family groups on Google.
Note: Family plans are currently not available in Belarus, Iceland, Slovenia, South Korea, and Venezuela.
Family plans on YouTube Premium
To get all the benefits of a YouTube Premium family plan, including ad-free watching, offline downloading, and background play, go here to select the family plan membership.
Family plans on YouTube Music Premium
To get all the benefits of a YouTube Music Premium family plan, including ad-free music, offline playing, and screen off audio, go here to select the family plan membership.
YouTube Music Premium members may still experience ads on podcasts.
Family plans on YouTube Premium Lite
To get all the benefits of a YouTube Premium Lite family plan, including ad-free watching, offline downloading, and background play on non-music content, go here to select the family plan membership.
YouTube Premium Lite does not include YouTube Music Premium or additional features such as continue watching, Jump Ahead, queuing or high-quality audio and video, and more. Ads may still appear on music content, Shorts, and when you search or browse.
Family group requirements
Important: You will not be able to start or join a family plan using a Google Workspace account. To sign up for a family plan, create or sign in to your regular Google Account.
Family Manager requirements
As the family manager, you're the only individual who can buy a YouTube family plan or make membership decisions for the family group. You'll set the household location and can invite or remove family members.
To share your YouTube family plan with your family group, you must:
Be 18 years of age or older (or the appropriate age in your geography).
Have a Google Account. If you have a Google Workspace account, you’ll need to create or sign in to a regular Google account.
Live in a country where YouTube Premium, YouTube Music Premium, YouTube Premium Lite, or Primetime Channels are available.
Note:
Family plans are not available in Korea.
YouTube Premium Lite family plans are currently only available to a randomly selected group of users in CA, JP, TW, CL, MX.
Primetime Channels are only available in the United States, Germany, France, Australia, and the United Kingdom.
Not be part of another family group.
Have not switched family groups in the last 12 months.
Family member requirements
The family manager can invite up to 5 family members to join a family group. To join a family group sharing a YouTube family plan, you must:
Have a Google account. If you have a Google Workspace account, you’ll need to create or sign in to a regular Google account.
Live within the same residential address as the family manager.
Live in a country or region where YouTube Premium, YouTube Music Premium, YouTube Premium Lite, or Primetime Channels are available.
Not be part of another family group.
Have not switched family groups in the last 12 months.
Tip: If you see an alert in the YouTube app informing you that your Premium access will be paused, it is because we cannot confirm that you are in the same household as your family manager. Please check the email you received from YouTube for guidance on resolving this issue.
Tip: Learn how to set up and manage your YouTube family plan. If you need help with your family plan, you can also get in touch with support at any time.
