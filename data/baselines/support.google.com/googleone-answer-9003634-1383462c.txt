Change your card & fix payment issues
If you have issues with Google One payments, try these solutions. You can also update your Google One payment methods.
If you're ever locked out of your Google Account, recovery options can help you get back in. Learn more about account recovery.
Change the card you use
You can edit the credit card you use for your Google One membership. If you want to change how much you pay, change your storage plan.
On your computer, go to Google One.
Click Settings .
Click Change payment method.
In Google Play, on the left, click My subscriptions.
Next to "Google One," click Manage.
Next to "Primary payment method," click Update.
Select an option and follow the steps on the screen.
Learn how it works
How to update your payment method on Google One | Computer
For this same video with audio descriptions: How to update your payment method on Google One.
Get help buying a Google One subscription
If you had difficulty signing up for Google One for the first time, you can ask a Google Expert to help you.
Fix issues with payments
If you find errors when you make transactions, try these troubleshooting steps.
Fix a declined payment
Sign in to Google Pay.
Click Update payment method information.
Choose a payment method.
Click Continue.
Fill out new payment method.
Tip: Declined payments are sometimes caused by expired cards. If your card has expired, you can update the expiration date or add a new payment method.
Change, update, or add payment method
If you recently added a new credit card, you may have to update the information on your account.
Go to Google Pay.
Click Payment methods.
Next to the card you want to edit, click Edit.
Enter the expiration date and CVC found on the card.
Make sure your billing address is correct.
Click Save.
Add another payment method
Sign in to Google Pay.
Click Payment methods.
Click Add payment method.
Choose a payment method:
Add new credit or debit card
Add a bank account
Follow the steps to add your payment method.
If your Google One membership is billed through a third-party partner, learn how to make changes to your Google One plan.
Make sure your payment method is supported
Credit card
Bank account (US only)
PayPal
Google Play balance (Selected countries/regions only)
Check the list of countries that supports Play balance as a payment method for subscriptions.
Contact your credit card provider
If your credit card information is correct but your payment won't go through, contact your credit card provider.
They may have more information on why your credit card can't be charged.
Unauthorized charge
Learn how to report unauthorized charges.
If someone in your family made an unauthorized charge, you can also limit what they can buy.
Can't get into your account
Sometimes, to finish transactions, you need to verify your identity.
If you can't sign in to your Google Account, get help.
Regional restrictions
If you can’t pay for Google One because of regional restrictions or other banking issues in your country/region, learn what to do.
Find purchases by family members
The family manager can find all the purchases made with the family payment method. Learn how to find your family's purchases.
The Google One plan manager can be a different person than your family manager. If so, the Google One plan manager can't find purchases made with the family payment method.
