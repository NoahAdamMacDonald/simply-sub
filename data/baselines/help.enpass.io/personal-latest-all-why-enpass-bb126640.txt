Why Enpass?
8 reasons to choose Enpass
A powerful, affordable, user-empowering alternative to all those cookie-cutter password managers.
Enpass has inherently stronger security
Choose your own safest storage location, adding several additional layers of multi-factor authentication
Unlike other password managers, Enpass does not have a central server full of user data for hackers to attack — to steal your passwords from Enpass, a hacker would have to...
Target you personally
AND know which cloud services you’ve chosen for your password vaults
AND discover the credentials to those cloud accounts
AND get past each cloud account's multi-factor authentication
AND know your Enpass Master Password
(Plus there’s an extra Keyfile option for the tech-savvy)
Enpass has many storage & syncing options
Setting up an Enpass password vault takes about a minute on Dropbox, Google Drive, iCloud, OneDrive (Personal or Business) or Box
You can also choose NextCloud, and WebDAV, or stay completely offline & sync via Wi-Fi
Enpass has unlimited separate password vaults
Keep a personal vault, a work vault, and vaults shared with a significant other or your kids
Keep different vaults for different projects, and securely share them with project partners
Help manage the vaults of elderly relatives, stored on their cloud accounts
Enpass has tons of customizability
Customizable templates, categories, and nesting tags
Customizable Items (add, remove, rearrange, and customize fields)
Customizable browser extension (autofill triggers, autosave options, shortcuts & more)
System-wide access (hotkeys, Task bar or Menu Bar icons, autolock & more)
Customizable user-interface options (item counts, items subtitles, sensitive fields, etc.)
Enpass can manage passkeys and authenticator codes
Generate authentication codes, eliminating the need for a separate app
Take the confusion out of passkeys, logging you into accounts with just a face or fingerprint
Enpass is family-friendly
Manage up to 6 household users
Enpass is affordable
Enpass is free up to 25 Items, including syncing across all platforms
And Enpass plans are far more affordable than other password managers with fewer features and more vulnerable vault storage
And Enpass has everything else you'd expect
