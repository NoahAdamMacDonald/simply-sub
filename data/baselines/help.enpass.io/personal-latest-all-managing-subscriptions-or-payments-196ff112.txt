Managing subscriptions or payments
Managing Enpass subscriptions or payments
How your Enpass subscription is managed depends on how you made your original purchase.
To manage an Enpass subscription purchased through the iOS or Mac App Stores:
Follow these instructions from Apple.
To manage an Enpass subscription purchased through the Google Play Store:
Follow these instructions from Google Play.
To manage an Enpass subscription or purchase from Enpass.io:
Visit enpass.onfastspring.com/account.
Enter the email address used for your purchase, and click Continue.
Follow the link in the email you receive to access your account, view your order, manage your subscription, and update your payment details.
If you have questions or need assistance:
Visit the Enpass Discussion Forum or contact Enpass Support
