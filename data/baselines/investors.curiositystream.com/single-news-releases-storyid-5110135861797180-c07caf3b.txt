CuriosityStream Delivers Strong Third Quarter 2026 Results and Raises Outlook; Revenue grows 13% and Adjusted EBITDA Grows 67%
Revenue of $20.7 million, up 13%
Licensing revenue of $11.7 million, up 35%
Net income of $4.8 million, an improvement of $8.6 million
Adjusted EBITDA of $5.0 million, up 67%; seventh consecutive quarter of positive Adjusted EBITDA
Earnings per share of $0.08
Raises full-year 2026 revenue and adjusted EBITDA guidance
Continued capital returns: cash dividend of $0.085 per share paid in Q3 and declared for Q4 2026
SILVER SPRING, MD / ACCESS Newswire / November 11, 2026 / CuriosityStream Inc. (NASDAQ: CURI), a global factual entertainment company, today announced its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The Company's Board of Directors also declared a fourth quarter cash dividend of $0.085 per share, payable on December 15, 2026, to stockholders of record on December 3, 2026.
"We delivered strong results in Q3. Revenue increased 13% and adjusted EBITDA grew 67%. This relationship is important as it illustrates the potential for CURI to generate meaningful earnings growth as we expand revenue without a corresponding increase in our fixed cost structure. Net income improved by $8.6M leading to an EPS roughly equivalent to our dividend payment." said Clint Stinchcomb, President & CEO of CuriosityStream.
Stinchcomb continued, "The value and durability of the extensive intellectual property we control - including our premium video, audio, and growing corpus of proprietary operational data - continues to create new opportunities for Curiosity. As demand for high-quality, rights-controlled content and data expands across traditional media and artificial intelligence, we believe our assets are becoming increasingly valuable. Our focus is on converting these opportunities into durable commercial relationships, recurring revenue streams, and sustained long-term shareholder value."
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Revenue was $20.7 million, up 13% from $18.4 million in the third quarter of 2025.
Gross profit was $10.1 million, with a gross margin of 48.8%, compared with $10.8 million and 58.7%, respectively, in the third quarter of 2025. The decline reflected higher revenue share expense on AI licensing revenue.
Net income was $4.8 million, an improvement of $8.6 million compared with a net loss of $3.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. Net income included a $0.9 million non-cash gain on the remeasurement of the Company's previously held interest in the Spiegel Venture.
Adjusted EBITDA was $5.0 million, an increase of $2.0 million from $3.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. This marked the seventh consecutive quarter of positive Adjusted EBITDA.
Operating expenses decreased by $5.8 million, or 25%, compared with the third quarter of 2025.
Net cash provided by operating activities was $1.5 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared with $9.1 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.
The Company paid an ordinary dividend of $0.085 per share, totaling $5.1 million.
As of September 30, 2026, cash, restricted cash and held-to-maturity securities totaled $10.0 million. The Company had no debt, and its $10.0 million revolving credit facility remained undrawn.
Third Quarter 2026 Business Highlights
Licensed thousands of hours of scripted entertainment.
Launched Curiosity Stream with Prime Video in Canada, expanding its Prime Video presence to five major English-speaking markets: the U.S., U.K., Canada, Australia and New Zealand.
Acquired the remaining 68% interest in the Spiegel Venture, the Company's German documentary channel business, for $1.9 million in cash. The acquisition increased ownership to 100% and expanded European content distribution capabilities.
Appointed Sean Piche, CPA, as Chief Financial Officer, effective September 1, 2026.
Financial Outlook
CuriosityStream expects the following for the full year of 2026:
Revenue in the range of $79 - $82 million.
Adjusted EBITDA1 in the range of $20 - $22 million.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
To supplement our unaudited consolidated statement of operations, which is prepared in accordance with GAAP, we present Adjusted EBITDA in this press release. Our use of non-GAAP financial measures, such as Adjusted EBITDA, has limitations as an analytical tool, and these measures should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of financial results as reported under GAAP.
We use these non-GAAP financial measures in conjunction with financial measures prepared in accordance with GAAP for planning purposes, including in the preparation of our annual operating budget, as a measure of our core operating results and the effectiveness of our business strategy, and in evaluating our financial performance. These measures provide consistency and comparability with past financial performance, facilitate period-to-period comparisons of core operating results, and also facilitate comparisons with other peer companies, many of which use similar non-GAAP financial measures to supplement their GAAP results. In addition, Adjusted EBITDA is widely used by investors and securities analysts to measure a company's operating performance. We exclude the following items from net income to calculate Adjusted EBITDA: interest and other income (expense), provision for income taxes, depreciation and non-content amortization, loss/(gain) on the change in fair value of our warrants, equity interests loss (gain), impairment of goodwill, intangible assets and content assets, restructuring charges, acquisition-related costs and stock-based compensation.
Investors are cautioned that there are material limitations associated with the use of non-GAAP financial measures as an analytical tool. In particular, (1) although depreciation and amortization expense are non-cash charges, the assets subject to depreciation and amortization may have to be replaced in the future, and Adjusted EBITDA does not reflect cash capital expenditure requirements for such replacements or for new capital expenditure requirements; and (2) Adjusted EBITDA does not reflect: (a) changes in, or cash requirements for, our working capital needs; or (b) tax payments that may represent a reduction in cash available to us. The non-GAAP financial measures we use may be different from non-GAAP financial measures used by other companies, limiting their usefulness for comparison purposes. We compensate for these limitations by providing specific information regarding the GAAP items excluded from these non-GAAP financial measures. A reconciliation of these non-GAAP financial measures has been provided in the financial statements tables included in this press release and investors are encouraged to review the reconciliation.
About CuriosityStream Inc.
CuriosityStream Inc. (Nasdaq: CURI) is the entertainment brand for people who want to know more. The global media company is home to award-winning original and curated factual films, shows, and series covering science, nature, history, technology, society, and lifestyle. CuriosityStream is also a leading provider of AI model training datasets, leveraging one of the world's largest and most valuable rights-cleared media corpora. Our portfolio spans millions of hours of premium video and audio alongside a production-grade code library of over 1,000 repositories with decades of contextual history, 100,000 pull requests, and assets from seven operating companies. Additionally, we offer dozens of bespoke video datasets created with our proprietary content intelligence tools. See our sample datasets in action here. CuriosityStream's data licensing partnerships enable leading technology companies to train and fine-tune generative, agentic, and physical AI systems that will power the next era of infrastructure and enterprise capabilities.
CuriosityStream also reaches millions of subscribers worldwide, operating the flagship Curiosity Stream SVOD service; Curiosity Channel, the linear television channel available via global distribution partners; Curiosity University, featuring talks from the best professors at the world's most renowned universities as well as courses, short and long-form videos, and podcasts; Curiosity Now, Curiosity History, Curiosity Animals, Curiosity Explora, and other free, ad-supported channels; Curiosity Audio Network, with original content and podcasts; and Curiosity Studios, which oversees original programming. For more information, visit CuriosityStream.com.
