How do I disable trackers on the Android app?
The RoboForm Android app uses Google Firebase for analytics and crash reporting. All data is aggregated and anonymized. No encrypted information stored in RoboForm is ever passed through Firebase.
To opt out of this tracking on your Android device, follow the steps below:
1) Open the RoboForm app and tap the 3 dots in the upper-right corner.
2) Select "Settings" from the menu. Then, tap the "General" option.
3) Toggle the "Disable analytics and crash reporting" option to the ON (blue) position.
