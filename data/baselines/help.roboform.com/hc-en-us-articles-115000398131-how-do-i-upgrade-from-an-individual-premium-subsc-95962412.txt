How do I upgrade from an individual Premium subscription to a Family Plan?
1) Open your browser and click the RoboForm extension icon. Click the 3 dots in the upper right corner. Then, select "Settings" from the menu.
2) Select "License & Billing" from the column on the left. Then, click the "Upgrade to Family Plan" button.
The upgrade price is calculated based on the number of days left in your current individual license. In order to upgrade, you have to renew for a full year. Your new family plan expiration date, however, will reflect the pro-rated remainder of your current individual subscription.
