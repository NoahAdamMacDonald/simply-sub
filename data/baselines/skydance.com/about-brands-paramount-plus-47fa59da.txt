Paramount+
Paramount+ is where culture-defining entertainment lives. The flagship streaming home of Paramount's premium original series and global brands delivering defining entertainment to audiences around the world. Drawing on decades of beloved and enduring IP, the platform brings together marquee live sports, breaking news and a robust slate of exclusive originals, hit series and blockbuster films from iconic brands across the Paramount portfolio including CBS hits like Marshals and Tracker, all BET Original Series, the Avatar animated universe from Nickelodeon, Comedy Central, MTV and Showtime. Premium and Essential plans are available, with Premium offering the full Showtime portfolio, live CBS streams and an expanded lineup of live sports and events, while all subscribers have access to CBS News Network and CBS Sports HQ.
