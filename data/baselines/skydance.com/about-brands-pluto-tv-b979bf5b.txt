Pluto TV
Pluto TV invented free streaming television in 2014, and the category it created is now one of the fastest-growing in television, expanding four times faster than the broader streaming market. As part of Paramount, Pluto TV has franchise access no other free streaming television service can match: 49 seasons of Survivor, full seasons of CSI and Criminal Minds, SpongeBob SquarePants, iCarly, and current hits like Yellowstone, Tracker, and Ghosts rolling on in-season. Paramount theatrical films make their free streaming television premiere exclusively on Pluto TV, the first place audiences can watch them for free.
Pluto TV programs offer exclusive free streaming television titles like The X-Files, Arrow, Fringe, The 100, Hart of Dixie and many more iconic series from the early 2000s. Available across connected TV, mobile, and web, Pluto TV reaches viewers globally across more than 35 markets.
