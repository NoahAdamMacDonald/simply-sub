Subscriptions & billing
Changing your XBOX subscription
Depending on your type of subscription, you may have the option to upgrade, downgrade, or change your plan's duration.
Upgrading subscriptions: Game Pass Essential, Game Pass Premium, Game Pass for Console, PC Game Pass, and EA Play can all be upgraded to a higher-tier Game Pass subscription. When you upgrade, you will be charged for the new subscription immediately and any prepaid time on the current subscription will be converted and applied to your new subscription. For details about this conversion, go to:
All about XBOX Game Pass Conversions
Downgrading subscriptions: Game Pass Ultimate and Game Pass Premium can be downgraded to lower-tier subscriptions. When you downgrade, there is no immediate charge. Your current subscription will continue until your next renewal date, and then it will change to the new subscription level, and you'll be charged the new subscription rate on that date.
Changing subscription types: Some subscriptions have different renewal periods (monthly, every three months, annually, etc.). If your subscription offers multiple renewal plans and you switch to a different one, there is no immediate charge. Your current plan will continue until your next renewal date, and then it will change to the new plan and you'll be charged the new price on that date.
