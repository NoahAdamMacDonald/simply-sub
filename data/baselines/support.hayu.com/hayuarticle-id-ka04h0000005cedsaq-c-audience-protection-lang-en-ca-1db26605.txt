How can I set up a PIN?
To keep content that is classified for mature/adult audiences away from little eyes that shouldn't see it, you can set a 4-digit PIN code to restrict access to it. Simply click "My Account", navigate to your "Settings" and turn the PIN Management feature on. Once enabled, when you log-in to Hayu, you will be prompted to enter your PIN each time you play stonger content for the first time - but you won't be asked again for 4 hours so you can binge without interruption!
