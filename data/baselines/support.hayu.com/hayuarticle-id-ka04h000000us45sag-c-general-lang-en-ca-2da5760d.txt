About Hayu
From NBCUniversal, Hayu is the first and only all-reality subscription video-on-demand streaming service of its kind – available in 45 markets globally across most of Europe, Australia, Canada, Hong Kong, New Zealand, The Philippines, Singapore and the UK on a full array of devices (mobile, tablet, laptop, connected TVs and selected consoles) at www.Hayu.com
Shows and episodes are downloadable to watch-on-the-go, including Keeping Up With the Kardashians and its spin-offs – along with The Real Housewives, Below Deck and Million Dollar Listing franchises. The majority of US shows debut on the service the same day as their US launch.
