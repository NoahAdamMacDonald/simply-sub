Can I change my subscription plan?
Yes! If you've subscribed via hayu.com, you can change your subscription plan anytime by visiting the payment page . Your change will take effect at your next billing date.
Unfortunately, changing plans is only available to users who subscribe via hayu.com and pay by credit/debit card. We are unable to offer subscription changes via mobile or TV application billing.
If you're unsure or have any further questions, please contact us using the link below. We'd love to help!
