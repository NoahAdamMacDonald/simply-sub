Can I subscribe via the mobile or TV app?
You can subscribe to the Hayu app using your payment card by visiting Hayu.com or via the app stores containing the Hayu app across mobile and TV devices. You can see the full list of locations where you can find Hayu here along with the ways you can subscribe.
Please note, when you subscribe via a partner platform (iTunes, Google, Amazon, Roku, etc) your billing will need to be managed from these accounts. If you are unsure how your account is managed, please contact us using the "Message Us" button below.
