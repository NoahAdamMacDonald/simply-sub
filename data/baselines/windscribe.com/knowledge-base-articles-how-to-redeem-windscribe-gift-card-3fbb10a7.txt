How Do I Redeem a Windscribe Gift Card?
Rebecca Rosenberg
Dec 12, 2024
Help
Redeeming your Windscribe Gift Card is simple! Follow these steps to activate your Pro account and start enjoying secure browsing:
If you already have a Windscribe account:
Log in and open the Billing tab of My Account: windscribe.com/myaccount/billing.
Click Redeem Voucher on the Plan row of the Plan Information card. The button is shown while your account is on the Free plan.
Enter your gift card’s voucher code and click Redeem. Your account will be upgraded to Pro immediately.
If you’re new to Windscribe:
Go to www.windscribe.com/signup.
On the registration page, open Add Voucher Code.
Enter your gift card’s voucher code and complete the signup process. Your new account will be Pro right from the start.
Enjoy your Windscribe Pro experience, and welcome to the community!
