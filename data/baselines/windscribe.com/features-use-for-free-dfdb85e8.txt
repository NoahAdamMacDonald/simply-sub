Frequently Asked Questions
Is a free VPN safe?
It depends entirely on who runs it. Many free VPNs make money by logging and selling your browsing data, or by using your device as a proxy node. Windscribe's free plan runs on the same infrastructure as our paid one, funded by Pro subscribers, with a no-identifying-logs policy and no ad networks. Check who's paying the bill before you trust any free VPN.
What's the catch with Windscribe's free plan?
A data cap. You get 10 GB a month with an email, or 2 GB without, and you can earn more permanently. That's the only limit. Same encryption, same no-logs policy, same ad blocking as paid.
Is there a free VPN with no sign-up?
You need a free account to use Windscribe, but you don't need to give an email. Create a username and password and you're on, with 2 GB a month. Add an email later to bump it to 10 GB.
Does Windscribe's free plan sell my data?
No. We don't sell, rent, or trade your data on any plan. Our free tier is funded by Pro subscribers, not advertisers. See our Privacy Policy for the specifics.
Is Windscribe's free VPN weaker or slower than paid?
Same AES-256 encryption, same protocols, same firewall, and we never throttle. Free is limited to 10 countries and a monthly data cap, and uses the shared free server pool, but the security is identical.
Can I use Windscribe for free on my phone, laptop, and smart TV at once?
Yes. One free account covers unlimited devices at the same time, across Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iPhone, smart TVs, and routers.
Are there any 100% free VPNs?
Very few that are actually safe. Running servers costs money, so a truly free VPN has to fund it somehow. Windscribe is freemium: the free plan is genuinely free forever, paid for by Pro subscribers rather than by selling your data.
Are free VPNs illegal?
Using a VPN is legal in most countries. A handful of governments restrict or ban them, so check your local laws if you're travelling. What you do while connected still has to be legal.
Which VPN is truly free?
Windscribe's free plan has no trial clock and no credit card. You get 10 GB a month, 10 countries, unlimited devices, and full encryption for $0, indefinitely.
Is there a lifetime free VPN?
Windscribe's free plan is free for as long as you use it, there's no expiry. It's capped by monthly data rather than time, and the cap resets every month.
What are the risks of free VPNs?
The main risks are data logging and resale, weak or fake encryption, injected ads, and bandwidth being sold to third parties. The safeguard is transparency: a tested no-logs policy, independent audits, and open-source code, all of which Windscribe has.
Do free VPNs steal data?
Some do, harvesting browsing history or turning your device into a proxy. That's exactly why we publish audits, open-source our apps, and keep no identifying logs, so you don't have to take our word for it.
