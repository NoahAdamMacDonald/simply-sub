How to cancel your ExpressVPN subscription
Search the Support Center
Last updated: November 2, 2026
Share in FacebookShare in TwitterShare in WhatsappShare in TelegramShare by email
This guide will show you how to cancel your ExpressVPN subscription before your subscription ends. The steps vary based on where you purchased your subscription from.
Important: To cancel your subscription, you need to go to the app store or the ExpressVPN website. You cannot cancel your subscription within the ExpressVPN app.

Subscriptions purchased via the ExpressVPN website
If you paid with a credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, or Google Pay directly on expressvpn.com, follow the steps below.
On desktop and mobile devices:
Open any browser and go to My subscription. If prompted, log into your ExpressVPN account.
Click Cancel subscription below Automatic renewal and follow the prompts.
If you don’t see a “Cancel subscription” option: This can happen if a recent renewal payment failed. To prevent further renewal attempts, you can remove your saved payment method to turn off automatic renewal. Note that removing your only saved payment method will automatically turn off auto-renewal for all subscription plans under that account.
After you have canceled, you will not be billed automatically for another subscription cycle. You can still use ExpressVPN until the last day of your current subscription cycle.
Learn how to renew your subscription.
Need help? Contact the ExpressVPN Support Team for assistance.
Back to top
Subscriptions purchased via an app store (Apple App Store or Google Play store)
Note: To avoid charges, cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the free trial or the next billing date. Otherwise, your account will be automatically billed based on your selected plan.
If you subscribed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store using a credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, or Google Pay, you can cancel your subscription by following these steps:
On iPhone/iPad (Apple App Store):
Open the App Store.
Tap your profile icon in the top right.
Tap Subscriptions.
Find and tap ExpressVPN.
Tap Cancel Subscription and follow the prompts.
On Android (Google Play Store):
Open the Google Play Store.
Tap your profile icon in the top right.
Tap Payment & subscriptions.
Tap Subscriptions.
Find and tap ExpressVPN.
Tap Cancel Subscription and follow the prompts.
After you cancel, you can still use ExpressVPN until the last day of your current subscription cycle.
Need help? Contact the ExpressVPN Support Team for assistance.
Back to top
Subscriptions purchased with Bitcoin or Paymentwall
Since subscriptions purchased with Paymentwall or Bitcoin will not renew automatically, you will not need to manually cancel your subscription.
Learn how to renew your subscription.
Need help? Contact the ExpressVPN Support Team for assistance.
Back to top
What happens to the logins stored in my ExpressKeys account when I cancel my ExpressVPN subscription?
You can still access the logins stored in your ExpressKeys account even if your subscription is no longer active. However, you can’t add any new entries.
Need help? Contact the ExpressVPN Support Team for assistance.
