How many devices can be connected to ExpressVPN simultaneously on one subscription?

How many devices can I connect to the VPN simultaneously?
The number of devices you can connect to the VPN at the same time depends on which subscription tier you chose at checkout:
Basic: 10 devices at the same time.
Advanced: 12 devices at the same time.
Express Pro: 14 devices at the same time.
Examples of possible device combinations
One Express Pro subscription can be used on:
14 computers, or
6 iPhones, 4 iPads, and 4 MacBooks, or
5 computers, 2 Kindle Fire devices, 6 iPhones, and 1 router, or
Any other similar combination regardless of device type.
If you have reached the connection limit and want to connect on another device, disconnect a connected device. To disconnect, click or tap .
If you are getting a “Connection Limit Reached” warning but have fewer devices connected than your subscription tier allows, contact the ExpressVPN Support Team for immediate assistance.

What if I need to connect more than 14 devices?
If you would like to use ExpressVPN with more than 14 devices simultaneously, you can:
Purchase additional subscriptions, or
Share your connection from a Windows computer, or
Set up a VPN router and connect as many devices to it as you wish.
Need help? Contact the ExpressVPN Support Team for immediate assistance.
