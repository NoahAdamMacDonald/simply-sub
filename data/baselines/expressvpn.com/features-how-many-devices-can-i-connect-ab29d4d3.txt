Compatible devices and platforms
Get the best VPN for multiple devices with ExpressVPN’s easy-to-use apps
Computers (Windows, macOS, Linux)
Whether you’re working on a Mac, or coding on Linux, ExpressVPN protects your activity across desktop platforms, so your work and personal data stay private.
Secure your banking or work connections while keeping local services accessible with split tunneling. This advanced feature is available on all desktop devices and lets you select which apps use the VPN. Windows users also benefit from Lightway Turbo, our fastest VPN protocol.
Mobile devices (iOS, Android)
Mobile apps for iOS and Android run ExpressVPN’s Lightway protocol, providing full encryption without the battery drain traditional VPN protocols cause. Leave protection on through your entire workday and commute home without needing to charge by afternoon.
View your virtual location on the map, run a speed test to find the fastest servers, or switch protocols from within the app’s user-friendly menu.
Smart TVs and media boxes
Most smart TVs can’t run VPN apps, but ExpressVPN offers native apps for Apple TV, Android TV, and Amazon Fire TV Stick. For older devices without app support, our MediaStreamer tool provides smart DNS options for changing your virtual location without encryption.
Encryption hides your streaming activity from your ISP, which can help you avoid content-based throttling that often causes buffering during peak hours.
Web browsers with extensions
ExpressVPN's extension for Chrome, Edge, and Firefox can protect your browser on its own or be paired with the VPN app for device-wide protection. Connect, switch servers, or adjust VPN settings directly from your browser.
The extension also blocks WebRTC leaks and spoofs your browser's geolocation to help keep your location private.

Built on privacy-first technology
ExpressVPN’s privacy-first tech safeguards your traffic data from the ground up
TrustedServer infrastructure and regular audits
Traditional servers store data on hard drives, which can leave information behind. ExpressVPN’s TrustedServer technology ensures all servers run entirely on RAM so no activity data is stored, and wipes all servers regularly to load each server’s operating system with a fresh version.
ExpressVPN’s privacy protections, including our no-logs policy, are independently audited by firms such as PwC and Cure53 to confirm that they operate as designed.
ExpressVPN’s Lightway protocol for reliable speed
Network changes and traffic spikes cause VPN slowdowns: buffering, lag, dropped connections. Lightway’s minimalist design enables faster processing and instant reconnection when you switch from Wi-Fi to cellular.
Walk from your home Wi-Fi to your car without your VPN session dropping. Lightway keeps connections responsive whether you're stationary or moving between networks throughout your day.
Based in a privacy-friendly jurisdiction
ExpressVPN is based in the British Virgin Islands, outside the 5/9/14 Eyes intelligence-sharing alliances. The British Virgin Islands has no data-retention laws, which helps support ExpressVPN's audited no-logs policy.
That legal foundation supports our strict no-logs policy and ongoing privacy work, including research into post-quantum protections designed to address future threats. Our Trust Center explains these commitments transparently.

FAQ
Get some clear answers to the most common questions
Which VPN allows multiple devices?
ExpressVPN lets you secure up to 14 devices at once, covering computers, laptops, tablets, phones, smart TVs, consoles, and more.
Do I need a VPN for each device?
No. With ExpressVPN, a single account protects multiple devices simultaneously. One subscription protects your whole setup, so you don’t need separate accounts. This gives families, roommates, and multitaskers consistent privacy across all their devices.
Does a VPN work on all device types?
Yes. ExpressVPN supports computers, phones, smart TVs, media boxes, and more. Even devices that can’t run VPN apps, like gaming consoles or some smart TVs, can connect via MediaStreamer, our smart DNS feature. While this feature doesn’t encrypt your connection, you can use it to change the location associated with your online traffic.
Can I share my VPN subscription?
Yes. One ExpressVPN subscription covers up to 14 active devices, making it easy to share protection with your whole household. Each connection will get strong encryption and IP address masking to maintain privacy, without the need for separate accounts or additional subscriptions.
Is there an ExpressVPN family plan?
ExpressVPN subscription options support up to 10 or 14 simultaneous connections. This covers every family member's laptops, phones, consoles, and smart home devices with consistent encryption under one subscription
