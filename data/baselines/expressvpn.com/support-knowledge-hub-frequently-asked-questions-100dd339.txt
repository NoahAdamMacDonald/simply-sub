Does ExpressVPN throttle my connection?
No. ExpressVPN does not impose any, monthly data caps or bandwidth limits of its own.
While a VPN cannot change the overall data caps set by your internet service provider (ISP), it can help if your ISP is throttling your connection based on your specific online activity (such as streaming or gaming). By encrypting your traffic, a VPN hides what you are doing, which may help you avoid this content-based throttling and experience more consistent speeds.
