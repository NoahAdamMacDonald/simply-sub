What is ExpressKeys?
Search the Support Center
Last updated: October 21, 2026
Share in FacebookShare in TwitterShare in WhatsappShare in TelegramShare by email
ExpressKeys is ExpressVPN’s password manager, designed to securely store passwords, payment cards, notes, and files in one encrypted vault that syncs across your devices. This guide explains what you can do with ExpressKeys, how it protects your data, and where it’s available.

What you can store in ExpressKeys
ExpressKeys offers secure storage for the following information:
Logins and passwords: Store an unlimited number of usernames and passwords for your accounts.
Credit and debit card details: Save your payment information for quick access.
Secure notes: Store sensitive text such as PINs, recovery codes, or private information.
Two-factor authentication (2FA) codes: Generate one-time passwords for accounts with 2FA enabled.
File attachments: Attach files to your vault items to securely store any file type, including text files, PDF, images, and videos.
Need help? Contact the ExpressVPN Support Team for immediate assistance.

ExpressKeys features
ExpressKeys includes the following features:
Autofill: Automatically fill in your login credentials and 2FA codes on websites and apps.
Built-in 2FA authenticator: Generate time-based one-time passwords (TOTPs) for accounts with 2FA enabled.
Password generator: Create strong, unique passwords with ease.
Password strength checker: Evaluate the strength of your existing passwords.
Password health: Identify weak, reused, or exposed passwords, accounts without 2FA, and insecure URLs.
Biometric unlock: Use your fingerprint or Face ID to access your vault.
Multi-device sync: Access your vault on an unlimited number of devices.
Import from other password managers: Easily move your existing logins into ExpressKeys from other password managers or browsers.
Secure export and backup: Export your data as an encrypted ZIP file for safekeeping or transfer.
Need help? Contact the ExpressVPN Support Team for immediate assistance.

ExpressKeys security
ExpressKeys uses zero-knowledge encryption to protect your data. All logins, passwords, credit and debit card details, secure notes, and file attachments are encrypted and decrypted on your device with the vault password that only you know.
No one, not even ExpressVPN, can see the items stored in your password manager without the vault password.
Need help? Contact the ExpressVPN Support Team for immediate assistance.
Back to top
ExpressKeys availability
ExpressKeys is available as a standalone app on iOS (18 or above), Android (13 or above), and macOS (14.0 or above), and as a browser extension for Chrome (also compatible with other Chromium-based browsers) and Firefox.
You can access ExpressKeys either as part of an ExpressVPN Advanced or Express Pro subscription via the ExpressVPN website or by purchasing a standalone ExpressKeys plan directly through the app.
Learn more about setting up and using ExpressKeys in the ExpressKeys Support Center.
