How to access Audiobooks on Spotify
I have my own Premium plan
You get 12 hours of audiobook listening hours included each month.
✓ Your hours renew every month on your billing date; any unused hours expire and don't carry over.
✓ Hours can be used on any titles in our subscriber catalog.
✓ You can add Audiobooks+ to get more listening hours each month.
✓ You can buy a one-off top-up of hours, or buy access to individual titles.
This all applies if you’re the plan manager for a Premium Family or Duo plan, but note that the included hours are for your account only, not shared with plan members.
Learn more.
I’m on someone else’s Premium Family or Duo plan
You don’t have included hours. They belong to the account that manages your plan.
✓ Your plan manager can give you access to audiobooks listening time with the Audiobooks+ add-on.
✓ You can buy a one-off top-up of hours, or buy access to individual titles.
✓ You can listen to a sample of any audiobook at no cost.
Request Audiobooks+ from your plan manager.
I’m on Premium Student
Premium Student doesn’t include audiobook listening hours.
✓ You can buy access to individual titles.
✓You can listen to a sample of any audiobook at no cost.
To get access to audiobooks, you can change to a Premium plan that includes audiobook listening hours.
Explore Premium plans.
I’m on Spotify’s free, ad-supported plan
Our free, ad-supported plan doesn’t include audiobook listening hours.
✓ You can buy access to individual titles.
✓You can listen to a sample of any audiobook at no cost.
To get access to audiobooks, you can upgrade to a Premium plan that includes audiobook listening hours.
Explore Premium plans.
You can also add Audiobooks+ to your free plan to get monthly listening hours and access to our subscriber catalog.
