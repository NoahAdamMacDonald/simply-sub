What payment methods do you accept?
IVPN accepts the following payment methods via our website:
Bitcoin (via our own BTCPay server)
Monero (self-hosted)
PayPal
Credit/Debit Cards (except American Express and any types of Prepaid/Gift Cards)
In-app payments for IVPN Standard via our mobile apps for iOS and Android. Note: Android in-app payments are temporarily unavailable while the app is not on Google Play. Add time via My account on ivpn.net instead.
Physical voucher cards and digital voucher codes
Cash
We run our own nodes for both Bitcoin and Monero.
