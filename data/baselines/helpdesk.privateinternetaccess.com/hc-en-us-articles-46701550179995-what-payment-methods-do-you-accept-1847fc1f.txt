What Payment Methods Do You Accept?
August 31, 2026 15:36
Private Internet Access (PIA) accepts various payment methods subject to automatic renewal according to the subscription terms you choose (e.g., monthly, yearly, etc.).
You may purchase a subscription via our website here, through Google Play on Android, or through the App Store on iOS.
Recurring Payment Methods
If you're interested in a recurring subscription (automatically drafted payments), you can use the following payment options:
Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover)
Apple Pay
Google Pay
PayPal
Amazon Pay (specific locations only)
Non-Recurring Payment Methods
If you're interested in a one-time purchase, you can use the following payment options:
Digital wallets such as iDEAL and Qiwi (via Paymentwall)
Gift cards
Prepaid Visa or Mastercard
Non-recurring subscriptions can only renew through a special link sent via email. If you take no action, the account terminates automatically at the end of the 11-day grace period following the due date.
If you experience any issues or have questions, please contact our support team.
Important: If you are experiencing issues with prepaid cards, you need to make sure the prepaid card is registered with the card issuer and that the billing address you enter at checkout matches that registration exactly.
