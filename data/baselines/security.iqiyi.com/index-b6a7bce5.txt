iQIYI Security Response Center
About Us
The iQIYI Security Response Center (a.k.a. 71SRC) is a vulnerability collection and emergency response platform established by the iQIYI security team.71SRC aims at protecting privacy and data for hundreds of millions of iQIYI users.
Our Policy
Security researchers are welcome to submit vulnerabilities and threat intelligence for iQIYI global products. (websites, mobile APPs, etc.)As soon as the report is confirmed by our staff, you will be rewarded certain 71SRC points which can be exchanged for Amazon gift cards.The rewards for one vulnerability report range from 30RMB to 18,000RMB (around 4.3USD to 2,580USD). In some cases, rewards can be doubled if a critical vulnerability related to iQIYI core business was reported.
