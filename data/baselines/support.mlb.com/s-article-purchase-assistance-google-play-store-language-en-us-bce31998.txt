Purchasing a Google Play Store Subscription
In-app purchases of MLB+ or MLB.TV are purchased from and billed by Google Play, not MLB.
These purchases are subject to the terms and conditions of Google Play. Google Play purchases are non-refundable after purchase. If you wish to confirm the type of subscription that has been purchased from Google Play, please consult your Google Play account or receipt as each subscription will have a unique billing name.
An MLB+ or MLB.TB seasonal purchase is valid for one year, and will be re-billed every year by Google Play until canceled by the user. Google Play will send an email following a successful billing. Google Play subscriptions can be managed from your Google Wallet. MLB can't manage Google Play subscriptions. Google Play subscription cancellation steps are listed below for your convenience.
An MLB+ or MLB.TV Monthly purchase is valid for one month and will be re-billed every month year-round by Google Play until canceled by the user. Google Play will send an email following a successful billing. Google Play subscriptions can be managed from your Google Wallet. MLB can't manage Google Play subscriptions. Google Play subscription cancellation steps are listed below for your convenience.

Accessing Your Google Play Purchase on Other Supported Devices
A major benefit of connecting your Google Play purchase to a registered MLB email and password is being able to access the purchase across other compatible devices. Connecting a purchase to an MLB account also allows Customer Service to better assist you with inquiries and troubleshooting. You'll have an opportunity to connect your purchase to an MLB account after making the purchase, or it can be completed at a later time. You can only connect a Google Play purchase to one MLB account. If you connect the purchase to a second MLB account, the first account will lose all entitlements.
Tap on the "Menu"
Tap on "Settings"
Tap on "MLB Account" and log in with your registered MLB email and password credentials, or create a new account. This will link the purchase to the MLB account.
Canceling a Google Play Monthly Subscription
Select "Settings"
Choose "Subscriptions"
Select MLB
Tap "Cancel Subscription"
