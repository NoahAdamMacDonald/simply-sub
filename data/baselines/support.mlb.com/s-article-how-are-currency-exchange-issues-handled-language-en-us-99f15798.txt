How are currency exchange issues handled?
All online subscription products and MLB.com Shop orders are transacted in U.S. Dollars. Banks and payment card issuers may convert currency to local currency in accordance with that bank or issuer's policies and practices regarding exchange rates and may charge foreign transaction and/or currency exchange fees. Any questions regarding currency conversion, exchange rates or any other associated fees that may apply should be directed to your bank or payment card issuer.
