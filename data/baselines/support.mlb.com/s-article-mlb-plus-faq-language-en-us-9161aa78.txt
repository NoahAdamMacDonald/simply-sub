MLB+ FAQs
MLB+ Yearly Features
What is MLB+?
MLB+ is the rebranded MLB At Bat subscription for the 2026 season.
What is included with my MLB+ Yearly subscription?
You get blackout-free live and on-demand audio of all regular season MLB games, 24/7 access to live *MLB Network programming (including select live out-of-market games and shows like MLB Tonight), live MiLB games, MLB Big Inning, and original series/documentaries through the 2026 season. Listen to live radio broadcasts without blackouts for the 2026 Postseason.
*MLB Network streams are available to subscribers in the U.S. ONLY (not available internationally).
When will my subscription automatically renew?
MLB+ Yearly: If you are an MLB+ yearly subscriber, your card on file will be charged on or about February 28, 2026. The subscription automatically renews on or around the anniversary date of your initial subscription each year.
MLB+ Monthly: Stream MLB Network 24/7 (US only) for an entire calendar year, plus, live audio for all 30 teams (no blackouts), live MiLB games and MLB Big Inning. Monthly billing twelve months per year.
What if the primary payment card on file fails?
MLB may charge any alternate card(s) saved in the Payment Methods Section of your MLB.com account to ensure uninterrupted access.
How do I cancel my automatic renewal?
You can cancel your subscription online by following the link in your renewal email. If you cancel prior to the billing date, you will not be billed.
Free Trial Policy
You may cancel a 7-day risk-free trial of MLB + Yearly and Monthly any time during the 7-day window and will still have access to MLB +for the full 7 days. If you do not cancel, you will be billed on the eighth day for the current Yearly or Monthly subscription price. MLB reserves the right to determine customer eligibility for trial memberships in its sole discretion.
If you purchased the MLB + monthly subscription, the payment card you have on file with us will be billed monthly on approximately the same monthly date that you originally purchased, with monthly billing 12 months per year. You can verify your monthly billing date in your MLB Account under “Manage Subscriptions.”
