How to Cancel your Subscription
Customer Service
Please visit our Main CS Help Center, click: https://support.mlb.com/s/
To cancel via our customer service online submission form, click here https://support.mlb.com/s/contact-us
If you would like to email us your cancel request, please email us at subscriptioncancel@mlb.com
If you would like to speak to a live agent to cancel your current subscription, please call us at:
US Phone and Text Support: 866-244-2291
International Phone Support: 315-203-6761
If you would like to cancel via Chat
Request human chat through the CS Virtual Assistant on the main CS Help Center found here https://support.mlb.com/s/
US Phone and Text Support: 866-244-2291
To send an email to our Executive Relations, please visit https://support.mlb.com/s/contact-us
Online
To manage your subscription online, please go to the Account Section on MLB.com. Click the Manage Subscriptions section of the page and cancel online.
If you would like to cancel your subscription online, click here to get started. If you cancel your automatic renewal prior to billing, you will not be billed.
If you would like to cancel via social support , please reach out to us @MLBFanSupport on X
To cancel through our Virtual Assistant, please click here https://support.mlb.com/s/
