For more information on updating or managing your MLB.com account click the topics below
Create an MLB Account
To create an MLB account, click on the image below. On the page, enter the requested fields under the "Register" heading. The email address and password you enter will be used for future sign-ins. Before you click the "Register" button, make sure to double-check the spelling of your e-mail address and remember that your password is case sensitive.

Managing Email Notifications
You can enable or disable all MLB email notifications by going to your Subscription Management page here: https://www.mlb.com/account/newsletters.
From there, you will need to go to the “Email Newsletter” tab and check (or uncheck) all the boxes listed on that page. Once you have done that, select “Save” at the bottom of the page to save your progress.
