How to Download Movies and TV Shows on Zee 5
How to Download Movies and TV Shows on Zee 5
How Can I Download Videos on Zee 5?
Watch offline
How to download Zee 5 content
No Wi-Fi? No problem! Whether you're travelling, commuting or saving data, you can download select movies and episodes and watch them offline. 🍿
⚠️ Before you start
✓
Downloads are available only on the Zee 5 Android and iOS apps.
✓
You need an active Zee 5 subscription to download.
✓
Downloaded content plays only in its original audio language. Audio language switching is not available.
📥 Step by step
How to download
1
Log in to your Zee 5 account.
2
Open the movie or episode you want to download.
3
Tap the Play icon.
4
Tap the Download (⬇) icon.
5
Select your preferred video quality.
6
Optional: choose Make this my default setting to use the same quality for future downloads.
✅ That's it! Once the download is complete, you can watch it offline.
🎥 Good to know
Important things to know
What can be downloaded?
✓
Individual movies and TV episodes
✕
Entire seasons cannot be downloaded at once
Why can't I download some titles?
Some titles don't have a download option because of licensing and copyright restrictions.
⏳ How long do downloads last?
Up to 30 days, as long as you don't uninstall the app or clear its cache.
Subtitles and audio
✕
Subtitles are not supported
✕
Only the original audio language is available
