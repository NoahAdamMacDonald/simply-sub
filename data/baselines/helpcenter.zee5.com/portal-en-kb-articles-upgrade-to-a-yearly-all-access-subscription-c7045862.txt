Upgrade to a Yearly All Access Subscription
✨ Yearly All Access
Upgrade once. Enjoy all year.
Why renew every month when you can enjoy uninterrupted entertainment all year long? Upgrade to a Yearly All Access Plan and unlock blockbuster movies, binge-worthy shows, Originals, live TV, and more - all in one subscription.

⚡ Quick and easy
How to upgrade
Already on a Monthly Subscription? Move to a yearly plan in four simple steps.
1
Tap the Upgrade button
Open your current plan from My Subscriptions.
2
Choose your yearly plan
Select the Yearly All Access Plan that suits you.
3
Review the difference
Your unused subscription value is adjusted automatically.
4
Pay and start watching
Your upgraded subscription is activated instantly.

💰 Fair pricing
How are upgrade charges calculated?

❓ Good to know
Frequently asked questions
Can I upgrade anytime during my subscription? Yes. You can upgrade at any time. The cost is adjusted based on the value remaining in your current subscription.
Will my subscription date change after the upgrade? Your new subscription will expire 12 months from your original subscription start date, not from the upgrade date.
What if I want to switch to a cheaper plan? Downgrades are not available during an active subscription. You can choose a different plan after your current plan expires.
Can I cancel my upgraded subscription? Yes, you can cancel at any time. Please note that refunds are not applicable to subscription upgrades.
