😔 Still want to cancel?
How to cancel your subscription
Follow the steps for the device you subscribed on.
📱 Android
1
Open the Zee 5 app
2
Tap Profile
3
Go to Control Center
4
Select Plans & Rentals
5
Tap Manage Subscription
6
Choose Cancel Subscription
💻 Website
1
Sign in to your Zee 5 account
2
Click Profile
3
Open Control Center
4
Select Account Management
5
Click My Subscription
6
Choose Cancel Renewal and confirm
🍎 iPhone & iPad
1
Open Settings
2
Tap your Apple ID
3
Select Subscriptions
4
Choose Zee 5
5
Tap Cancel Subscription
🛠️ Trouble cancelling?
Try a desktop or laptop browser:
1
Sign in to Zee 5
2
Go to Profile > Control Center
3
Open Account Management
4
Select My Subscription
5
Click Cancel Subscription
