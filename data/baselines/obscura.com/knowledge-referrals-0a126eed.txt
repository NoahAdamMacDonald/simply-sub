Referring a Friend to Obscura
Last updated: Dec 23, 2025
If you share an Obscura referral link with a friend, then you both earn a free month of Obscura!
Referral Link Privacy
Section titled “Referral Link Privacy”
Each referral link includes a random identifier that you can regenerate to prevent correlation to a previous link. Previously generated links stay active, allowing you to share different links in separate contexts at the same time.
One month after the free months are given, we automatically delete the association between the new account and the referrer.
How to Use Referrals
Section titled “How to Use Referrals”
Open the Referrals Page in your Obscura user portal
Click the Copy to clipboard button under the referral link
Share the link with your friend
Your friend opens the link
They save their account number and log in
Once your friend pays for Obscura, you both automatically earn a free month
