How Does the Amazon Music Unlimited Family Plan Work?
This plan allows up to six members of your family to receive the benefits of Amazon
Music Unlimited with either a monthly or annual subscription. Up to six people can
listen at the same time.
Account Holders
You can invite or remove family members from your plan, and manage the payment method
for the subscription.
You can create multiple profiles under a single account, or invite a family member
to join their Family plan using their own account.
You can invite family members to sign in with their own account. Share the invitation
link through email, or SMS/text messages.
Note:
Family members must all reside in the same country, and
can only join one Amazon Music Unlimited Family Plan at a
time.
You can listen to one audiobook each month with an active subscription.
Invited Family Members
You can:
Join the plan by accepting invitations.
Remove yourself from the plan.
Edit your profile.
Transfer your profile to a different account.
