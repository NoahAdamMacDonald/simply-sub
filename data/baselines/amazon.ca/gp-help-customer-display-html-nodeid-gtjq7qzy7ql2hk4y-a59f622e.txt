End your Amazon Prime Membership
When you sign up for Amazon Prime, your membership is set to automatically renew each
year. You can turn off this automatic renewal at any time.
Paid members who don't use their benefits are eligible for a full refund of the current
membership period. This refund will be processed in three to five business days.
End Membership
Note: At the end of the free trial period, you will automatically upgrade to a paid membership.
To turn off the automatic renewal or upgrade:
Visit Manage Prime Membership.
Review the renewal date:
If you have an Amazon Prime free trial, select Do Not Continue.
If you have a paid Amazon Prime membership, select End Membership.
Note: Customers who sign up for Amazon Prime using the Android mobile shopping app or Prime
Video Android app must manage their subscription through Google Subscription services.
Subscriptions:
Subscriptions on Prime Video, like Paramount+, will remain once your Prime membership
ends.
For more information, review your Memberships and Subscriptions.
