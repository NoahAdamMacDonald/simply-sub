Your Privacy Choices: Opt out of sale or sharing of personal information and opt out of targeted ads
When you interact with us or our services, we may disclose personal information to third parties, partners, and NBCUniversal’s related businesses as described in our Privacy Policy. Some of this activity may be considered a "sale" or "share” under applicable law (residents of applicable states can learn more here). To opt out of such “sale” or “sharing,” follow the steps below. Opting out will also prevent the processing of your personal information for targeted ads.
Select the “Your Privacy Choices” link in the footer or settings menu of the brand with which you engage.
For direct contact information such as your name, email address and other associated personal information, please submit this online form. If you select the online form from Your Privacy Choices, complete that form first.
For cookies and devices identifiers, use the toggle on the Your Privacy Choices tool to switch the “Allow Sale/Share/Targeted Ads” toggle under Manage Preferences to OFF (grey color) by moving it LEFT and clicking “Confirm My Choice.” You must log in to your account first if you want your choice to apply across browsers and devices that you access through your account. This will close this box, so please open the Opt-Out Form first.
Please note: Opting out will also prevent disclosure of personal information to our related businesses (except as necessary for the provision of services or when you permit to such disclosure). You will still see ads, but they may be less relevant or based only on our first-party information about you.
Your choices related to cookies and device identifiers will apply to devices accessed with your logged in account if you opt out (or opt back in) when you are logged in. If you are not logged in, your choice will only apply to this browser, and you will need to renew this choice on each browser or device where you want your choice to apply or if you clear your browser cookies.
Additional Targeted Advertising Opt-Out Tools
In addition to the opt out tools, above, there are third party tools you may use with respect to certain targeted advertising. Please visit our Cookie Notice for more information.
Sensitive Personal Information
Where you have opted in on a mobile device to provide precise geolocation data, you can change your permissions in your device settings.
