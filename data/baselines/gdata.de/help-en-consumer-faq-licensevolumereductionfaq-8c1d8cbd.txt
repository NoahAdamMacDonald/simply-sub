How can I reduce my license, i.e., reduce the number of devices to be protected?
Unlike increasing the number of devices covered by a license, reducing the number cannot be carried out in an automated manner
and requires the involvement of our accounting department. The main reason for this is that if advance payments have already
been made, too much money will have been paid in this case.
If you would like to reduce your license, i.e., reduce the number of devices to be protected, please call up the following
form: https://www.gdata.de/en/customer-service.
Enter your license details in this form and let us know in the description how many devices you would like to reduce the
license to.
