The Features of MSA Light and MSA Premium
In this chapter, you will learn which features MSA Light and MSA Premium provide you with.
Basic information
MSA Light is free of charge and provides you with only a subset of the features of MSA Premium. During installation, we provide you with a trial version. You will then receive the full functionality of the Premium version for 30 days. After this trial period, the functions will be limited to those of the Light version.
MSA Premium is subject to a fee and contains the full range of functions. So you need a license to install it. You can find out about the different options for acquiring this license in chapter → About the licences for MSA Premium.
You can control and manage your devices on which G DATA Mobile Security is installed via a web portal, the G DATA Mobile Security Center.
