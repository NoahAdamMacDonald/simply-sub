How can I have my personal data deleted by G DATA?
Under Article 17 GDPR, you have the right to erasure of your personal data stored by us.
Would you like to delete your personal data?
Use the contact form at
My G DATA or send us an email to dsgvo@gdata.de. In the email, be sure to provide
your customer number and user login/registration number of your license.
