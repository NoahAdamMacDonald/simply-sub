This address serves as the contact point in accordance with Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content of the European Union for the implementation of transparency requirements.
Labeling of AI-generated and AI-modified content
G DATA labels content that has been generated or modified using artificial intelligence and is subject to the labeling requirement. This is based on Article 50(4) of the EU AI Regulation (2024/1689). We use the official EU label for this purpose. We also adhere to the Code of Practice.
What do we label, and how?
Deepfakes subject to labeling requirements are marked with an AI label. Content that is clearly unrealistic is not marked.
The following labels are used. The color and transparency of the labels may vary.
AI generated: content generated entirely by AI
AI modified: content has been substantially modified using AI
AI: AI label if neither of the more specific variants applies
AI translated: special label for AI generated translations
