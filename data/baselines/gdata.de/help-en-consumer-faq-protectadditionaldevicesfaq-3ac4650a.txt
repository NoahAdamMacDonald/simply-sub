Protect additional devices with a G DATA license
With your G DATA license, you can protect up to 10 devices. You can conveniently manage your license (extension, product
change) via My G DATA.
To protect an additional device, proceed as follows:
1
Take the device on which the installation is to be performed.
2
Open the My G DATA WebPortal on the device and log in with your username and password.
3
Navigate to the view "My devices". Click on + Protect device there.
4
Select the operating system of the device.
5
Download the software either by clicking on Download software or by scanning the QR code.
6
Install the software on your device.
Further information on the installation process for
Windows,
Android,
iOS,
macOS.
7
Once installation and activation are complete, the device is protected.
