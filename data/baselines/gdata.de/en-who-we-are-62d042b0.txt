40 years of experience

No-Backdoor Guarantee
No backdoors—not even for government agencies
From a pioneer in antivirus technology to a leading player in the world of cybersecurity.
In 1987, we developed the world’s first antivirus software. Today, drawing on our expertise and innovation, over 500 employees based in Germany ensure the security of small, large, and medium-sized businesses, critical infrastructure such as hospitals and airports, and millions of private customers around the world.
Several decades after the release of its first antivirus software, G DATA has become one of the leading players in the cybersecurity industry. We protect companies’ digital assets through a comprehensive security portfolio.
Andreas Lüning • CEO of G DATA CyberDefense AG
Comprehensive Cyber Defense
From prevention and detection to immediate assistance in the event of an IT emergency: Our cyber defense strategies are designed to help companies stop attacks early on and remain capable of acting quickly when the need arises.
Trustworthy and secure
All research and software development takes place exclusively in Germany. Our solutions are designed to meet strict German and European data protection requirements—for organizations that do not compromise on sovereignty and compliance.
Connected to the region
Our service and support teams are based in Bochum—right next to the development department. This streamlines communication, strengthens our commitment, and ensures quick assistance: by phone or email. We make time for you, as much time as it takes.

2024 ISO 27001 certification
ISO 27001 certification confirms G DATA's high standards for information security and its responsible handling of customer data.

2020 New name: G DATA CyberDefense AG
The name change to G DATA CyberDefense AG reflects what the company stands for today: comprehensive cybersecurity comprising software, services, and expertise.
2019 Innovations DeepRay® and BEAST
After years of research, two radically new approaches are being integrated into all solutions: DeepRay® uses AI to detect new malware. BEAST maps behavior in a graph to stop attackers.

IT security goes beyond technology: With Security Awareness Trainings, G DATA helps its customers raise employee awareness of cyber threats.

2018 No backdoor guarantee
With the campaign “Meine Daten bleiben in Deutschland”, G DATA reaffirms: Our security solutions are guaranteed to contain no backdoors, whether for cybercriminals or intelligence agencies.

G DATA tests networks with simulated attacks. The Incident Response Service helps quickly contain and resolve cyberattacks. This marked the start of numerous additional services in the years that followed.

2017 Ransomware protection
Ransomware has the world on edge. G DATA therefore enhances its solutions with behavioral monitoring that detects and blocks encryption attempts early on.

Our Management
Kai Figge
Founder & Board Member
Kai Figge is one of the founders who launched G DATA as a garage startup in 1985. He has been at the forefront of technological change from the very beginning and has set a clear customer-focused direction for the company’s services and products. As Chief Financial and Human Resources Officer, Kai Figge is responsible for the company’s long-term financing and for recruiting skilled professionals. In the competition for the best talent, his goal is to make G DATA an attractive employer. Lifelong learning, sports opportunities, and events in a culturally inclusive atmosphere define the unique G DATA spirit.
Andreas Lüning
Founder & Board Member
As a co-founder of G DATA CyberDefense AG, Andreas Lüning has helped shape the direction of this leading IT security provider from the very beginning: in 1985, he developed the world’s first virus scanner to protect computers from malware. In his role as Executive Board Member and CTO, he oversees all technical departments of the company and, together with our employees, will drive the further development of G DATA CyberDefense AG into a leading provider of security technologies.
Frank Heisler
Board member
Frank Heisler knows the IT security industry like few others. As a long-time employee and experienced manager, he previously served on the Executive Board from 2006 to 2014. Since then, as Managing Director of G DATA Service GmbH, he has been driving the expansion of customer support. In 2020, he was reappointed to the Executive Board. In his role, Frank Heisler oversees the areas of Sales, Marketing, Corporate Communications, and Service & Support—with the goal of delivering the company’s IT security services to customers even more efficiently.
