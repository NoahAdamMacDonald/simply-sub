What is a VPN?
VPN stands for Virtual Private Network and allows you to mask your IP address and establish an encrypted connection to the internet. Once the VPN client is activated, all online activity on your device remains anonymous. You are effectively invisible to criminal hackers, data-hungry companies, or government agencies. A VPN service also helps you bypass artificial access restrictions on media and websites or censorship. With just one click, it routes your IP address to another country that is not affected by the restrictions.
Why use G DATA VPN?
With a VPN connection, you can protect your privacy quickly and reliably. Our app offers you unlimited, encrypted data traffic on your devices (Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS). A vast selection of high-speed VPN servers in over 75 locations worldwide ensures maximum anonymity and security when accessing media and data. Artificial barriers that restrict your online activity are bypassed. Especially important for your privacy: Your data traffic is never logged.
