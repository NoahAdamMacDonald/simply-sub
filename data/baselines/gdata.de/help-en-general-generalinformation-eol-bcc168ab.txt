Products, components, and features that are no longer supported (end of life, EOL)
There are many reasons why software reaches the end of life.
Technological developments
Changing demand due to market developments
Rising maintenance costs for outdated products
Stricter legal requirements
There are many other reasons as well. Even for our products and components that have performed reliably over a long period of time, there will eventually come a point when we discontinue support for these products.
On this page, you will find a list of all products and components that we are removing or have already removed from our product portfolio (end of life).
