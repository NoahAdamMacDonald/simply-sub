HOW DO I USE STRIPE LINK?
Link is a digital wallet that facilitates a quicker checkout with credit/debit cards. If you already have a Link account, you can checkout with 1-Click. If you don’t have a Link account, you can create one during the check out process.
Navigate to the checkout page for the item you wish to purchase
For Subscriptions, if available, select the “Monthly” or “Yearly” option
Enter your email address
If you already have a saved Link account, you will receive a text with a verification code at the phone number associated with Link your account.
Enter the verification code in the new field below the email address box Entering this code will pre-fill your payment information.
Enter your email into the email confirmation field and add a password
Enter your credit card’s CVC
If you don’t have a saved Link account, confirm your email address and add a password
Enter your credit card information
Once you have added your credit card information, a new pre-selected checkbox will appear below the credit card fields “Save your info for secure 1-click checkout with Link”
IF you don't want to opt-in to Stripe Link, uncheck this box
Enter your phone number
Select Start Subscription // Complete Purchase to transact
Once the purchase is complete, you can start watching.
If you can no longer access the phone number associated with your Link account, you can reset your Link account by following the steps in this help doc - https://support.link.com/how-to-reset-your-account?status=phone This will only reset your Link account and will not affect your Subscription.
