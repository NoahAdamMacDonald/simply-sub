What are cookies?
Cookies are text files containing small amounts of information which are downloaded to your computer or mobile device when you visit a site, which allow a site to recognize your device. When you visit our website or app, we may use cookies and similar technologies (i.e. pixels).
Why do we use cookies and similar technologies?
Cookies do a lot of different jobs, such as keeping you logged in, remembering your preferences and generally improving the user experience. They can also help us measure the effectiveness of our advertising efforts and generate analytics insights that help drive product improvements.
Do we use cookies?
We may use cookies or similar technologies for essential and functional purposes, such as enhancing the security of our platform and remembering your preferences. We may also use cookies for analytics and advertising purposes, such as understanding what content is most popular with our subscribers, and supporting our advertising efforts.
