1. Introduction
This privacy policy describes how we collect and process your personal data, in compliance with applicable data protection laws protecting your privacy when you use PureVPN.
In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our”, “ PureVPN” refer to PureVPN and/or GZ Systems Private Ltd Intershore Chambers, P.O Box 4342 Road Town, Tortola, VG1110 British Virgin Islands.
We are committed to safeguarding your privacy when you use our services. Before using PureVPN, please carefully read and agree to the privacy policy. By subscribing to our services, you are giving informed consent to have read, understood, and agree to abide by this privacy policy. If you do not agree with the privacy policy, please refrain from using PureVPN.
2. Our Services and Platform
PureVPN is available as a mobile, TV and web-based application. The mobile app, including Android TV app and Apple TV app can be downloaded from the Google “Play Store” and Apple “App Store”, respectively (hereinafter collectively referred to as the “App”). The web-based version and proxy browser extensions (hereinafter referred as the “Web-App”) are available via recommended web browsers such as Google Chrome, Brave, Mozilla Firefox for Windows, Mac and Linux devices. All of our applications, mobile, TV and Web-Apps are designed to protect your online privacy and security.
3. What Information We Collect
In order to use our App and Web app, you need to create a user account. This account serves as your identity with PureVPN and is required to access our services. When you sign up with PureVPN, we will ask for the following information:
Name.
Email address.
Password.
Payment method.
This information is collected and stored to provide and improve our services to you (“stored data”). No other information is collected or stored at signup.
Additionally, we also collect the following information for customer support during service usage:
Day of connection (Date) – We do NOT track or store the time you connected to our services.
ISP Tracking (ISP Name and Routes) – We do NOT track or store your personal IP or exact location.
Connection time – We do NOT track or store the specific times you connected to our server.
Usage and Activity logs – We do NOT monitor or store any activity log for any user.
For app and service improvements, we collect the following information:
Software analytics and statistics.
Data from emails, live chats, and feedback forms.
None of the data listed above can be used to identify or track users.
4. Information We Do NOT Collect
We do NOT store any logs that can identify or help in monitoring your activity. We do this to ensure PureVPN has no information to share even if we are forced to. None of the following records are stored by PureVPN.
Browsing activity.
Identifiable connection logs.
Records of IPs assigned.
A customer’s original IP.
Connection timestamps.
Browsing history.
Sites visited.
Outgoing traffic.
Content or data accessed.
DNS queries generated by the user.
You are invisible – even we can’t see what you do online
NOTE: In case you have enabled the Tracker and Ad Blocker feature we will be able to view the domains during the connection period.
Our systems, processes, and servers are designed not to store any of the above-mentioned user information. We do this by design to ensure no data can be stored.

7. Dark Web Monitoring and Data Broker Removal Services
Our Digital Privacy Protection features comprise a free exposure scan, Dark Web Monitoring and Data Broker Service and Removal, available through our PureVPN apps and web app. Each is described below. We do not sell the information you give us for any of these features, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. We process this information to perform our contract with you and, where consent or a separate authorisation is required, we obtain it separately, as described in Section 15.
Free Exposure Scan
You can run a free scan, without subscribing, to check whether your email address appears in known data breaches and other online exposure sources. To run the scan, we collect your email address and check it against breach intelligence from our third-party intelligence providers. We use your email address to run the scan and to send you a small number of reminders about your scan. Your email address is automatically and permanently deleted within 30 days of your scan or after the final reminder is sent, whichever is earlier.
You can stop the reminders at any time using the unsubscribe link in each email, or contact us using the details in Section 18 to have your email address deleted sooner.
Information We Process for Dark Web Monitoring and Data Broker Service and Removal
When you use Dark Web Monitoring or Data Broker Service and Removal, we process information you provide to activate and deliver the relevant feature. Depending on the feature, this may include your name, email addresses, phone numbers, credit card number (without the CVV code), Social Security or national identity number, passport number, date of birth, and current or previous home addresses. Other than your account email address, providing these details is optional.
For Data Broker Service and Removal, we may also require your authorization to act on your behalf, including a power of attorney where required, and information necessary to verify your identity or match your records.
We also obtain information from intelligence providers, breach datasets, public records, people-search websites and data brokers. This may include exposure findings, associated personal information, listing details and records of removal requests and their outcomes. The information processed depends on the features you activate and the information available from the relevant sources.
Dark Web Monitoring
PureVPN delivers its Dark Web Monitoring service through the processing of monitoring identifiers, searches, matching, analysis and delivery of exposure findings to your PureVPN account. When you subscribe, we automatically check your account email address for data breaches, and you can add the other details listed under “Information We Process” above as monitoring identifiers.
Standard and Plus Plan subscribers can monitor an email address and see how many breaches have been found. Max Plan subscribers can also add the other monitoring identifiers, see full breach details and receive recommendations from us. You can turn off auto-monitoring at any time in the settings.
Historical searches and ongoing monitoring depend on the identifier, source and feature activated. Not every supported identifier is monitored continuously.
We use your monitoring identifiers only to provide this feature under your subscription with us.
Who We Share Information With
Depending on the feature, the service may search intelligence maintained within our own intelligence platform and obtain results from third-party intelligence providers. We disclose to those providers only the information necessary to perform the relevant search or monitoring function. Each provider handles that information under its own privacy policy and may use it only to provide this service to us.
For Dark Web Monitoring and Data Broker Service and Removal, we also make the disclosures necessary to deliver the service to removal service providers, verification and notification providers, and data brokers receiving authorised requests.
Security, Verification and Alerts
We do not store your monitoring identifiers in readable form; they are encrypted. Before we show you breach details, we send a one-time password to your phone or email address to confirm it is you. Breach details are shown only to verified Max Plan subscribers and are never included in public search results.
If your details appear in a new breach, we notify you by email or in the app. We only send these alerts after you have confirmed your email address.
Data Broker Service and Removal
PureVPN delivers data broker discovery and managed removal services where discovery scans identify potential matches on supported people-search websites and data broker sources. A discovery scan does not, by itself, authorise or initiate removal requests.
If you activate managed removal, you appoint us as your authorised agent and we process the identifying information and authorisation needed to locate relevant profiles and submit deletion and opt-out requests on your behalf (see “Information We Process” above). Depending on the broker’s requirements, this may include a power of attorney or other written authorisation, and we will ask you to sign an authorisation form. We also keep a record of each request we send, including the data broker’s name and the request reference.
We disclose the information reasonably necessary to locate your record, demonstrate our authority and fulfil the relevant request to the applicable data broker and to any service provider assisting with the removal process. Brokers receiving requests may process that information under their own privacy notices and applicable legal obligations.
We will send your details to data brokers only with your explicit consent, which we ask for separately when you sign the relevant authorisation. You can withdraw that consent at any time as described below; this does not affect requests already sent.
We use relevant profile information, correspondence, submission records and available evidence to manage requests, follow up, verify outcomes and display service status. Where included in your service, we conduct recurring checks for supported listings and their reappearance.
You may revoke your authorisation or deactivate Data Broker Service and Removal by using the available controls or by contacting PureVPN. We will stop initiating new requests after the revocation or deactivation takes effect. Requests already received by brokers may continue to be processed by those brokers, and information already disclosed cannot necessarily be recalled.
Keeping and Deleting Your Information
We keep your monitoring identifiers while Dark Web Monitoring is turned on. If you turn off auto-monitoring, remove a monitoring identifier or close your account, we stop monitoring and delete the relevant details from our systems in line with Section 10, unless we are legally required to keep them. Our intelligence providers keep information in line with their own privacy policies.
For Data Broker Service and Removal, we delete or anonymise your details within 30 days after you stop using the feature or your subscription ends, whichever is earlier. Requests to delete information held by PureVPN are separate from requests to remove information held by independent data brokers or intelligence sources. We will coordinate requests with the relevant service providers where required.

Provisioning/entitlement data: subscription/entitlement status; Partner-provided service identifiers (including non-public user/device reference IDs).
Device/app data: device type, OS/app version, language/time zone; diagnostic data (crash reports, error codes) and performance indicators.
Network/service operation data: session/service events needed to operate the service (e.g., session start/stop, selected region/gateway, protocol/transport); security and integrity signals to prevent misuse..
Enterprise admin/support inputs: limited troubleshooting inputs where the Partner enables a workflow involving PureVPN (typically via Partner administrators).
Data minimization: PureVPN processes Personal Data only to the extent necessary to provide the VPN service and support the Partner’s documented operational requirements, and does not process Personal Data for any unrelated purpose.
IMPORTANT: PureVPN does not ask for or require Sensitive Personal Data (including special category data) and does not intentionally process it in Partner Deployments.
HOW WE USE THE INFORMATION
PureVPN processes Personal Data solely as a processor on behalf of the Partner and only as necessary to:
provide, operate, and maintain the VPN service
monitor performance, troubleshoot, provide support, and respond to security/service incidents
protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the services and Personal Data (including abuse/fraud prevention)
comply with applicable legal obligations that apply to PureVPN as a processor and, where permitted, notify the Partner before processing.
SHARING AND DISCLOSURES
Where applicable, PureVPN may disclose Personal Data ONLY as permitted by the Agreement/DPA and on the Partner’s documented instructions, including (but not limited to) with the Partner and its authorized administrators/users; and approved sub-processors supporting the services, as authorized under the DPA.
Compelled Disclosure: PureVPN may disclose Personal Data to third parties if compelled by binding applicable law, court order, or regulatory authority. In such an event, PureVPN will provide the Partner with prior written notice of the legal requirement unless the applicable law prohibits PureVPN from providing such notice.
IMPORTANT: PureVPN does not sell or share Personal Data for advertising or other purposes.
INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS
Partner Deployments may involve processing or remote access from multiple countries. Where Personal Data is transferred internationally, PureVPN will use a valid transfer mechanism (e.g., EU SCCs or equivalent) and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures.
DATA RETENTION
Retention periods (or criteria) for operational records, diagnostic data, and support inputs are described in the applicable Partner agreement and/or DPA and may depend on the selected deployment option and configuration. Where information is routed to Partner-controlled systems, the Partner controls retention under its policies and legal obligations. Unless otherwise agreed or required by law, PureVPN retains Personal Data only as long as necessary for limited operational purposes only.
DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS AND REQUESTS
End Users have the right to access, correct, or erase their Personal Data, and to object to or restrict processing. To exercise these rights, End Users should submit their requests to the Partner using the contact details the Partner has provided.
End Users also have the right to lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.
