About us
At Kape Technologies, we envision a world where people retain their security, anonymity, and freedom online. As more of our daily lives shift to the digital world, security and privacy have never been more important.
Our story
Kape has been focused on protecting consumers as they go about their daily digital lives since the acquisition of our first VPN brand in 2017. In the last few years we have grown our customer base to over 7 million paying users.
To realize our vision, Kape develops, acquires, and distributes industry-leading digital security software and provides expert advice to consumers. We believe in having the best products, supported by top teams developing world-class technology.
Our guiding principles
Honesty
01
We take a transparent and open approach to protecting our users’ privacy, security, and freedom online.
Privacy
02
You don’t have to trust us with your data because we don’t collect or store it — proof is better than trust.
Service
03
We make decisions with the end-user in mind while balancing social, environmental, and economic profit.
Freedom
04
We believe the internet should be democratized and that all data should be treated equally without manipulation.
Autonomy
05
We believe in individuals having sovereign control of their own data without it being collected, stored, and sold without permission.
