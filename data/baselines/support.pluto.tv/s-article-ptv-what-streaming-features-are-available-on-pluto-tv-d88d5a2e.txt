What streaming features are available on Pluto TV?
To learn more, check out any topic below:
Watch List
The Watch List feature lets you save content to a list, so you can view it at a later time. Plus, it's device-specific, and no free account is needed to use it! To add content to your Watch List, use these steps:
1. Tap on the video screen
2. Tap on the plus icon to add to your Watch List
3. Content has been added when you see the check mark icon. Note: Not all content is available to add to your Watch List. Only content that has a plus icon can be added to a Watch List.
4. The content will then appear in your Watch List category, found in the “On Demand” section.
Push notifications
Alerts from within the app with updates on new content.
Viewers can enable or disable push notifications by going to the "Settings" app on their tablet or mobile device and toggling the switch on or off.
There is no setting for notifications within the Pluto TV app itself.
iPhone or iPad view / Android phone or tablet view
Favorites
Frequently watched channels can be saved to Favorites in the Live TV section under the Favorites section. You'll see the heart icon next to them.
You can add any Pluto TV channel to Favorites with these steps:
Tap on the video screen. If you're in portrait view, tap the full-screen icon to switch to landscape view, which displays the Favorites heart icon
Tap on the heart
The channel has been favorited when the heart is fully white in color. To remove a channel from favorites, simply tap the heart again. The heart now displays with no color.
Newsletters
Pluto TV viewers can receive newsletters, which are emails that go out to registered users. Our newsletters feature newly added content or highlight what will be featured.
There are 2 ways to unsubscribe:
1. At the top right portion of the email, click on the 3-dot menu. Select "unsubscribe" from the dropdown menu.
-OR-
2. At the bottom of the email thread, click the “Unsubscribe” link.
Social Share button
The social share button can be found in the video player. This feature lets the user share the video to social media or copy the URL link to share.
Viewers can share from their mobile device, tablet, or the Pluto TV website by tapping on the arrow icon in the video player.
When sharing from a mobile device or tablet, viewers can copy the link or share it to multiple apps.
From the website, viewers can only copy the link or share to Twitter.
Closed captions
Users can enable closed captions that display subtitles with customizable settings.
Closed Captions are available on all devices, although the location and options to customize may not be available on all platforms.
To turn on closed captions, tap on the screen and tap the CC icon.
For more information on how to turn on Closed Captions, check out Instructions for Closed Captioning.
Search
Viewers can use the search feature to easily find shows and movies. It's super easy to search for a show or movie. On the Pluto TV app and via mobile web, the search feature is located in the bottom panel menu bar. On the web, you'll find it at the top of the page.
The search feature is not available on every device.
Type in the name of the content you want to search for, and search results populate in a list below.
That's it!
