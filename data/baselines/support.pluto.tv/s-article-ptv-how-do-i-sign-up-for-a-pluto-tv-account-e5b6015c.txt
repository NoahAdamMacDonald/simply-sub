How do I sign up for a Pluto TV account?
Signing up for a free account is fast and easy! You can sign up in one of the following ways:
Sign up via the link
Signing up for Pluto TV is quick and straightforward with a direct link. Click the link, follow the steps, and enjoy Pluto TV right away.
Go directly to pluto.tv/account/check-email in your browser to access the Sign Up page.
Enter your email address, and click Next.
Enter your password, name, date of birth, gender, and zip code, and click Sign Up.
You will know you are signed in when you see your name next to the Profile icon in the top right corner.
Sign up on Pluto.tv on the web
Sign up directly on Pluto.tv and start streaming your favorite content instantly from your browser.
On the Pluto.tv site, select Sign In in the top right corner of the screen.
Enter your email address, and click Next.
Enter your password, name, date of birth, gender, and zip code, and click Agree & Continue.
You will know you are signed in when you see your name next to the Profile icon in the top right corner.
Signing up on the Android and iOS app
Getting started on the Pluto TV app has never been easier. Simply download the app from the App Store or Google Play Store, sign up, and enjoy instant access to a world of entertainment wherever you are!
After downloading the app, tap on the Profile Icon at the top right of the menu.
Enter your email address and tap Next.
You will then be directed to the Sign Up page.
Enter your password, name, gender, birthday, and zip code, and tap Sign Up.
Once your registration is complete, you will automatically be signed into your account.
Signing up via QR code on a connected device
Getting started with Pluto TV is as easy as scanning a QR code. Whether you’re joining us for the first time or signing back in—follow these steps to start watching your favorite shows and movies in no time.
On your connected TV (CTV), go to the Sign In screen.
Scan the QR code displayed on your TV using your mobile device, or visit pluto.tv/activate.
Enter the activation code shown on your TV.
Fill in your details: email address, password, name, gender, date of birth, and ZIP code, then tap Sign Up.
Enter the code from your TV screen again and tap Continue.
Welcome! Your account is now connected. Stream thousands of hit movies & TV shows, save your favorites across all your devices, and resume watching where you left off on Pluto TV.
If you are having trouble signing up using the QR code or activation code, visit our Help Center homepage and click on the yellow chat bubble at the bottom of the screen for support.
