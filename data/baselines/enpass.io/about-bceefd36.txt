Every Password Manager Asked Us to Give Up Control. So We Built One That Didn't
Enpass was built with a mission: to provide complete control of the most sensitive data, without the cost or complexity of self-hosting.
Download
See Pricing
It all started with a problem
When Hemant (CEO) and Vinod (CTO) were working as consultants and evaluating password managers, they found something fundamentally broken: every option asked them to hand over their data sovereignty in exchange for convenience.
Hemant and Vinod wanted full control of their password data without taking on the cost and complexity of self-hosting their own infrastructure. So they built a password manager that delivered this. When they shared it with colleagues, the response was immediate: people recognized the same problem and the same relief at finally solving it.
That's how Enpass was born.
Enpass has since grown into a multinational tech company headquartered in Wilmington, Delaware, with offices across the US, India, UK, Ireland, Belgium, Indonesia, Japan, and Singapore. We’re trusted by Fortune 500 companies and government organizations, with a core strength in regulated industries like financial services, healthcare, and manufacturing.
Our Values
Control
Enpass is the only password manager where you maintain 100% control of your data, 100% of the time, in your own Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, or other trusted environment. It's true data sovereignty without the hassle of self-hosting.
Compliance
Most password managers treat compliance as a checklist to complete after the fact. Enpass treats it as an architectural default – because when your data never leaves your own infrastructure, there's nothing to retrofit, no policy exception to chase, and no new risk to explain.
Confidence
Hackers can’t breach what was never there. You can be completely confident in your password security because Enpass never touches your data.
Convenience
The best password manager is the one nobody notices – until it saves them time. Enpass deploys fast, works flawlessly across every device and browser, and gives you advanced centralized admin capabilities that make management easy.
