Why Enpass is different
The confidence you get with Enpass isn’t just a feeling, it comes from how it’s actually built.
Most password managers keep everyone's vaults their servers. That's convenient for them, but it also makes them a single, valuable target for hackers: breach the vendor once, and every customer's passwords are exposed at the same time. Enpass doesn't create that target. Your vault lives inside your own personal cloud account, so there's nothing centralized for an attacker to go after.
Your data, safe in your cloud
You choose where your vault lives: like iCloud, Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, or Box.
No Enpass server involved
We never receive, store, or see your passwords, not even in encrypted form. This is a key difference from other solutions.
Nothing to breach at scale
There's no central Enpass vault holding everyone's data, so there's nothing valuable enough for a hacker to target.

Frequently asked questions
Does Enpass work across all my devices?
Yes. Enpass is available on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android, plus every major browser, so your vault is available wherever you're signed in.
Is my data really private if I store it in my own Google Drive or iCloud?
Yes. Your vault is encrypted, so even though the encrypted file sits inside your Google Drive or iCloud account, only you, with your master password, can unlock it. Enpass never has access to that account or your data. This is safer than other password managers that store all user vaults on their servers because that creates a central target for hackers. Because your encrypted Enpass vault is stored in your own cloud account, it’s protected by that platform’s robust security, too – and you’re immune from the mass breaches you read about in the headlines.
What happens if I forget my master password?
Your master password is the only key to your vault, and Enpass never has a copy of it. That's why it's important to keep it somewhere safe: if you lose it, your encrypted data can't be recovered, precisely because Enpass never had access to it in the first place.
Can I try Enpass before I pay?
Yes. A Free account lets you use Enpass with up to 25 credentials in one vault. You can create a Free account and create/import passwords without having to provide credit card details. To manage more than 25 passwords, you need a Premium account, which gives you unlimited credentials and vaults. You can upgrade easily from a Free account, with big discounts available for three-year and lifetime licences.
