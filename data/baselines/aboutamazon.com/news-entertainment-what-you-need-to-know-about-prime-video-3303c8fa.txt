Everything you need to know about Prime Video
Prime Video includes a vast collection of movies, series, and live sports for customers in the U.S., UK, India, and beyond.
Share
Written by Christian de Looper and
Giorgio Bounous
5 min read
Key takeaways:
Prime Video is included with an Amazon Prime membership for $14.99/month or $139/year in the U.S.
Prime Video is available in more than 240 other countries, such as for £8.99/month or £95/year in the UK, and starting at ₹299/month in India.
You can stream movies, series, and live sports, including Thursday Night Football and Fallout, as well as add premium subscriptions like MGM+, Apple TV, HBO Max, and more, with no extra apps required.
Prime Video offers more content than you might realize—and more places to enjoy it than ever before.
Available in over 240 countries and territories, it's a first-stop entertainment destination bringing customers a vast collection of premium programming in one app, accessible across thousands of devices worldwide.
From ‘Reacher’ Season 5 to the ‘Fourth Wing’ series, here's what's just been announced by Prime Video
Prime Video also announced the premiere date for ‘The Rings of Power’ Season 3, a renewal for ‘Jury Duty,’ and a new series starring Brett Goldstein.
Whether you're in the United States, United Kingdom, or India, Prime Video delivers blockbuster movies, hit shows, award-winning Originals, premium subscriptions, sports, and live events—all in one place.
Here's everything you need to know about Prime Video, from wherever you're watching.
What is Prime Video?
Prime Video is Amazon's streaming service included with an Amazon Prime membership.
Customers can customize their viewing experience and find their favorite movies, series, and live events. This includes Amazon MGM Studios-produced series and movies, licensed fan favorites, exclusive live sports coverage, and acclaimed sports documentaries.
All customers, regardless of whether they have a Prime membership, can access programming via Prime Video subscriptions such as MGM+, HBO Max, Apple TV, Peacock Premium Plus, FOX One, and Crunchyroll in the U.S., as well as more than 1,000 free ad-supported (FAST) Channels in the U.S., UK, Germany, Austria, France, Italy, and Spain. They can also rent or buy titles, and enjoy even more content for free with ads.
Customers can also go behind the scenes of their favorite movies and series with exclusive X-Ray access, and watch and shop their favorite titles with the fan-fueled shopping experience Shop the Show in the U.S.
Everything to know about the ‘Fourth Wing’ series coming to Prime Video
Esther McGregor and Emilio Sakraya will star in Amazon MGM Studios’ series adaptation of the fantasy novels by Rebecca Yarros.
How much is Prime Video in the U.S.?
Prime Video is one of many Amazon Prime membership benefits that provide savings, convenience, and entertainment. A Prime membership costs $14.99 a month or $139 a year in the U.S.
A Prime membership in the U.S. also includes benefits like free two-day delivery on eligible items. Amazon offers discounted memberships as well: Prime Access for recipients of select government assistance programs, and Prime for Young Adults for 18-to-24-year-olds and higher education students.
Prime Video shows and movies include limited advertisements, or you can sign up for the ad-free option, Prime Video Ultra in the U.S., priced at $4.99 a month. Learn more about Prime Video Ultra.
What can I watch on Prime Video?
Once you're signed up for Prime Video, you will have access to a massive library of content that includes thousands of movies, series, and live sports.
Amazon MGM Studios-produced content includes Fallout, Heads of State, Reacher, Red One, Road House, The Accountant 2, Beast Games, The Boys, The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power, and The Summer I Turned Pretty.
Prime members get exclusive access to live sports coverage which in the U.S. includes Thursday Night Football, NBA, WNBA, NASCAR, NWSL, and the Masters golf tournament. Acclaimed sports documentaries like Bye Bye Barry, Kelce, and Earnhardt are also available on Prime Video.
Customers can also buy or rent licensed content, like Wicked: For Good and Sinners, stream programming from other services via Prime Video Subscriptions, access more than 900 free ad-supported (FAST) Channels in the U.S., and enjoy even more content for free with ads.
Can I watch Prime Video in the UK?
Yes, Prime Video is available to Amazon Prime members in the UK, where membership costs £8.99 a month or £95 a year. UK customers can stream thousands of movies and series, including Amazon MGM Studios titles like Fallout, Reacher, and The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power. Exclusive live sports coverage, such as selected games from the UEFA Champions League, is also included in your membership.
Customers can also add Prime Video Subscriptions such as MGM+, discovery+, Paramount+, and more—all without needing extra apps or a cable subscription.
DAZN is now available on Prime Video in the US and UK: Here’s everything to know
Prime Video customers can now watch even more sports through DAZN, including boxing, MMA, and international soccer.
Prime members in the UK also benefit from free next-day delivery on eligible items, access to Prime Music, Prime Reading, and more.
Can I watch Prime Video in India?
In India, Prime Video is included with an Amazon Prime membership, which starts at ₹299 for a monthly plan or ₹1,499 a year. Indian customers have access to a rich library of content that includes global Prime Originals as well as a growing slate of Indian-language originals produced specifically for the region.
Prime Video in India features acclaimed Indian originals like Panchayat, Mirzapur, The Family Man, and Made in Heaven, alongside international hits and live sports.
With Prime Video available in multiple Indian languages—including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, and more—customers across the country can enjoy content in their preferred language.
What are Prime Video Subscriptions?
You can unlock additional content with Prime Video Subscriptions that include premium content from third-party streaming services.
These include services like MGM+, HBO Max, Apple TV, Peacock Premium Plus, FOX One, and Paramount+, which you can add to your account with no extra apps to download and no cable required. You only pay for the ones you want and can cancel at any time. Once you add these channels, you will be able to access and manage them directly from your Prime Video account.
How do I sign up for Prime Video?
Prime Video is available on thousands of compatible devices. Stream from the web or using the Prime Video app on your smartphone, tablet, set-top box, game console, or select smart TVs.
Here’s how to sign up and access Prime Video in the U.S.:
Visit Amazon Prime or download the Prime Video app
Click "Start your free trial" or "Join Prime"
Create an Amazon account or sign in
Enter payment information
Start streaming immediately
If you are based outside of the U.S., check out how to sign up to Prime Video via your local Amazon website.
Can I try Prime Video for free?
Yes. In the U.S. and UK, Amazon offers a free trial for eligible non-Prime members which includes access to the service for a limited time before you start paying. Membership offers vary by country, so visit your local Amazon website for pricing details and offers in your region.
