Cookie Policy
Last Updated: March 12, 2026
This Cookie Policy explains how Suno, Inc. (“we,” “us,” or “our”) uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you use our AI music generation platform, website, mobile applications, and related services (collectively, the “Services”). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in our Privacy Policy.
1. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?
We use cookies and similar technologies to collect User Activity Information — including your browsing activities and preferences — to improve and customize your experience and interactions with us. Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device that help us recognize you and remember your preferences. We also use:
Pixels / Web Beacons — Tiny images embedded in web pages or emails that communicate with our servers (and third-party servers) when loaded, allowing us to measure engagement and deliver targeted advertising. We also use these technologies in our advertisements, email newsletters, and other communications to understand whether you opened a communication or clicked on any of the content contained within it.
Software Development Kits (SDKs) — Code packages embedded in our mobile applications that enable functionality, analytics, and advertising.
Local Storage — Browser-based storage that improves performance and remembers preferences.
Server-Side APIs — Direct server-to-server data integrations with advertising and analytics platforms.
We refer to all of these collectively as “Cookies” in this policy.
2. Types of Cookies
First-Party Cookies are set by Suno directly to operate and improve the Services.
Third-Party Cookies are set by our partners and service providers, and other advertising, analytics, and social media partners listed in our Cookie List. We do not control how these third parties use the data they collect. Analytical, targeting, and advertising Cookies, and the User Activity Information generated from these Cookies, may be created, stored, and/or managed by these third-party service providers.
3. Categories of Cookies We Use
We or third parties use Cookies on the Services for the following purposes:
Necessary: These Cookies are required for the operation of certain Services. They enable core functions such as authentication, security, fraud prevention, and load balancing. Necessary Cookies cannot be disabled.
Functional: These Cookies remember choices you make and are used to recognize you when you log in to the Services. This enables us to personalize the Services for you, greet you by name, and remember your preferences (for example, your choice of language, region, or audio settings for our AI music tools).
Analytics: These Cookies collect information about how you use the Services. They allow us to recognize and count the number of visitors, see how visitors move around within the Services, and understand which features are most popular — including how AI-generated music is created and shared — so we can improve performance.
Advertising & Retargeting: These Cookies collect information about your browsing activities to understand your interests for marketing purposes. We or third parties may place or recognize these on your browser when you visit certain websites for the purpose of serving you with advertisements regarding our products and services that may be of interest to you. These Cookies are also used for frequency capping purposes (e.g., to ensure we do not display the same advertisement to you repeatedly) and to help us regulate the advertisements you receive and measure their effectiveness.
These technologies do not fire until you have provided consent (where consent is required by applicable law). For a complete list of third-party technologies, see our Cookie List.
4. Your Choices and How to Manage Cookies
Cookie Preference Center. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the “Cookie Settings” link in our website footer or in your app settings. You may grant or withdraw consent by category (Functional, Analytics, Social Media, Advertising).
Global Privacy Control (GPC) & Universal Opt-Out Mechanisms. We recognize and honor GPC signals and other Universal Opt-Out Mechanisms. If your browser sends a GPC signal, we will automatically treat it as a request to opt out of targeted advertising and the sale/sharing of your personal information.
“Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information.” If you are a resident of California or another U.S. state with applicable privacy laws, you may opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising by clicking the “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link in our website footer or by sending a GPC signal.
Browser Settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete Cookies through their settings. However, if you choose to disable this function, your experience using the Services may be diminished and some features may not work as intended. If you delete, block, or otherwise restrict Cookies, or if you use a different device or internet browser, you may need to renew your preferences again.
Platform-Specific Opt-Outs:
Meta: Facebook Ad Preferences
TikTok: TikTok Privacy Settings
Google: Google Ad Settings | Google Analytics Opt-Out
X.com: X Privacy Settings
Industry tools: NAI Opt-Out | DAA Opt-Out
For additional information about how we handle children's data, see our Privacy Policy.
