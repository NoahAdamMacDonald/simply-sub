Compare Devin’s Free, Pro, Max, and Teams self-serve plans, including seat types, the Teams member limit and minimum, usage quota, and on-demand credits.
Copy pageCopy page
Devin has four self-serve plans you can sign up for directly at app.devin.ai. For authoritative pricing, see the Devin pricing page. This page explains how the plans relate to each other and how billing mechanics work.
Plan overview
Plan For Price Members
Free Individuals trying Devin Free 1
Pro Individual users $20/month 1
Max Power users who need more quota $200/month 1
Teams Teams working with Devin together $80/month minimum Up to 100
The Pro and Max plans are individual plans. They cannot be shared across multiple users. If you want multiple people to use Devin under a single subscription, you need the Teams plan.
Free
The Free plan lets you try Devin with limited usage. It includes:
Limited Devin usage
Access to Devin Review
Access to DeepWiki
Free users can upgrade to any paid plan at any time from Settings > Plans.
Pro
Pro is Devin’s entry-level individual plan. It’s designed for a single developer who uses Devin regularly. Pro includes:
A daily and weekly usage quota that covers Devin sessions, Devin CLI, and Devin Desktop
Pay-as-you-go on-demand credits for usage past your quota
Slack, Linear, and MCP integrations
Pro is a single-user plan. Pro subscribers cannot invite additional members to their organization. To work with teammates on a shared subscription, use the Teams plan.
Max
Max is for individual users who consistently exceed the Pro quota. It includes everything in Pro, plus a significantly larger weekly usage quota (with no daily cap), also shared between Devin sessions, Devin CLI, and Devin Desktop.
Like Pro, Max is a single-user plan and does not support multiple members.
Teams
The Teams plan is Devin’s self-serve plan for teams of up to 100 members. Key properties:
Up to 100 members: invite teammates until your organization reaches 100 members. Full and flex seats both count toward the limit. Larger teams should contact sales about Enterprise.
$80/month minimum: every Teams account pays at least $80/month.
Each member gets either a full seat or a flex seat, and every seat includes Devin Desktop.
On-demand credits are shared across the whole team.
SAML or OIDC single sign-on for your team account. See SSO for Teams.
Cost controls with 3 usage tiers to cap how much each member can use. See Cost controls.
Free mode: each member can run up to 2 cloud sessions at a time at no cost. See Free mode.
Full seats vs. flex seats
Every member of a Teams account holds one of two seat types:
Full seat
$40/month per seat, billed as a fixed recurring line item.Best for members who use Devin regularly. Each full seat includes:
A daily and weekly usage quota equivalent to the Pro plan
Access to Devin Desktop, Devin CLI, and Devin cloud sessions
Flex seat
Free. There is no separate limit on flex seats, but every member (full or flex) counts toward the 100-member limit.Best for occasional users. Flex seats:
Draw entirely from the team’s shared pool of on-demand credits
Include access to Devin Desktop, Devin CLI, and Devin cloud sessions
Have no fixed monthly charge per seat
Teams accounts that already had more than 100 members keep their current members, but can’t add new members until they are below 100.
Admins choose the seat type when inviting a member, and can convert a member between seat types later from Settings > Members.
The $80 Teams minimum
Every Teams subscription costs at least $80/month. You can hit this minimum in any combination of full seats ($40 each) and on-demand credits:
Full seats On-demand credits included Monthly total
0 $80 $80
1 $40 $80
2 $0 $80
3 $0 $120
N ≥ 2 $0 N × $40
When you have fewer than two full seats, the remainder of the $80 minimum is automatically charged as prepaid on-demand credits that the whole team can draw from. Once you have two or more full seats, you’ve cleared the minimum and no additional on-demand credits are included, but you can still top up on-demand credits at any time.
Give a full seat to anyone who uses Devin regularly. Full seats include their own Pro-equivalent quota at a predictable fixed cost, making them the best fit for power users. Use flex seats for occasional or trial users: they get the same products, including Devin Desktop, and draw on the team’s shared on-demand credits.
SSO
Teams admins can connect a SAML 2.0 or OIDC identity provider (Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, or any compliant IdP) from Settings > Authentication. On a Teams account, SSO signs in existing members only, so invite people before they sign in through SSO. Follow the identity provider setup steps in the SSO onboarding guide; its auto-membership, role assignment, and SCIM provisioning steps apply to Enterprise only.
Cost controls
Teams admins can cap each member’s usage with up to 3 usage tiers from Settings > Usage policies. Each tier sets a usage limit, members are assigned to a tier or fall back to the default tier, and new work is blocked once a member reaches their limit. The usage tiers section of the Usage policies guide explains how tiers work; budgets, budget-scoped limits, and the beta enablement described there apply to Enterprise only.
Free mode
On the Teams plan, every member can pick Free (powered by SWE-2) in the agent selector to run Devin cloud sessions at no cost. Each member can run up to 2 Free sessions at a time; to start a third, wait for one to finish or pick another mode. Sessions in other modes are billed as usual.
How quotas work
Each paid plan and full seat includes a usage allowance that refreshes automatically on a calendar basis:
Pro and Teams full seats have a daily and weekly allowance. The daily allowance is more than 1/7 of the weekly, so you can keep working through weekends without giving up overall capacity for the week.
Max has a weekly allowance only, with no daily cap.
When you’ve used up your allowance, on-demand credits keep you working without interruption.
If you have an eligible ChatGPT Go, Plus, or Pro subscription, you can also use your ChatGPT plan to pay for GPT model usage so it doesn’t count against your Devin allowance.
On-demand credits
On-demand credits are prepaid usage credit that fund any work past your plan’s included quota:
Roll over month-to-month. Purchased credits never expire.
Can be topped up at any time from Settings > Plans, and optionally refilled automatically via auto-reload.
Admins can set auto-reload thresholds and default session spending limits from Settings > Usage & limits.
On the Teams plan, credits are shared across all members, with no per-member balance. Any teammate can draw from the shared pool.
On the Teams plan, credits fund all usage on flex seats and any full seat usage past its included quota, and cover any portion of the $80/month minimum not already covered by full seats.
Devin Review and Automations Pricing
Automations and Devin Review are available on self-serve plans.
Teams use shared on-demand credits. On the Teams plan, Automations and Devin Review draw directly from the team’s shared on-demand credit pool and do not consume full-seat quota.
What happens when you run out of credits. If you run out of credits, Automations stop running and Devin Review switches to its diff viewer. Top up on-demand credits to start Automations again and re-enable AI-powered review.
Public PRs are free. Anyone can review a public GitHub PR at devinreview.com — or by replacing github.com with devinreview.com in any PR URL — without a Devin account, and no on-demand credits are consumed.
Review managers can keep usage predictable with auto-review rules in Settings > Review. Choose when a rule starts (PR is created or PR is ready) and its cadence (Only once or Every push), then narrow its scope with repository or author conditions. See Trigger Modes in the Devin Review docs for details.
Managing your plan
Admins can view and change the account’s plan from Settings > Plans. From there you can:
Upgrade or downgrade between Free, Pro, Max, and Teams
Add or cancel Teams full seats
Purchase on-demand credits or configure auto-reload to replenish them automatically
Past invoices are available to download from Settings > Invoices. For accepted payment methods and how to update your billing details, see Plans and Usage.
For tips on keeping consumption under control across all plans, see Usage.
