How to subscribe to STARZ through Apple/iTunes
January 10 2024 11:08am
Follow the steps below to subscribe to STARZ through Apple/iTunes:
From the Apple App Store, download the STARZ app
Open the app
Select ‘Sign up Now'
Approve the subscription terms through Apple
Once approved, STARZ will prompt you to create an account (which can be used to log in on other devices)
Start streaming
