When will I be billed and can I change my billing date?
April 22 2023 10:41am
You can see when your bill is due under My Subscription by following the steps below:
Log into the STARZ website and click on the profile icon (upper right), then select Settings:
The next billing date is located in the account settings and will say "Access Until"
Unfortunately, STARZ does not provide a way to change your billing date.
