How to download content from STARZ?
January 5 2024 5:32am • Est. Read Time: 2 MIN
You can download titles and watch them offline using the STARZ mobile app for Apple or Android.
Note: The content cannot be saved to an SD card.
Follow these steps to download a movie or episode:
In the STARZ app, tap on the film or episode you wish to download.
On the description page of a Movie, there is a Download button below the movie description:
On the description page of a Series, there will be an Episodes tab with a Download button to the right:
Please note that if the download button is not available, then the movie or episode is not currently available for download.
How to watch downloaded content?
Go to the home screen of the STARZ application
Select the three stacked lines in the bottom right corner
Select Downloads and the downloaded files will be listed, as well as a progress bar for content that is still downloading
How to view downloaded content when offline?
Simply turn the device to Airplane Mode or disconnect the internet source (including Wi-Fi), and follow the same steps as above for accessing the downloaded content.
How to delete downloaded content?
To delete downloaded content:
Open the STARZ app and log in
Tap the three stacked lines (top left ) on Android
To access the menu for iOS devices, tap the STARZ login (bottom of the screen)
Select My List. (All downloaded titles will be listed here)
Click on the pencil icon or Edit button (upper-right side of the screen)
Select the title you wish to delete. (Note that a small check mark will appear on the screen image's upper-right side for the title you selected)
Once you have selected all the titles you want to delete, click on Delete (upper right of your screen)
Why can't I find my downloaded content?
Common reasons for downloaded content not appearing in the download list
Downloaded titles are automatically deleted from the downloads list after 2 weeks
If the title goes out of its licensing window, it will automatically be removed from the downloads list
