Cancelling Your SiriusXM Service
New wheels? Let’s roll.
Did you know you can roll the remainder of your paid service into your new car? It’s a quick and easy switch when you contact Customer Care, contact us here.
Unfortunately, pre-packaged automotive subscriptions and Lifetime automotive subscriptions are non-transferable unless your radio is stolen or defective.
If you wish to cancel for any other reason, please contact us as we would like to understand and resolve any issue you may have had. Our Customer Care agents are available Monday through Saturday from 8 a.m. to 11 p.m. ET, and 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. ET on Sundays and holidays.
