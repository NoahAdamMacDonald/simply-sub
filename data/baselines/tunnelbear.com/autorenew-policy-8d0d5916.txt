Auto-renew Policy
Last revised: December 5, 2025
To help make sure your TunnelBear is always ready to protect you, your subscription will automatically renew at the end of your billing period, until you decide to cancel your subscription. Don’t worry though, if you are on an annual plan, you’ll receive a subscription renewal reminder email 30 days before your billing date.
On your billing date, we'll use the payment method attached to your account to charge you the full price of your subscription ($10.99 for monthly or $69.99 for annual). After your payment is processed, you will receive a receipt confirming the charge.
You can cancel at any time by logging into your TunnelBear account, selecting the "Overview" tab, and clicking "Cancel". Follow the onscreen steps, and you're done. If you would like to cancel your iOS or Android subscription, please read Apple's or Google's cancelation articles for help. If you have any questions, our friendly Support Bears are always available through the support form.
TunnelBear may revise this policy from time-to-time, but we will let you know by updating the "Last Revised Date" at the top of this page.
