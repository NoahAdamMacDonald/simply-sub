We all have times where we need to change our payment details for subscriptions because we've got a new card, or because we've moved banks. This article will explain how to change or update your payment details for your Deezer subscription, so you can continue enjoying quality music!

How to change or update your payment details
It is important to make sure your payment details are up to date. Here's how to change them if they're not:
iOS Android Desktop
Navigate to your profile icon in the top left hand side of your application
Select the Settings cog icon in the top right hand side of your application
Note: If you're logging in with Deezer Family, please use the Main profile to change your details
Under Subscription, select Plan management
Select Subscription details & invoices
Go to Payment method
Change or update your payment method and enter your new details
Note: Your changes will take effect on your next payment
Navigate to your profile icon at the top left hand side of your application
Click on the Settings cog icon at the top right hand side of your application
Note: If you're logging in with Deezer Family, please use the Main profile to change your details
Under My Subscription Plan, select Manage my subscription
Scroll down to Payment Method
Select Change payment method and enter your new payment details.
Note: Your changes will take effect on your next payment
Go to your profile
Note: If you're logging in with Deezer Family, please use the Main profile to change your details
Select Account Settings
Select MANAGE MY SUBSCRIPTION
Go to Payment method
Change or update your payment method and enter your new details
Note: We recommend switching to a PC or desktop device with a secure web browser. Your changes will take effect on your next payment
FAQ
Why can't I update my payment method? Occasionally, you may need to clear your browser cache. Please open your account from a computer, clear the browser cache and try updating your details. If this does not work, you can use our Help Center webform.
How do I resolve an Apple payment issue? If you subscribed to Deezer through Apple, your subscription can only be managed through Apple. Sign in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe on an Apple device. Then choose an option:
Update your payment method
Change your subscription plan
Cancel your subscription
Changes may take a few minutes to appear in Deezer. To avoid duplicate charges, do not start a new Deezer subscription while your Apple subscription is still active, even if your payment didn't go through.
How do I resolve a Google Play payment issue? If you subscribed to Deezer through Google Play, your subscription can only be managed through Google Play. Sign in with the Google Account you used to subscribe on an Android device. Then choose an option:
Update your payment method
Change your subscription plan
Cancel your subscription (or on Deezer.com)
Changes may take a few minutes to appear in Deezer. To avoid duplicate charges, do not start a new Deezer subscription while your Google Play subscription is still active, even if your payment didn't go through.
Note: Our team can still review payments.
I recently moved to a new country. How can I pay for Deezer in the local currency? You'll need to cancel your current subscription and ensure your payment period is complete. After your last bill, you can activate a new subscription on your Deezer Free account. You'll then be charged with the corresponding currency. Please keep in mind that you must use a local payment method (payment method issued in the default country) when you re-subscribe.
Note: Your music library may be different as it is region dependent
I've missed a payment/payment was refused - what will happen next? Don't worry! We will attempt to take payment a few days after your usual subscription date and we do not display outstanding payments. In the event that your payment is refused twice or more, your subscription will be cancelled. If this is the case, you will need to wait until your account reverts to Deezer Free and enter a payment method to reactive your subscription. If you wish to change your payment details after a payment has been refused:
Go to your profile icon at the top left hand side of your application
Select the Settings cog icon at the top right hand side of your application
Select Plan management
Select My subscription plan
You will be able to amend your details and see when your next payment will be taken.
I have a Deezer subscription through a partnership. Will I lose my music if I change to a direct subscription? Absolutely not! Providing you use the same email address you used to sign up with via the partnership deal, you will keep your account, all your playlists and favorites. To switch to a direct subscription, you will need to wait until the subscription with your chosen partnership has finished and your account has reverted to free, at which point you can subscribe to your chosen subscription with Deezer!
