Cancel Your Deezer Subscription
Simone
18 August 2026 00:22
Updated
You can cancel your subscription at any time through the Deezer app or website. If you cancel your Deezer Premium subscription, you'll still have access to your Premium features until the end of your current subscription period. After that, your account will revert to Deezer Free and any downloaded music will no longer be playable offline.
All of your favorite tracks, playlists and content will still be visible, but some features, like unlimited skips, downloading and offline listening, are only available to paid subscribers. All downloaded content will become grey and unplayable.

Apple payments
If you subscribe to Deezer through your Apple account, you can only cancel directly with Apple. Find out how to cancel your subscription here.
Tip: Learn more about paying for Deezer through Apple
Subscriptions made through Partners
If you are paying for Deezer as part of a partnership, for instance with partners such as TIM and Orange, you will need to cancel through the respective partner directly, as Deezer cannot cancel your subscription. For more details, please navigate to the Partnership section of our support site for specific partnership information.

FAQ
Why can't I find the option to cancel my account? You may already be logged into Deezer Free and therefore have no subscription to cancel. Alternatively, you may also have more than one subscription linked to different emails. Find out how to check if you have an account.
Why have I been charged after canceling?
You have more than one account, so the payment has been taken for another account
You have not completed the cancelation process. You will receive an email when you have successfully canceled your account
If you're subscribed to Deezer through Apple or your mobile provider, it's possible they have not finished processing your cancelation. Please contact your third party provider directly
How can I confirm my cancellation went through? You will receive a confirmation email from Deezer once your cancellation has been successfully processed.
You can also verify your cancellation status directly in the app:
iOS: Go to Settings → Subscription → Plan management → Subscription details & invoices. Your plan will show as cancelled if the cancellation was successful.
Android: Go to Settings → My Subscription Plan → Manage my subscription. If your plan shows as cancelled or has reverted to Deezer Free, the cancellation has been successful.
Desktop: Go to Account Settings → Manage my subscription. Your cancellation status will be displayed there.
If you don't see your cancellation reflected yet, it may take a few minutes to update. If you were subscribed through Apple or a third-party partner, please check your cancellation status directly with them.
