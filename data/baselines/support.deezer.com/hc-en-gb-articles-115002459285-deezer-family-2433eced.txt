Deezer Family
Simone
25 August 2026 12:20
Updated
So you want the benefits of Deezer Premium but want to share it among your family and across 13 devices? Then Deezer Family is the plan for you. With up to 6 family member accounts, with amazing HiFi sound and the ability to create Kids accounts for the younger ones, sign up for Deezer Family subscription today!

With Deezer Family, you and your Family members can listen to ad-free, unlimited music in high quality.
Here is what's included:
Up to 6 Family members can discover playlists, favorite songs and use Flow
You can connect up to 13 devices. The main Admin can link up to 3 connected devices, while each additional Family member can link up to 2 devices
HiFi sound quality for all your members. You can find out more about HiFi here
You can set up Family members as Independent accounts with their own login details and password. Or, if you prefer, members can have their own Profile (with the same login details as Admin)
You are able to sync to a wide range of speakers, home devices and game consoles
You can set up Kid profiles for children up to 12 years old
Our music acquisition is negotiated between artists & right-holders. Don't forget that if you have an active subscription you can upload the song to your own mp3 section on Deezer and then you can add it to playlists. The step by step guide is here.
Tip: Visit the Deezer Family plan to learn more
Requirements for Deezer Family
To use Deezer Family:
The Admin must meet the minimum age requirement outlined in our Terms and Conditions
All Family members must reside in the same household
Note: Deezer Family is available worldwide for Android, iOS and desktop
If you already have an account that isn't connected to a Deezer Family plan and you want to join Deezer Family, all of your library will be kept if you follow the information below:
If you are going to be the Admin, all you need to do is switch from your existing Deezer Premium plan to Deezer Family
If you are not going to be the Admin, you must join as an independent account to keep your existing library. You must receive an invitation to join a Family
Warning: It is not possible to combine subscription plans
Supported devices
A wide range of devices are compatible with Deezer Family:
Amazon Alexa
Apple Watch
Android TV
Google Home
Note: It is not possible to access Profiles or Kid profiles from some car entertainment systems, TVs and select devices
How to join Deezer Family
iOS Android Desktop
If you're subscribed to a Deezer Free or Deezer Premium plan and you'd like to switch to more music for the rest of your family, you can join Deezer Family following these steps:
Navigate to your profile icon at the top left hand side of the application
Select the Settings cog at the top right hand side of the application
From the Subscription section, select Plan Management
Select Manage my subscription
Depending on your current plan, select Subscribe or Switch to Deezer Family
Note: If you cannot view all your available plans, please access your account from a web browser on your mobile or computer
How to subscribe through iTunes
If you're currently subscribed through iTunes, all you need to do is log in to your Deezer account via your application on your device.
Then, go to the Manage my subscription and Change plan
Note: If you're paying through the Apple App Store, your total charge will be slightly more due to the Apple commission charge
If you're subscribed to a Deezer Free or Deezer Premium plan and you'd like to switch to more music for the rest of your family, you can join Deezer Family following these steps:
Navigate to your profile icon at the top left hand side of your application
Select the Settings cog icon at the top right hand side of your application
Select Manage my Account
Select Manage my subscription
Depending on your current plan, select Subscribe or Switch to Deezer Family
Note: If you cannot view all your available subscriptions, please access your account from a web browser on your mobile or computer
Go to your profile
Select Account Settings
In the My information tab, select MANAGE MY SUBSCRIPTION located under My subscription
Depending on your current subscription, select Subscribe or Switch to Deezer Family

FAQ
How do I connect an existing Deezer Premium account? If you are subscribed to a Deezer Premium plan and you want to connect to a Deezer Family plan, you'll need to cancel first. Once your subscription has reverted back to Deezer Free, you can connect your Deezer account as an independent account using the invite link.
Note: If you have a Deezer Free account the process is the same, but there is no need to cancel first
Can I pay for my Family subscription annually instead of monthly? Yes, we offer annual payment options, but this is not available in all countries. Please check the link to see the offers available in your country.
Can existing Deezer Family profiles rejoin as independent accounts? Yes, existing Deezer Family Profiles can become independent accounts with their own login credentials. You can find out how to convert profiles into independent accounts here.
How many devices can I use on Deezer Family? With Deezer Family, you can link or pair up to 13 devices across your accounts - the Admin of the account will be able to link three devices, and the remaining accounts/profiles will be able to link two. These paired devices are the devices in which you can download music to, so may be your phone or desktop. However, you can stream from as many devices as you want. If you have, for instance, Google Home, this will not count as a paired device but a streaming device, and there is no limit to the number of devices you stream from.
Why do I get error messages that someone else is listening on another device? Ensure all listeners access the correct Profile to prevent listening error messages from occurring while music is being played on another device or page.
You may also need to reset your password.
What happens if I cancel my Deezer Family subscription? If you cancel your Deezer Family plan, your account will revert to Deezer Free and all Profiles will be suspended. Independent accounts will revert back to Deezer Free, keeping all their favorites, playlists and recommendations (except downloaded content). Once they've reverted back, they have the possibility to switch to any Deezer plan to access playlists, music on demand and offline listening.
