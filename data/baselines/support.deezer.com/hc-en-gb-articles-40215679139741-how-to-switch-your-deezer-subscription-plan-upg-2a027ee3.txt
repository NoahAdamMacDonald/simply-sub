Leonie

Want to switch your Deezer plan? Learn how to change, upgrade or downgrade between Premium, Duo, Family and Free — including steps for Apple and Google users.
Whether you're looking to share music with your household, move to a plan that better suits your budget, or switch from a Deezer Duo to an individual Premium account, this article has everything you need in one place.

What plans are available?
Deezer offers a range of subscription plans to suit every listener:
Deezer Premium – Ad-free music, offline listening and unlimited skips for one individual account.
Deezer Duo – All the benefits of Premium across two separate accounts, perfect for couples or housemates, at a reduced cost.
Deezer Family – Up to 6 individual accounts (including Kids mode) for the whole household, with HiFi sound quality.
Deezer Student – All Premium benefits at a discounted price, available while you're in education.
Deezer Free – A free, ad-supported account with access to millions of tracks.
Tip: Visit our Deezer offers page to compare plans and find the one that's right for you.

If you're already on Deezer Free or Premium and would like to switch to Deezer Duo or Deezer Family, you can do this directly from the app or website no need to cancel first!

Apple subscribers
If you subscribed to Deezer through Apple (App Store / iTunes), your subscription can only be managed directly through Apple — Deezer is unable to make changes to Apple-billed subscriptions on your behalf.
To switch your plan via Apple, sign in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe, then choose an option:
Change your subscription plan
Cancel your subscription
Note: If you need to switch to a lower-tier plan (e.g. from Deezer Family to Premium), you will need to cancel your Apple subscription first. Once your account has reverted to Deezer Free, you can then re-subscribe to your new plan directly through Deezer.
⚠️ Important: To avoid being charged twice, do not start a new Deezer subscription while your Apple subscription is still active — even if a payment didn't go through. Changes may take a few minutes to appear in Deezer.
Tip: Learn more about paying for Deezer through Apple.
Google Play subscribers
If you subscribed to Deezer through Google Play, your subscription can only be managed directly through Google Play. Sign in with the Google Account you used to subscribe on an Android device, then choose an option:
Change your subscription plan
Cancel your subscription
Note: If you need to switch to a lower-tier plan, cancel your Google Play subscription first. Once your account reverts to Deezer Free, you can re-subscribe to the plan of your choice directly through Deezer.
⚠️ Important: To avoid duplicate charges, do not start a new Deezer subscription while your Google Play subscription is still active. Changes may take a few minutes to appear in Deezer.
Partner subscribers
If you are paying for Deezer as part of a partnership (for example, through a mobile provider such as Orange or TIM, or another official Deezer partner), you will need to manage and cancel your subscription directly with your partner. Deezer is unable to cancel partner subscriptions on your behalf.
Once your partner subscription has ended and your account has reverted to Deezer Free, you can then subscribe directly to your chosen Deezer plan via our app or website.
Note: As long as you use the same email address associated with your existing account, all your playlists, favourite tracks, and library content will remain intact when you switch to a direct Deezer subscription.
Tip: For more details about specific partnerships, visit the Deezer Partnerships section of our support site.
FAQ
Will I lose my music if I switch plans? No! All your playlists, favourite tracks and library content will remain on your account when you switch plans. However, please note that any downloaded tracks for offline listening will no longer be available once your account reverts to Deezer Free. Once you re-subscribe to a paid plan, you can re-download your content.
Can I switch plans at any time? Yes. You can switch to a higher-tier plan at any time directly through the app or website. To switch to a lower-tier plan, you will need to cancel first and wait for your account to revert to Deezer Free before re-subscribing.
I pay through Apple, can Deezer switch my plan for me? Unfortunately not. Apple-billed subscriptions can only be managed through Apple directly. Please visit Apple's support page for steps to change your plan, or cancel your subscription if needed.
How long do I have to wait before I can re-subscribe after cancelling? You don't need to wait! As soon as your account reverts to Deezer Free (at the end of your current billing period), you can immediately subscribe to your new chosen plan.
I'm on a partner plan will I lose my data when I switch to a direct Deezer plan? Absolutely not! As long as you use the same email address you originally signed up with, all your playlists and favourites will be waiting for you. Simply cancel your partner subscription, wait for your account to revert to Deezer Free, and then subscribe directly with Deezer.
What happens to my billing when I switch to a higher-tier plan mid-month? When you switch to a higher-tier plan, you will be charged immediately for the new plan. Any credit consideration for the remaining time on your previous plan will depend on your subscription type, payment method, and applicable terms.
Why can I only see some plans in the app? If you can't see all available plans in the app, try accessing your account from a web browser on your phone or computer at deezer.com. All plans should be visible there.
