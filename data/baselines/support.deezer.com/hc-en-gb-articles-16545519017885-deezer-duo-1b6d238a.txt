Deezer Duo
Rebecca
14 March 2026 15:20
Updated
Deezer Duo is our subscription service that allows two people to have our premium features under one ownership - but better yet, at a reduced cost of 33% the value of two premium accounts. Want to share with your flatmate? Your partner? All of this is possible with Deezer Duo!

Duo requirements
You will have all the benefits of Deezer Premium - you can listen to all your favorite tunes offline and create playlists. You can even identify tracks or sing along to your favorite songs with built-in features like SongCatcher and Lyrics! Not only that, but experience Flow, the infinite self-learning feature that plays and recommends songs based on your listening preferences. Explore a rich catalog of millions songs, ready for you - together is better!
What is available on Duo?
You can connect to up to 5 devices across two profiles from a range of speakers, home devices and game consoles
You can set up a Duo member as an independent account with their own login details and password or as a sub profile associated to the main account
You will both be able to enjoy HiFi sound streaming quality!
Note: Your Duo subscription can include a Kids profile, as long as the Admin of the subscription meets the minimum age requirement outlined in our Terms and Conditions. For more information about setting up a Kids profile, click here
Note: You must be logged in to the admin account to add someone to your Duo account
Our music acquisition is negotiated between artists & right-holders. Don't forget that if you have an active subscription you can upload the song to your own mp3 section on Deezer and then you can add it to playlists.The step by step guide is here.

Duo requirements
As with our Family subscription, Deezer Duo requires both accounts to reside in the same household to redeem the offer.
FAQ
How much does Deezer Duo cost per month? Go to Deezer Duo offer page for more information on pricing.
How do I pay for my Deezer Duo subscription? One profile will be responsible for paying the Duo subscription.
We offer a wide range of globally supported payment methods to make life easier, including: PayPal, Mastercard, Visa, American Express and iOS in-app purchases (iTunes).
Locally, we offer 20+ payment methods in the 180+ countries we cover.
How many devices can I use on Deezer Duo? With Deezer Duo, you can link or pair up to five devices across two accounts. These paired devices are the devices in which you can download music to, so may be your phone or desktop. However, you can stream from as many devices as you want. If you have, for instance, Google Home, this will not count as a paired device but a streaming device, and there is no limit to the number of devices you stream from.
Will the music stop if I cancel my Deezer Duo subscription? No. With Deezer, the music never stops. If you cancel your subscription, you'll still keep all your playlists and favorite tracks stored in Library, but your account will revert to Deezer Free. Any music you've downloaded to your device won't be playable offline.
