Change your discovery+ plan
The steps for changing your discovery+ subscription plan vary depending on who bills you. If you're not sure who bills you for discovery+, search your email accounts for a discovery+ subscription email (try searching for discovery+).
Step 1: Find who bills you
If you're not sure who bills you for discovery+, search your email accounts for a discovery+ subscription email (try searching for discovery+).
You can also find who bills you on your Subscription page. Here's how:
Sign in at discoveryplus.com.
Choose your profile (or the Settings icon on TVs) and then Subscription.
Find who your subscription is billed through (top of screen).
Step 2: Change your plan
If you started your subscription at discoveryplus.com, here's how to change your plan:
discovery+
Go to discoveryplus.com/subscription and sign in (if needed).
Choose Change Your Plan.
Choose a subscription plan.
Follow the on-screen prompts to change your plan.
If you change to a lower-priced plan, your plan change will happen on your next billing date.
If you change to a higher-priced plan, your plan change happens immediately. You'll be charged the price difference between plans (plus applicable taxes) and your next billing date will be adjusted.
Amazon Appstore
In discovery+, do one of the following:
Fire Tablet: Choose your profile.
Fire TV: Choose the Settings icon.
Choose Subscription and then Change Your Plan.
Choose a subscription plan.
Follow the on-screen prompts to change your plan.
If you change to a lower-priced plan, your plan change will happen on your next billing date.
If you change to a higher-priced plan, your plan change happens immediately. Amazon will refund you for the remaining days on your subscription plan and charge you for your new plan. Your next billing date will be adjusted.
Amazon Prime Video Channels
To change your plan, cancel your subscription, wait for it to expire, then resubscribe to discovery+ and choose a different plan.
Apple
On your iPhone, iPad, or Apple TV:
In discovery+, choose your profile (or the Settings icon on your TV).
Choose Subscription and then Change Your Plan.
Choose a subscription plan.
Follow the on-screen prompts to change your plan.
If you change to a lower-priced plan, your plan change will happen on your next billing date.
If you change to a higher-priced plan, your plan change happens immediately. Apple will refund you for the remaining days on your subscription and charge you for your new plan. Your next billing date will be adjusted.
Google Play
On your Android phone or TV:
In discovery+, choose your profile (or the Settings icon on your TV).
Choose Subscription and then Change Your Plan.
Choose a subscription plan.
Follow the on-screen prompts to change your plan.
If you change to a lower-priced plan, your plan change will happen on your next billing date.
If you change to a higher-priced plan, your plan change happens immediately. Google will give you a credit for the remaining days on your subscription and apply it to your new plan. Your next billing date will be adjusted once the credit is used.
Roku
On your Roku:
In discovery+, choose the Settings icon.
Choose Subscription and then Change Your Plan.
Choose a subscription plan.
Follow the on-screen prompts to change your plan.
If you change to a lower-priced plan, your plan change will happen on your next billing date.
If you change to a higher-priced plan, your plan change happens immediately. You&#039;ll be charged the price difference between plans (plus applicable taxes) and your next billing date will be adjusted.
Samsung
On your Samsung TV:
In discovery+, choose the Settings icon.
Choose Subscription and then Change Your Plan.
Choose a subscription plan.
Follow the on-screen prompts to change your plan.
If you change to a lower-priced plan, your plan change will happen on your next billing date.
If you change to a higher-priced plan, your plan change happens immediately. Samsung will give you a credit for the remaining days on your subscription and apply it to your new plan. Your next billing date will be adjusted once the credit is used.
Xfinity
To change your plan, cancel your subscription, wait for it to expire, then resubscribe to discovery+ and choose a different plan.
Other provider (e.g. DISH, Sling, Vizio)
DISH: To manage your discovery+ On Demand subscription, sign in at my.dish.com/login. For help, go to my.dish.com/support/contact.
Sling: To manage your discovery+ add-on channel, sign in at sling.com/my-account. For help, go to sling.com/help.
Vizio: To manage your discovery+ subscription, sign in at vizio.com. For help, go to support.vizio.com.
