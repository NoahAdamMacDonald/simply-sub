Frequently asked questions
How much does each Ideogram plan cost on monthly and yearly billing? On monthly billing, Plus costs $20 per month. On yearly billing, Plus costs $15 per month, $180 billed annually. Plus includes 2,400 priority credits/mo.
On monthly billing, Pro costs $60 per month. On yearly billing, Pro costs $42 per month, $504 billed annually. Pro includes 7,500 priority credits/mo.
On monthly billing, Team costs $30 per user per month. On yearly billing, Team costs $20 per user per month, $240 per user billed annually. Team includes 3,600 priority credits/user/mo.
Pay as you go costs 1¢ per credit.
Enterprise has custom pricing, and you can contact sales for a quote.
What are priority credits? Priority credits let you generate images instantly. They can only be obtained through paid subscription plans and top-up credit purchases.
What happens when I run out of priority credits? Once you've used all your priority credits, you'll generate images in the slow queue, where wait times vary based on server capacity and usage. Note that AI-powered editing features, such as Editing with AI and Image Reference, require priority credits and are not available in the slow queue. Upgrade to a higher-tier plan with more priority credits
Purchase top-up credits without changing your plan
Wait for your priority credits to reset in the next billing cycle
Do my unused priority credits carry over to the next monthly period? Unused subscription priority credits expire at the end of each billing cycle, so if you don't use them you will lose them. However, top-up credits are only used after your subscription credits run out, and will carry over to the next period if unused.
Can I generate private, unpublished images? On the free plan, images you generate are public. On Plus, Pro, Team and Enterprise, new images stay private until you publish them, and you can unpublish images you published before.
Can I delete my images? Yes, image deletion is available for Ideogram Plus and Ideogram Pro subscribers.
Can I use generated images for commercial purposes? We do not claim ownership of your generated images, and you're free to use them for any purpose, including commercial use. For details, see our Terms of Service. Running Ideogram model weights on your own infrastructure is licensed separately, and the licensing page explains the non-commercial and commercial options.
How can I update my credit card and billing information? Click 'Manage Billing' in the top-right corner to update your payment method, download receipts, cancel your plan, or view your subscription renewal date.
Can I cancel my plan? Yes, you can cancel your monthly or yearly subscription at any time. You'll be able to use all the benefits and features of your plan until the end of your billing period and won't be charged again after that.
How do I upgrade my plan? Will I be double charged? Select your desired plan from the list above, ensuring you check the billing frequency. Upgrades are automatically prorated - you'll only pay the difference based on the unused portion of your current plan.
What payment methods do you support? We process all payments through Stripe, which accepts credit or debit cards issued by networks like Mastercard, VISA, or American Express. PayPal and other digital wallets are not available at this time.
Does the price include tax? No, prices exclude tax. If applicable, tax is added at checkout based on your billing address. Tax-exempt organizations can select "I'm purchasing as a business" and enter their details. We don't charge sales tax in some regions but may adjust this based on tax obligations. Any changes will apply to future invoices.
Can I use Ideogram for free? Yes, eligible free accounts receive weekly credits when they sign in with Google, Apple or Microsoft, and the amount may vary. When you need more, you can buy pay-as-you-go credits or choose a plan.
Do you offer discounts or free trials? Yearly billing costs less per month than monthly billing, as the plan cards show. We don't offer free trials for subscription plans.
