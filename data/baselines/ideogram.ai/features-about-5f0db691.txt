About Ideogram
Ideogram is a generative media platform for AI image generation and editing, creative apps, and API workflows. The company's mission is to help people become more creative.

Company facts
Founded
Ideogram was founded in 2023.
Founders
Researchers from Google Brain and the University of Toronto founded Ideogram.
Mohammad Norouzi is a co-founder and the CEO.

Model release history
Ideogram model releases with a published date, newest first Model Release date Summary
Ideogram Vector October 22, 2026 Ideogram Vector creates scalable, editable SVG artwork from a prompt or an existing image.
Ideogram 4.0 June 3, 2026 Ideogram 4.0 is Ideogram's first open-weight model. It offers dense text rendering across languages, object and text placement through bounding box control, and 2K output.
Ideogram 3.0 March 26, 2025 Ideogram 3.0 generates photorealistic images and legible text in a single generation. It is the only Ideogram model with style presets, style reference images, negative prompts and color palettes.
Ideogram 0.1 August 29, 2023 Ideogram 0.1 opened to everyone for free on this date.
Ideogram 4.5 is the latest Ideogram image model. It supports
text in images and precise editing that preserves details across repeated edits.
Ideogram 2.0 and 2a are older models that may still appear in the model picker.
Ideogram recommends Ideogram 4.5 or 4.0 for new work.
