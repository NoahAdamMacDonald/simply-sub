We value open source
At Mullvad, we believe in the open-source model in which a program's source code is made
available, or open, to anyone for viewing and using. In a broader perspective, we believe that
this sharing of knowledge will advance the world more quickly and help it to become a better
place.
The majority of software that we use and even develop ourselves is open source.
