Investor FAQs
What is the symbol and the exchange that the company trades under?
You’ll find our stock under the symbol XYZ on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE), and our CHESS Depositary Interests (CDIs) under the symbol XYZ on the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX) via a Foreign Exempt Listing. A Block, Inc. CDI has rights that are economically equivalent to the rights attaching to a share of Block, Inc. Class A common stock. CDIs are quoted and traded on the ASX in Australian dollars.
When did Block, Inc. go public, at what price?
Block, Inc. became a publicly traded company on November 18, 2015 and began trading on the NYSE on November 19, 2015. Block, Inc. sold shares to the public at a price of $9 per share in its initial public offering.
