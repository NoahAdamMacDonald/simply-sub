FAQ Technical details
What is online scam protection, and how does it defend against online fraud and cyber crime?
Online scam protection is a security solution designed to shield your personal and financial information from unauthorised access and online fraud. It uses advanced detection techniques to identify online scams and other suspicious activity, such as phishing emails, fraudulent text messages, and fake websites, that may trick you into revealing personal information or transferring money. By analysing emails, text messages, and websites for signs of phishing, online scam protection can alert you to online fraud.
What types of online scams and phishing can Scam Protection detect and block?
Scam Protection is designed to detect a wide range of online scams, from phishing emails and text messages used to trick people into sending money to sophisticated phishing attacks aimed at capturing personal or financial information. By helping you identify these scams, online scam protection makes you a harder target for cyber criminals and other bad actors. Filtering out scams ensures you only interact with reputable companies and buy online from legitimate businesses.
How can I recognise a phishing attack or scam before clicking on a suspicious link?
Recognising a phishing attack and online fraud often comes down to vigilance. Look for warning signs such as unexpected requests for personal or financial information, urgent messages that trick you into transferring money, or messages with poor grammar, sketchy links, and unfamiliar sender details. A legitimate company will typically not ask for your account details, phone number or other personal information, so any request for these details should be treated with caution.
Does Scam Protection work on mobile devices?
Yes. Modern online scam protection is built to function seamlessly on mobile devices, where users often encounter phishing scams and fraudulent text messages. By scanning SMS content and identifying patterns that indicate a phishing attack, Scam Protection helps stop cyber criminals who trick people into revealing sensitive information or sending money.
What’s the difference between online scam protection and antivirus software?
While both solutions play important roles in your digital security, online scam protection focuses on detecting and preventing fraud attempts, such as phishing emails, suspicious text messages, and deceptive banking sites. Antivirus software, on the other hand, primarily protects your system from malware and viruses. By combining both, you get comprehensive security that fends off scammers and malware. With F‑Secure Internet Security and F‑Secure Total, you get both our award-winning antivirus software and all the great online scam protection features.

Can I still get scammed while using Scam Protection?
Yes. While Scam Protection actively protects you from many common scams, no solution can guarantee 100% safety. Some scams may still bypass protection, such as when you willingly transfer money to a scam account (e.g., investment scams), provide your personal information via text, or get deceived by a scam call. Scam Protection acts as a strong line of defense and makes you a harder target for scammers. Still, staying alert and responsible online behavior remain essential.
How does Scam Protection identify and block phishing attempts?
Scam Protection uses multiple methods to identify and block scam attempts, including:
AI-based detection: Advanced AI technology analyses text messages in real-time to detect and block SMS scams.
Reputation-based analysis: It references extensive and ever-growing databases of known scam websites, advertisers, cookie consent providers and malicious links. Based on its analysis, Scam Protection distinguishes safe websites and links from malicious ones and actively blocks scams before they can harm you.
This layered approach ensures strong protection against many common phishing threats and other online scams.
