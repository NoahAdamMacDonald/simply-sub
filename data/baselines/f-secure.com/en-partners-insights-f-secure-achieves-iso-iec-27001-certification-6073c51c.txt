F-Secure achieves ISO/IEC 27001 certification
F-Secure
Nov 13, 2024 3 min read
We are honored to announce that F-Secure has received the prestigious ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification, marking a milestone in our commitment to information security. ISO/IEC 27001 provides a comprehensive framework to manage and protect information through a structured Information Security Management System (ISMS).
Recognized worldwide as the de facto standard in information security management, the certification is based on the standard set by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) and the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC).
From dedication to certification
While this certification marks a significant achievement, the values it represents are not new to us. F-Secure has been committed to the best practices and principles required by ISO/IEC 27001 since before the demerger with WithSecure in 2022, implementing robust security policies, risk management measures, and data protection protocols to ensure our customers’ information is handled with the utmost care. The recent certification audit conducted by KPMG has now formally recognized the effectiveness of our efforts.
What this means for our customers and partners
For our valued customers and partners, our ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification confirms that their information is handled in secure manner in accordance with industry best practices. By adhering to ISO 27001 standards, we are not only protecting our own information but also prioritizing the security and confidentiality of all those we work with. We understand that trust is the foundation of all digital moments, and this achievement serves to strengthen that foundation.
“Achieving the ISO 27001 certification is a great milestone for F-Secure, as it is truly a cross-company effort, and it highlights to our customers and partners that we are committed to maintaining a high standard of security.” says Johannes Kossila, Chief Information Security Officer at F-Secure. “The standard requires continual improvement and maintenance monitored via yearly follow-up audits, that ensure our processes and ways of working will keep on improving in future as well. With the certification, we are even better equipped to protect our customers digital moments.”
Information security is a continuous journey
While the ISO 27001 certification is a powerful step, we are committed to the constant improvement that this standard encourages. We will continue to enhance our information security measures, embracing advancements in technology, addressing emerging security risks, and adapting to new challenges.
As we celebrate this accomplishment, we are more inspired than ever to maintain the trust placed in us. Thank you for being a part of F-Secure’s journey in making every digital moment secure, for everyone.
