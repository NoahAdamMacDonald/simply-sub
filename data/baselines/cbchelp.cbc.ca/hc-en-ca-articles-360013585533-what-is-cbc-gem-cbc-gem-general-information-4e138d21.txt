CBC Gem is CBC's digital video streaming service.
Discover the free way to stream the best shows, movies, news, documentaries, and more.
Plus access live TV streams from up to 14 regions across Canada.
Or try Premium and enjoy ad-free video-on-demand content (excludes live streams), early access to Gem exclusive programming, and access to CBC News Network.
CBC Gem is available for free as an App for iOS, tvOS, Fire TV, Android TV, Android phones and tablets, LG and Samsung Smart TVs, Roku, Xbox One/S/X, and Rogers Xfinity.
Gem is also accessible online at gem.cbc.ca.
If you wish to stream our content to your television, this can be achieved via Apple AirPlay or Google Chromecast.
Sign up for a free CBC Gem account
Signing up for a Gem account is quick, simple and free.
Within moments you will have access to content and features above and beyond what you've experienced without one.
With a free CBC Gem account you can enjoy features such as Favourites.
Build a list of your favourite on-demand content (Coronation Street, Heartland, The National...) for easy access!
An account will also enable you to continue any on-demand content from where you left off on any device supported by Gem, including our new app for Rogers Xfinity.
With a free Gem account, you can:
Watch current and past seasons of favourite shows on demand (ad-supported)
Add video-on-demand content to a Favourites list for easy access
Resume watching programs on any supported device
Live stream CBC TV, including access to all 14 local channels from across the country and their news broadcasts.
Live News and Sports streams
Live event specials such as The JUNOS and The Legacy Awards
Ad-free kids programming
Additional benefits of your free account:
Opt in or out of subscriptions to CBC newsletters on a wide range of subjects
Submit comments on CBC News stories and receive notifications when someone likes or replies to your comment
What if you choose not to create a free Gem account?
If you don't wish to sign in or create a free Gem account, you will still have access to the following content on Gem:
Ad-free kids programming
Trailers for video on demand content or films
Select live news and sporting events
What are the benefits of choosing to sign up for Gem Premium?
For $5.99+tax a month (with a 7 day free trial for new subscriptions), Canadians have the option of upgrading their viewing experience to enjoy these additional features:
Watch current and past seasons of favourite shows on demand (ad-free)
Live stream CBC News Network 24/7
Early access to CBC Gem exclusive content. More information can be found here.
We do not offer yearly subscriptions for Gem.
For more information on subscription fees, and payment types accepted on Gem, please follow this link.
Creating your account
To create your Free Gem account just head over to the CBC Gem website and select the Create an Account option on the top right corner of the screen. Account creation requires a Canadian address.
You can also create an account from most of the devices the Gem app is supported on.
Quickly create a Gem account using the Gem app for:
iPhone
iPad
Apple TV
Android TV
Android Mobile (phones and tablets)
Roku
If using our apps for LG TV, Samsung TV, Fire TV, Xbox or Rogers Xfinity, you will need to create an account on our website, or through one of the apps mentioned above (iPhone, Apple TV, Android mobile...).
Subscription fees/Payment types accepted on Gem:
For more information on subscription fees, and payment types accepted on Gem, please click here.
We do not accept payment information via phone, email, or through our help centre.
All transactions need to be processed through the CBC Gem app or website.
Offline viewing:
Offline viewing is not supported on CBC Gem. You cannot download content to your device.
Please note that all live streams will still contain ads as they are essentially mirrors of what is being broadcast on television.
