Cancel an Amazon Prime Video Channel add-on
If you purchased the Sundance Now add-on channel via Amazon Prime, you will need to go into the Prime Video Subscription settings within your Amazon Prime account to cancel your subscription to the Sundance Now Channel.
The instructions are below:
Go to Manage Your Prime Video Channels
Look under Prime Video Channels to find Sundance Now
Select the Prime Video Channels option and confirm
Once you cancel, the renewal date in your Sundance Now Channel details becomes the end date, and you'll no longer be charged for it each month. However, you can continue to access the Sundance Now Channel through Prime Video up until the end date. You can find the Amazon Prime Video Support site here.
Still need help or have a refund request? Contact Amazon Support.
Please note: Deleting the Sundance Now app from your device does not cancel your subscription. You must follow the cancel instructions below in order to stop being billed.
