How do I sign up?
How to sign up for Sundance Now
You can create an account with a debit or credit card by clicking here. Your billing address must match your streaming location for successful payment. You’ll be automatically signed up for a one-time 7-day free trial, and you can cancel any time.
You can also sign up for a Sundance Now subscription via the devices below, or use your existing Sundance Now login created on our website to start streaming on them.
Apple
Android
Amazon
Roku
Note: If you sign up using an app store and want to make changes to your subscription, you will need to do so within the app store.
