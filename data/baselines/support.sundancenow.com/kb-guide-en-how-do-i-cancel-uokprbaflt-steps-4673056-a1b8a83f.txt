Cancel a direct subscription
SundanceNow.com Sign-ups:
If you signed up with a credit card via our website, you must cancel through a web browser on your computer, tablet, or via the mobile web on your smartphone. At the moment, you cannot cancel your account in our apps for iOS and Android.
1. Sign in via a web browser and click ‘My Account’ in the top right-hand corner of the site
2. Click 'Cancel Membership' on the lower right-hand side under 'Membership Settings'
Signed up on the website but charged via Paypal?
If you signed up on the website using PayPal as payment method, please log into your PayPal account and cancel the recurring payment to Sundance Now. Otherwise, PayPal will continue to bill you and forward the recurring payment to us. Once you cancel the recurring payment, we will be notified and the subscription will be canceled.
