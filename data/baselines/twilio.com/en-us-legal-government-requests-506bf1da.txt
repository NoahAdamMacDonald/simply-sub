All information requests must follow our Law Enforcement Guidelines.
Twilio’s Privacy Policy compels us to notify our customer of requests unless explicitly prohibited from doing so by law. Agencies requesting confidentiality must cite the specific legal authority prohibiting this notification. If a request does not legally prohibit disclosure and the agency wishes to maintain confidentiality, the agency must either explicitly withdraw the request or obtain a non-disclosure order.
