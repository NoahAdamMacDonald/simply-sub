How do I cancel my subscription?
September 23, 2026 12:10
Updated
Follow
We are sorry to see you go, but we thank you for giving Criterion Channel a try!
To cancel your subscription, follow these steps:
Sign in at CriterionChannel.com.
Click your circle avatar in the upper-right corner.
Select Subscription.
Under Current plan, choose Cancel Subscription.
Select Cancel subscription to complete.
Note that the cancellation will go into effect at the end of your current billing cycle or free trial period. You will continue to have access through the duration you already paid for or your free trial. If the option to cancel is not available, you may be subscribed via another platform or under another account.
📱 Third-Party Subscriptions
If you subscribed through a third-party platform, you will need to cancel your subscription directly with them:
Manage your Apple (iOS app or Apple TV) subscription
Manage your Google Play (Android app or Android TV) subscription
Manage your Roku subscription
Manage your Amazon (Fire TV) subscription
