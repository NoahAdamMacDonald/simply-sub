Pricing and Payment Information
Subscription Pricing
Monthly subscription: $10.99 USD
Annual subscription: $99.99 USD
This subscription rate does not include any applicable taxes for your region.
Our pricing is in USD, as we are a US-based company. Subscription charges will be converted to CAD by your bank or credit card issuer at the time of the charge. We cannot predict any conversion rates or fees collected by your bank or card issuer.
A Criterion Channel subscription starts with a one-time 7-day free trial, so you can see if Criterion Channel is right for you! After your free trial ends, your credit card will be charged at either the monthly or annual rate you selected when you registered for an account. You may cancel or pause your subscription at any time.
Even without an active subscription, you can take a look at what we’re currently streaming:
Browse: https://www.criterionchannel.com/browse
Collection Teasers: https://www.criterionchannel.com/teasers-and-trailers
Trailers: https://www.criterionchannel.com/trailers
All Films that you can filter by decade, director, country, and genre: https://films.criterionchannel.com
I hope our curated and exclusive content will entice you to give the Channel a try!
Accepted Payment Methods

Streaming Apps / Third-Party Payments
Apple (iOS app): Payment is processed through your Apple ID, which can include credit/debit cards or PayPal.
Google Play (Android app): Payment is processed through your Google Play account, which can include credit/debit cards or PayPal.
Amazon Fire TV app: Payment is processed through your Amazon account.
Roku app: Payment is processed through your Roku account.
Refund Eligibility
Please contact us and we will review your request on a case by case basis.
Refunds on our end can only be issued for subscriptions billed directly through our website.
Please note that we do not offer prorated refunds.
