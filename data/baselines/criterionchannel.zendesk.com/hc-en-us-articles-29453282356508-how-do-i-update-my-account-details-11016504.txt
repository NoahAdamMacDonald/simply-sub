How do I update my account details?
August 07, 2026 16:57
Updated
Follow
To update your account details, please log into your account and navigate to your profile page.
Here, you will be able to find your subscription cost, billing frequency, payment information, and the date of your next expected charge.
Please note that by default all subscriptions are billed on a recurring basis and you will be automatically charged unless you have formally canceled before the next payment cycle transaction.
From the account page, you can can change or review the following:
Name
Email
Subscription to the Criterion Channel newsletter
Subscription status and frequency
Payment details
Log out of all devices
