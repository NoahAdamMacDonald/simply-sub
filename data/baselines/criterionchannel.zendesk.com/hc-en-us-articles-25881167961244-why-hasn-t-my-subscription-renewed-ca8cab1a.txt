Why hasn't my subscription renewed?
April 28, 2026 21:14
Updated
Follow
By default, all subscriptions renew automatically.
If your subscription plan wasn't renewed automatically, you will receive an emailed notice regarding the reason. You will have 7-15 days hours to take action and avoid service interruption.
These are the main reasons for a failed renewal:
Your bank did not allow for the transaction to go through. Please contact your bank directly for more insight.
Your credit card has expired or had insufficient funds. You can update your credit card details in your account. Simply click on the link sent to you in the automated notification email and update your credit card info.
Select regions do not allow for ongoing subscription renewals as part of consumer protection laws.
** After 7-15 days without action from your side, the auto-renewal won't be possible and your subscription will be automatically canceled. You can then buy a new subscription at your convenience.
