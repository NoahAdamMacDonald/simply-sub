How do I get a refund?
October 07, 2026 18:20
Updated
Follow
Refunds can only be processed for subscriptions billed directly through the website.
This is the only subscription type our support team is able to refund.
Website refund timeframes:
Annual subscriptions: within 30 days of billing
Monthly subscriptions: within 7 days of billing
If you subscribed through a third-party platform, refund requests must be submitted directly to the provider that processed your payment:
Apple (iOS app or Apple TV): https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204084
Google Play (Android app or Android TV): https://support.google.com/googleplay/contact/play_request_refund_apps
Roku: https://support.roku.com/contactus
Amazon (Fire TV): https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/contact-us
