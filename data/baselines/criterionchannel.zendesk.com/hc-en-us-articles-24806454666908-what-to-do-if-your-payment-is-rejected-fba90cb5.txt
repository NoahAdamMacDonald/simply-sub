What to Do If Your Payment Is Rejected
March 03, 2026 18:04
Updated
Follow
If you’ve registered but your payment didn’t go through or was rejected by your bank, follow the steps below.
📝 Ensure the information you entered is correct:
Card number
Expiration date
CVC code
If all details are correct but your payment still fails, contact your bank. Banks do not share rejection reasons with us due to privacy policies, so they are the best source for guidance on what went wrong and how to resolve it.
💳 Using a Credit Card
Our system requests authorization from your bank.
Banks may refuse payment for various reasons; we do not receive specifics to protect your privacy.
⚠️ Possible Causes of Rejection
Bank refusal for unspecified reasons
Our fraud filters blocking the transaction (rare)
If a fraud filter blocks your payment, contact our Support Team via chatbot. We can manually review and remove the block.
🔑 What You’ll Need to Provide
Date and amount of the charge
Last four digits of the card used
Important: Our Support Team will never ask for your full card number, CVC, or expiration date. Any full card information entered is automatically masked by our security system.
🅿️ Using PayPal
Payments from your PayPal account balance usually process smoothly.
If using an external payment method linked to PayPal (like a credit card), similar issues may occur. Follow the same steps above or contact your bank for assistance.
