What devices can I watch The Criterion Channel on?
October 08, 2026 17:10
Updated
Follow
To watch movies on the Criterion Channel, your device must meet the minimum system requirements listed below.
📺 Smart TVs, Streaming Devices & Game Consoles
Roku: v15.1.4 b100
Apple TV (tvOS: 17.4)
Android/Amazon devices: Mobile: 8.0, TV: Android 7.0
Xbox (UWP / Windows.Universal): 10.0.17763.0 — Windows 10 1809
Samsung (Tizen): 6.0 — 2021 TVs and newer
📱 Mobile Devices
iPhone and iPad running iOS 17
Select Android devices running OS 7.0 and up (compatible with Chromecast)
🌐 Web & Computer Viewing
You can watch directly on a computer using a supported web browser:
Safari on Mac computers
Microsoft Edge on Windows 10 PCs
You can also access content directly at https://www.criterionchannel.com/browse
Additionally, you may connect a computer to a television using an HDMI cable to mirror your laptop screen in SD quality while watching on CriterionChannel.com.
Concurrent Streams
You can log in and stream on up to three devices at the same time under one Criterion Channel subscription.
Please note: anyone logged into your account can change the password or the email address associated with the account.
🔧 Troubleshooting Tip
If you experience technical or streaming issues:
Power cycle the streaming device
Reinstall the Criterion Channel App
This resolves most software-related issues and ensures the App is up to date.
