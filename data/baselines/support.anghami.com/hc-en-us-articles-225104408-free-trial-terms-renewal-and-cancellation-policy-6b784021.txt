Free Trial Terms: Renewal and Cancellation Policy
Updated 1 year ago
Anghami offers a one-time free trial so you can enjoy our premium features before committing to a subscription. Here's everything you need to know about how the trial works, when billing starts, and how to cancel.
🔹 Free Trial Terms
Each user is eligible for one free trial only.
The free trial lets you explore Anghami Plus without being charged.
If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the subscription you selected will automatically begin, and you’ll be charged the standard rate.
You are responsible for tracking the trial end date and canceling before it renews if you do not wish to continue.
Once your trial ends, any music you downloaded during the trial will no longer be available offline, unless you subscribe.
🔹 Free Trial Cancellation Policy
You can cancel your trial at any time before the renewal date through the Manage Subscriptions section in your app or account settings.
You won’t be charged if you cancel during the free trial period.
If you cancel your free trial, you won’t be able to activate another free trial in the future.
