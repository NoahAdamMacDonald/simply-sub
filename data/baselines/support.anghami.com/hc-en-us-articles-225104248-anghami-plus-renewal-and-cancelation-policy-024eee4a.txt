Anghami Plus: Renewal and Cancelation Policy
Updated May 13, 2025 13:24
To ensure uninterrupted access to premium features, Anghami Plus subscriptions automatically renew at the end of each billing cycle. If you no longer wish to continue, it’s important to cancel before your renewal date.
🔹 Automatic Renewal Policy
All Anghami Plus subscriptions are auto-renewed by default.
Your subscription will renew at the end of each billing cycle — monthly or annually — based on your plan.
By subscribing, you agree to allow Anghami to charge you for the renewal unless you cancel it at least 48 hours prior to the renewal date.
Your next renewal date is shown in your account settings within the Anghami app or on our website.
If your payment method fails, your account will automatically switch to Anghami Free at the end of your current subscription period.
🔹 Cancellation Terms
Cancelling your subscription stops auto-renewal and prevents future charges.
After cancelling, your subscription remains active until the end of your current billing cycle.
There are no cancellation fees and you can re-subscribe at any time.
Cancel at least 48 hours before your renewal date to ensure cancellation takes effect in time.
Cancelling your subscription does not delete your account.
To learn how to cancel, follow our step-by-step cancellation guide.
