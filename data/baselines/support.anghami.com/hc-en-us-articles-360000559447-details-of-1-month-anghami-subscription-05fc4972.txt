Details of 1-Month Anghami Subscription
Updated 1 year ago
Upgrade Your Music Listening Experience
With Anghami Plus, you will have a full experience of ALL Anghami Features like downloading your favorite songs and listening to them while offline with no interruptions of ads. Moreover, you can view the lyrics of the songs you are listening to and link your account to any speaker.
How to Subscribe
You can subscribe in lots of ways. iOS users are advised to log in to the website for more payment methods than Apple in-app.
To check the payment methods available for you:
Login with your account in-App or www.anghami.com
Tap on Subscribe
Pick the 1-month offer
Choose the payment method you want
Follow the steps and you’re done!
Payment Options
Apple/or Google Play from the Mobile app directly
Credit card from Android App or from the website
PayPal from the website
By Mobile credits - Find your operator on https://www.anghami.com/plus or the Ways to Subscribe section.
Our subscription is auto-renewable, but you can cancel your subscription anytime before 48 hours of the automatic renewal. Please note, that the subscription doesn't end immediately upon cancellation. It will be canceled at the end of your current subscription cycle.
