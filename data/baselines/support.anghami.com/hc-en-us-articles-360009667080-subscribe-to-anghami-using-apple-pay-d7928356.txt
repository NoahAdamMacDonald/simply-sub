Subscribe to Anghami Using Apple Pay
Updated 1 year ago
Your device should support Apple Pay payments. The supported devices are:
Macs, iPads, and iPhones with Touch ID or Face ID Only on Safari.
How to set up your Apple Pay:
Open wallet App
Tap +
Scan Card and add it
Follow bank instructions
How to subscribe via Apple Pay?
From the App
From Anghami Menu > Subscribe to Anghami Plus
Choose Apple Pay as your payment method
Confirm
From the Web
From Safari, go to www.anghami.com and log in with your account
From the menu, click on Subscribe to Anghami Plus
Choose Apple Pay as your payment method
Confirm
PS: Apple Pay is only available for users located in the UAE
