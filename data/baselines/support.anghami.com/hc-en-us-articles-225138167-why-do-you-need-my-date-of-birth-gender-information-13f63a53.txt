Why Do You Need My Date of Birth & Gender Information?
Updated 1 year ago
When signing up for a new Anghami account, we ask for your month and year of birth as well as your gender identity for the following reasons:
To help us deliver more relevant advertisements
To improve marketing campaigns tailored to different audiences
For analytics that help enhance the overall user experience on Anghami
👉 Please note: This information is used strictly for internal purposes. Your birth date and gender identity will not be publicly visible on your profile.
