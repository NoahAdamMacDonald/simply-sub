What happens when you cancel your subscription?
Updated 1 year ago
When you cancel your Anghami Plus subscription, it will remain active until the end of your current billing period. After that, your account will automatically switch to Anghami Free, and no additional charges will be made.
Key points to know:
Your subscription stays active until the end of the current billing cycle.
You will not be charged again after cancellation.
No refunds are issued for any unused time remaining in your subscription.
Once your subscription ends, offline downloads will no longer be accessible. This feature is only available for active Anghami Plus subscribers.
