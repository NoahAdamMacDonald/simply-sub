Details of 12-Months Anghami Subscription
Updated 1 year ago
Get free months of Anghami Plus when you subscribe for 12 months.
Subscribing to Anghami Plus usually costs $4.99 per month. This means that users who choose to pay annually will get 12 months for the price of only 10 months.
Purchasing a yearly subscription to Anghami Plus saves $10 compared to buying 12 months at the per-month price, which totals $60.
To subscribe to the yearly plan:
Tap the subscribe icon in the app or visit www.anghami.com.
Choose the 12-month plan.
Enter your billing details, and you're done!
Your next renewal will occur one year from now.
Payment options include Apple/Google Play from the mobile app directly and credit card from the website.
Note: If you are currently subscribed, you must cancel your current subscription and wait until your account switches to FREE to opt for the 12-month plan. For more information on how to cancel your subscription, see the "Ways to unsubscribe" section.
*The monthly price of Anghami Plus may vary in some countries; for example, the monthly subscription in Egypt costs $2.5, and $5.99 in some other countries.
