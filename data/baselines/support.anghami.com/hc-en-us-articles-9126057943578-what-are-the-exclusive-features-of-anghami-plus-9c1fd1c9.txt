What Are the Exclusive Features of Anghami Plus?
Updated 1 year ago
Unlock the ultimate music experience with Anghami Plus! When you subscribe, you get a set of powerful features designed to give you complete control over your music, with no limitations.
🎧 Anghami Plus gives you:
✅ Ad-Free Listening
Enjoy uninterrupted music—no audio ads, no banners, just pure music.
✅ Full Track Controls
Skip unlimited songs, rewind, fast-forward, and replay your favorite tracks as often as you like.
✅ Unlimited Downloads
Download all the music you love and listen offline anytime, anywhere—no internet needed.
✅ High Audio Quality
Stream in crystal-clear sound with enhanced bitrate for a richer audio experience.
✅ Live Radio
Host your own live broadcast and share your favorite music in real-time. Engage with your audience through text comments and interactions.
Upgrade to Anghami Plus and take your music wherever you go—with total freedom. 🎶​
