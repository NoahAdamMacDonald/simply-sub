How to Cancel Your Anghami Plus Subscription?
Updated April 30, 2025 08:31
If you no longer want to continue with Anghami Plus, you can cancel your subscription based on how you initially subscribed:
💻 From the Web or Desktop App:
Go to anghami.com/manage-account
Click on "Cancel your Subscription"
Confirm cancellation
📱 From the Anghami Mobile App:
Open the Anghami app and log in
Tap Your Library → Settings
Select Subscription → Manage
Choose "Cancel your Subscription" and confirm
If redirected, follow the listed steps
🍎 If Subscribed via Apple Store (iOS):
Please follow the steps in Apple’s guide on managing subscriptions
🤖 If Subscribed via Google Play (Android):
Follow Google’s instructions here
📶 If Subscribed via a Mobile Operator:
Check this page to find your mobile provider and follow the cancellation instructions:
👉 Manage Mobile Operator Subscriptions
💳 If Subscribed via Fawry or Promo Code:
Fawry or Promo Code subscriptions cannot be canceled manually
The subscription will stay active until its expiry date
Refunds are not available
✅ After cancellation, you will continue to enjoy Anghami Plus until the end of your current billing cycle.
