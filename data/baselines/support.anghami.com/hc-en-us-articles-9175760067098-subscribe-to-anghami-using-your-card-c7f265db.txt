Subscribe to Anghami Using Your Card
Updated 1 year ago
Want to upgrade to Anghami Plus quickly and securely? You can subscribe directly using your credit or debit card from our website in just a few easy steps.
💳 How to Subscribe via Card:
Visit anghami.com/plus
Click on "Subscribe Now"
Select your preferred plan
Enter your credit or debit card details (Visa or Mastercard)
Confirm your payment – and you’re in!
Your Anghami Plus subscription will activate immediately, and you’ll enjoy all the benefits of unlimited streaming.
🔒 Is it secure?
Absolutely. All payments are processed through secure and encrypted channels to protect your data and ensure safe transactions.
🌍 Available Worldwide
Card payments work in most countries. Just make sure your card is internationally enabled.
