Storage and Plans ​
Understanding Storage ​
Why does Ente consume less storage than other providers? ​
Most storage providers compute your storage quota in GigaBytes (GBs) by dividing your total bytes uploaded by 1000 x 1000 x 1000.
Ente on the other hand, computes your storage quota in GibiBytes (GiBs) by dividing your total bytes uploaded by 1024 x 1024 x 1024.
We decided to leave out the i from GiBs to reduce noise on our interfaces.
This means that the same file appears to take up less "storage" on Ente compared to providers using GB (base-1000) calculation.
Storage usage in the mobile app is rounded for display, so a small upload may not visibly change the total. To check pending uploads, see Backup status.
How does Ente deduplicate photos? ​
Ente automatically detects and handles duplicate files during backup to save storage space:
During uploads:
If you try to upload the same file to the same album, Ente will skip it entirely
If you upload the same file to different albums, Ente creates a symlink (reference) instead of storing it again
This means storage is only counted once, even if the photo appears in multiple albums
This happens automatically in the background without any action required from you.
Learn more in the Duplicate detection guide.
Does Ente deduplicate across different albums? ​
Yes! During the initial upload, if the same file is being uploaded to multiple albums, Ente creates symlinks (references) instead of uploading duplicates. This means the storage is only counted once, even if the photo appears in multiple albums.
Learn more in the Duplicate detection guide.
How can I optimize my storage usage? ​
Ente provides several tools to help you optimize both your device storage and cloud storage:
Free up device space:
Remove backed-up photos from your phone to reclaim device storage
Photos remain in Ente and can be re-downloaded anytime
Remove exact duplicates:
Find and remove duplicate files across your entire library
Maintains your album structure while freeing up storage
Remove similar images:
Use ML to find visually similar (but not identical) photos
Keep only the best shots and delete the rest
View and delete large files:
Identify and delete the largest files taking up storage space
Learn more in the Storage optimization guide.
Why is "Free up device space" taking so long? ​
Freeing up a very large number of files at once can take longer than expected.
When you have a large library, free up space album by album instead. Open On device, open an album, tap the three-dot menu, and select Free up device space.
Smaller batches usually work more reliably.
Can I free up space for one device folder only? ​
Yes. Free up space album by album instead of clearing everything at once.
Open On device (device albums).
Open the folder or album you want to clear from the device.
Tap the three-dot menu and select Free up device space.
That removes local copies for photos already backed up in that device album, without requiring a separate multi-folder picker. See also Why is "Free up device space" taking so long?.
Do items in trash count against my storage? ​
Items in Ente Trash count toward your Ente storage quota. Photos in Android's system trash use storage on your device. To free up Ente storage, you can:
Manually empty your trash
Permanently delete specific items
Wait for automatic deletion after 30 days
Once files are permanently deleted (either manually or after 30 days), the storage space will be freed up and reflected in your account.
Learn more in Albums and Organization FAQ.
I deleted photos from Ente, but my storage didn’t decrease. Why? ​
Photos deleted from your Ente account move to Trash first, where they still count toward your storage quota.
To free up space immediately, empty Trash from the app. If both trash tabs are shown, select Ente. Emptying Device only frees storage on your device. Photos in Ente Trash are permanently deleted after 30 days.
What happens if I exceed my storage limit? ​
Ente will stop backing up your files and you will receive an email alerting you of the same.
Your backed up files will remain accessible for as long as you have an active subscription.
To continue backing up:
Upgrade to a higher storage plan, OR
Delete files you no longer need and empty trash to free up space
Subscription Plans ​
What plans does Ente offer? ​
See our website for the complete list of supported plans and pricing.
We offer:
Free plan: 10 GB storage
Paid plans: Multiple tiers ranging from 50 GB to multiple TBs
Monthly and annual billing: Annual plans offer better value
Family plans: Share your storage with up to 5 family members
Does Ente offer regional pricing? ​
No. We keep a single price globally because our infrastructure and payment costs are billed to us in USD/EUR and remain constant across regions. This helps us maintain predictable, sustainable pricing for everyone. If our cost structure changes in the future, we will reassess and update our pricing fairly.
Is there a forever-free plan? ​
Yes, we offer 10 GB of storage for free, forever.
Does Ente offer a lifetime plan? ​
We don't offer lifetime plans. Storage, bandwidth and infrastructure all have recurring costs, so a one-time payment model isn't currently sustainable.
Ente offers monthly and annual plans, and annual plans cost less than paying monthly. You also get 10 GB of storage for free, forever.
See our website for current plans and prices.
What happens if my free account becomes inactive? ​
If a free account has no active paid subscription and remains inactive for 12 consecutive months, we will notify you at your registered email address multiple times before deleting the account.
If you have configured Legacy with trusted contacts, we will notify those trusted contacts as well, so they have a chance to recover the account if needed.
Visitors opening or viewing your public albums do not count as you using your account. Do not rely on public-link views to keep a free account active.
To keep the account and its data, simply sign in before the date mentioned in the emails.
What are the limitations of the free plan? ​
On the free plan, you cannot:
Set up family plans
All other features work on the free plan, including:
Unlimited devices
End-to-end encryption
Map view
Machine learning (face recognition, magic search)
Creating /receiving shared albums
Public links (device limit of 10)
Background sync
Is there an x GB plan? ​
We have experimented quite a bit and have found it hard to design a single structure that fits all needs. Some customers wish for many options, some even wish to go to an extreme of dynamic per GB pricing. Other customers wish to keep everything simple, some even wish for a single unlimited plan.
To keep things fair, our plans don't increase linearly, and the tiers are such that they cover the most requested patterns.
In addition, we also offer family plans so that you can gain more value out of a single subscription.
If you have any queries regarding pricing or require a plan above 2TB, please contact support@ente.com.
Does my free plan storage stack on top of a paid plan? ​
No. The free plan's 10 GB is replaced, not added to, when you upgrade. Your paid plan's allowance, plus any bonuses included with that plan, becomes your total.
For example, the Starter plan is 50 GB plus a 10 GB bonus (referral code from a friend), which gives 60 GB total. The free plan's 10 GB does not stack on top.
Referral storage is separate and adds on top of your plan, up to the referral cap.
Family Plans ​
Does Ente have Family Plans? ​
Yes we do! Please check out our announcement post here.
In brief:
Your family members can use storage space from your plan without paying extra.
Ask them to sign up for Ente, and then just add them to your existing plan using the "Manage family" option within your Subscription settings.
Each member gets their own private space, and cannot see each other's files unless they're shared.
You can invite 5 family members. So including yourself, it will be 6 people who can share a single subscription, paying only once.
Note that family plans are meant as a way to share storage. For sharing photos, you can create shared albums and links.
Learn more in the Family plans guide.
Where do I manage my family plan? ​
The path depends on the app:
Mobile (Android and iOS): Open Settings > Family.
Web and desktop: Open Settings > Account > Manage subscription, then select Manage family. You can also open your subscription from the storage card at the top of Settings. There is no separate Family item in web or desktop Settings.
On mobile, Family appears only when you are signed in to an Ente account. It is not shown in gallery mode. If you use the free plan and are not in a family, this page shows an overview with View plans. On web and desktop, Manage family appears after you subscribe to a paid plan.
If you manage the family plan, you can see shared storage and members. From there, you can invite people, resend or revoke pending invitations, set storage limits, and remove members.
If you are a member, you cannot manage invitations, members, or storage limits. On mobile, Settings > Family shows family storage and active members. Use the top-right menu there to Leave family plan. On web and desktop, open Manage subscription or the storage card to see the managing account and Leave family plan.
Each member's photos stay private unless they choose to share them.
Where do I manage Family in the apps? ​
The Family entry is not in the same place on every platform.
Mobile (Android and iOS): Open Settings > Family. This item appears when you are signed in. It is not shown in gallery mode.
Web and desktop: There is no separate Family row in Settings. Open Settings > Account > Manage subscription, then Manage family. You can also open your subscription from the storage card at the top of Settings.
For invites, limits, and leaving a family, see Where do I manage my family plan?.
How many family members can I add to my plan? ​
You can invite up to 5 family members to your plan. Including yourself, a total of 6 people can share a single subscription while paying only once.
Do family plan storage amounts stack across members? ​
No. Storage from different accounts does not stack.
When you create a family and add members, everyone in the family shares the storage of the admin's subscription.
(Example: If you have a 1 TB plan and add someone with a 200 GB plan, your family will still have 1 TB total shared storage. Their 200 GB does not combine with yours. Once they join your family, their own individual subscription becomes redundant - they will use the shared family storage instead.)
Family plans are designed to allow multiple people to use a single subscription, not to combine multiple plans into a larger pool.
Can family members see each other's photos? ​
No. Each family member gets their own private space, and members cannot see each other's files unless they are explicitly shared using Ente's sharing features. Family plans are only for sharing storage space, not for sharing photos automatically.
Can I set storage limits for family members? ​
Yes! As the family plan admin, you can set storage limits for each member:
Open family settings
Find the member you want to limit
Click the edit icon
Set the storage limit (e.g., 10 GB)
Save
If you want to remove any storage limit from a member's account, you can click "Remove Limit" and they can upload photos without any limit (up to the total family plan storage).
Why does my family member show 0 GB storage after accepting the invite? ​
If a family member accepted your invitation but their account shows 0 GB of available storage, try these solutions:
Solution 1: Check if they actually accepted the invite
Open family settings, check the "Members" list, and confirm their status shows "Active" (not "Invited" or "Pending"). If still showing as "Invited", ask them to check their email and accept the invitation.
Solution 2: Check if you've set a storage limit
Open family settings, find the member in the list, select the edit option, and check if a storage limit is set to 0 GB or a very low amount. Either increase the limit or click "Remove Limit" to give them unlimited access to the family plan storage.
Solution 3: Have the member log out and log back in
Ask the family member to log out of their Ente account
Have them log back in
The storage quota should refresh and show correctly
Solution 4: Check your total family plan storage
Verify your family plan has available storage
If you've exceeded your total plan storage, family members may not be able to upload
Consider upgrading your plan or deleting unwanted files
If the issue persists:
Contact support@ente.com with:
Your email address (family plan admin)
The family member's email address
Screenshot of the "Manage family" screen showing the member's status
Why can't I add a family member? ​
Common reasons and solutions:
Problem 1: "You've reached the maximum number of family members"
Family plans allow up to 5 additional members (6 total including you)
Remove an existing member before adding a new one
Problem 2: "This user is already part of another family plan"
Each Ente account can only be part of one family plan at a time
The person needs to leave their current family plan first
Or create a new Ente account with a different email
Problem 3: "Invalid email address" or invitation fails
Make sure the email address is correct
The person must have an Ente account already (ask them to sign up first at ente.com)
Free accounts can be invited to family plans
Problem 4: Family plan not available
Family plans are only available for paid subscriptions
Free plan users cannot create family plans
Upgrade to any paid plan to access family sharing
Problem 5: Purchased through App Store
Family plans may have limited functionality for App Store purchases
Consider managing your subscription through photos.ente.com instead
How do I add a family member who already has a paid subscription? ​
Subscribe to a paid plan on your account if you have not already.
Ask your family member to cancel their existing subscription, then contact support@ente.com so we can switch their account to the free plan.
Once their account is on the free plan, add them via Settings > Storage/Subscription > Manage subscription > Manage family, or via Settings > Account > Manage subscription.
NOTE
Each family member gets their own private space. Members cannot see each other's files unless explicitly shared. Family plans are for sharing storage, not for sharing photos automatically.
If an account is ever left on the free plan with no active paid subscription and becomes inactive, the inactive free-account policy applies. We will notify the account owner, any family plan members, and any Legacy trusted contacts multiple times before any account deletion.
How do I remove someone from my family plan? ​
Open family settings, find the member you want to remove, select the remove option, and confirm the removal.
What happens when you remove someone:
They lose access to the shared family storage
Their photos remain in their account but they'll need their own subscription to continue uploading
They'll revert to the free 10 GB plan unless they purchase their own subscription
Photos they've already uploaded remain accessible to them
What happens to referral bonus storage if I join someone's family plan? ​
Referral bonus storage is not included when you join a family plan. You will only have access to the shared family storage.
When you join a family plan, your individual plan (including any referral bonuses) is replaced by the shared family storage. Referral bonus storage does not carry over or get added on top of the family plan.
(Example: If you were on the free 10 GB plan with an additional 10 GB from referrals - 20 GB total - and you join a family plan with 200 GB of storage, you will have access to 200 GB of shared family storage, not 220 GB.)
Can a family admin reset another family member's account? ​
Ente accounts are end-to-end encrypted, so another family member or admin cannot reset an account or recover its contents directly.
However, users can set up a trusted Legacy contact for emergency access.
On mobile:
Open Settings > Legacy > Add trusted contact.
Learn more in the Legacy contact guide.
Are duplicate photos deduplicated across family members? ​
No. Family plans are a way to share storage, but each family member has their own separate account. Ente cannot deduplicate files across different accounts.
If you and a family member both upload the same photo (for example, from Google Takeout), it will be counted separately in each account's storage usage. Unlike deduplication within a single account, which avoids counting the same file twice, deduplication does not apply across different accounts in a family plan.
Student Discounts ​
Does Ente offer discounts to students? ​
Yes we do!
We believe that privacy should be made accessible to everyone. In this spirit, we offer 30% off our subscription plans to students.
To apply for this discount, please verify your enrollment status in a school / college / university by writing to students@ente.com from the email address assigned to you by your institute.
In case you do not have access to such an email address, please send us proof (such as your institute's identity card) that verifies your identity as a student.
Once we've verified your enrollment, we'll reply with a personal coupon code for 30% off. The code can be used by one person only, so please keep it to yourself. Enter it in the Apply discount code field before you complete your purchase. See how to apply a discount code for the steps.
Please note that these discounts are valid for a year, after which you may reapply to reclaim the discount.
The discount works on monthly and yearly plans. On a monthly plan, enter the code once at checkout and it applies to each monthly payment for 12 months.
Already subscribed?
Contact students@ente.com before your next renewal invoice is generated so we can review your eligibility and help renew the discount on your existing subscription. Include the email address registered with Ente if it differs from your institute email.
You do not need to cancel, resubscribe, or change your billing interval just to look for a coupon field. If an invoice has already been issued, contact us about the options for your account. An adjustment or refund is not automatic.
Do existing subscribers get a renewal discount? ​
No. We don't offer a separate renewal or loyalty discount, so your plan renews at the regular price.
Ways to pay less:
Switch to a yearly plan. A year on a yearly plan costs about two months less than paying monthly. See Can I switch between monthly and yearly plans?
Students can get 30% off, including on an existing subscription. See Does Ente offer discounts to students?
You can also refer friends to earn extra storage at the same subscription price.

How do I apply a discount code to my subscription? ​
For renewal of an existing student discount, follow the student discount instructions instead of changing your plan to enter a promotional code.
If you have a discount code (e.g., from partnerships like Kagi Friends 25% off), follow these steps carefully:
⚠️ CRITICAL: You MUST apply the discount code BEFORE purchasing your subscription. Discount codes cannot be applied retroactively to existing purchases.
Step-by-step process:

Important notes:

Cannot be applied to existing paid subscriptions
Must purchase from photos.ente.com or desktop app, NOT mobile app stores
If you already purchased without applying a code:
Unfortunately, we cannot apply discount codes retroactively. However, you can:
Cancel your current subscription

Resubscribe using the discount code

Referral Program ​
How can I earn free storage? ​
Use our referral program:
Open Settings > Referrals and share your referral code with friends. New users get an extra 10 GB immediately after applying your code. You get an extra 10 GB when they upgrade to a paid plan.
How much storage can I earn through referrals? ​
For each friend you refer who upgrades to a paid plan, you receive 10 GB of free storage.
The amount of free storage you can earn is capped to your current plan. This means you can at most double your storage. For example, if you're on a 100 GB plan, you can earn another 100 GB (by referring 10 friends), taking your total available storage to 200 GB.
You can keep track of your earned storage and referral details on the Claim free storage screen.
If you refer more paid customers than is allowed by your current plan, the extra storage earned will be reserved and will become usable once you upgrade your plan.
Learn more in the Referral program guide.
For how long do I have access to referred storage? ​
Earned storage will be accessible as long as your subscription is active, provided there has been no abuse.
What happens if I refer more people than my plan allows? ​
If you refer more paid customers than your current plan allows, the extra storage earned will be reserved. This reserved storage will become usable once you upgrade to a higher storage plan.
For example, if you're on a 100 GB plan (max 100 GB earned storage) but refer 15 friends (150 GB worth), the extra 50 GB will be held in reserve until you upgrade to a plan that allows more referral storage.
What counts as abuse of the referral program? ​
In case our systems detect abuse, we may notify you and take back credited storage. Examples of abuse include:
Low quality referrals (users who don't renew their plans)
Creation of fake accounts
Coordinated schemes to game the referral system
Legitimate referrals of real users who maintain active subscriptions are never affected by abuse detection.
Payment Methods ​
What payment methods does Ente support? ​
On Web, Desktop and Android, Stripe helps us process payments from all major prepaid and credit card providers.
On iOS, we (have to) use the billing platforms provided by the app store.
Apart from these, we also support PayPal (more details below).
Can I pay with PayPal? ​
We support annual subscriptions over PayPal.
How to purchase with PayPal:
Email paypal@ente.com from your registered Ente email address
In your email, mention the storage plan of your choice (for example, "200 GB annual plan")
We will send you an invoice with a PayPal payment link
Complete the payment through PayPal
Your account will be upgraded once payment is confirmed
Important notes:
Only annual plans are available via PayPal (monthly plans not supported)
You must email from the same email address registered with your Ente account
Invoice generation is manual, so expect a response within 1-2 business days
Renewing a PayPal plan
PayPal plans are renewed manually. Before your paid period ends, email paypal@ente.com from your registered Ente email address and mention the annual plan you want to renew. We will send the payment instructions for your renewal.
Why is my card payment being declined? ​
Card payments on web, desktop and Android are processed by Stripe. Most declines come from your bank, not from Ente.
Check your bank's app or website for a blocked or pending payment. Approve it, or allow online and international card payments if your bank blocks them.
Wait a little before trying again. Several failed attempts in a row can cause further attempts to be blocked for a while.
Try again from photos.ente.com in a desktop browser.
Try a different card.
If it still fails, you can pay with PayPal for annual plans, or contact support@ente.com from your registered email address. Never send your full card number.
Does Ente store my card details? ​
Ente does not store any of your sensitive payment related information.
We use Stripe to handle our card payments, and all of your payment information is sent directly to Stripe's PCI DSS validated servers.
Stripe has been audited by a PCI-certified auditor and is certified to PCI Service Provider Level 1. This is the most stringent level of certification available in the payments industry.
All of this said, if you would still like to pay without sharing your card details, you can pay using PayPal.
Managing Your Subscription ​
What happens when my subscription expires? ​
When your paid plan expires, your photos and files are not deleted straight away. You have time to renew or export them.
After your plan expires:
✅ Your existing photos and files stay in your account
✅ You can still view and download them
❌ New backups to Ente Photos and Ente Locker are blocked
📧 If your data is scheduled for deletion, we send warning emails with the deletion date
Not every expired account receives a deletion-date email. If you have not received one, contact support@ente.com to check your account's status. Do not wait for an email to renew or export your data.
If you receive a deletion-date email:
📧 You receive reminder emails, including one the day before the deletion date
✅ You can renew at any time to restore full access
✅ You can export your data using the desktop app or CLI
The date in the email is several months after your plan expires.
After the deletion date:
❌ Your Ente Photos and Ente Locker data is scheduled for deletion
❌ Signing in to Ente Photos and Ente Locker is restricted
❌ Recovery is not possible once the data is deleted
This applies to Ente Photos and Ente Locker data. Ente Auth codes stored in the same account are not deleted when a Photos subscription expires.
If you need more time or are having trouble paying, reply to a warning email if you received one, or contact support@ente.com as soon as possible.
If you are on a family plan, the family members' data and access are affected too.
To avoid losing your data:
Renew your subscription as soon as possible, and before any deletion date you were given:
All your data will be immediately accessible again
Uploads will resume automatically
No data loss
Export your data as soon as possible, and before any deletion date you were given:
Use the desktop app's export feature
Or use the CLI tool for automated export
Download all photos to your computer or NAS
Learn more in the Export guide
Can I get my free plan back after my subscription expires?
Contact support@ente.com if you want to return to the free plan after a paid subscription expires. Your account may need to be switched back by support even if your usage is already within the free 10 GB allowance. You do not need to delete photos just to meet that allowance if you are already below it.
If your usage is above the free allowance, contact support about your options. Keep a verified export of anything you want to retain before removing files.
What about subscriptions purchased through app stores?
If you have a subscription billed through an app store:
Manage your subscription through the respective app store
Cancellation policies follow the app store's terms
The same timeline applies after expiration
What happens when I upgrade my plan? ​
Your new plan will go into effect immediately, and you only have to pay the difference. We will adjust your remaining pro-rated balance on the old plan when invoicing you for the new plan.
How prorating works for upgrades:
Prices may change. See our website for current plans and prices.
When you upgrade, you receive credit for the unused portion of your current plan, which is applied toward the cost of your new plan.
Example 1: Mid-year upgrade (yearly plans)
You're 6 months into a 50 GB yearly plan ($29.88/year) and upgrade to 200 GB yearly ($59.88/year):
Unused balance from old plan: $29.88 ÷ 2 = $14.94 (6 months remaining)
New plan cost: $59.88/year
Amount you pay now: $59.88 - $14.94 = $44.94
New plan duration: 1 full year from today
Example 2: Mid-month upgrade (monthly plans)
You're 15 days into a 50 GB monthly plan ($2.99/month) and upgrade to 200 GB monthly ($5.99/month):
Unused balance from old plan: $2.99 × (15/30) = $1.50 (15 days remaining)
New plan cost: $5.99/month
Amount you pay now: $5.99 - $1.50 = $4.49
New plan duration: 1 full month from today
Key points:
✅ Upgrades take effect immediately
✅ You get credit for unused time on your old plan
✅ Your new renewal date starts from the upgrade date
✅ You pay the difference between plans (minus your credit)
Subscribed through the Apple App Store?
Plan changes made through the App Store follow Apple's rules. Some changes, such as switching to a plan with a different billing period, start on your next renewal date instead of immediately. The app shows the new plan with the date it starts. You can check the pending change in your Apple subscription settings.
If you'd rather start the new plan now, see Switching from Apple to direct billing.
What happens when I downgrade my plan? ​
Your new plan will go into effect immediately. Any extra amount you have paid will be credited to your account. This credit will be discounted from your future invoices.
How prorating works for downgrades:
Prices may change. See our website for current plans and prices.
When you downgrade, you receive credit for the price difference between what you paid and what you would have paid on the lower plan.
Example 1: Mid-year downgrade (yearly plans)
You're 6 months into a 200 GB yearly plan ($59.88/year) and downgrade to 50 GB yearly ($29.88/year):
What you paid for old plan: $59.88
What you would have paid for 6 months of new plan: $29.88 ÷ 2 = $14.94
What you paid for 6 months of old plan: $59.88 ÷ 2 = $29.94
Credit to your account: $29.94 - $14.94 = $15.00
When credit is used: Applied to your next renewal in 6 months
Next invoice (in 6 months): $29.88 - $15.00 = $14.88
Example 2: Mid-month downgrade (monthly plans)
You're 10 days into a 200 GB monthly plan ($5.99/month) and downgrade to 50 GB monthly ($2.99/month):
What you paid for old plan: $5.99
What you would have paid for 10 days of new plan: $2.99 × (10/30) = $1.00
What you paid for 10 days of old plan: $5.99 × (10/30) = $2.00
Credit to your account: $2.00 - $1.00 = $1.00
When credit is used: Applied to your next renewal in 20 days
Next invoice (in 20 days): $2.99 - $1.00 = $1.99
Key points:
✅ Downgrades take effect immediately
✅ You get credit for overpayment
✅ Credit is automatically applied to future renewals
✅ Your renewal date remains the same
⚠️ Make sure you don't exceed your new storage limit
If you downgrade all the way back to the free plan, please also see what happens when a free account becomes inactive. We will notify you, any family plan members, and any Legacy trusted contacts multiple times before deleting an inactive free account.
Want a refund instead of credit?
If you prefer to have your credit refunded to your original payment method instead of storing it for future invoices, contact support@ente.com and we'll assist you.
Can I switch between monthly and yearly plans? ​
Yes! Switching between monthly and yearly billing works the same as upgrading or downgrading:
Monthly to yearly:
Treated as an upgrade (yearly plans are better value)
You get credit for unused days on your monthly plan
Pay the yearly price minus your credit
New yearly renewal date starts immediately
Yearly to monthly:
Treated as a downgrade
You get credit for the price difference
Credit applied to future monthly renewals
Renewal date remains the same
The same prorating calculation applies as described above.
If I subscribed to a plan with a reference code and a discount, and then need to upgrade/downgrade, what happens to my discount and bonus? ​
The referral bonus remains, and the discount stays valid for 12 months even on an upgraded/downgraded plan. The amount is prorated.
How can I update my payment method? ​
You can view and manage your payment method via Settings > click on the Storage (subscription) card at the top > Manage payment method.
You can also access this via Settings > Account > Manage subscription > Manage payment method.
NOTE
On iOS, the "Manage payment method" option is not available. iOS users must manage their subscriptions and payment methods through the Apple App Store.
You will be able to see all of your previous invoices, with details regarding their payment status. In case of failed payments, you will also have an option to retry those charges.
If you would like to change your payment method entirely (for example, switch between an app store, card, PayPal, or crypto), please contact support@ente.com.
Switching from Apple to direct billing
Cancel renewal in your Apple subscription settings. Cancelling renewal can leave your current paid period active until the end date shown by Apple.
Contact support@ente.com about switching the same Ente account to direct billing. Support can check when your account can be moved to the free plan so you can purchase directly.
Once the account is ready, sign in to photos.ente.com with the same Ente email address and buy your plan there. Purchasing again inside the iOS app uses Apple billing and may reactivate that subscription.
Apple decides requests for refunds of App Store purchases. Contacting Ente or cancelling renewal does not guarantee a refund.
Why did I get an "Ente account on hold" email? ​
We send this email when we try to renew your subscription but the payment doesn't go through. This usually happens when a card has expired, has insufficient funds, or your bank has flagged the payment.
Your photos and files are safe, but new uploads stop until the renewal is paid.
If your subscription is billed through an app store, update your payment details in that store's subscription settings. Store payments cannot be retried from an Ente invoice.
If you pay Ente directly:
We retry a failed renewal automatically over the next few days. If the problem was temporary, for example your bank blocked the payment and has now unblocked it, you don't need to do anything. You'll see the renewal under Settings > Account > Manage subscription once it goes through.
If your card has expired or been replaced:
Open Settings > Account > Manage subscription > Manage payment method in the app, or on photos.ente.com.
Add your new card or update the expired one.
To pay straight away instead of waiting for the next retry, open the unpaid invoice in the invoices list on the same page and retry the payment.
Want to switch to a smaller plan? Pay the current renewal first, then change plans. Any difference is credited to your account. See What happens when I downgrade my plan?
If you don't see an unpaid invoice, or want to change your plan at the same time, reply to the email or contact support@ente.com and we'll help you renew.
If the renewal isn't paid, your plan expires. See What happens when my subscription expires?.
How can I cancel my subscription? ​
For a subscription purchased directly from Ente, open Settings > Storage/Subscription > Cancel subscription, or Settings > Account > Manage subscription > Cancel subscription.
For a subscription billed through an app store, cancel renewal in that store's subscription settings. Check the end date shown by the store; cancelling renewal does not necessarily end the current paid period immediately.
If you want to switch from Apple to direct billing, follow the payment-provider transition steps.
Cancelling renewal does not delete your Ente account. Your paid plan continues until its expiry date. See What happens when my subscription expires? for what happens next. If you want to keep your photos, export them promptly and before any deletion date in a warning email.
If support helps move your account back to the free plan, the inactive free-account policy applies if the account is not used for 12 consecutive months. We will notify you, any family plan members, and any Legacy trusted contacts multiple times before any account deletion.
Storage ​
How can I earn free storage? ​
Use our referral program. New users get an extra 10 GB immediately after applying a referral code. The referrer gets an extra 10 GB when the new user upgrades to a paid plan.
Inactive Account Email ​
Why did I receive this email? ​
We recently sent an email to accounts that have not shown activity for 12 months as part of our inactive-account policy. Free accounts with no active paid subscription are deleted after 12 consecutive months of inactivity.
We send multiple notices to the registered email address so you have time to sign in and keep the account if you still need it. If the account is part of a family plan, we will also reach out to the family plan members multiple times. If you have configured Legacy with trusted contacts, we will notify those trusted contacts multiple times as well. For more details, see what happens if a free account becomes inactive.
I use Ente regularly. Why did I receive this email? ​
The email includes the email address associated with the inactive account.
If you are using Ente regularly, check the registered email address shown inside the app to confirm which account you are currently using.
Open Settings in the Ente app. The registered email address will appear at the very top.
What should I do if I received this email? ​
Sign in to the account named in the email before the date specified. Check the registered email address in the app to make sure you are using the correct account. Signing in marks that account as active.
Visitors viewing your public albums do not replace signing in yourself. For details, see the inactive free-account policy.
I think this email was sent by mistake ​
Contact support at support@ente.com and share a screenshot of the Settings showing the registered email address at the top so we can verify the account.
