Installation & Setup ​
How do I install Ente Photos on my Android phone? ​
The easiest way to install Ente Photos for Android is through PlayStore. However, you can also download it through other channels, based on your preferences:
F-Droid
GitHub
Obtanium
How do I install Ente Photos on my iPhone or iPad? ​
You can install Ente Photos on your iPhone or iPad from the iOS AppStore.
How do I install Ente Photos on my desktop? ​
You can install Ente Photos on your desktop by downloading the installer from our website.
Supported platforms:
Windows: Download and run the installer
macOS: Download the DMG file
Linux: Download the installer or AppImage
NOTE
Ente Photos for macOS requires macOS 13.3 or later.
How do I install CLI for Ente Photos? ​
Follow Install Ente CLI to download and set up the CLI on Linux, macOS or Windows. Use it to export your library to a local disk or NAS, choose albums, and download individual files.
Can I access Ente Photos from my browser? ​
Yes. Ente Photos is also available on the web at photos.ente.com. It offers most of the functionality of the desktop app, except for machine learning and generating video streams.
You don't need to install anything - just log in with your email and password.
Can I use Ente Photos on a Chromebook? ​
There's no separate Ente Photos app made for ChromeOS. On a Chromebook, use the web app at photos.ente.com and sign in with your Ente account.
If your Chromebook can install Android apps, you may also find Ente Photos in the Google Play Store. It only appears there if Google Play lists it as compatible with your Chromebook.
Why is the latest version not available on F-Droid? ​
Releases over F-Droid are generally slower than other stores since they run their own build servers. The latest updates are usually available within 2 weeks of release.
How can I manually setup Ente Photos installation with Obtanium? ​
You can setup Obtainium to track our GitHub releases by clicking this link to add it instantly.
Alternatively, you can follow these manual steps:
Click Add App to open a new app config screen
Enter https://github.com/ente/ente in the App Source URL field
Enable the Retain release order from API toggle
Scroll down to the Filter APKs by Regular Expression field and enter ente-photos*
Enable the Attempt to filter APKs by CPU architecture if possible toggle
Select any applicable app category labels you may have setup
Enable the Try inferring app ID from source code toggle
Scroll back to the top and click Add to complete the setup
How do I update to the latest version? ​
Mobile apps:
PlayStore, AppStore, F-Droid: The stores handle updates based on your auto-update settings
GitHub/Obtanium: You'll get a notification to update and can download the latest APK
Desktop app: You'll get an in-app notification to update. Ente desktop includes auto-update functionality - whenever updates are deployed, the app will automatically download and install them.
Update the app on each device that needs it, using its existing distribution channel first. If uploads report that the upload API is no longer supported, follow the specific troubleshooting steps.
On Android, changing distribution channels may install a separate app and require signing in and setting up backup again. See Android installation before switching.
Why is my language not an option in Ente? ​
Ente relies on community translations through Crowdin. A language is usually added to the apps once about 90% of it has been translated. New languages also need approval from Ente before they appear.
You can help translate your language, even if it is not close to 90% yet:
Ente Photos mobile app
Ente Photos web and desktop
If your language is not listed on Crowdin, request it on GitHub for mobile or web and desktop before you start translating.
If a new language translation is complete but is still not available in the app, email support@ente.com so we can confirm its approval status.
Multi-Device Setup ​
How do I log in on multiple devices? ​
After creating your account on one device, you can log in to Ente Photos on all your other devices:
Install Ente Photos on the device (see installation guides above)
Open the app and select "Existing user"
Enter your email address and password
You may be asked to verify your email with a 6-digit code
Once logged in, your photos will begin syncing automatically
Your entire photo library will sync to this device, and any new photos you back up from any device will appear everywhere.
Learn more in Creating an account and logging in.
How do I manage all devices connected to my account? ​
You can view and manage all devices logged into your Ente account:
Open Ente Photos on any device
Go to Settings: Web/Desktop: Settings → Account → Active sessions
Mobile: Settings → Security → Active sessions
View all devices where you're currently logged in
To terminate a device, click on it and select Terminate from the popup
This is useful if you've lost a device or want to ensure old devices no longer have access to your account.
If you received a "New login to your Ente account" email, see Why did I get a "New login to your Ente account" email?.
How do I log out of Ente Photos? ​
Mobile: Open Settings, scroll to the bottom and tap Logout, then confirm.
Web/Desktop: Open the sidebar, scroll to the bottom and click Logout, then confirm.
Logging out does not delete your account or your backed-up photos. You can log in again at any time with your email and password.
To sign out a device you no longer have, use Active sessions instead.
Can I use Ente on both mobile and desktop? ​
Yes! You can use Ente Photos on as many devices as you want:
Install on multiple phones and tablets
Install on multiple computers
Use the web app in any browser
All devices stay in sync automatically
Each device can have its own backup configuration (which albums to back up on mobile, which folders to watch on desktop).
Do I need separate accounts for different devices? ​
No. Use the same account across all your devices. Your photos, albums, and all your data will sync seamlessly across all devices where you're logged in.
Only use separate accounts if you want to keep photo libraries completely separate (for example, personal vs. work photos).
Can I merge separate Ente accounts? ​
No. Changing an account's email address does not merge it with another account. Photos libraries, subscriptions, and account history remain separate.
If you want to use your Photos account for Auth too, you can export and import your Auth codes. Verify the imported codes and sync before removing any source data.
Before deleting an old account, check what it contains in Photos, Auth, and Locker. Delete account removes that account's data across all three apps. See shared-account deletion.
Account Creation & Security ​
How do I create a new Ente account? ​
When you first open Ente Photos, you'll be guided through account creation:
Enter your email address and verify it with a code
Create a strong password (this encrypts all your photos)
Save your recovery key - this is critical for account recovery
Choose a plan - Start with 10 GB free or select a paid plan
For a detailed walkthrough, see Creating an account.
Why am I not receiving the email verification code during signup? ​
If you're not receiving the email verification code, try the following:
Check your spam folder to see if the email was filtered there
Make sure your email domain or provider isn't blocking emails from ente.com
Resend the verification code
If you still don't receive it after these steps, email support@ente.com for further help.
What is the recovery key and why is it important? ​
Your recovery key is the only way to recover your account if you:
Forget your password
Are logged out of all devices
Since Ente uses end-to-end encryption, we cannot reset your password without your recovery key.
How to save it:
Write it down on paper and store it safely
Save it in a password manager
Store it in any secure location you trust
Never skip this step! Without your recovery key, losing your password means permanently losing access to your photos.
Can the recovery key be changed? ​
No, the recovery key cannot be changed or rotated. Changing your password does not change it. If you suspect it was exposed, see what to do next before deleting or moving any data.
How do I reset my password if I lost it? ​
On the login page:
Enter your email
Click "Forgot Password"
Enter your recovery key
Create a new password
If you don't have your recovery key and are logged out of all devices, we cannot recover your account due to end-to-end encryption. You can start over with the same email.
Can I change my password later? ​
Yes, you can change your password at any time from Settings. Ente's architecture allows password changes without re-encrypting your entire library.
The privacy of your account depends on password strength - choose a strong one.
Does Ente support two-factor authentication (2FA)? ​
Yes! Ente recommends enabling 2FA for additional security. Supported methods include:
TOTP (Time-based One-Time Passwords)
WebAuthn/FIDO2 for hardware security keys
Passkeys as an alternative 2FA mechanism
You can set up 2FA in Settings on mobile or desktop apps.
Warning: if you use the same account in Ente Auth, enabling 2FA in Ente Photos also enables it for Ente Auth, which can be a problem if you store your Ente account's 2FA code inside Ente Auth itself. See Enteception.
Learn more in our Security and Privacy FAQ.
Why does Ente reject my codes when I set up two-factor authentication? ​
You can use any authenticator app that supports time-based codes (TOTP), such as Ente Auth, KeePassDX or Aegis. If Ente keeps saying the code is wrong while you set up 2FA, check these:
The setup has expired. Each 2FA setup is valid for 10 minutes, even if the setup screen is still open. After that, codes from the QR code or secret you added are rejected. Close the setup screen, open Settings > Security > Two-factor again, add the new QR code or secret to your authenticator as a new entry, and enter a code from that entry within 10 minutes.
The code changed. Each code is valid for about 30 seconds. Enter the code currently shown, not one that has just changed.
Your device's clock is off. Codes depend on the time. Turn on automatic date and time on the device that runs your authenticator app.
Two-factor authentication is turned on only once Ente accepts a code, so restarting the setup does not change your password or sign you out. Once setup works, delete the old, unused entries for your Ente account from your authenticator app.
If you use the same Ente account in Ente Auth, read Enteception before storing your Ente 2FA code inside Ente Auth.
Can I use passkeys instead of passwords? ​
Passkeys serve as a second factor (2FA) for your Ente account, not as a replacement for your password. When you add a passkey to your account, it will be used instead of TOTP codes for two-factor authentication.
Passkeys use strong cryptography built into your devices (like Windows Hello or Apple's Touch ID), providing a more secure and convenient 2FA method than traditional codes.
Learn more in the Passkeys feature guide.
What happens if I lose my passkey? ​
If you lose access to your passkey (for example, if you misplace a hardware security key), you can recover your account using your Ente recovery key.
To recover:
During login, press cancel on the browser dialog asking for passkey verification
Select "Recover two-factor" in the error message
Enter your Ente recovery key to log in
Go to the Passkey settings page to delete the lost passkey and/or add a new one
If you've lost both your passkey and recovery key, contact support@ente.com for assistance.
I'm not receiving the verification code email ​
If you're not receiving verification codes:
Check your spam/junk folder
Wait a few minutes - emails can be delayed
Try requesting a new code
Make sure you entered the correct email address
Contact support@ente.com if the issue persists
What if I lose access to my email address? ​
The recovery key does not bypass email verification. For security reasons, Ente does not disable or bypass email verification unless the account owner reaches out and successfully verifies their identity by providing account details.
If you lose access to your email, contact support@ente.com.
I forgot which email address I used for Ente. How do I find it? ​
Your Ente account is tied to the email address you signed up with, so finding that address is the first step. Try these:
A device where Ente is still signed in. Open Settings in the Ente app. Your account email is shown at the top. Keep that device signed in while you sort things out.
Your inboxes. Search every mailbox you use, including any email aliases or hide-my-email addresses, for emails from Ente, such as the welcome email, verification codes or payment receipts.
Your password manager or saved browser logins. Search for "ente" to find the address you saved.
Your purchase records. If you have a paid plan, look for Ente receipts. A Stripe receipt goes to the email your Ente account had when you paid. An App Store receipt goes to your Apple ID email, which may be different from the email you used for Ente.
Once you've found the address, sign in with it. If you've also forgotten your password, see What happens if I forget my password?.
If Ente says the address isn't registered, see Why does Ente say my email address is not registered?.
Why does Ente say my email address is not registered? ​
This usually means the account was created with a different email address.
Check whether you may have signed up using:
An email alias or hide-my-email service
A different personal email address
Apple Sign-In or another provider-linked email
If you have a paid account, also check how the payment was made. App Store and Stripe payments can help identify which account was used.
An App Store purchase may already be linked to a different Ente account. Making another purchase does not transfer that subscription or its storage to the account you are currently using. If the purchase appears on the wrong account, contact support@ente.com so we can check the association before you buy again.
Why does Ente say "Account setup incomplete"? ​
You started creating an account with this email but didn't finish, for example before you set a password and saved your recovery key. Tap Sign up, enter the same email and complete the steps.
