How do I cancel?
To cancel your account, sign in to BritBox.com using a computer or a web browser on your phone or tablet. If your mobile device redirects you to the BritBox app, use a laptop or desktop computer instead.
Here is how to cancel:
Sign in to your account at BritBox.com
Click on your name in the top right corner
Click on 'My Account'
Under 'Plan & Billing'
Click on 'Cancel subscription'
The next screen will ask, 'Are you sure?'
Click 'I still want to cancel' and follow the prompts
Important cancellation details:
Cancel at least 24 hours before your next renewal or payment date to avoid being charged for the next billing cycle.
After canceling, you'll receive a confirmation email with the last day you’ll have access to your subscription.
Uninstalling the app does not cancel your subscription. You’ll continue to be charged unless you cancel first.
