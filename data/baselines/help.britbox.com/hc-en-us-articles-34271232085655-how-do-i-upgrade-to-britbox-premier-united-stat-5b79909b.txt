How do I upgrade to BritBox Premier? (United States & Canada)
You can upgrade to BritBox Premier at any time!
If you're already a BritBox subscriber, switching to Premier is easy. Once you upgrade, you'll instantly unlock Premier features like:
4K Ultra HD streaming (on compatible titles and devices)
Access to downloads so you can watch on the go
Early Access to hand-picked shows and films
When you upgrade, your new Premier annual subscription will start right away. We’ll automatically apply a prorated credit for the unused portion of your current plan toward your new Premier subscription. Your billing will then continue annually from the date of your upgrade.
BritBox Premier does not include a free trial. When you upgrade, you’ll be charged immediately and can start watching right away.
Here is how to upgrade:
Sign in to your account at BritBox.com
Click on your name in the top right corner
Click on 'My Account'
Under 'Plan & Billing'
Click 'Change plan'
Choose ‘Premier’
Confirm by clicking 'Change plan' again
On the ‘Confirm your plan change’ screen, click ‘Agree & change plan’
If you subscribe through Amazon Prime Video Channels, you can upgrade to BritBox Premier through Amazon. Amazon manages billing, subscription management, and upgrades. For more information, please visit Amazon’s Customer Service pages.
If you subscribe through Apple TV Channels or The Roku Channel, BritBox Premier is not currently available. To upgrade, you’ll need to cancel your current subscription and resubscribe to Premier through BritBox once your billing period ends.
