Can I watch on multiple devices?
You can sign in to your BritBox account using the same login credentials on any of our supported devices, including streaming devices like smart TVs, mobile phones, tablets, and the web.
The maximum number of devices varies by region, as outlined below:
Canada: 2 devices
Denmark: 4 devices
Finland: 4 devices
Norway: 4 devices
Sweden: 2 devices
United States: 2 devices
You don’t need to manage your devices manually, BritBox keeps track for you. Your account uses a rolling list of devices, which updates automatically. When you sign in on a new device after reaching the limit, the oldest device on your list will be signed out. If that happens, you can simply sign back in on that device to refresh the list.
Premier (United States and Canada)
BritBox Premier allows you to stream on up to 4 devices at the same time.
Subscription Access via Partner Platforms
If you subscribed through a third-party service (like Amazon Prime Video Channels, Apple TV Channels, The Roku Channel, or TV4 Play), your subscription can only be accessed and managed on that platform.
