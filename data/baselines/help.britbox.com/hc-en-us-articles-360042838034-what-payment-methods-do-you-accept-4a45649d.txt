What payment methods do you accept?
We accept multiple payment methods. For a full list of accepted payment options, please check below.
Credit & Debit Cards
BritBox accepts most major credit cards. Below is a short list of commonly accepted options, though it may not include every available method:
American Express
Bancontact
Cartes Bancaires
China UnionPay
Dankort
Diners
Discover
Hipercard
JCB
Maestro
Mastercard
Visa
V Pay
Some cards don't accept recurring payments, such as subscriptions.
If you're using a prepaid card (such as a Visa, Mastercard, or Amex gift card or prepaid card), acceptance depends on the card provider's policies.
If you are in Sweden, Norway, Denmark, or Finland, you can only use a credit card issued in the country where you are subscribing.
Apple Pay
We accept Apple Pay in the United States. To use Apple Pay, sign up for BritBox on your iOS mobile or tablet device.
You can also sign up at britbox.com using the Safari browser on your desktop. If you're using iOS 16 or later, Apple Pay is also available in other browsers.
Partner Platforms
If you sign up or re-subscribe through our app on our partner platforms, you can pay for your subscription using your billing account with those platforms. Below is a regional breakdown of where these partner platforms are available.
Amazon Prime Video Channels - available in all regions where BritBox is supported
Apple TV Channels - in the United States, Canada, Australia, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, and Norway
Ruutu+ - in Finland
Telia - in Finland, Norway, and Sweden
The Roku Channel - in the United States
TV2 - in Denmark
TV4 - in Sweden
Prepaid Credit Cards
Some prepaid cards do not support recurring payments, including subscriptions. If you're using a prepaid card (such as a Visa, Mastercard, Amex gift card, or other prepaid card), please check the card's terms and conditions.
PayPal
We accept PayPal for subscriptions in the United States.
If you're in Canada, Denmark, Sweden, Norway, or Finland, you may still be able to use PayPal through your Google Play, Roku, or Apple account. For more information on using PayPal with these platforms, please get in touch with the partner support teams.
