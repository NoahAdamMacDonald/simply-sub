How much is a BritBox subscription?
We offer both monthly and annual subscription options. An annual subscription costs less over a year compared to paying for 12 months individually.
Here is a breakdown by region:
Canada
$10.99 a month, $109.99 a year or $149.99 a year for Premier. GST is additional and not included in the subscription price. Additional taxes may be required depending on your location. Find out more.
Denmark
79DDK a month, 599DDK a year. VAT Included. Find out more.
Finland
€7.99 a month, €59.99 a year. VAT Included. Find out more.
Norway
89NOK a month, 699NOK a year. VAT Included. Find out more.
Sweden
89SEK a month, 695SEK a year. VAT Included. Find out more.
United States
$10.99 a month, $109.99 per year or $149.99 a year for Premier. Taxes may be added depending on the state. Find out more.
