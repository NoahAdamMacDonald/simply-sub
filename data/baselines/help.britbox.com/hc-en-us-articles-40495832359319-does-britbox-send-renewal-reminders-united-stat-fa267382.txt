Does BritBox send renewal reminders? (United States & Canada)
We don’t send renewal reminders but we understand how important it is to stay on top of your account and manage your subscription and expenses.
You can log in to your BritBox account at any time to view your subscription details and check when your next monthly payment is due.
To make things even easier, you may also want to add the date to your personal calendar so you’re always one step ahead of upcoming charges—that’s a simple way to stay in control of your subscription.
