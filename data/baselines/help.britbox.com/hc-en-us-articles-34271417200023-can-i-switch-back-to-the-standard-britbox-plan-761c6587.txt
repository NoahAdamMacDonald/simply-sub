Can I switch back to the standard BritBox plan? (United States & Canada)
Yes! If you’ve upgraded to BritBox Premier and decide to return to the standard plan, you can do that at the end of your billing period.
Because Premier is an annual-only plan, we don’t offer partial refunds if you cancel early, but your access to Premier features will continue through your renewal date.
