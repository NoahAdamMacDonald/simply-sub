When will I be charged for my subscription?
You will be billed automatically on the same day as your original subscription start date for monthly and annual subscriptions, when applicable after the initial 7-day free trial ends or on the sign-up date.
Your subscription will automatically renew unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of your billing cycle.
You can keep watching until your billing cycle ends, regardless of when you cancel.
For subscriptions in Denmark and Finland:
Annual subscriptions direct with BritBox will not automatically renew each year. Unless you are subscribed through Google Play or Apple TV Channels*, these subscriptions will renew unless otherwise canceled.
*Annual subscriptions through Google Play or Apple TV Channels can be canceled at any time, effective immediately before the end of the subscription term, and be entitled to a refund as per the platform’s policies.
You can learn more about cancelations here.
