Is BritBox Premier available to me?
BritBox Premier is available to all subscribers who are billed directly through BritBox, through supported app store payment methods or Amazon Prime Video Channels.
You can upgrade to BritBox Premier if your subscription is billed through any of the following:
Debit or credit card
Apple Pay (US subscribers only)
PayPal (US subscribers only)
Apple App Store
Google Play
Amazon Prime Video Channels
What if I subscribed through Apple TV Channels or The Roku Channel?
If you subscribed to BritBox as an add-on channel through Apple TV Channels or The Roku Channel, BritBox Premier is not currently available with your subscription.
If you'd like to upgrade to Premier, you'll need to cancel your existing subscription through your current provider and wait until your billing period ends. Once your subscription has ended, you can sign up directly through BritBox using one of the eligible payment methods listed above.
Note: You can continue enjoying your current BritBox subscription until the end of your billing period. You can then choose a Premier subscription when signing up directly through BritBox.
