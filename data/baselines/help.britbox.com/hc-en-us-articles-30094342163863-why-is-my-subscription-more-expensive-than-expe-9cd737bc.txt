Why is my subscription more expensive than expected?
If your subscription seems more expensive than expected, here are a few reasons why this might happen:
Taxes and Fees
Depending on where you subscribe, taxes or additional fees may be added to your subscription. These are set by local regulations and will show up in your final charge.
Partner Platform Pricing
Our pricing is similar to our partner platforms' base price. However, the final cost of your subscription may vary when subscribing through a partner platform due to additional service fees and regional adjustments.
Subscription Plan Changes
If you upgrade or recently changed your subscription plan, your billing amount may appear higher than expected or usual.
End of Promotional Pricing
If you signed up with a special offer or discount, your subscription may have now returned to the standard price.
