Frequently asked questions
Am I eligible to purchase access to the Sportsnet+ exclusive content?
+
Customers with participating TV providers who have Sportsnet channels as part of their TV package are eligible to purchase the TV provider add-on.
I’m a Rogers or Shaw TV customer, do I need the add-on subscription?
+
If your Rogers, Rogers West, or Shaw Direct TV package includes Sports, you can access additional NHL, NBA and MLB games at no extra cost.
Simply sign in to start watching.
I’ve purchased the add-on but don’t have access to the content on my device?
+
If you have recently made the add-on purchase, you may need to logout and log back in to Sportsnet+ on your device to access content. Please ensure you are using your TV provider account credentials to login.
