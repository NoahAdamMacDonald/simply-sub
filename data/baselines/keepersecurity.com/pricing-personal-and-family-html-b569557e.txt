How do I protect myself from the dark web?
BreachWatch is Keeper’s dark web monitoring solution, designed to help protect users from data breach risk. It continuously scans the dark web for stolen credentials and alerts users if their information has been compromised so they can take immediate steps to secure affected accounts.
With more than 20 billion stolen usernames and passwords from public breaches circulating on the dark web and traded among cybercriminals, these credentials are often used to take over accounts through credential stuffing and other common attack methods. That’s why BreachWatch monitoring is an essential layer of protection to keep your online accounts secure.
If BreachWatch identifies a weak or compromised password, it’s important to update it right away. Watch this short video to learn how to replace a weak password with a strong one in Keeper.
