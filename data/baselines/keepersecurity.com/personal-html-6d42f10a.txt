What is a password manager?
Rated the Best Password Manager in the World
Password manager features designed for your protection
Best in-class security
Keeper® uses zero-trust and zero-knowledge encryption to ensure only you have access to your stored data.
Generate strong, unique passwords
Generate a complex password with the click of a button and save it in your password vault.
Seamlessly share passwords and files
Avoid insecurely sharing passwords and files through text or other messaging apps with One-Time Share.
Store 2FA codes
Generate and store 2FA codes for each of your accounts without having to juggle multiple applications.
Easily autofill usernames and passwords
Don't stress about having to manually type in your usernames and passwords – KeeperFill® will autofill them for you.
Manage your passkeys from multiple devices
Access your passkeys from anywhere on any device no matter what Operating System (OS) you use.
How it works
1. Create new and strong passwords
Use the built-in password generator to create strong and unique passwords.
2. Save your passwords in your Keeper Vault
Once your passwords are created, store them in the Keeper Password Manager encrypted vault alongside your usernames and any other info. Create folders to keep them organized.
3. Safely autofill your credentials
Use KeeperFill to autofill your credentials on any device the next time you log in to your account.
