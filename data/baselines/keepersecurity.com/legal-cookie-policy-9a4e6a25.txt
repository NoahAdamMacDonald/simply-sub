Cookie Policy
For convenience, this policy may be translated into your local language depending on your resident country when visiting the website. Where there is any ambiguity or conflict of terms, the english version will take precedence.
Keeper Security and our marketing partners, affiliates or analytics or service providers, use technologies on Keeper web domains and sub domains such as cookies, beacons, tags, pixels and scripts, to analyse trends, administer website pages, track users' movements around the website and to gather demographic information about our user base as a whole.
Cookies are small text files sent to your browser from a web server that is stored on your computer's hard drive and cookies are never used by Keeper to store web-vault authentication information or vault user record data. For the purposes of this policy, we will call both cookies and other tracking technologies "cookies."
We also use third party solutions and trackers for our public pages that may set cookies on your computer. These cookies collect identifiable information that allow us to better understand our audience and serve more relevant advertisements to visitors. We also use these cookies to understand broad and anonymous/aggregated user behavior when you visit our websites. Such user behavior may include time spent by a visitor on the site, most visited webpage, etc.
What Types of Cookies Do We Use and Why?
The following information lists the different types of cookies implemented on Keeper Security websites:
Category Purpose Who Serves these Cookies
Required Cookies Required for basic operation of the site, security, bot protection, account sign-in and storing your cookie preferences. Keeper, AddSearch, Cloudflare, Font Awesome, TrustArc

Managing Cookies
You can withdraw or change your cookie preferences at any time by selecting the link in the footer of our site. When you do so, all non essential analytics and marketing cookies will not load on your device. If you choose to disable cookies, it may limit your use of certain features or functions on our website.
California and US State Laws
Users in states such as California have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of their personal information, which may include cookies used to serve advertising. Although we do not sell your personal information for any monetary value, our use of certain online cookies for this purpose may qualify as the "sale" or "sharing" of information under applicable law. You can opt out via the link in the website footer.
Keeper honors Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals and these signals also operate as an opt-out of sale or sharing.
How Long Cookies Stay on Your Device
Some cookies only work while you're visiting the Keeper website. These are called session cookies and they're deleted automatically when you close your browser.
Other cookies, known as persistent cookies, stay on your device after you close your browser and between visits. How long they remain depends on the specific cookie. Persistent cookies are used for things like remembering your preferences, helping us understand how you use our site over time and measuring how effective our advertising is.
Additional Technical Information
As is true of most websites, we gather certain information automatically and store it in log files. This information may include Internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, internet service provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, operating system, date/time stamp and/or clickstream data.
We use Local Storage, such as HTML5, to store content information and preferences. Third parties with whom we partner to provide certain features on our website or to display advertising based upon your web browsing activity also use HTML5 to collect and store information. Various browsers may offer their own management tools for removing HTML5.
We partner with third parties to manage advertising of Keeper products on other websites. Our third-party partner may use technologies such as cookies to gather information about your activities on other websites in order to provide you advertising based upon your browsing activities and interests. As a result, you may receive advertisements for Keeper products on other third-party websites based on your browsing activity on our website. If you wish to opt out of interest-based advertising visit https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout, https://optout.aboutads.info/ and https://thenai.org/how-to-opt-out/ [or if located in the European Union visit https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/].
For security and privacy reasons, Keeper Security does not run third-party ads in its application.
