Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Ad-Free Experience?
Hide
As a premium subscriber you can stream any of our on-demand content ad-free, with some exceptions.
Due to existing licensing and rights requirements:
A small number of on-demand TV shows and films may contain promotional interruptions.
Live TV channels, including CBC News Network, will contain advertising breaks.
Some replays of live events (including Olympic live content) may contain advertising.
Kids programming on CBC Gem (live and on-demand) will always be ad-free.
If you have any further questions, please contact the CBC Help Center​
