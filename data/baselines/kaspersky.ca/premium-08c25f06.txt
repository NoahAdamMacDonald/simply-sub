Frequently asked questions
How can you protect your devices, privacy, and identity in one plan?
Juggling a separate antivirus, VPN, password manager and identity tool is where gaps appear. Kaspersky Premium brings device protection, privacy, and identity safeguards into one plan, combining award-winning antivirus with an unlimited VPN, a password manager, and identity protection across Windows, macOS, Android and iOS. This closes the gaps that separate tools leave and gives one place to manage security for everything you own.
How do you keep online banking and payments safe from fraud?
How do you stay private and anonymous online?
Everyday browsing leaks your location, habits and identity to networks, sites and trackers. Kaspersky Premium includes an unlimited VPN that encrypts your connection and masks your IP, plus private browsing that stops sites tracking your activity. Together they let you use the web without your movements being traced, which matters most on public Wi-Fi and shared networks.
How do you find out if your identity or accounts are exposed on the dark web?
How do you keep children safe online and manage their screen time?
Parents want their children protected without watching over their shoulder constantly. Kaspersky Premium includes Kaspersky Safe Kids free for a year, with GPS location and safe-area alerts, YouTube Safe Search, website filters, and screen-time and app-usage management. This lets a family set healthy boundaries and know where children are, from the same plan that protects the household's devices.
How do you stop one leaked password from putting all your accounts at risk?
How do you protect your data on public Wi-Fi in cafes, airports, and hotels?
Public networks are easy for others to snoop on, which is a problem for anyone working or traveling. Kaspersky Premium includes an unlimited VPN that encrypts your data on public Wi-Fi so it cannot be intercepted while you shop, bank, or send email. For remote workers and travelers, it makes untrusted networks safe to use without avoiding them.

How your subscription works
Prices
When you subscribe, you pay the introductory price for your first term. The introductory price is indicated on your offer page, in the shopping cart, and in your email receipt, along with other offer details, such as:
Number of devices
Duration of the first term and subsequent terms
Renewal price for the next and following terms
Renewal price may differ from the introductory price and is subject to change. We will notify you of the renewal price for your subscription via email prior to the renewal date.
Please note, discounts and offers may be available at the time of your next renewal that are not applicable to you.
Auto-renewal
Your subscription will automatically renew each term until you cancel it. To ensure uninterrupted protection, we will attempt to charge your payment method 15 days before your subscription ends. We will notify you about your upcoming auto-renewal via email.
Auto-renewal cancellation
You can cancel the auto-renewal of your subscription at any time.
If you cancel, you will have full protection until the end of the already paid term. After this date, your subscription will end, and you will not be charged for any subsequent periods.
You can cancel the auto-renewal of your subscription through your My Kaspersky account. To cancel:
Log in to my.kaspersky.com
Go to your Subscription page
Click Manage Subscription
Select Cancel Subscription and follow the steps to confirm cancellation
You can also cancel auto-renewal by contacting Kaspersky Customer Support.
Right of withdrawal and refunds
You have the right to withdraw from the subscription contract and get full refund within 30 days from the date of the order.
Your initial subscription purchase can be fully refunded as well as the automatic payments for subscription renewal.
To request a refund, please fill out our cancellation form or contact Kaspersky Customer Support .
Before reaching out, make sure you have the following information ready:
Date of purchase
Name and email address you specified during purchase
Order number
When you make a request, our customer support team will check if the information provided is correct and the request meets refund policy criteria.
Once a refund is initiated, funds will be credited to your account within 5-7 business days.
