Manage your billing details on the Shudder website
Here's how:
In a browser, login into your account on the Shudder website.
Click here or navigate to Account Settings manually by clicking on your Profile Avatar in the top corner of the screen and then find the “Billing Info” section.
Select an existing card to edit, or select “Add Card” to add another payment method.
💡Heads up! For billing to work, your billing address needs to be in the same country you're watching from.
