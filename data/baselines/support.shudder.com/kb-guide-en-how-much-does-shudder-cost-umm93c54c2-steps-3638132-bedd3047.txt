Shudder Pricing in the US

On a budget? Opt for the ad-supported Shudder experience for even less, and subscribe to our "Monthly with Ads" plan for only $7.99 per month. Taxes may apply. You will have access to all of Shudder's content in the US, on all supported devices, excluding Xbox.
