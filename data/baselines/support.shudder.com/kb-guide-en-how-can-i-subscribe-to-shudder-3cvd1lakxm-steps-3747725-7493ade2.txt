Channel Add-on
You can get Shudder by adding it as an additional channel ("add-on channel") to:
Amazon Prime Video
Apple TV Channels
Roku Channels
YoutubeTV
⚠️ Adding the Shudder channel to Apple TV Channels, Prime Video or Roku Channels does not also give access via the Shudder app.
Ex: If you add Shudder to your Prime Video account, you must watch in the Prime Video app, not the Shudder app.
