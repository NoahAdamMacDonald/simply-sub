How to use Offline Downloads
Some stories are too dark to be contained by a mere internet connection. Whether you’re retreating to a cabin in the woods or find yourself trapped in a dead zone, Shudder will be right by your side.
Available on the Shudder app for iOS and Android, you can now download up to 20 eligible titles directly to your phone or tablet and watch them later without an internet connection.
Unwatched downloads will expire after 30 days. Once you've started watching a downloaded title, it will expire within 48 hours.
If a download expires, you can simply download the title again - as long as the title is still available in our catalog.
