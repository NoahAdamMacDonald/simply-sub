In-App Purchase
Download the Shudder app from the app store on your preferred device
Open the Shudder app
Follow the prompts to subscribe
If you already have an old, inactive account and you want to restart it, sign in with that first
The final step will be your device's app store asking you to confirm your subscription
In-app purchase is available on:
iPhone, iPad
Android phone, tablet
Apple TV
Android TV / Google TV
Fire TV
Roku
Samsung TV
💡Your subscription allows to stream within the Shudder app on all supported devices.
