Cancel a direct subscription
If you signed up directly on the Shudder website, and are billed by Shudder.com, you must cancel through a web browser on your computer, tablet, or via the mobile web on your smartphone.
How to cancel:
Log into your Shudder account on the Shudder website.
You can access your Account settings here or by clicking on your Profile Avatar in the top corner of the Shudder website.
Navigate to your subscription and click the “Cancel” button.
Follow the prompts on the screen to complete the cancelation and receive an email confirmation.
Signed up on the website but charged via Paypal?
If you signed up on the website using PayPal as payment method, please log into your PayPal account and cancel the recurring payment to Shudder. Otherwise, PayPal will continue to bill you.
