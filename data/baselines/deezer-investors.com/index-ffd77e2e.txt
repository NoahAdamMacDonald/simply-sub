Our strategy and history
Deezer is one of the world’s largest independent music experiences platforms, connecting fans with artists and creating ways for people to Live the music. The company provides access to a full-range catalog of high quality music, lossless HiFi audio and industry-defining features on a scalable platform available in 180+ countries. Founded in 2007 in Paris, Deezer is now a global company with around 530 people based in France, Germany, UK, Brazil and the US, all brought together by their passion for music, technology and innovation.
By building strategic partnerships in key markets across Europe and the Americas, Deezer keeps delivering brand value and end-user engagement across a wide variety of industries, including telecommunications, media, audio hardware and e-retail. As an industry thought leader, Deezer was the first platform to introduce a new monetization model since the inception of music streaming, designed to better reward the artists, and the music that fans value the most.

Deezer’s growth strategy is based on four strategic pillars:
Focus on large attractive markets
Focus on selected, large music streaming markets where there is both a strong willingness to pay for music streaming services, and attractive user economics.
Partnership-led growth
Deezer has historically built its business and reputation by capitalizing on the distribution opportunities offered by partnerships. Deezer’s go-to-market strategy is to replicate its historical partnership-led success in France and Brazil in other attractive music streaming markets.
In 2025, Deezer continued to build on its partnership momentum, renewing key partnerships with Sonos, Orange, Bouygues, TIM and Itaú, confirming the value Deezer brings to its partners. In 2026, the Group launched Deezer for Business, a new global brand bringing together all its B2B and distribution activities.
Differentiation through innovation
Deezer is an experience services platform, with expression and connection as guiding principles to help artists, fans and partners to be and belong through music. In 2025, Deezer launched the world’s first tagging system for 100% AI-generated music and was named to Newsweek’s World’s Most Trustworthy Companies 2025 list and Fast Company’s World’s Most Innovative Companies (MIC) 2026 list. Deezer also continued to scale its ‘Purple Door’ events and its ‘Deezer Club’ program in France, inviting fans to immerse themselves in unforgettable musical moments.
Operational Excellence
Data-driven and ROI-based decision making with particular attention paid to pricing and marketing efficiency. Cross-selling into new verticals to expand lifetime value and streamlined operations to efficiently, to ensure profitable growth.
