A player that plays it all.
Infuse elegantly bridges the gap between your media collection and every Apple device you own. Stunning organization, effortless streaming, and playback that just works.
LIBRARY
Your media, beautifully organized.
Infuse automatically fetches rich metadata, artwork, cast info, ratings, and trailers for every item in your library — from any source. Whether you're browsing locally stored files, a NAS on your network, or a cloud drive, everything looks like it belongs in the App Store.
Powered by TMDB, Infuse keeps your library up to date with fresh posters and episode artwork as soon as new seasons air. Filter by genre, rating, resolution, or watched status. Pin favorites. Build custom collections and playlists.
PLAYBACK
Silky smooth. Every format. Every time.
Infuse handles formats other players choke on — MKV, MP4, ISO, VIDEO_TS, BDMV — without transcoding. Hardware-accelerated decoding on Apple Silicon means you get butter-smooth 4K HDR playback while barely touching the battery.
Full Dolby Vision (Profile 5 & 8), HDR10, HDR10+, and HLG support on compatible hardware. Dolby Atmos (E-AC3) passthrough available when connected to a compatible receiver. Intelligent buffering keeps playback smooth even on slower connections.
STREAMING
Stream from anywhere. No server required.
Connect to a Mac, PC, or NAS over SMB, NFS, FTP, WebDAV, or media server like Emby, Jellyfin, and Plex. Or go cloud — Infuse connects directly to Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, Box, and more without downloading a single file.
Infuse Pro adds native iCloud syncing, keeping your entire library, watched statuses, and in-progress queue all in sync. One app, every source.
