Outsmart scams with Scam Protection
Scams are getting harder to spot. Webroot's Scam Protection helps you identify suspicious messages and potential scams before you fall for them. Explore the seven features that make up Webroot's Scam Protection below.
Feature spotlight:
ID Check
Using proprietary software, ID Check helps you verify someone's identity before you initiate a transaction with them. Helps prevent payment scams commonly seen on Facebook Marketplace, Paypal, Venmo, and more.
Before engaging in an transaction with someone you don't know online, share a private link through your Webroot account to start a verification process.
Through this link, the recipient will complete a few steps to verify that they are who they claim to be.
Once the review is complete, Webroot will provide you with a risk assessment, so you'll know if it's safe to proceed.
Additional features:
Scam check
Scans texts, images, screenshots, video files, URLs, and more to see whether it is safe or a scam. Includes protection against AI deepfakes.
Scam reimbursement3
If scam check and ID check fail to catch a scam, you're backed by up to $25k in scam reimbursement3.
Expert support
If you have a question or feel unsure if something is a scam, call our hotline for expert assistance from real people, not bots.
Web Threat Shield
Search result annotation that shows you what links are safe with a Webroot green checkmark. Available across major search platforms (Google Chrome, Safari, etc.) and compatible with Windows, Mac, Android and iOS devices.
Text scam detection1
Checks to see if a SMS link is fraudulent before you tap it. Accessible for Android and iOS through integration with the Webroot Mobile Security app.
Breach monitor1
Scans the dark web to check if up to 3 emails were involved in any known data breaches. Accessible from the Webroot Mobile Security app.
All Scam Protection features are included in both Webroot Smart Protection and Webroot Total Protection. If you're interested in our most comprehensive all-in-one digital security solution, explore Webroot Total Protection.

Frequently asked questions
What is Webroot Smart Protection?
Webroot Smart Protection is a powerful digital security bundle that includes antivirus and malware protection, password manager, VPN, scam protection, identity monitoring, and 24/7 support for identity and scam concerns. There are two plan options available: 3 device/1 identity and 5 device/1 identity, both device count options include the same features.
What's the difference between Webroot Smart Protection and Webroot Total Protection?
Webroot Smart Protection does not include five features that are in Webroot Total Protection: system performance optimizer, parental controls, cloud backup, credit bureau monitoring, and up-to-$1M identity fraud expense reimbursement2. The other features: antivirus, VPN, password manager, identity monitoring, and scam protection features are identical between the two plans.
Does Webroot Smart Protection include cloud backup?
No, Webroot Smart Protection does not include cloud backup. Our Webroot Total Protection product features automatic cloud backup and storage for 1 PC or Mac.
Does Webroot Smart Protection include identity protection?
Yes, Webroot Smart Protection includes identity monitoring and recovery services designed to help detect potential identity-related risks and assist if identity theft occurs. It also includes access to 24/7 support from identity restoration experts.
Does Webroot Smart Protection include credit monitoring?
No. Credit bureau monitoring is not included in Webroot Smart Protection. It is available through our Webroot Premium and Webroot Total Protection products.
Does Webroot Smart Protection include identity theft insurance/reimbursement?
No, Webroot Smart Protection does not include identity theft insurance/reimbursement. Webroot Total Protection offers up to $1M in identity fraud expense reimbursement2. Webroot Smart Protection does include dark web monitoring, identity monitoring, financial monitoring, and the full Webroot Scam Protection features, including up to $25,000 in scam reimbursement3 for covered transactions.
Does Webroot Smart Protection include scam protection?
Yes. Webroot Smart Protection includes all available Scam Protection features: Web Threat Shield, Text Scam Detection1, Breach Monitor1, Scam Check, ID Check, 24/7 expert support, and up to $25k in scam reimbursement3 — the same scam features that come with Webroot Total Protection.
How many devices does Webroot Smart Protection cover?
Webroot Smart Protection offers your choice of two plans. You can choose to cover 3 devices and 1 identity, or 5 devices and 1 identity. Both plans include the same features. Devices include Windows, Mac, iOS and Android.
Who is Webroot Smart Protection best for?
Webroot Smart Protection is designed for people who want powerful device and identity protection without paying for cloud backup, credit monitoring, or up to $1M in identity fraud insurance2 they don't need, such as students, young professionals or anyone on a fixed income who wants dependable digital security without breaking the bank.
Can I upgrade from Webroot Smart Protection to Webroot Total Protection later?
Yes. Customers can upgrade to Webroot Total Protection at any time to add system optimizer, parental controls, cloud backup, credit monitoring, and identity fraud expense reimbursement2.
Does Webroot Smart Protection work on Mac and mobile?
Yes. Webroot Smart Protection supports Windows, Mac, Android, iOS.
