Reporting a technical problem
Please include the following so we can investigate:
The affected model
The affected harness or tool, if applicable
The error code, if you saw one (such as 400, 429, or 500), or a description of the bug
The approximate time the problem occurred
Tips for a faster reply
Email from the account associated with your plan. We need your email address or username to look up your account information. If you can't access that email anymore, please include your old email or username in the body of your message.
Describe the problem in the body of your email. We sometimes have trouble with attachments or forwarded information. If you include attachments, such as screenshots or invoices, please use PNG or PDF format.
