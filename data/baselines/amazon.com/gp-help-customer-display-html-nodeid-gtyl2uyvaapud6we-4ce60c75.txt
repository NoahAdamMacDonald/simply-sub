What are Prime Video Kid's Profiles?
Prime Video ensures only age-appropriate content (12 and under) is visible in Kids
profile.
Prime Video makes sure that only age-appropriate TV shows and movies (with maturity
rating 12 and under) are visible in Kids profile. However, all downloads including
those from an adult's profile, will still be available and can be accessed through
the Kids profiles. Search results and search suggestions will also be filtered.
Purchases are not allowed in Kids profiles. To prevent kids from purchasing on non-Kids
profiles, you can turn on 'Purchase Restrictions' under Prime Video 'Settings' > 'Restrictions'
to prevent purchasing without a Prime Video PIN.
To prevent kids from switching to an adult profile and accessing inappropriate content,
you can PIN-lock adult profiles. See Lock Your Prime Video Profile. You can also restrict creation of profiles to prevent new profiles from being created
to bypass restrictions. See Restrict Creation and Removal of Prime Video Profiles.
Note: Some video content may contain promotional breaks.
Important: Child profiles that are inactive for more than 18 months are automatically removed.
You will no longer be able to access a child profile after it is removed. To remove
a child profile, or to manage inactive child profile removal, go to your child profile’s
information page at https://www.amazon.com/manage-your-profiles
