You may be wondering
Where can I learn more about the various Prime benefits?
To learn more about all the benefits Prime has to offer, visit amazon.com/exploreprimebenefits
Can I share my Prime benefits with other household members?
Prime members can share certain benefits with another adult in their Amazon Household. Prime for Young Adults does not include Household sharing of Prime benefits.
If I’m not satisfied, what is the Prime cancellation policy? Will I be refunded?
If you signed-up for your Prime membership directly through us, you may cancel your Prime membership at any time by visiting Your Account and adjusting your membership settings. Paid members who haven't used their benefits are eligible for up to a full refund of the current membership period.
When will I be billed for my membership?
When your free trial or membership period ends we'll automatically charge for the next membership period.
