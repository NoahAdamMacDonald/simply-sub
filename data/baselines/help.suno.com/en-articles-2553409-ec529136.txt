I can't log in with my email address!
Edited
Suno accounts are most often connected to an email address, but we do not use the email/password format to sign up or sign in. Instead, we use Single Sign On (SSO) with connected logins from Apple, Discord, Facebook, Google, and Microsoft.
If you have the same email to sign into Facebook and Google, for example, please use the method you selected during sign up!
If you sign in and do not see your previous songs in your Library, there's a good chance you have more than one account. Contact our team at support@suno.com so we can help you navigate and recover your account details. We can also help you merge accounts if you have songs in two or more accounts!
