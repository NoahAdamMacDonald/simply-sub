What types of payments are accepted?
Edited
We currently accept the following currencies:
USD AUD BRL CAD EUR GBP IDR INR JPY KRW MXN NOK PLN SEK THB TRY UAH
We currently accept credit and debit cards from major payment networks such as VISA, Mastercard, and American Express. Stablecoin, Google Pay, Apple Pay, and Cash App Pay are available in some regions. Other available methods are Korean Card, UPI, KakaoPay, NaverPay.
Options not yet available with our direct processor may be available on mobile! For example, you can set your default payment method for Google Play(Android) or the App Store(iOS) to PayPal, then purchase a plan. Your plan will apply to both the mobile app and our web version of Suno.
