How do I cancel my paid subscription?
Edited
To cancel a subscription you purchased from our direct billing processor (Stripe):
Please visit suno.com/account then choose Cancel Subscription at the top of the page. You will need to confirm the changes before moving forward.
Also from suno.com/account, you can also choose Update Payment to cancel the subscription directly within Stripe.
Mobile subscriptions (paid via the App Store or Google Play Store) should be canceled directly through those third party services. You can typically find a list of subscriptions in your device's settings and cancel directly in this area.
