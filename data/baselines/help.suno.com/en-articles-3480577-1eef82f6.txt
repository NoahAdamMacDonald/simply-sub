How do I delete my account?
Edited
Before making any changes or attempting to delete your account, it's helpful to note that accounts may not be recoverable if you change your mind!
After opening Suno, tap your profile photo at the bottom right
Tap the gear to access Settings, then scroll to the bottom of the page
Located under the Save icon, tap Delete account
The next steps vary slightly between iOS and Android, but both are intended to confirm the delete action in a way that may not be harmful. You may see a simple confirm button, or you may have to type out a phrase to confirm you are a human!
Deleting an account is a permanent action. Songs and any personal details will not be recoverable. If you need help before deleting, please reach support@suno.com for assistance!
