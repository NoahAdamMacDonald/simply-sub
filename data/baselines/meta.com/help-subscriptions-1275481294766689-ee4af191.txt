Meta One is currently in limited testing and isn’t available everywhere yet. The options you see may vary based on your location, account type, and what’s currently available in testing.
This article explains how to subscribe to Meta One and what to know before you subscribe.
Meta One is a paid subscription that adds extra features across different Meta technologies, depending on what’s available to your account. You'll see available benefits during onboarding before you subscribe.
Meta One includes multiple plans, each with its own pricing and benefits. Learn about the benefits of different Meta One plans.
What to know before you subscribe to Meta One
Review the following before you subscribe to Meta One:
Location: You must be in a country where Meta One is available.
Device: You can only subscribe through a supported Meta mobile app (iPhone or Android).
Automatic renewal: Meta One renews automatically each billing period unless you cancel.
In-app purchase: Payments are processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, depending on your device.
Accounts Center: Your Meta One subscription can apply to Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp accounts that are linked to the same Accounts Center.
Manage where you purchased: You must manage your subscription on the same platform, device type, and account where you purchased it.
