Export a copy of your Meta AI and AI glasses device information
Export a copy of your Meta AI and AI glasses device information
Updated:
a week ago
The Meta AI and Vibes apps and websites are not yet available everywhere. If they are available for you, some features may not yet be available in your area.
If you want a copy of your information from your Meta AI account, you can request an export of your information in a machine readable HTML or JSON format.
The report will include your information from the:
Meta AI app
Meta.ai website
Vibes app
Meta AI glasses
You’ll need access to the email address attached to your Meta account or your account password to request this information.
Meta AI app
Vibes app
Desktop browser
Mobile browser
Export a copy of your information from Accounts Center to a device
In the Meta AI app, tap Menu in the top left.
Tap Settings in the bottom left, then tap Data & privacy.
Tap Manage your information, then tap Download your information.
Tap Export your information.
Tap Create export.
Select the profile you’d like to export information from.
Tap Next.
Select Export to device.
From here, you can choose specific information to export, select a date range, format, the notification email and media quality.
Learn more about exporting data logs and device logs.
Once you have customized your export, tap Start export.
Export a copy of your information from Accounts Center to an external service
In the Meta AI app, tap Menu in the top left.
Tap Settings in the bottom left, then tap Data & privacy.
Tap Manage your information, then tap Download your information.
Tap Export your information.
Tap Create export.
Select the profile you’d like to export information from.
Tap Next.
Select Export to external service.
Choose an external service to export your information to and tap Next.
You will need to connect to the external service you’ve selected.
Choose how often you want to export and tap Next.
Enter your profile password and tap Next.
From here, you can customize your export, choose your notification email and format.
Learn more about exporting data logs and device logs.
Once you have customized your export, tap Start export.
When your export is ready, you’ll have 4 days to download your information in the Available downloads section in the Export your information tool in Accounts Center. You can also delete your export or view past file requests in the Past activity section.
Note: It may take up to 30 days for us to email you a download link. Your file won’t include content that you’ve deleted from your Meta AI account.
