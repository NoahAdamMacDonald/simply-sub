Remove posts, chats and media from Meta AI and Vibes
Updated:
3 weeks ago
The Meta AI and Vibes apps and websites are not yet available everywhere. If they are available for you, some features may not yet be available in your area.
When you choose to remove publicly posted vibes:
People can't find them any more when browsing AI videos in Feeds on the Meta AI and Vibes apps and websites.
They won't appear on your public Meta AI or Vibes profile anymore.
Meta AI App
Vibes App
Desktop Browser
Mobile Browser
You can still see any generated media from removed vibes in the Media section of the Meta AI app and choose to repost them later.
Remove a public vibe
To remove a public vibe from your profile and Vibes:
In the Meta AI app, tap Menu.
Tap your profile picture in the bottom right to open your profile and view your vibes.
Tap on the vibe you want to remove.
Tap Menu and then Remove from feed.
Remove all of your public vibes
To remove all public vibes from your profile and feed:
In the Meta AI app, tap Menu in the top left.
Tap Settings in the bottom left.
Under App settings, tap Data & privacy.
Tap Manage your information.
Tap Remove all public vibes > Remove all.
Delete all of your generated media and chats from Meta AI
To delete all of your chats with Meta AI and any media that you've created:
In the Meta AI app, tap Menu in the top left.
Tap Settings in the bottom left.
Under App settings, tap Data & privacy
Tap Manage your information.
Tap Delete all chats and media > Delete all.
You'll no longer see chats, prompts or media:
In the History section
In the Media section of the Meta AI or Vibes apps.
Your public vibes won't be deleted. Other people's vibes that include you will still be visible. You can still delete these at any time in Media that includes me on the Meta AI app. Learn how to delete media that includes you.
Note:
Similar to other chats, interactions with AI are subject to our Community Standards and Terms of Service.
An AI’s response may be inaccurate or inappropriate and should not be used to make important decisions.
Meta uses your interactions with AIs to improve AI at Meta. If the AIs cannot answer your query, Meta shares the messages you send to AI and general information, like your region, with select partners so you can get better results. Visit our Meta AI Terms of Service to learn more.
Meta AI uses your location information to give you more relevant responses. Your location information is based on things like the settings you've chosen on your device, or your activity across our products. Learn more about how to manage your location in the Privacy Center.
Other information from your profile such as your age and gender, and interests based on your activity across our products, such as the types of content, including ads, you view or interact with and how you interact with it, will be used to help personalize your interactions with AI. Learn more about how Meta uses this information in the Privacy Center. You can also see how information is used across your accounts in the same Accounts Center and export your information. Learn how to Export a copy of your information.
Depending on your prompt, the content generated may go against our Community Standards or Terms of Service of Meta's platforms. Sharing AI-generated content on our platforms that go against these standards will be treated the same as sharing other content that go against our standards. If you share generated content that goes against our standards, your content may be removed and your account may be restricted or disabled. Learn more about the Community Standards and Terms of Service on:
Facebook, Messenger and Instagram
WhatsApp
Depending on your region, your interactions with AI products can be used to personalize content and ads.
If you have a voice conversation with Meta AI, your voice recording is initially available in your history temporarily. Your text transcript will remain available in your history. If you post your prompt with your voice, your voice recording will be available until you've deleted the post.
