Can you tell me more about the Paramount+ subscription plans?
Paramount+ is available to stream on a number of supported devices. You need a subscription to watch Paramount+. Here's what each plan offers:
Basic (Mobile Only) (available in Mexico and Brazil)
Experience in full HD
Stream on 1 phone or tablet at a time
Watch on supported mobile devices only (Note: casting from a mobile device is not supported)
Download and watch on the go
Basic (with ads) (available in Australia, Canada, Germany, Switzerland, Austria, UK & Ireland)
Experience in full HD
Stream on 1 device at a time
Stream with ads
Standard (available in Australia, Austria, Canada, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Switzerland, UK and Latin America (Argentina, Brazil, Bolivia, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua, Mexico, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Uruguay, Venezuela))
Experience in full HD
Stream on 2 devices at once
Ad-free streaming
Watch on supported TVs, computers, mobile devices and more
Download and watch on the go
Premium (available in Australia, Austria, Canada, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Switzerland, UK and Latin America (Argentina, Brazil, Bolivia, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua, Mexico, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Uruguay, Venezuela))
Experience in 4K UHD, HDR10, Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos
Stream on 4 devices at once
Ad-free streaming
Watch on supported TVs, computers, mobile devices and more
Download and watch on the go
All plans include promotions about Paramount+, trailers before/after content & sponsorship (or similar). Live content (if available) contains advertising breaks.
Set Up Your Plan
You can sign up for a Paramount+ subscription on our website or via a third-party app store or partner.
You can sign up for a Paramount+ subscription on our website or via a third-party app store or partner.
If you are setting up a subscription for the first time and your subscription includes a free trial* or a promotional offer period that covers the full cost of your subscription, you will be billed automatically once your free trial* or promotional offer period ends and thereafter, at the start of each billing period, unless cancelled. Cancel any time, effective at end of billing period.​
If you subscribe to Paramount+ via a third party (i.e. Amazon, Apple, Google Play, or Roku), you should contact that third party for specific billing questions.
Ready to sign up? Find out how to subscribe to Paramount+ here.
Change Your Plan
If you have an active subscription, you can switch plans through your Paramount+ account. Click on either Change Billing or, if there are multiple plans available in your market, Change Plan to upgrade or downgrade. Please note, that you will not be able to downgrade during a free trial period*.
If you purchased your subscription on paramountplus.com and wish to upgrade, your new plan will start right away. If there is a promotional code applied to your current plan, it may not apply to your new plan.
Please note: if you upgrade plans, your new plan will start right away. If you are currently in a free trial, your free trial will be lost. If there is a promotional code applied to your current plan, it may not apply to your new plan. If you have already paid for your current plan, any unused days from your current plan will be credited towards the cost of your new plan. If you are not changing your billing frequency, then on switching plans you will be billed a prorated amount for your new plan to cover the cost until the end of the current billing period. Billing will then continue on your regular billing date.
If you downgrade plans, your new plan will start at the end of your current billing period. If there is a promotional code applied to your current plan, it may not apply to your new plan.
If you subscribed to Paramount+ via a third party (i.e. Amazon, Apple, Google Play, or Roku), you should contact that third party for specific billing questions.
Cancel Your Subscription
For information about how to cancel your subscription, please visit How Do I Cancel My Subscription?
*7-day free trials are only available in Austria, France, Germany, Italy, Switzerland, the UK & Ireland.
