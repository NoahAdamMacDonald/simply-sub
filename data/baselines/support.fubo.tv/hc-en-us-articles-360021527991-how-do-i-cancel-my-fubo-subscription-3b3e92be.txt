How do I Cancel My Fubo Subscription?
October 27, 2026 22:12
Updated
Overview
We think we have the best deal for the most live sports and beyond. If you still want to go, follow the steps below to cancel your subscription.
The steps to cancel your paid or free trial subscription vary based on how you signed up, and you must complete all steps before your subscription is cancelled. Use this article to find help with canceling your subscription.
Additionally, you can use this article to find details on how to confirm you've already cancelled your subscription.

I Signed Up on the Fubo Website, and I Want to Cancel
If you signed up on the Fubo website, follow these steps to cancel your paid or free trial subscription:
Sign in at fubo.tv (link will open in a new tab) If you're not currently signed in, you'll need to sign in using your Fubo email and password
Select a profile from the Who's Watching? screen You can select any available profile to continue
Click the settings icon, then select My Account from the drop-down menu
From the My Account screen, select Subscription
Click on your plan to expand it and see more details
Scroll to the bottom of the screen and select Cancel
You may be offered the option to keep your subscription and receive a discount
If you see this screen, your subscription isn't canceled yet
You can choose to accept the offer on this screen by clicking REDEEM OFFER
Or you can complete your cancellation request by clicking CANCEL SUBSCRIPTION
7. If you choose to complete your cancellation, a confirmation page will display, indicating you have successfully canceled your subscription
Click OK to dismiss this page
Notes:
If you cancel your free trial Fubo subscription, your trial will expire immediately, and another free trial period will not be given
If you cancel your paid Fubo subscription, you will continue to have service until the end of your current billing period
I Signed Up on the Roku Website or on My Roku Device, and I Want to Cancel
If you subscribed through Roku, your Fubo subscription automatically renews each month and is managed through your Roku subscription. If you'd like to cancel your subscription, follow the steps to cancel online or through your Roku device.
Cancel Online at my.roku.com
Clickor tap here to view your subscriptions on roku.com If you're not already signed in to roku.com, you'll need to enter your username and password
Click Unsubscribe next to your Fubo plan to cancel your subscription
Confirm your cancellation by selecting Yes, Unsubscribe
You'll see the option to RENEW until the current service period expires.
👉 For additional help, please check out Roku Support: How do I manage or cancel a channel subscription.
.
Cancel on Your Roku Device
Press the Home button on your Roku remote.
Use the directional pad to navigate the channel grid and highlight the Fubo app The directional pad is plus-shaped and is in the center of your Roku remote
Press the Star button on your remote to open the options menu
Select Manage subscription to view the renewal date and access additional options
To unsubscribe, select Cancel subscription; a message will appear telling you how long you can continue to access the content and services
Confirm by selecting Cancel subscription A new message will appear confirming the cancellation, along with a reminder of the date when you will no longer have access to Fubo
Select Done.
I'm Not Sure How I Signed Up, and I Want to Cancel
Not sure how you originally signed up for Fubo? No problem! You can easily check by logging in to your subscription at fubo.tv/subscription
If your subscription was created through Fubo, you'll see your subscription details along with your next billing date; follow the steps listed under I Signed Up on the Fubo Website, and I Want to Cancel to continue with canceling your subscription
If your subscription was created through Roku, under your subscription details, it will say Roku Subscription; follow the steps listed under I Signed Up on the Roku Website or on My Roku Device, and I Want to Cancel to continue with canceling your subscription
I Want to Confirm My Fubo Subscription is Already Canceled
We're sorry to see you go, but if you've already canceled your Fubo subscription and just want to confirm, you can do so on the Subscription page in your Fubo subscription (and not to worry, just logging in won't reactivate anything).
If your subscription is set to cancel, meaning you will continue to have access to Fubo through the end of your current billing cycle, the subscription Information page will show the date and time your subscription will terminate.
If your subscription is canceled - meaning you no longer have access to Fubo - once logged in, you will see options to reactivate your subscription. If you want your subscription to stay canceled, don't make any changes, and make sure you sign out once you have confirmed the status of your subscription.
