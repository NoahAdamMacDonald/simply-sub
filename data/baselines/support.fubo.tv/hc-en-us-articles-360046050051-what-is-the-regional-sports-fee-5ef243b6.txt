What is the Regional Sports Fee?
August 26, 2025 01:38
Updated
If you live in a certain area and subscribe to a plan that includes regional sports coverage, your subscription is subject to a monthly regional sports fee. The amount of this fee may vary from customer to customer.
How much is the Regional Sports Fee?
The Regional Sports Fee is based on your area, and will be up to $16.99/month.
If you're new to Fubo and signing up for service, you can see the amount you'll be billed for this fee during the signup process.
If you're a current Fubo subscriber, you can find the amount you're billed for this fee under Taxes, Fees, and Surcharges under the breakdown of your current subscription on your Plan Management page.
If you signed up through your Roku device, the Regional Sports Fee will be $16.00/month, regardless of your area.
Am I subject to a fee?
Our Virtual Assistant can let you know the regional sports coverage you'll receive in your area. Select the widget in the bottom corner of this page to get started.
If one or more results are returned, you'll be subject to a Regional Sports Fee based on the amounts above.
If no result is returned, you do not receive any regional sports coverage and are not subject to a Regional Sports Fee.

Why am I billed for this fee?
In order to continue bringing you the best in sports programming, if you subscribe to Fubo and receive regional sports coverage, you are subject to a Regional Sports Fee.
Can I opt-out of Regional Sports Networks to avoid this fee?
If you subscribe to a plan that includes regional sports programming, the regional sports fee will automatically be applied to your bill, and it cannot be removed from your account.
If you do not wish to receive regional sports programming or fees, you can change your base plan to a plan that does not include regional sports programming.
