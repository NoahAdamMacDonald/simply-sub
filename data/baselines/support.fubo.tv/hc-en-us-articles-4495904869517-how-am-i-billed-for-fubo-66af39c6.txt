How am I billed for Fubo?
October 13, 2026 22:23
Updated
Overview
Fubo is billed on a monthly basis, unless you subscribe to a quarterly or annual plan.
As a paid Fubo subscriber, you will be billed in advance of each billing cycle. To view your next billing cycle date, log in to your account:
Sign in at fubo.tv (link will open in a new tab) If you're not currently signed in, you'll need to sign in using your Fubo email and password
Select a profile from the Who's Watching? screen You can select any available profile to continue
Click the settings icon, then select My Account from the drop-down menu
Select Billing, then under Billing Summary, find the Next Payment line to see when you'll next be billed for Fubo service.
For full details about billing policies and procedures, please review our Terms of Service.
Your Billing Date
The date that you are billed is based on the date you became a paying Fubo subscriber.
For example, if you were first billed for service on March 17th, you will be billed and your subscription will automatically renew on the 17th of each subsequent month unless you choose to cancel.
How You Are Billed
Payments are processed automatically at the beginning of each billing cycle using the valid payment method linked to your account.
Additional Billing Questions
Can I view my billing information?
View your billing history: Log in to your account at fubo.tv/account and on the Billing page, view previous payments in the Payment history section
View your next payment date: From the Billing page in your Fubo account, check the Next payment field to find your next payment date
Can I update my payment information?
Update your payment information by logging in to your account at fubo.tv/account, navigating to the Billing page, then selecting Update in the Payment method field
See: How do I update my credit/debit card on file?
Can I change my billing date?
Eligibility: You can change your billing date if you have made at least one successful payment to Fubo, and have no outstanding balance on your account
Note that billing date changes can only be made once per the lifetime of your account
Once the change is processed, the new date will be your billing cycle date for each subsequent billing cycle
Process: Contact us using the widget at the bottom of this page to request a billing date change
How am I billed if I change my subscription?
Base plan changes
I'm upgrading my base plan: You'll start receiving your new plan right away, and at the time the change is made, you'll be billed a prorated amount based on the time remaining on your current billing cycle; you'll be billed the full plan amount on your next billing cycle date
I'm downgrading my base plan: You'll start receiving your new plan right away, and on your next billing cycle date, you'll receive a prorated credit for the price difference between the two plans
Add-on changes
I'm adding add-ons: You'll start receiving your new add-on right away, and at the time the change is made, you'll be billed a prorated amount based on the time remaining in your current billing cycle; you'll be billed the full amount for the add-on on your next billing cycle date
I'm removing add-ons: You'll continue to have access to your add-on until the end of your current billing cycle, at which time the channels will be removed from your subscription and you'll no longer be billed for the add-on
Billing frequency changes
If you are changing your subscription to or from a quarterly or annual plan, the day you change your subscription will become your new renewal date
