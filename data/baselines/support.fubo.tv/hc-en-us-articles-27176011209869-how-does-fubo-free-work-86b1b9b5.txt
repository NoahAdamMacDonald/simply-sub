How does Fubo Free work?
2 months ago Updated
If you’re a former Fubo subscriber, you can still watch over 170 free ad-supported channels of great live and On-Demand content without a paid subscription with Fubo Free.
Fubo Free is available on select devices and to select former Fubo subscribers who have expired from a paying subscription or free trial. If you cancel your free trial subscription, it will automatically expire at the time of cancellation. If you cancel a paid subscription, it will expire at the end of your current billing period.
Read below for more information:
How do I access Fubo Free?
Once your subscription expires, simply log in to your Fubo account at fubo.tv with your previous credentials (email and password), and you can either reactivate your previous subscription, or select Go to free content instead to check out Fubo Free.
I don’t see this option
Initially, the Fubo Free experience will only be available to select former Fubo subscribers on select devices. Fubo Free is not currently available on Roku or Samsung TV devices.
I’m a Fubo subscriber, how do I get Fubo Free?
The channels and programming included with Fubo Free are already a part of your Fubo subscription.
What is included with Fubo Free?
In addition to 170+ free channels, Fubo Free will look similar to the standard Fubo experience. You’ll have Home, Guide, Sports, My Stuff, Shows, and Movies screens. You’ll be able to:
Set and reorder favorite channels, search for specific channel, or filter by genre in the guide
Use Search to find a channel, show, movie, or game
Add series and movies to your Watchlist, and access them from My Stuff
Find games to watch, or search available leagues and teams to see live, upcoming, or previously-aired games on the Sports screen
Find free programming on the Shows and Movies screens to watch, and filter by genre to narrow your search
What channels will I get with Fubo Free?
Many of the channels available with Fubo Free are also included with paid Fubo TV plans and services.
To see which channels are available in Fubo Free, use this article and search "Fubo Free."
Additional Questions
Can I record shows on Fubo Free?
Cloud DVR capabilities are available on Connected TV Devices and Smart TVs with Fubo Free (web browsers and mobile devices are not currently supported).
Why can't I watch some content that shows up on Fubo Free?
You may see some programming on screens and in search results in Fubo Free that appears with a lock icon. This programming is not viewable in Fubo Free, and is only available with a paid Fubo subscription.
Will my Cloud DVR recordings from my paid subscription be available on Fubo Free?
No. When canceling your paid subscription, you’ll have had access to your recordings until your subscription expired. Once your subscription expired, your recordings were automatically deleted.
Will I get my local or regional channels?
No. Local channels and regional sports programming is only available with a paid Fubo subscription.
How do I reactivate my paid subscription?
Log in to your Fubo Free account at fubo.tv, and click the Settings cog at the top of the screen. Then select My Account.
From there, select Reactivate your subscription, and follow the instructions to reactivate your Fubo subscription.
