How can I manage my privacy?
3 years ago Updated
Visit the Fubo Privacy Center for details on what information we collect, and how that information is used.
Read below for additional information on managing your personal information.
Can I delete my personal information?
You can.
To request deletion of your personal data, follow the steps below:
Click here to visit the Privacy Center and Make a Privacy Request
Select Delete all my data
Enter your Fubo account email address, then select Send Email
Follow the instructions in the email to confirm your identity and submit your deletion request
Once received, we will delete your Fubo account and all personal information, with the exception of some data we keep in order to comply with applicable laws.
Before proceeding, please note that data deletion is a permanent action, and upon account deletion, you will immediately lose access to your account, and we will be unable to assist with any matters related to that account.
To avoid immediately losing account access and any remaining subscription, please cancel your subscription first and wait for it to end prior to proceeding with this deletion request. For instructions on how to cancel your subscription, see this article.
