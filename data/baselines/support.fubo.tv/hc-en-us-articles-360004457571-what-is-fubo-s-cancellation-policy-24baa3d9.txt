What Is Fubo's Cancellation Policy?
1 year ago Updated
With Fubo, you can cancel at any time. We hate to see you go, but if you want to cancel your Fubo subscription, find help here.
Paid Subscription Cancellations:
Your subscription will automatically renew each month until you choose to cancel your subscription. You may cancel at any time so that it will not renew after the current subscription period. If you choose to cancel, your current paid subscription will continue through the end of your current billing cycle. Note that we do not provide refunds for prepaid or partial months of service.
Free Trial Cancellations:
Signed up on Fubo's Website or through Fubo Sales Team: If you signed up on Fubo’s website and cancel during a free trial, your subscription will be terminated immediately and you will lose access to Fubo.
Signed up through Roku: If you signed up through your Roku account and cancel during a free trial, your subscription will terminate at the end of your free trial.
Are there contracts or cancellation fees?
Most Fubo plans don’t include cancellation fees. However, if you signed up for a promotional offer with a commitment period, an early cancellation fee may apply if you cancel before that period ends.
To learn more, please see our Terms of Service.
