How do I sign up using my Roku?
04 April 2024 22:04
Updated
Use your Roku.com account to sign up for your free trial1 to Fubo2. Please note, when creating your subscription through In-App Billing on your Roku device you must manage your subscription via your Roku.com account.
For help with managing your Roku-billed Fubo subscription, go here.
Download and install the Fubo app onto your Roku
When you open Fubo, you'll see the option to Sign Up or Sign In. Select Sign Up
To sign up for Fubo using the same email address as your Roku.com account, select Continue; to sign up using a different email address, select Cancel
Enter the email address you want to use as your Fubo user name (if needed), a secure password, and your current zip code, then select Create Account
Select the plan you'd like to sign up with. Choose View Details to see all the channels you'll receive in the selected plan. Select Choose Plan on your remote to select the highlighted plan
Note that plan availability can change, and that some plans may not include a free trial
Enter your Roku PIN and select Start Subscription
Select manage or add profiles, then start streaming
1The free trial is for new customers only.
2The Pro, Elite, Ultimate and Latino plans are offered through Roku In-App Billing. If you'd like to subscribe to a different basic package and/or apply any Add-ons not included in the available bundles to your subscription, you'll need to sign-up directly on www.fubo.tv.
