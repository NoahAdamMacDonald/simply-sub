How do Annual Plans with Monthly Billing Work?
12 October 2026 22:06
Updated
Overview
New Fubo subscribers in Canada can now commit to annual plans and pay monthly.
Select plans let you pay the full annual rate over 12 months at a discounted price.
Use this article for answers to common questions and additional information about annual plans that are billed monthly.
Frequently Asked Questions about Annual Plans with Monthly Billing
Select an option below to expand and see more information.
What Annual Plans are Available with Monthly Billing?
Currently, the Sports Pro plan is available in Canada as an annual subscription with monthly billing.
👉 For more on available plans and pricing, visit fubo.tv
Note: Annual plans with monthly billing are not available in Quebec or where prohibited by law
I’m Already a Fubo Subscriber; Can I Switch to an Annual Plan with Monthly Billing? Currently, only new Fubo subscribers are able to sign up for annual plans that are billed monthly.
What Happens if I Cancel My Annual Plan with Monthly Billing?
If you choose to cancel your annual plan with monthly billing before the end of your 12-month commitment, you will incur an early termination fee.
This fee will be automatically billed to the card on file associated with your Fubo account.
👉 For more information, see: What is Fubo’s Cancellation Policy?
How Much is the Early Termination Fee?
The amount of the early termination fee is based on the plan you subscribe to:
Sports: C$49.50
Sports Pro: C$90.00
These fees apply if your subscription is canceled before the end of your 12-month commitment.
What Happens Once My 12-Month Commitment Ends?
Once the 12-month commitment on your annual plan that’s billed monthly ends, your subscription will revert to standard month-to-month billing.
If you choose to cancel your subscription once your 12-month commitment has ended, you will not incur an early termination fee.
👉 For more information, see: How am I billed for Fubo?
Can I Change My Subscription to Another Plan Without a Commitment? Plan changes are not allowed until after the end of your 12-month commitment.
Select plans may not be available in all areas, including where prohibited by law. Plan pricing, fees, and terms subject to applicable laws and regulations.
