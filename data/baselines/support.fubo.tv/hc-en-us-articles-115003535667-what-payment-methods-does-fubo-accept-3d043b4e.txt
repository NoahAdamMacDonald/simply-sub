What Payment Methods Does Fubo Accept?
January 26, 2026 20:37
Updated
Overview
Fubo accepts a variety of payment methods, including debit cards and credit cards. Use this article to find a list of payment methods we currently accept, along with answers to common questions.
✅ Accepted Payment Methods
Fubo accepts the following payment methods:
Credit and Debit Cards
Visa
MasterCard
American Express
Discover
Digital Wallets
CashApp
🙋 Frequently Asked Questions
Can I Change or Update My Payment Method?
Yes, you can update your payment method at any time.
👉 For help with updating your payment method, see How do I Update My Credit/Debit Card on File?
If you are using a digital wallet for your Fubo payments and need to update your card details, please update your card details through your digital wallet provider and not through Fubo
You can change your digital wallet to be directly billed through your credit or debit card; however, if you created your Fubo account with a credit or debit card on file, you will not be able to switch your payments to a digital wallet at a later time
How am I Billed for Fubo?
Fubo is billed on a monthly basis, unless you subscribe to a quarterly or annual Fubo plan. You can find details for the payments you've made to Fubo in the Billing section of your My Account page.
👉 For more information about how you'll be billed for your Fubo subscription, see How am I Billed for Fubo?
Why was My Payment Declined?
Your payment could have been declined due to an issue with your card or a security block.
👉 For more information and assistance, see Why Was My Card Declined?
