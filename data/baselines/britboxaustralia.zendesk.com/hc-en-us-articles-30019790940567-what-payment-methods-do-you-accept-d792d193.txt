What payment methods do you accept?
We accept multiple payment methods. For a full list of accepted payment options, please check below.
Credit & Debit Cards
BritBox accepts most major credit cards. Below is a short list of commonly accepted options, though it may not include every available method:
American Express
Bancontact
Cartes Bancaires
China UnionPay
Dankort
Diners
Discover
Hipercard
JCB
Maestro
Mastercard
Visa
V Pay
Some cards don't accept recurring payments, such as subscriptions.
If you're using a prepaid card (such as a Visa, Mastercard, or Amex gift card or prepaid card), acceptance depends on the card provider's policies.
At this time, we can only accept credit cards issued in Australia as a payment method.
Apple Pay
We accept Apple Pay. To use Apple Pay, sign up for BritBox on your iOS mobile or tablet device.
You can also sign up at britbox.com using the Safari browser on your desktop. If you're using iOS 16 or later, Apple Pay is also available in other browsers.
Amazon Prime Video Channels or Apple TV Channels
If you sign up or re-subscribe through our app on Amazon Prime Video Channels or the Apple TV Channels, you can pay for your subscription using your billing account with these partner platforms.
Prepaid Credit Cards
Some prepaid cards do not support recurring payments, including subscriptions. If you're using a prepaid card (such as a Visa, Mastercard, Amex gift card, or other prepaid card), please check the card's terms and conditions.
