Frequently asked questions
How do I get access to the features?
As soon as your support is active, the features unlock automatically on every device where you are signed in with the same Stremio account. Just keep the app updated to the latest version — there is nothing to redeem or enter manually.
What am I not getting?
You are supporting the app, not buying content. Your support only unlocks in-app software features — it is a software service and nothing more. Stremio is not an IPTV provider and does not host, supply, or stream any movies, series, or copyrighted material.
Will the features work on all my devices?
Yes. Your benefits are tied to your Stremio account, so signing in on desktop, web, mobile, or TV with the same account gives you the same features everywhere.
Can I cancel anytime?
Of course. Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically and can be cancelled at any time from your account settings — you keep your benefits until the end of the period you have already paid for. The Lifetime plan is a one-time payment with nothing to renew or cancel.
What is the difference between the plans?
Monthly and yearly are recurring subscriptions, with yearly costing less over time. Lifetime is a single payment that unlocks the features for good. Every plan gives you the exact same features.
Where does my money go?
Your support goes directly into building and maintaining Stremio — new features, faster apps, and keeping the service running for everyone. It is the simplest way to help the project stay independent.
