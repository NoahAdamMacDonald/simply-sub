Cookie Policy
What are cookies?
Cookies are small pieces of text used to store information on web browsers. Cookies are used to store and receive identifiers and other information on computers, phones, and other devices. Other technologies, like identifiers associated with your device, are used for similar purposes. In this policy, we refer to all of these technologies as “cookies.”
A tracking pixel is a small piece of code that can be embedded on websites and in emails. We use pixels and cookies to learn how you interact with our website and emails. This information helps us, and the advertising services providers we use, better promote our services. For example, if you visit Stremio website, you may later see an ad for Stremio when you visit a tech news site.
What types of cookies do we collect?
We collect two general types of cookies: session (temporary) cookies and persistent (fixed) cookies.
Session cookies are temporary files that are stored on your device until you log out, leave the website or shut down your browser.
Persistent cookies are stored on the your device for the time specified in the parameters of cookies or until you remove them.
Why do we use cookies?
We use cookies and tracking pixels to collect anonymous information for the following purposes:
To analyse traffic to the Stremio website and Stremio apps. We use cookies to better understand how people use the Stremio website and Stremio apps. These cookies help us understand which parts of the Stremio website and Stremio apps people find most useful and engaging, and identify features that can be improved.
The analytics technology we use is Google Analytics. To learn more about Google Analytics and your privacy, visit https://www.google.com/analytics/learn/privacy.html. To opt out of being tracked by Google Analytics you can use various browser addons such as https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout or https://www.ghostery.com/.
To provide social media features. The Stremio website and Stremio apps use social media features provided by services such as Twitter, Facebook, and Google+ to enable people to share information with these channels. Cookies used by these services are owned and controlled by third parties, and therefore said the third parties are responsible for their use. Below are links to some of the most commonly used social networks and their privacy policies.
Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/about/privacy
Google+ and YouTube: https://www.google.com/policies/privacy
Twitter: https://twitter.com/privacycy
To customise content and advertising. We use cookies to help us conduct website content experiments (A\B tests). Cookies also allow us to advertise to people who have previously visited certain pages of the Stremio website.
To analyse advertising and email marketing campaigns. From time to time, we may use various advertising networks and third-party marketing partners that use cookies, tracking pixels or similar technologies to collect information about your visits and actions on certain pages of Stremio website and about whether you open certain emails that we send you.
Managing your cookie preferences
Ypou can manage your cookie preferences via your online browser and set your browser to refuse cookies, or delete certain cookies. This may prevent some functions on Stremio website and Stremio apps work properly.
For more information about the use of cookies and how to block them, visit www.youronlinechoices.eu (Europe) or www.aboutads.info/choices/ (United States).
