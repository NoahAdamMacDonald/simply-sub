Purchases and Family Sharing
Updated 6 months ago
In this article
Family Sharing
Upgrading From Older Versions
Infuse Pro is available as a subscription (Monthly or Yearly) or as a one-time purchase (Lifetime). All future updates (even major releases like v9, v10. v42, etc…) are included with all in-app purchase options.
Download Infuse 8 (free w/ optional in-app purchase)
Family Sharing
Family Sharing is supported for both in-app purchases and subscriptions. Learn more about Family Sharing
Tips for Family Sharing
Accounts must be in the same App Store region
Family members have been added to Family Sharing with Purchase Sharing enabled (Note: Both the account sharing the purchase and the account using the purchase need to activate Purchase Sharing)
For subscriptions, ensure sharing has been enabled in the Infuse subscription page (App Store > Subscriptions > Infuse)
Tap the Restore button on the purchase prompt (if it appears)
In some cases it may be necessary to delete and re-download the app from the main family member’s list of Purchased apps in the App Store.
Alternative option for Family Sharing via Trakt (can be used between App Store regions)
Log into Trakt on the purchaser’s device in Infuse’s Settings menu (Note: On iOS/macOS, look for the orange Trakt icon in the upper right corner of the Settings menu).
Log into the same Trakt account on other devices
Notes for Trakt sharing
A single purchase can be shared with up to 10 devices
At least one device must remain logged in to the App Store account which was originally used to purchase the Pro license
If the device count exceeds 10 sharing may become disabled for a period of time
