How to Access or Cancel Your Rumble Premium Subscription (and Refund Policy)
Step-by-step instructions for accessing your Premium account, canceling your subscription (web, iOS, Android), and understanding Rumble’s refund policy.
Last updated on 18 Dec, 2025
This guide walks you through accessing your Rumble Premium account, canceling your subscription on any platform, and understanding our refund policy.
Steps to Access or Cancel Your Premium Subscription
Access Your Premium Subscription
Go to https://rumble.com/account/recurring-subs and sign in. You’ll see your active subscriptions, payment method, and renewal dates.
Cancel Your Premium Subscription
(Web Purchases Only) Click Cancel next to your Premium subscription. Follow the prompts to confirm. You’ll keep Premium access until your billing period ends.
iOS App Store (Apple Purchases) Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Find Rumble Premium and tap Cancel Subscription. Apple manages billing and cancellations for App Store purchases.
Cancel via Google Play Store (Android Purchases) Open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Find Rumble Premium and tap Cancel Subscription. Google manages billing and cancellations for Play Store purchases.
Important: If you subscribed via iOS or Google Play, you must cancel through your Apple or Google account. You can only manage web-based subscriptions at rumble.com.
Refund Policy
Rumble Premium subscriptions are non-refundable. If you cancel, you’ll keep access until the end of your billing period. Apple and Google may have different refund policies for App Store purchases.
Troubleshooting
I don’t see my Premium subscription on the Rumble website. If you purchased via iOS App Store or Google Play, your subscription will not appear on rumble.com. Manage it directly in your Apple or Google account.
Can’t find the cancel button on my subscription page. Make sure you are logged into the correct Rumble account. Only web-based subscriptions can be managed at rumble.com/account/recurring-subs.
How do I access Premium features? After purchase, sign in to your Rumble account. Premium benefits are automatically applied to your account on the web and in the app. For Mug Club, link your Locals account as described in our Mug Club guide.
FAQ
What happens after I cancel my Premium subscription? You keep Premium access until the period you paid for ends. No further charges after that.
Can I get a refund if I cancel early? No, Rumble Premium subscriptions are non-refundable. Apple or Google may have different rules for purchases made through their platforms.
How do I know how I subscribed? Check your purchase confirmation email or your account subscriptions page. If you subscribed via iOS or Google Play, you must manage your subscription there.
