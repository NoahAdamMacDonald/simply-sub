How to change your payment details
To add or change your payment details for Spotify:
Go to Payment methods.
Select Add to enter a new payment method, or Change to edit an existing one.
Select Add/Change payment method to confirm.
Note: When you add a payment method, you may see an authorization charge for a small amount appear on your account. This authorization charge allows us to verify the validity of your payment method. It’s only a temporary hold and will disappear within a short time.
Manage your payment details
Payments through a partner
If your plan is with a partner company (ex. your phone or internet provider), they manage your payments. You’ll need to speak to them about anything payment related.
To check who your provider is, or to reach out to them directly:
Go to Manage your subscription.
Check the contact link under Payment.
To change your payment details:
Go to your partner’s website to cancel your current Premium plan.
Tip: Find their details on your account page.
When your Spotify account switches to free, sign up to any Premium plan.
Saved payment methods
You can save and manage your payment methods for future purchases.
Add a payment card
To save the details for a card:
Go to Payment methods.
Select Add.
Enter the card details and select Save card for future orders.
Select Add payment method to confirm.
Remove a payment card
To remove the details of a saved card:
Go to Payment methods.
Select Saved payment methods.
Select Remove next to the card you want to remove.
Select Remove to confirm.
Issues adding a payment card
If you’re experiencing issues when entering card details:
Make sure your bank has the right information, and that your personal details are up to date and correct
Contact your bank for assistance
Note: You won’t be charged until your bank has verified your identity.
