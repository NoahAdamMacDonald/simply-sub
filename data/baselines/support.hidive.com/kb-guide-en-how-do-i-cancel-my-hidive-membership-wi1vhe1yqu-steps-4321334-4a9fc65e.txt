❗ Deleting the HIDIVE app or channel from your device does not cancel your subscription. You must follow the correct cancel instructions in order to stop being billed.
If you signed up via Google Play through the HIDIVE Android app, you can cancel your subscription by following these steps:
Cancel on a Computer Go to play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions
Next to HIDIVE, select Cancel Subscription
Select Cancel Subscription again to confirm
Once you've completed the steps above, you will see a message stating, “You have successfully cancelled your subscription,” and you will receive an email from Google Play confirming the cancellation.
Cancel on an Android Phone Open the Google Play Store
Tap the Menu icon
Select Subscriptions
Select HIDIVE (⚠️)
Select Cancel Subscription.
Select Cancel Subscription again to confirm.
Once you've completed the above steps, you will see a brief pop-up stating “Subscription will be canceled,” and you will receive an email from Google Play confirming the cancellation.
Your subscription will stop at the end of the current billing cycle.
⚠️ If you don't see HIDIVE listed as one of your subscriptions, then the Google Play account that you are signed in to is not the one paying for your subscription.
Please repeat the steps above while signed in to the Google Play account that is paying for the HIDIVE subscription.
If you can't locate the Google Play account attached to your HIDIVE subscription please contact Google Play for support below.
