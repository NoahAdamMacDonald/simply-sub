Offline download is an exclusive feature that allows you to save content to your HIDIVE app for later viewing. To access and watch your downloads offline, ensure that you remain signed in to your HIDIVE account. Simply open the HIDIVE app and navigate to your downloads page by following these steps:
For iPhone, iPad, Android, or Fire devices: Tap on the three lines on the top left of the screen > then you will see "Downloads." This is where you will be able to see all your downloads listed.
