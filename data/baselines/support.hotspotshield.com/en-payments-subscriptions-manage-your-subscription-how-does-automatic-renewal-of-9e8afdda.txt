How does automatic renewal of Premium subscriptions work?
Published 12 years ago
· updated 2 months ago
Most subscriptions are set to renew automatically every month, or year depending on the subscription length, on the same date.
Auto-renewals are done for your convenience. There's no need to remember when your subscription ends or when to pay. This prevents any interruption in protection and access.
