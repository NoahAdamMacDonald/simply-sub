How do I get a Premium subscription using PayPal?
Published 12 years ago
· updated 2 months ago
Note: If you choose to purchase your Premium subscription with PayPal, you do not need to fill the credit card or personal information sections of the shopping cart.
* *
Purchasing with PayPal
To purchase a subscription with PayPal:
Go to the Hotspot Shield purchase page.
Choose Paypal as your payment method, then click on the yellow Paypal button.
You will be directed to a Paypal login page. Once there, enter the email address associated with your Paypal account and the password.
Follow the on-screen instructions to complete your purchase.
