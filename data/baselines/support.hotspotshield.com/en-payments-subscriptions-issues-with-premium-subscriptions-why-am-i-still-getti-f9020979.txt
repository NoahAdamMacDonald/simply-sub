Why am I still getting ads/bandwidth limit when I paid for Premium?
Published 12 years ago
· updated 2 months ago
If you purchased a Premium Subscription and still see ads or have a bandwidth limit, then:
Your Payment might not have been linked properly.
You might not be signed in to your premium account on your device.
If your payment was not properly linked, please contact our Support Team by filling out this form.
To add a new device, you need to remove a device from your Premium account. Learn how to remove a device from your account.
