What are the benefits of a Premium subscription?
Published 12 years ago
· updated 2 months ago
Our Premium subscription has additional features compared to the Basic version such as:
4x faster connection speed
Unlimited data bandwidth for streaming HD videos
Connect to over 100 virtual locations
Ability to use the same subscription on up to 10 devices
And also has extras like:
24/7 personal support via chat or email
\*Not available for multi-year subscriptions.
In case you have a free account, you can try a Premium account for free here.
* *
Premium subscriptions
We offer different Premium subscription plans. The subscriptions available to a user and what's available in the subscription will differ from country to country.
All Premium subscriptions will auto-renew at the end of the subscription period.
* *
Purchase a Premium subscription
Before purchasing your Premium Subscription, please make sure you are signed in to your Hotspot Shield account.
You can purchase a Premium subscription from the following places:
- Hotspot Shield Premium website
- Your Hotspot Shield Dashboard
- Hotspot Shield app
To purchase from the Hotspot Shield Premium website:
Go to the order page.
Select the subscription you wish to purchase.
Enter your payment information and submit your order.
To purchase from your Hotspot Shield Dashboard:
Sign in to your Dashboard.
In the Account section, click on Purchase
Select the subscription you wish to purchase.
Enter your payment information and submit your order.
To purchase from the Hotspot Shield Android app (Google Play):
Launch Hotspot Shield on your Android device.
On the home screen, tap the subscription you want.
Tap SUBSCRIBE.
Enter your Google password, and tap CONFIRM.
To purchase from the Hotspot Shield iOS app (iTunes):
Launch Hotspot Shield on your iPhone, iPad, or iTouch.
On the home screen, tap the price of the subscription you want to purchase.
Enter your Apple ID password, and tap OK.
