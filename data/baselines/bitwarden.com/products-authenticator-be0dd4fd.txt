Understanding two-factor authentication
How two-factor authentication works
When you log in to a protected account, you'll enter your password first, then open your Bitwarden Authenticator app to get a 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds.
This multi-factor authentication process ensures that even if your password is compromised, they can't access your account without physical access to your device.
Authenticator app vs. SMS two-factor authentication
While SMS-based 2FA is better than no protection, authenticator apps provide superior security:
No network dependency: Codes generate offline
SIM-swap protection: Codes aren't sent via vulnerable SMS
Faster access: Instant code generation without waiting for texts
International reliability: Works anywhere without roaming issues
Why multi-factor authentication matters
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) and two-factor authentication (2FA) provide critical protection beyond passwords alone. If a cyberattack were to occur , malicious actors could not access your account without the time-based verification code generated by our authenticator app.
Unlike SMS-based two-factor authentication, which can be intercepted, app-based authenticators like Bitwarden generate codes locally on your device, making them significantly more secure for protecting your sensitive accounts.
Best-in-class authenticator app security
Enable multi-factor authentication
Strong passwords aren't enough. Use Bitwarden Authenticator to enable multi-factor authentication across all your online accounts and applications. The Bitwarden Authenticator app is open source and provides enterprise-grade security with the transparency and trust that comes from publicly audited code.
Generate two-factor authentication codes instantly
The Bitwarden Authenticator app generates time-based one-time passwords (TOTP) for any service that supports two-factor authentication. These 30-second rotating codes ensure your identity is verified, keeping your sensitive data protected from unauthorized access even if your password is compromised.
Quickly scan a QR code to add a new authentication code
A simple, intuitive design and straightforward interface makes it easy to start incorporating authentication into your digital life.

Complete security with Bitwarden Password Manager integration
While Bitwarden Authenticator works perfectly as an end-to-end encrypted standalone app, it pairs seamlessly with Bitwarden Password Manager for comprehensive account security. The integration enables access to time-based one-time passwords from either application, Bitwarden Authenticator or Bitwarden Password Manager, reducing the need to manage codes across different apps.
Bitwarden Password Manager also includes built-in TOTP generation as a premium feature, allowing you to manage both passwords and authentication codes in one secure, encrypted vault.
