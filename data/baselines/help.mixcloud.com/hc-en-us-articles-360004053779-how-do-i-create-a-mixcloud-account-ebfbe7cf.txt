How do I create a Mixcloud account?
How you can create a Mixcloud account will depend on the type of device you're using:
If you're on a desktop:
Go to mixcloud.com and click the Sign up button in the top-right corner.
Enter your email address, the username you want to use and a password.
Click Sign Up.
If you're using the app:
Once the app has loaded, click the Sign up button.
Enter the username you'd like to use, your email address and a password.
Click Create My Account.
Please note we recommend not using special characters in your username (e.g. '_' or '@') as usernames with these characters aren't found as easily when searching the platform.
If you need further assistance with creating an account or have any questions, please fill out this form and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
