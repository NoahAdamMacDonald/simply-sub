Containment
Comodo Containment offers comprehensive protection against today’s sophisticated threats. It categorizes files into three types: known good, known bad, and unknown. Known good files are valid and safe, known bad files are recognized threats that are dealt with accordingly, and unknown files are isolated in a secure environment until their safety is determined. This ensures that your system remains secure without sacrificing performance or usability.
