How do I delete my account?
We’re sorry to see you go — but if you're sure, here's how to delete your Tubi account:
Option 1: Delete from your account settings
Sign in to your Tubi account.
Click or tap your name in the top-right corner of the homepage.
Select Account Settings.
Scroll to the bottom and click Delete My Account.
Click Delete My Account again to confirm.
Enter your password, then click Delete to finish.
🔐 Don’t have a password?
If you signed up with Facebook, Google, or Apple ID, you’ll need to create a password using the same email address. That way, you can complete the steps above.
Option 2: Use our secure deletion request form
If you’re unable to delete your account through the settings page, please use this secure request form. We’ll ask for some information to verify your identity and handle the request on your behalf. Please allow a few weeks for processing.
Special circumstances
If your request is related to fraud or the death of a loved one, you'll need to connect with our Customer Experience team for help
