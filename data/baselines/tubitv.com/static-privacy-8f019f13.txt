6. CHILDREN’S PRIVACY
This Section explains how Tubi collects, uses, and discloses personal information, as defined by the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (“COPPA”) and other applicable laws, of users under the age of 13 (or the age set by applicable laws) that is collected through the Tubi Services.
What areas of Tubi can I use to manage Tubi Services for my kids?
At Tubi, we offer two key features to manage the experience for children: Tubi Kids Accounts and Tubi Kids Mode.
A Tubi Kids Account is an account linked to your Tubi account. As a parent or guardian, you create and manage the Tubi Kids Account. Each Tubi Kids Account you create has its own settings that you, the adult, control. Tubi Kids Mode is a quick way for you to switch the entire Tubi experience, temporarily, to a kid-friendly viewing environment. For more information, please see this Help Center article.
What personal information does Tubi collect when my child uses Tubi?
Tubi and its service providers may collect:
Tubi Kids Account Name: We ask you, the parent or guardian, to name your Tubi accounts, including Tubi Kids Accounts, so that you can easily select between your accounts, but you have full creative control.
Parental Controls and Settings: If you change account settings or choose to select an age rating for the Tubi content that can be viewed, we will remember those settings, and associate them with your Tubi account(s), including the linked Tubi Kids Account(s).
Online Identifiers and Activity: Whenever someone uses an area of Tubi intended for children, including Tubi Kids Mode and Tubi Kids Accounts, your device automatically sends Tubi the technical information needed to maintain a network connection and use the Tubi Services. We collect that technical information from the device(s) needed to communicate with Tubi, including an IP address or a device identifier. We use these online identifiers to support the operations of the Tubi Services. For example, we use this information to remember a show that the user saves to a watchlist.
How does Tubi use this information?
We use this information to provide the Tubi Services, including:
To operate and improve the Tubi Services.
To authenticate users of, personalize the content on, maintain or analyze the functioning of the Tubi Services.
To perform network communications.
To show contextual ads, to limit how many times a user sees the same ad, and to measure performance.
To enable the Parental Controls you set, the user’s viewing history, and their watchlist.
To secure the Tubi Services, especially users accessing Tubi Kids Mode and Tubi Kids Account.
To ensure legal or regulatory compliance.
We have implemented appropriate administrative, technical, and organizational measures so that persistent identifiers collected from children (for example, in Tubi Kids Mode and Tubi Kids Accounts) are not used or disclosed except for the purposes of internal operations specified in this Section 6 of the Privacy Policy.
How does Tubi disclose this information?
We may disclose personal information that we collect to certain service providers and Tubi Affiliates that perform internal operations functions for Tubi.
We may also be required to disclose personal information as described in Section 3, above, under “to protect the rights of Tubi and others,” and “to complete a merger or sale of assets or other corporate transaction.”
We do not enable children to make personal information publicly available through the Tubi Services.
Creating a Tubi Kids Account
Only a parent or guardian may create a Tubi Kids Account and it is permanently linked to their adult’s Tubi account. By creating a Tubi Kids Account, you are confirming you are the parent or legal guardian of any child who will use it. When you create a Tubi Kids Account linked to your Tubi account, you will first need to securely log in to your Tubi account to approve the creation of a linked Tubi Kids Account, as the parent or legal guardian. You will also receive an email notification confirming the account’s creation.
What are my rights as a parent or legal guardian?
As a parent or legal guardian, you have the right to:
Review the personal information Tubi collected from your child.
Request that we delete this information.
Ask us to stop further collection or use of information about your child.
You can exercise these rights at any time by contacting us as outlined in Section 8. We will try to comply with your request as soon as reasonably practicable and in accordance with the law.
How long does Tubi retain this information?
We will retain an Account Name and Parental Controls and Settings associated with a Tubi Kids Account in order to administer the account for as long as the account is active, unless a longer retention period is required or allowed by applicable law. We may retain Online Identifiers and Activity information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the internal operations purposes specified in this Section 6, unless a longer retention period is required or allowed by applicable law.
If you have questions, please contact us by the methods provided below in Section 8.
