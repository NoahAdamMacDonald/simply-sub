Your Privacy Choices
Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information/Opt Out of Targeted Advertising/Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to opt out of the “Sale or Sharing” of your personal information and/or to opt out of targeted advertising. We and our advertising partners use certain personal information to deliver online advertisements to you on your devices tailored to your interests. This may be considered “Selling or Sharing” under California law and “Targeted Advertising” under Virginia and other state privacy laws.
If you are a California resident and wish to opt out of the “Sale or Sharing” of your personal information in connection with targeted advertising on Tubi’s apps, please make a request through the form at https://tubitv.com/privacy/opt-out.
If you are a Virginia consumer and wish to opt out of “Targeted Advertising” on Tubi’s apps, please make a request through the form at https://tubitv.com/privacy/opt-out.
You will need to follow these opt-out instructions for each of your devices.
Tubi may use certain types of personal information that may be considered “sensitive” under applicable laws to provide the services requested and to show you relevant ads. If you are a California resident, you have the right to request that Tubi limit the use of your “Sensitive Personal Information” on Tubi’s apps. You may make a request to limit the use of this personal information through the form at https://tubitv.com/privacy/sensitive.
Please note that opting out does not mean that you will stop seeing all ads. And after opting out of the “Sale or Sharing” of your personal information, opting out of “Targeted Advertising” as described above, or limiting the use of your sensitive personal information, you may still see interest-based ads. Because interest-based advertising relies on cookies and other similar technologies, opting out needs to be controlled at the device and/or browser level. To learn more about interest-based advertising across other websites and apps, and your additional choices for opting out of interest-based advertising, please visit http://optout.aboutads.info/#/ or http://optout.networkadvertising.org/#.
To learn more about our information practices, please see our Privacy Policy at https://www.tubitv.com/static/privacy.
