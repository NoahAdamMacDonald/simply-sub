How do I export all my data? How can I export my chats?
character.ai
Updated July 10, 2025 23:47
If you would like to export your data please go to your profile settings and follow the steps below for Web and Mobile App:
Web
Navigate to your Profile.
Click on Profile Settings.
Go to Account.
Manage Account & Data.
Select Export data
Mobile App
Open your Profile.
Look for the settings icon.
Select Export my data.
