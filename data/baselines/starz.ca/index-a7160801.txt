FAQs
Need help? We’re here to assist you. You can contact us on Facebook or Twitter @StarzCA
What is STARZ?
Distributed by Lionsgate in Canada, STARZ is the home in Canada for all-new, library titles, and past seasons of STARZ Original series, and your all-time favourite classic Hollywood movies.
How can I get STARZ?
STARZ is available through most TV service providers in Canada, direct to consumer as an add-on to Crave,
through Amazon Prime Video
and Apple Channels.
My TV Provider doesn’t have STARZ available, how do I get access to STARZ?
STARZ is available directly to consumers as an add-on to a Crave subscription. With the STARZ add-on you get all of the latest seasons and series from STARZ as well as hundreds of Hollywood movie favourites. A Crave subscription without the STARZ add-on contains a small sample of great STARZ originals. STARZ is also available through Amazon Prime Video Channels.
How much does STARZ cost?
The STARZ add-on to Crave is an additional $7.99 per month, plus tax to your Crave subscription and is billed as part of your Crave subscription when signing up direct to consumer. Pricing from TV providers may vary. Contact your TV Provider for details. STARZ from Amazon Prime Video Channels is $7.99 per month, plus tax.
Can I watch STARZ content on the go?
Yes. When subscribing to STARZ from a TV provider or as an add-on to Crave, STARZ content can be accessed through the Crave app or your TV service provider app (where applicable). For how to watch STARZ on the go with a subscription from Amazon Prime Video Channels please visit Amazon.ca.
Is STARZ available On Demand?
Yes. When subscribing to STARZ from a TV provider or as an add-on to Crave, STARZ content can be accessed on demand through the Crave app and with participating TV service providers. For how to watch STARZ on demand with a subscription from Amazon Prime Video Channels please visit Amazon.ca.
I have a TV provider and have a subscription to Crave, do I get STARZ?
STARZ packaging from TV providers may vary and may or may not be packaged with Crave. Contact your TV provider for details on your current packaging. A Crave subscription without STARZ will give you access to a sample of STARZ Original Series. If you do not currently have a STARZ subscription from your TV provider you can add the STARZ to get access to all of the latest seasons and series from STARZ as well as even more Hollywood movies.
I have a subscription to STARZ from Amazon Prime Video Channels, can I watch on Crave?
No. If you have subscribed to STARZ through Amazon Prime Video Channels you are not able to watch STARZ content on the Crave website or apps.
