Sign In and Accounts
Sign in to Ideogram with Google, Apple, Microsoft, SSO or email. Learn which accounts get free credits, the password rules and how to fix sign-in errors. Copy page

Use this page to sign in or sign up for Ideogram, see which accounts get free credits, and reset a forgotten password.
Ways to sign in
Sign in or sign up
Go to ideogram.ai.
Select Sign in or Get started.
On Log in or sign up, select one of the options below.
Continue with Google
Continue with Apple
Continue with Microsoft
Continue with SSO, if your company uses single sign-on. See the steps below.
Continue with email, with an email address and password. The same form signs you in or creates an account, depending on whether the email is new.
When you come back, Ideogram may show only the method you used last. Select Use another account to see the rest.
Sign in with email
Choose Continue with email, enter your Email and Password, then select Continue with email in the form. The button becomes available when the address has a valid email format and the password has at least six characters. Use the same email/password pair when you return.
If the form remembers an email address, check that it is the account you intend to use. Use another account returns to the other sign-in choices; it does not merge accounts.
Sign in with SSO
On Log in or sign up, select Continue with SSO.
Enter your Work email.
Click Continue with SSO.
If your company requires SSO, other methods are refused and the SSO option opens with your email filled in.
No SSO is configured for your organization: check the work email and ask your organization's administrator whether SSO has been set up for that domain.
Something went wrong checking SSO: retry the lookup. This is different from a message saying SSO has not been configured.
Your organization requires signing in with SSO: continue in the SSO panel with the work email shown, rather than repeatedly trying a different provider.
Free credits
Sign-in method Free credits
Google or Apple 30 a week
Microsoft, or email and password None, unless you later link a Google or Apple sign-in that uses the same verified email
With a Microsoft or email account, you need to buy credits or subscribe to generate. If you want to try Ideogram for free first, sign up with Google or Apple.
Your first sign-in
On Tell us a bit about you., enter a Username. Ideogram suggests one from the part of your email before the @.
Add a Full name (optional) if you want one.
Select Continue.
Under What best describes you?, pick any roles that fit and select Continue, or select Skip.
On Choose a plan that's right for you, pick a plan or select Skip for now to stay on the free plan.
Then you land on Home. The Quick Start takes it from there.
Username rules
Your username is your handle on Ideogram. It must:
be 3 to 16 characters long
use only letters, numbers and underscores
not match anyone else's, ignoring upper and lower case
not contain restricted words
Warning You can't change your username once it's set, and support can't change it either. You can change your display name in Account Settings.
One account per sign-in method
Each sign-in method makes its own account, even if the email address is the same. You can't link methods, change your email or add a password, and support can't merge accounts made with different methods.
If you see Account exists with a different sign-in method, that email already has an account made another way. Sign in with the method you first used. If you can't remember it, try each one, or contact [email protected].
If you deleted an account, wait 48 hours before you sign up again with the same email.
Forgot your password
This applies to email accounts only.
Enter your email in the sign-in form.
Select Forgot password?
Open the reset link Ideogram sends to your inbox and set a new password.
The email field must contain a valid address before Forgot password? can send a reset link. A successful request shows Check your inbox and the address it was sent to. Check that address before making another request.
Accounts made with Google, Apple, Microsoft or SSO have no Ideogram password. Reset it with that provider or your organization's sign-in system instead.
If email sign-in fails
Incorrect email or password: check both fields and confirm that this account uses email sign-in. Use Forgot password? for an email account rather than creating another account with a different provider.
Too many attempts: wait a moment before trying again, as the message requests.
This account has been disabled: contact support with the account's email and the message you see.
