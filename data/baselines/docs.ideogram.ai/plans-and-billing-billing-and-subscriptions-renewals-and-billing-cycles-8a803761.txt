Renewals and Billing Cycles
Ideogram subscriptions auto-renew monthly or yearly. Find your renewal date, see what happens to your credits and stop a renewal. Copy page

Ideogram subscriptions renew automatically at the billing frequency you chose when subscribing: monthly or annual. Your payment method is charged on each renewal date until you cancel.
Finding your next renewal date
Click your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar and select Wallet & Billing. The plan card shows Renews on and the date.
Renews on shows your next billing date.
The billing portal shows it too. Click Manage plan & billing in the plan card, and the date appears under your current subscription.
Your next billing date is shown under your current subscription.
Credits on renewal
Your monthly credits reset each month on your renewal day, and unused monthly credits don't carry over. Yearly plans work the same way: you pay once a year, but credits arrive each month. Credits you bought separately aren't affected. See Plans and Credits.
Why did my annual plan's credits refresh without a renewal charge?
An annual plan has two schedules: monthly credit refreshes and the yearly subscription renewal. A monthly allowance refresh doesn't start another paid year. Use Renews on for the next subscription charge date, and your credit balance for the credits you have left.
For annual plans started near the end of a month, a refresh is due on the last day of a shorter month when the original day doesn't exist. For example, a plan started on January 31 has its next refresh due at February's month end, then on March 31. This describes the monthly credit schedule, not a new annual charge.
Which plan will I have at the next renewal?
If you've scheduled a downgrade, open Settings → Plans. The pending-change banner names the destination plan and switch date. Your current plan stays active until that change takes effect. Use Keep [plan name] if you want to undo it; see Undo a pending downgrade.
Avoiding an unwanted renewal
Cancel at least 5 days before your renewal date. Per the Terms of Service, cancellations must be made five days ahead of renewal to take effect for that period. See Cancelling Your Subscription.
Cancelling early does not shorten your access: you keep your plan benefits until the end of the period you already paid for. There is no reason to wait until the last day.
Annual plans renew once a year, which makes the renewal easy to forget. If you don't plan to continue, cancel ahead of time. Ideogram doesn't offer refunds (see Refunds).
Invoices for renewal charges
Every charge has an invoice under Invoice history on Wallet & Billing. See Invoices, Receipts, and Tax Details.
