How do I upgrade from a lower plan to a higher plan?
When you upgrade an existing paid plan mid-cycle, you pay only the time-adjusted
difference — not the full cost of the new plan.

When you downgrade to a plan mid-cycle — for example from annual

If I switch from Annual to Monthly, what happens to the difference of
the
payment I made?
If you switch from an annual to a monthly plan, the remaining balance
of
the annual payment will be added to your account as an invoice credit
balance.
Future monthly subscriptions will automatically deduct from this credit
balance until fully exhausted before charging the card on file.
If you'd instead prefer a refund of this balance, please
get in touch with our support.
Why am I unable to switch plans?
If payment hasn't completed yet, the transaction may show as "pending"
for up to 24 hours. Wait, then try again and select monthly. Once the charge goes through, follow the steps above to switch
to a monthly plan and receive a balance credit for the difference.
If I switch from annual to monthly, what happens to the difference of the
payment I made?
The unused part of your annual payment is refunded automatically to the card
you paid with as soon as you switch. The exceptions listed under
Downgrading your plan apply —
in those cases the amount stays on your account as credit and our support
team can help.
Managing your invoices
Your subscription invoices and billing profile are on the Plans and Billing page. You can download past invoices there, and update the name, address and tax ID that appear on them from your billing profile.
Select your profile icon in the bottom-left corner
Select Plans and Billing
Select Manage billing
Select Wallet in the left-hand sidebar
Under Billing profile, select Edit profile
Update your details
Select Save
Updated invoices are available on the Plans and Billing page, under Invoices. Your payment methods are on the same Wallet page, under Payment methods. For the full walkthrough, see Updating your Runway Invoice.

Understanding your billing cycle
Your billing day is the date you first activated your plan. To find it:
Go to your billing page
Scroll to the Invoices section — your billing day matches the date on your last invoice
Subscription renewal
All subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Monthly plans
renew on your billing day each month. Annual plans renew one year after
initiation — your credits still reset monthly, but you won't be charged
again until the following year.
Monthly credits
Plan credits reset within 24 hours of your billing day. On Max and Team,
up to one month of unused credits rolls over; on Standard and Pro plans,
unused credits
don't carry over.
Purchased credits
Purchased credits never expire. They stay on your account until spent
and aren't affected by your billing cycle. Generations always draw from
monthly plan credits first — you can't manually choose which balance
to use.
Cancelling your plan
You can cancel your plan at any time by downgrading to a Free plan. See the links below for step-by-step instructions based on how you originally subscribed, whether directly through Runway or through the Apple App Store.
Runway Purchases
How do I cancel my plan?
iOS Purchases
Billing for iOS
After cancellation, you keep access to paid features until the end of your current billing cycle. The subscription will not renew.
Note: If you only see Update subscription and Don't cancel subscription, your cancellation is already confirmed. The cancellation date is shown at the top of the Stripe confirmation page.
What happens after cancellation?
You keep access to paid features until the end of your billing cycle.
Your assets and projects stay on your account unless you delete your
account
entirely.
On the Free plan, you can't create new projects if you already have
more
than three.
Purchased credits remain on your account — but your plan level determines
feature availability (e.g., generations on the Free tier include
watermarks
regardless of when credits were purchased).
Requesting a refund
For subscription and payment refunds, see Requesting a refund for your plan or payment.
FAQs
Do credits roll over to the next month?
It depends on your plan. On Standard, Pro and Unlimited, plan credits
reset
on your billing day and don't carry over. On Max and Team, up to one
month
of unused credits rolls over. Purchased credits never expire. See
How do credits work?
for the full breakdown.
Can I subscribe for a set amount of time, like three months?
Not currently. Runway doesn't offer custom subscription durations — you'll
need to cancel manually when you're ready to stop.
Can I change my plan at any time?
Yes. If you upgrade, you pay only the difference for the rest of your billing cycle. If you downgrade, the unused amount of your current plan is refunded to your card. See Downgrading your plan for the few cases where it stays as account credit instead.
What happens to my credits if I upgrade mid-cycle?
Your credits update immediately to match your new plan's allocation,
and
you're charged the prorated difference.
Can I cancel if my renewal payment failed?
Yes. Select Cancel plan on your
billing page,
share
any feedback, then confirm in Stripe. To keep your plan instead, select
Update Payment Details and add a new card. See
How do I cancel my plan?
for the full steps.
I cancelled but I'm still being charged. What happened?
Make sure you completed all cancellation steps, including the final confirmation
in Stripe. If your billing page only shows "Update subscription" and
"Don't
cancel subscription," your cancellation is already confirmed.
