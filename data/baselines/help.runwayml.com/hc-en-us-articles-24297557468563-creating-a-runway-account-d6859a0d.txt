Creating a Runway Account
Table of Contents
To create a Runway account, head to the sign up page.
Signing up with an email address
Enter the email address for the account
Enter a username
Enter a password that is at least 6 characters with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols
Confirm your selected password
On the next screen, enter your first name and last name
After submitting your information, the email you entered will be sent a verification code. You have 1 hour to retrieve the code from the message, and enter it on the following screen
Signing up with Google
Select Sign up with Google
In the pop-up window that appears, select the Google account you want to make your account under
An account will automatically be created under the email associated with your Google account. Your username and account name will also be pulled directly from your Google account's name and email handle
Signing up with Apple
Select Sign up with Apple
In the pop-up window that appears, log into your Apple ID
An account will automatically be created under the email associated with your Apple ID. Your username and account name will also be pulled directly from your Apple ID's name and email handle.
