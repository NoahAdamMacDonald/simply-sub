Batch downloads allow you to simultaneously download multiple files from your Runway account to your computer.
You can batch download from the Assets page.
Select Assets in the left-hand sidebar
Select multiple assets or folders using hot keys
Select a range: select the first asset, then hold SHIFT and select the last asset to include everything in between
Select individual items: hold CTRL (PC) or CMD (Mac) while selecting each asset
In the floating menu at the bottom of the screen, open the Actions dropdown and select Download
Your assets are compressed into a ZIP file and downloaded to your computer.
Tip: If your batch download doesn't work, the attempted selection may be too large. Try working in smaller batches if your download doesn't complete.
