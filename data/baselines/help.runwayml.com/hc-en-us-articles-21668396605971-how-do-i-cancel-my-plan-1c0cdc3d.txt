How do I cancel my plan?
Table of Contents
You can cancel your plan by following the steps below:
Go to your billing page (https://app.runwayml.com/settings/billing)
Click Cancel plan
Provide any feedback, then proceed by selecting Continue to cancel plan
Click Confirm cancellation in Stripe
You'll retain your subscription until the end of your billing cycle after cancellation, but the subscription will not renew. This means you’ll still have access to the paid features until the renewal date.
If the Cancel plan button is inactive on your billing page, this means that your subscription is already scheduled for cancellation at the end of your current billing cycle and your plan will not renew. The cancellation date will be indicated by the Expires on... field.
Cancelling a plan purchased through the iOS App Store
Subscriptions purchased through the iOS App Store can be cancelled by following this tutorial from Apple: View or cancel subscriptions on iPhone
FAQ
Can I cancel my plan if my renewal payment failed?
Yes. If you're getting failed renewal emails, you can cancel from your
billing page without
contacting Support. Select Cancel plan, share any feedback,
then confirm the cancellation in Stripe.
To keep your plan instead, select Update Payment Details
on the same page and add a new card.
I just signed up but don’t want an ongoing monthly subscription. Can I cancel and still use the plan features?
Yes, you can cancel immediately after subscribing and will have access to
the plan features until the end of the billing cycle.
Can I subscribe for a set amount of time, such as three months?
We do not currently offer the ability to schedule a custom subscription duration.
You’d need to cancel the subscription manually once you’re ready to end the
plan.
Will I lose my assets and projects after cancelling?
No. Your assets and projects remain on your account after you cancel your plan, unless you
delete your account
entirely.
Please note you will not be able to create any new projects once on the Free
plan if you have over three projects.
Can I still use my purchased credits after cancelling my subscription?
Yes, purchased credits will remain on your account until spent. It’s important
to note that your current plan level determines the availability
of features, not the plan type at the time the credits were purchased. For
example, any generations made after downgrading to the Basic tier will have
watermarks.
