Plan FAQs
Can I change my Runway plan at any time?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Upgrades are
prorated, and downgrades will apply credits toward your next billing
cycle.
What happens to my credits if I upgrade mid-cycle?
When you upgrade, your credits refresh immediately to match your new
plan's allocation while considering the current cycle's spend, and you'll
be charged the prorated difference. See
How do credits work?
for more information.
Can I add collaborators to my workspace?
Yes, but adding collaborators requires a Team plan or higher. The Team
plan is built with collaboration in mind.
Do credits rollover to the next month?
Yes, rollover up to one month of unused credits on the Max plan and higher.
What happens if I run out of credits and need more generations?
Standard, Pro, Max and Team plans can purchase additional credits at
any
time from the
Plan & Billing page.
Credits cannot be purchased on a Free plan.
If you find yourself purchasing credits often, you might consider upgrading
to a plan that includes more.
