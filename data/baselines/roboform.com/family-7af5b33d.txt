Frequently asked questions
Will family members see each other's passwords?
No. Each member has their own separate RoboForm account.
Can a family member choose to securely share passwords with another member?
Yes. You can opt to securely share an individual password or create a shared folder for your family.

You can upgrade to Family at a pro-rated discount based on the amount of time left on your existing subscription.
