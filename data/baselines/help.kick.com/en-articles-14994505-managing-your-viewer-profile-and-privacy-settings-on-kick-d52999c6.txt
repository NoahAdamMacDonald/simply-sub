Managing your Viewer profile and privacy settings on KICK
Managing your Viewer profile and privacy settings on KICK
Updated over 2 weeks ago
Table of contents
Your KICK profile is how you appear to other Viewers, Chatters, and Streamers. This guide covers how to set up your profile, what other people can see about you, and the tools you have to stay safe and in control.
Updating your profile
Your profile is the first thing people see when they click your username. To customise it:
Click your avatar in the top right corner
Go to your account settings
Open the profile section
From here, you can update:
Profile picture: a small image that appears next to your username
Display name: what other users see when you appear in chat or on a channel. You can change it once every 60 days.
About section: a short bio where you can share a little about yourself
Connected social media: links to your social profiles, if you want to share them
For a step-by-step walkthrough, see How to update your profile.
💡 Think carefully about what you share publicly. Information on your profile is visible to anyone on KICK, including people you do not know.

Your privacy settings
You can manage some preferences from the privacy section of your account settings:
Notification preferences: choose which alerts you receive
Mature content settings: decide whether you want to see 18+ streams
You can change these at any time.
Your subscriber badge is always shown in the chat of channels you subscribe to. It's not currently possible to hide it.
Choosing what to share
A few good habits to protect your privacy on KICK:
Avoid using your real name as your username unless you are comfortable with it being public
Do not share your full name, address, phone number, or other personal details on your profile or in chat
Use a profile picture that does not reveal your location or identity if you want to stay anonymous
If you ever decide to start streaming, these habits become even more important. See Streamer safety tips: how to stream safely on KICK.
Tools to protect yourself
Block: stop a user from interacting with you
Mute: hide a Chatter's messages from your chat view only. Other Viewers still see them
Report: flag users or content that break our Community Guidelines
For how to do each of these, see Blocking, muting, and reporting other users on KICK.
If you are a Streamer, you have a much wider set of controls over your own channel, including chat modes, banned words, timeouts and bans. See How to moderate your KICK chat.
Connected services
You can connect your KICK account to some external services, like the official KICK Discord. When you connect one, you give that service permission to access basic information about your account. Only authorise services you trust.
To review or remove a connection:
Open your account settings
Find the connections section
Remove any service you no longer want connected
💡 If you see a connection you do not recognise, remove it immediately and change your password. It could mean someone has accessed your account.
Account security
Privacy also depends on keeping your account secure:
Use a strong, unique password that you do not reuse on other sites
Enable Two-Factor Authentication to stop unauthorised access
Watch out for phishing: KICK will never ask for your password by email or chat

Deleting your account
If you no longer want a KICK account, you can request account deletion. This permanently removes:
Your profile and personal information
Your following list
Your messages, clips, and other content
Your subscription history
Before deleting:
Cancel any active subscriptions
Download any clips or content you want to keep
If you are a Streamer with a payout balance, withdraw it first
To request deletion, contact our Support Team (details below).
