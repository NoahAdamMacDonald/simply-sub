How do I sign up for Keeper?
Creating a Keeper account takes less than 2 minutes.
Signing up for an Individual (Personal) plan: Go to the Web Vault or open the mobile app, enter your email address, and follow the prompts. You'll be asked to create a Master Password, this is the only password you need to remember, so choose something strong that you'll remember, and don't forget it.
Signing up for a Family Plan: Purchase the Family Plan from keepersecurity.com, which creates the primary account the same way as above. Once you're signed up, you can invite up to 4 additional family members from your Account Dropdown Menu, see "How do I invite members to Family Plan?" for the exact steps.
Signing up on mobile: The mobile app (iOS or Android) walks you through the same steps as the Web Vault, just tap to enter your email and create your Master Password when prompted.
