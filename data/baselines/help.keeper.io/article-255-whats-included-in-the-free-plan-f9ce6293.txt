What's included in the free plan?
After the 30-day free trial, you can still access your data in the free version on the same mobile device without any paid features. Support will be limited to our FAQ, Troubleshooting Guide, and User Guides for assistance. If you wish to get full data protection, please upgrade to Keeper Unlimited or Keeper Family Plan for live chat, email, or phone support.
