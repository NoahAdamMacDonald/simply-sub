Export or Import Tokens in the Authy app Not Supported
Objective
In order to maintain security for our users, the Authy application does not allow importing or exporting 2FA account tokens.
The alternative to import and export of tokens is to follow this process.
Product
Authy
Procedure
Login to the desired online account with your existing 2FA token.
Disable 2FA in the app's site.
Re-enable 2FA again in the app's site.
Scan the QR code, optionally write the Authentication Key, this time on the desired 2FA App.
