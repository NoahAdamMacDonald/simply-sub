Account and Billing
Account Settings
How do I subscribe directly to TSN?
▼
You can subscribe directly at tsn.ca/subscribe or via in-app purchase on the TSN app. Once signed up, you can stream on your computer, mobile device, or smart TV. (Note: TSN does not provide refunds.).
Do I need a new TSN account if I log in through a TV provider?
▼
No. Keep using your current TV provider login credentials. Simply select Sign In and click the I am a TV Subscriber tab to log in.
How do I update my billing information?
▼
You can update your payment details anytime through the Account Management portal to avoid service interruptions. If your credit card issuer supports automatic updates (ABU/VAU), your details might update automatically.
How do I change my TSN subscription billed through Bell?
▼
Log in to your MyBell account, navigate to Manage my Subscriptions, and make your changes there. For extra help, visit the Bell Subscriptions Support page.
Where do I get support for a Bell-billed TSN subscription?
▼
Please visit the Bell Subscriptions Support page directly for all billing and account help.
Cancellation & Refunds
How do I cancel my TSN subscription?
▼
Log into your Account here.
Go to Manage Subscription.
Click on the Subscriptions tab.
Scroll down and select Cancel Subscription.
Enter the reason for cancellation and click Yes, Cancel. You will receive a confirmation email.
You will continue to enjoy TSN until the end of the billing period in which you cancel. If you have cancelled TSN, your credit card will not be charged on the next billing date.
If you pay for your subscription by in-app purchase, you can cancel directly with the provider:
If subscribed through Google, click here.
If subscribed through Apple, click here.
If subscribed through Roku, click here.
Can I get a refund if I cancel my TSN subscription?
▼
No, TSN does not offer refunds. However, you will keep full access to TSN until the end of your current billing cycle.
How do I cancel my bundle subscription?
▼
To cancel your bundle subscription:
Visit your account here.
Select Subscriptions.
Select "Manage" next to your bundle.
From the Manage Plan page, select "Change or Cancel Plan".
Select Cancel Plan under your bundle.
Follow the steps until you see the confirmation page.
Note: Cancelling your Crave subscription will also cancel your add-on subscriptions.
How do I cancel only one of my bundled subscriptions?
▼
To cancel one of your bundled subscriptions:
Visit your account here.
Select Subscriptions.
Select "Manage" next to your bundle.
From the Manage Plan page, select "Change or Cancel Plan".
Select the standalone monthly plan that you would like to keep.
Follow the steps until you see the confirmation page.
Devices and Platforms
General Support
What devices and platforms support TSN?
▼
TSN content is available on the TSN app or on TSN.ca (as applicable) on the following devices and platforms:
iPad and iPhone running iOS 16+ with AirPlay
Android tablets and smartphones running version 8.0+
Chromecast
Samsung Smart TV 2019 and above
Apple TV, tvOS 16+
LG Smart TV, 2020 and later
Roku TV and streaming players
Amazon Fire TV Stick
Android TV
Xbox
Hisense & Toshiba Smart TVs using VIDAA U6+ OS
Can I watch TSN on multiple devices at once?
▼
Yes. You can stream on up to two devices at the same time. If you subscribe through a TV provider, watching on your household TV box does not count toward this limit.
How do I sign in and activate my TSN account on my Connected TV?
▼
Launch the TSN app on your device and select any video to play or go to the profile icon on the top right and select the Sign In button.
Go to www.tsn.ca/activate on your PC or mobile device and enter the activation code that has been displayed on your television.
Choose the "New subscriber" or "Already a subscriber" sign-in option as applicable.
Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the subscription or sign-in process.
Once complete, you should see a 'Success!' message on your browser.
Return to your television and begin enjoying TSN!
Do I need to disable my firewall or antivirus to watch TSN.ca?
▼
Usually, no. TSN uses standard, secure web protocols. However, if your security software is blocking the site, configure your firewall or antivirus to whitelist TSN.ca. For immediate help, call us at 1-833-TSN-HELP (876-4357).
Does streaming TSN use my internet or cellular data?
▼
Yes. Streaming TSN on Wi-Fi counts against your home internet data allowance. Streaming over cellular networks (3G/4G/5G) will consume your mobile data plan.
Plans and Bundles
About Plans
What subscription plans does TSN offer?
▼
TSN offers flexible Monthly and Annual plans. To view our current prices, bundles, and latest promotions, please visit tsn.ca/subscribe.
How do I subscribe to TSN Premium?
▼
You can purchase a TSN Premium subscription directly at tsn.ca/subscribe or via your device's app store. Premium grants you full access to all feeds, on-demand content, and NFL RedZone on your computer, mobile device, or TV.
I don’t live in Canada. Can I subscribe to TSN?
▼
No. TSN is only available for subscription and use within Canada due to licensing agreements.
About Bundles
What streaming bundles are available?
▼
You can bundle Crave, TSN, and RDS together to enjoy premium entertainment and sports while saving money compared to purchasing them separately. See prices and sign up at tsn.ca/subscribe.
How do I switch to a bundle if I already have TSN or Crave?
▼
Log in to your account at tsn.ca/subscribe. You will see all eligible bundle options available to upgrade your existing service.
How do I buy a bundle if I'm a new user?
▼
Visit tsn.ca/subscribe, choose your preferred bundle, and follow the prompts to create your new Bell Media account.
Can I get a bundle if I pay through a third-party TV or mobile provider?
▼
No. Bundles are currently only available when subscribing directly through TSN. If you pay through a TV, mobile, or app store provider, contact them directly to see what promotions they offer.
Can I upgrade to a bundle if I have an Annual Plan?
▼
No. Bundles are only available on monthly subscription cycles. If you have an annual plan, you can switch to a monthly bundle once your current annual term expires.
Will I receive a refund for my current plan if I purchase a bundle?
▼
Yes, you will be refunded for the unused duration of your billing cycle on your existing subscriptions at the time of your bundle purchase. You will stay on the same billing cycle if you purchase a new bundle and only pay a prorated amount of the bundle from the date of purchase to your usual billing date.

Schedules & Programming
Where can I find the TSN schedule?
▼
You can view up-to-the-minute listings for all TSN feeds on our Live Schedule.
Why is the online schedule different from my TV guide?
▼
Our online schedule is updated automatically in real time and is always the most accurate source for program changes.
What NHL hockey airs on TSN?
▼
TSN owns regional broadcast rights to a package of Winnipeg Jets, Toronto Maple Leafs, Ottawa Senators, and Montreal Canadiens regular season games. These games are aired within each team’s designated broadcast region, as defined by the NHL.
For regional Winnipeg Jets games, fans must live in the Jets’ designated broadcast region as defined by the NHL, which includes Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Nunavut, the Northwest Territories, and parts of Northwestern Ontario.
For regional Toronto Maple Leafs games, fans must live in the Leafs’ designated broadcast region as defined by the NHL, which includes Ontario (excluding the Ottawa Valley region).
For regional Ottawa Senators games, fans must live in the Sens’ designated broadcast region as defined by the NHL, which includes Eastern Ontario, Québec, and Atlantic Canada.
For regional Montreal Canadiens games, fans must live in the Habs’ designated broadcast region as defined by the NHL, which includes Québec and Atlantic Canada.
If you are receiving a blackout message during a Jets/Leafs/Sens/Habs game on TSN, then you live outside the teams’ respective territory, as defined by the NHL.
Is all TV programming available on demand?
▼
No. Due to broadcasting rights, only select events and matches are available to watch on replay. View all active listings on our Games On Demand page.
Does TSN include NFL RedZone?
▼
Yes. NFL RedZone is included at no extra cost with any active TSN Premium subscription.

Location and Language
Do regional NHL blackouts apply to streaming?
▼
Yes. NHL broadcast rules require regional blackouts to be enforced on all streaming devices, just like traditional cable TV. For more information about blackouts during NHL games, please contact the NHL.
Can I watch TSN while traveling outside of Canada?
▼
No. TSN is geo-restricted and can only be streamed when you are physically located within Canada.
Does TSN support closed captioning?
▼
Yes. Closed captioning is available on iOS, Android, Apple TV, Samsung TV, and all supported TV provider set-top boxes.
Troubleshooting
Account Issues
Why can't I sign in to my account?
▼
Your support path depends on how you pay for TSN:
Subscribed directly? Contact us at 1-833-TSN-HELP (876-4357), tweet @TSNHelp, or open our live chat.
Subscribed through a TV provider? Please reach out directly to your provider's support team to reset your login.
I forgot my username or password. What should I do?
▼
Don’t worry, it happens to the best of us!
Forgot Password? Select Forgot Password on the TSN sign-in screen to trigger a reset email.
Forgot Username/Email? (This is the email address you signed up with). If you can't remember it, call us at 1-833-TSN-HELP (876-4357) for quick recovery assistance.
Playback & App Issues
I’ve downloaded the TSN app but it won’t open or it keeps crashing. What should I do?
▼
There can be multiple reasons why the app won’t open or keeps crashing. Try the following steps:
Check your internet connection
Make sure your TSN app is up to date
Force close and re-open the TSN app
Restart your device
Delete and reinstall the TSN app
If you continue to experience difficulty, please contact us at 1-833-TSN-HELP (876-4357), tweet @TSNHelp, or chat with us.
What should I do if my TV activation code is missing?
▼
Select Cancel on your TV screen to go back, then click Sign In again to generate a new 6-digit activation code.
What should I do if the sign-in page won't load?
▼
You can access the browser sign-in screen directly at account.bellmedia.ca. If you are on an app, try hard-closing the TSN app and restarting your device to clear cached errors.
To hard-close the TSN app on an iOS device, press the Home button twice, then swipe upwards on the TSN App to close it. On Android, use the Android menu button on the bottom of the screen and swipe the TSN app away to close it.
Why does my stream keep buffering, freezing, or showing errors?
▼
This is typically caused by a temporary slow down in internet speed. Try resetting your Wi-Fi router. If this only happens on one specific video, contact us at 1-833-TSN-HELP (876-4357) to report a video playback issue.
There is an issue with my television broadcast. Who do I contact?
▼
If you are experiencing technical issues with your broadcast, please contact your television provider.
Blackouts Troubleshooting
I am experiencing a blackout incorrectly
▼
If you believe you are experiencing a blackout incorrectly visit the Blackout Checker Tool. You will need to provide your Postal Code to check eligibility.
If the Blackout Checker indicates you should be able to watch the content, click here to determine your current public IP address. Ensure that your IP address's reported location ("My IP Location") accurately reflects your current physical location and corresponding postal code.
If not, please follow these troubleshooting steps:
If you are using a Virtual Private Network (VPN), disable it and try again. VPNs can mask your true location, leading to incorrect blackout enforcement.
If your "My IP Location" does not match your actual physical location, please contact us here with the following information: your public IP address, your current postal code, the team you're trying to watch and your internet service provider.
Customers using Satellite Internet providers (e.g., Xplore, Starlink) may experience more frequent blackouts. We strongly recommend that customers contact their Internet Service Provider directly to request that your geolocation records be updated.
As a workaround, you can often watch games on mobile devices. Ensure that Location Services on the mobile device are enabled and set to "While Using the App" or "Always" to assist with accurate location detection. Depending on your devices, you may be able to cast the content from your mobile device to a larger screen.
Why does TSN say I'm outside Canada when I'm not?
▼
This happens if your internet connection masks your true location. Try these quick fixes:
Turn off your VPN: Virtual Private Networks will route your traffic out of the country and block your access.
Verify your IP address: Go to whatismyip.com. If your reported IP location is outside Canada, contact your Internet Service Provider to update your geolocation records.
Blackouts
Understanding NHL Blackouts
What are Blackouts?
▼
TSN holds regional broadcast rights for select Winnipeg Jets, Toronto Maple Leafs, Ottawa Senators, and Montreal Canadiens regular season games. These games are aired within each team’s designated broadcast region, as defined by the NHL. Regional blackouts are enforced to comply with these NHL broadcasting agreements.
Why am I getting a blackout message for a live game?
▼
This means you are currently outside your team's NHL-defined local broadcast territory. If you believe your physical postal code should have access, see our Troubleshooting Guide above or check your eligibility via the NHL Blackout Checker Tool.
Types of Blackouts and Scenarios
NHL Regional Blackouts
▼
These blackouts occur when a game is broadcast in one region but is restricted in another due to NHL regional broadcast rights. You will typically see a "Global blackout" image on the app. For example: A Toronto resident is eligible to watch Toronto Maple Leafs games when they are in the Toronto broadcast region. However, if that same individual travels to Vancouver, they will be subject to a regional blackout for a Maple Leaf’s game because Vancouver falls outside the designated broadcast region for that specific game.
Global Blackouts
▼
These blackouts apply to all users and occur when TSN does not possess the rights to broadcast a particular program or event, regardless of location. This will appear on all platforms. For example: A special event or a game not covered by TSN's broadcast rights will be blacked out for everyone.
Geo-Restrictions (Outside Canada)
▼
This category primarily addresses situations where a user is attempting to access content from outside of Canada, as TSN's broadcast rights are generally limited to Canada. You may see the message: "Sorry, the video you have selected is not available for viewing this region." This message can sometimes be similar to regional blackout messages, so it's important to consider your physical location. For example: If you are physically located outside of Canada, you will encounter geo-restrictions.
