Frequently Asked Questions
I currently access TSN using my TV provider username and password. Do I need to create a new TSN account?
The Bell Media account has replaced individual brand accounts (e.g. TSN, Crave, CTV), providing a single, unified account for all brands.
If you currently access TSN through a TV provider, sign in as you usually do. After you select "Sign In", click on "Sign in Through TV Provider" and follow the steps. You will be prompted to link your existing TV provider account to a new Bell Media account. Linking helps us to improve your watching experience. This one-time set-up is quick and your existing plan and account settings will be carried over.
Next time you sign in, you can use the credentials for your new Bell Media account.
What are regional blackouts?
These blackouts occur when a game is broadcast in one region but is restricted in another due to NHL regional broadcast rights. You will typically see a "global blackout" image on the app. For example: A Toronto resident is eligible to watch Toronto Maple Leafs games when they are in the Toronto broadcast region. However, if that same individual travels to Vancouver and signs into their TSN account, they will be subject to a regional blackout for a Maple Leafs game because Vancouver falls outside the designated broadcast region for that specific game.
Can I watch TSN on more than one device at a time?
Yes, you can stream TSN concurrently on up to 2 devices.
I don’t live in Canada. Can I subscribe to TSN?
Unfortunately not. TSN is only available for subscription and use within Canada due to licensing agreements.
