Back to list What data do you acquire?
We care about the privacy of our users. Therefore, we’ve removed all analytics from our app. However, we’ve decided to stick with Crashlytics (anonymous crash log from Google) – this tool allows us to react instantly if there are any major issues with our app. But, as we stated before, we value our users’ privacy, so there’s an easy option for an opt-out from crash logs with one toggle in the app in the ‘About’ section of the Settings!
Additionally, in case of problems with the application, we may ask you to send anonymous logs regarding the operation of the application. Then it is up to you to decide whether you will pass them on to us.
However, we never send or collect your keys, tokens, passwords, PINs, or other private and personal data.
