Back to list How do I cancel the subscription?
On iOS:
Go to System Settings
Tap on your Account
Tap Subscriptions
Select the 2FAS Pass subscription
Cancel it
On Android:
Go to Subscriptions in Google Play
Select the 2FAS Pass subscription
Cancel it
