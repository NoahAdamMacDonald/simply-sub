Subscribing to discovery+ through TELUS
16 September 2026 14:20
Updated
TELUS & Koodo Customers
Your discovery+ subscription including billing will be managed through TELUS and Koodo. There will be no change to your subscription price or entertainment. Please refer to our discovery+ support pages at TELUS or Koodo for more information regarding your service.
TELUS: https://www.telus.com/en/support/article/discovery-plus-subscriptions-through-telus
Koodo: https://www.koodomobile.com/en/help/discoveryplussupport
To activate your subscription to discovery+ through TELUS:
You should have received an email from TELUS with directions on how to transfer your subscription to discovery+ from the old app to the new discovery+ provided by Rogers Sports & Media.
Please reference that email for step-by-step instructions. If you need additional support, please reach out to TELUS customer support directly.
