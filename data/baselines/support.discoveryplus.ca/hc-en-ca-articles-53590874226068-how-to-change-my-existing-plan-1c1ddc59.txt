How to change my existing plan?
15 September 2026 15:22
Updated
Your plan can only be changed through the platform where you originally purchased your subscription.
If you subscribed through our website, you must manage and change your plan in Account Settings on the website.
If you subscribed through an app store (such as the Apple App Store or Google Play), you must manage and change your plan through that same app store.
