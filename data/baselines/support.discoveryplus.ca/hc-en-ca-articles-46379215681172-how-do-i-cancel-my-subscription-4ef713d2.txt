How do I cancel my subscription?
23 April 2026 20:10
Updated
To cancel your subscription, log in to your account using a web browser. From the top-right menu, go to Account > Billing > Cancel Subscription. If you subscribed through the iOS App Store or Google Play Store, you’ll need to cancel your subscription through the same platform you used to sign up.
iOS (Apple App Store): Go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, then select your subscription and tap Cancel
Android (Google Play): Open the Google Play Store, tap your profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, then select your subscription and tap Cancel
