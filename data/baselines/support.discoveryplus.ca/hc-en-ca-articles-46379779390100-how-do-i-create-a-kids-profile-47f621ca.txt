How do I create a kids profile?
09 September 2026 20:09
Updated
You can create a Kids Profile to limit viewing to age-appropriate content for children 12 and under. Kids Profiles automatically filter the catalog and only show titles suitable for younger audiences.
To create a Kids Profile:
Select your profile icon and choose Manage Profiles.
Select Add Profile.
Choose the Kids profile type.
Enter a profile name and select Save.
Once created, the Kids Profile will only display child-friendly content. You can switch between profiles anytime from the profile menu.
