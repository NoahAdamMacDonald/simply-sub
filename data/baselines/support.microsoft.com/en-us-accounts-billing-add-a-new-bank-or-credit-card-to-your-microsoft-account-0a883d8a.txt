How can I add a mobile phone as payment method?
You can add your credit card, debit card, bank account, PayPal account, or mobile phone as a new payment method. Select 'Personal account' or 'Work account' tab below for your account type.
Personal account
Work account
Note
To add a Microsoft gift card to your account, go to redeem.microsoft.com. Learn more about how to Redeem a gift card or code to your Microsoft account.
If you want to edit an existing payment method, read Update a Microsoft account payment method.
Not all payment methods are supported in all regions.
How to add a new payment method to your Microsoft personal account
To add a new credit card, bank card or other payment method to your Microsoft account, follow the steps below:
Select Sign in below to go to the Payment options page of your Microsoft account dashboard.
Sign in
Add the new details by selecting
Add a new payment method.
Note
Make sure you enter all details correctly. If your card is not accepted, try adding the postal code with and without spaces.
The payment method will show up as an option during the checkout step for your next online purchase.
Add a new payment method for a different country
To add a payment method for another country, you must change your country before adding the card.
Important
We recommend you change your region only if you're moving to a new country or region for a long time. This is important because items you get in one region might not work in another. Learn more.
Change your account's country or region first, by selecting Your info then Edit profile info.
Sign in
Once updated, go to the Payment options page of your Microsoft account dashboard.
Add the new details by selecting
Add a new payment method.
Add a mobile phone as payment method
In Microsoft Store, select Buy. If asked, sign in with your Microsoft account.
Select Get Started! Add a way to pay or Change > Add a new payment method.
Select Mobile phone.
Select your mobile operator from the list, enter your phone number, and then hit Next.
To make sure its really you, well send you a code via text message. Enter the code and hit Next.
Check the terms and payment details, and then hit Buy.

How to add a new payment method to your Microsoft work account
Note
If you want to edit an existing payment method, read Update an account payment method.
Not all payment methods are supported in all regions.
About payment methods
Payment methods for business purchases are linked to your billing profile in Microsoft 365 admin center, not to a personal Microsoft account. You need a Work account with billing permissions to manage payment methods.
Add or change a payment method
During checkout
The easiest way to add a payment method is during your first purchase:
Proceed to checkout.
In the Payment section, select Add or Change a payment method. If this option isn’t available, check with your administrator to request changes or access to billing permissions.
Enter your payment details and billing address.
Save your changes.
Update an existing payment method in Microsoft 365 admin center
Authorized users can update details like the expiration date, billing address, or name on an existing payment method.
Open Microsoft 365 admin center.
Go to Billing, then Payment methods.
If you have access, you can update your payment methods and Save your changes.
Troubleshooting
My payment was declined
Verify your payment details are correct.
Confirm your billing address matches your payment provider's records.
Check that your bank or payment provider isn't blocking the transaction.
Make sure you have sufficient funds or credit.
I don't see the option to add or edit payment methods
You may not have billing permission. Contact your IT administrator for billing access.
If you want to use a different payment method, call customer support.
I don't see an option to to finance my purchase
If you would like information about business financing, go to the business financing page.
