Applies To Microsoft Authenticator
Microsoft Authenticator is a mobile app that helps you sign in to all your accounts without using a password.
Note
Microsoft Authenticator is not available for PC or Mac, because authenticator apps are designed for smartphones for security reasons. Learn more.
Microsoft no longer supports Authenticator versions that are more than one year old. Always keep your device up to date.
Users in China should read how to download and use Microsoft Authenticator in China.
