How do I share my Microsoft 365 subscription?
Can I share an Microsoft 365 family?
Microsoft 365 Family, Microsoft 365 Premium, and Microsoft 365 Pro subscribers can share subscription benefits with up to five other people in their family group by sending an email or copying & sharing a link.
Note
Sharing is not available in Microsoft 365 Basic or Microsoft 365 Personal subscriptions.
Once the invitee has accepted the sharing invitation from the subscription owner, the invitee automatically becomes a member of the subscription owner's family group.
Sharing is only possible if the invitee remains in the subscription owners' family group.
If invitees are already in another family group and try to accept the sharing invitation, they will see a message that they are already a member of another family group. They would need to first leave that family group before re-accepting the sharing invitation from the subscription owner.
Extended storage options and AI features like Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook are only available to the subscription owner and cannot be shared with additional family group members.
Do not share your Microsoft 365 product key. Product keys cannot be used for sharing your subscription; please use the steps below to send an email or link to each person you want to share with.
How to share your Microsoft 365 subscription
Go to your Microsoft 365 Services & subscriptions sharing page at account.microsoft.com/services/office/sharing
Sign in
Select Share.
Note
If you don't see the Start sharing button, you may not be the owner of the Microsoft 365 Family, Microsoft 365 Premium, or Microsoft 365 Pro subscription. If you're using a Microsoft 365 subscription that someone else shared with you, or if you have another type of Microsoft 365 subscription, you can't share your subscription with other people.
If the person you want to share your Microsoft 365 Family, Microsoft 365 Premium, or Microsoft 365 Pro subscription with is not yet in your family group, send them an email invitation or link to join your family group. If they are already in another family group, they will need to leave it to join yours.
Choose how you'd like to share,
Select
Email invite then enter their email and select Invite.
Select
Create link then select Copy link. You can then paste the link in an email, text, or other message and send it to those you want to share with.
Note
When you share your subscription with someone, they get an email from Microsoft telling them that you've shared your Microsoft 365 subscription with them.
Each person you've shared with will need to select Get started in the email they receive to go to their Microsoft Family Safety site. If they don't have a Microsoft account, they'll be prompted to create one in order to move on.
They should then select Join now to accept the invitation to join your family group before they can access the benefits of your Microsoft 365 subscription.
If you need to send an invitation again, find their name, and choose Resend invite.
They will need to accept your invitation within 48 hours. During this time, you will see "Pending invite" in your Microsoft 365 subscriptions dashboard.
Note
Once you have shared, they can select the Install Microsoft 365 on all your devices option on their account dashboard to install Microsoft 365 and access their OneDrive storage.
Troubleshooting
Troubleshoot "It looks like you're already in a family group" error message
Sharing is only possible if the invitee joins the subscription owners' family group. If the invitee is already in another family group, they will need to leave it to join the subscription owners' family group.
Once the invitee has joined the family group, then the subscription owner can share the Microsoft 365 Family, Microsoft 365 Premium, or Microsoft 365 Pro benefits.
