How do you download music on Audiomack?
July 01, 2025 18:39
Updated
Audiomack allows you to download songs and playlists for offline listening. Whether you're on the go or have limited data, this feature ensures you can enjoy your music anytime, anywhere.
Just choose a song, a playlist, or an album.
Tap the Download icon ⬇
It only takes seconds to download a song. You'll see this message once the download is done.
All your downloaded songs will be available in your Library. Just tap on your avatar in the lower-right corner of your app.
Can I play my downloads offline, with no data or internet connection?
Our offline aspect reflects that your downloaded music can be played without a connection. Download a few songs, turn your data off, then go to the offline library and play.
Why are my downloads not in my device's internal media player or folder?
Due to copyright restrictions, downloads to your device’s internal memory or sharing with other devices isn't possible. However, you can still enjoy offline play within the app.
Can I download all the songs from Audiomack?
You can download thousands of songs! Songs with a star are free to download, while those with a padlock are premium. Most artists offer free downloads, but some opt for premium depending on the artist. You can download up to 20 premium tracks, while Audiomack+ subscribers get even more. Start your free 7-day trial at audiomack.com/plus!
Why are there a lot of songs missing on Audiomack?
We're adding thousands of new songs daily: https://audiomack.com/playlists/browse. We're all about diverse music from up-and-coming artists and actively working with labels to bring you what you love. Some popular tunes may be missing on Audiomack as certain artists haven't joined yet. Share your artist/song suggestions at support@audiomack.com
Didn't find the answer to your question? Please email us at support@audiomack.com for more info.
