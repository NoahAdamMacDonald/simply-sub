How do I cancel or get a refund for my Audiomack+ iOS subscription?
July 01, 2025 18:33
Updated
Unfortunately, we are unable to cancel or issue refunds for purchases made through the iOS App Store, as these transactions are handled directly by Apple.
To cancel your Audiomack+ subscription from your iOS:
1. Open the “Settings” app on your device
2. Tap your name/Apple ID at the top of your screen
3. Tap “Subscriptions”
4. Select the “Audiomack” option under Active Subscriptions
5. Tap “Cancel Subscription”
For refunds, please contact Apple Support or the App Store directly.
