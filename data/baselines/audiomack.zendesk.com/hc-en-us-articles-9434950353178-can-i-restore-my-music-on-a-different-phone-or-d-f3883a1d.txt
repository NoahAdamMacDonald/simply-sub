Can I restore my music on a different phone or device?
July 01, 2025 19:45
Updated
Unfortunately, no, but there is a good reason for that. Downloaded music on Audiomack is only stored on the original device, so it can not be restored to another phone or device. This helps keep artists properly credited and ensures streams are counted fairly.
If you’ve switched devices, you will need to re-download your music on the new phone or device.
