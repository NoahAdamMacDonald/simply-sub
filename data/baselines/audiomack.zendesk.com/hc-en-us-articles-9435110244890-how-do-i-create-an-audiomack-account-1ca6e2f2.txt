How do I create an Audiomack account?
July 01, 2025 18:53
Updated
That's easy! You can start by downloading the latest Audiomack app version for your device, or go to our website on your desktop.
For Android, click here
For iOS, click here
How to Sign Up:
1.) Open Audiomack on the website or app
2.) Choose your preferred sign-up option (Facebook, Google, or Apple)
3.) Choose a ‘username’ then create a secured password (8+ characters, mix of upper/lowercase, numbers, and symbols)
Remember: Creating an Audiomack account enhances your experience with personalized recommendations, playlist management, engagement, and monetization for creators, all while ensuring security and privacy.
