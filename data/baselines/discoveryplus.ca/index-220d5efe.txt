FAQs
What does discovery+ include?
A subscription to discovery+ gives you instant, unlimited access to more than 70,000 episodes of 2,500+ current and classic shows from the most beloved TV brands, including Discovery, Animal Planet, Food Network, HGTV, TLC and more. Fresh new series to inspire, inform and entertain you are added all the time, from lifestyle and true crime to home improvement, food, adventure and more. You’ll find all of your favorite personalities like Chip and Joanna Gaines, Jonathan and Drew Scott, Guy Fieri, Bobby Flay, Theresa Caputo, Oprah Winfrey and so many more – all in one place.
The service also includes exclusives and discovery+ Originals – exciting, can’t-miss series and specials you won’t see anywhere else. And discovery+ is home to the definitive collection of award-winning natural history series, including exclusive streaming access to the BBC’s largest natural history offering, featuring shows like Planet Earth, Blue Planet and Frozen Planet.
Go to watch.discoveryplus.ca to explore all of the shows, Originals and more that discovery+ has to offer.
