RUM Group Inc.
RUM’s mission is to maximize the power of human imagination. We’re a holding company with assets designed to foster creativity, and put real power in the hands of the people who don’t just dream them up, they live them.
What we believe
The company is built on two core beliefs:
As AI democratizes knowledge, the world needs a free and open internet to express and explore our scarcest and most valuable assets, human imagination, creativity, and agency.
The future enterprise will be run by humans empowered to explore to the edges of their imagination, powered by decentralized agentic AI and transacted on the rails of blockchain technology.
We believe this is one of the most incredible moments to be alive. When the cost of knowing becomes accessible to all, the value of imagining explodes. The future won’t belong to the biggest companies or the largest headcounts. It will belong to creative individuals directing AI agents working on their behalf, coordinated and trusted on the blockchain. The enterprise of tomorrow is built by human imagination, amplified beyond anything we’ve seen before.
RUM exists to enable that future, and to make sure it belongs to the dreamers and doers.
How we’re built
The company is built on the belief that future value accrues to two things that machines cannot replicate at scale: physical infrastructure and human creativity.
Quake AI | The Physical Foundation
Quake AI is the physical foundation — where ideas are built, explored, and scaled to the edges of agentic compute. It is the ground floor of the next enterprise: the infrastructure that turns a single imagination into the force of millions, leveraging the compute, cloud, data centers, and blockchain trust layer necessary for AI agents to coordinate and transact.
Quake AI combines a unique set of assets, now under one roof:
CPU: Low-latency, CPU-based compute, network and storage originally designed to deliver world-class global video distribution at scale.
GPU: A scaled GPU estate across an intercontinental footprint of data centers and energized capacity.
Blockchain: A strong strategic partnership with Tether – the world’s largest company in the digital asset industry.
Quake AI is built to be the rails of the agentic enterprise – the foundation everything else stands on.

Rumble | The Foundation of Creativity
Where Quake AI is the physical foundation, Rumble is the foundation of creativity, where ideas are expressed and the freedom is granted to push to the edges of imagination. Rumble is where human authenticity and creativity come alive. As the internet fills with synthetic sameness, Rumble is the vibrant, unfiltered home of the human voice, where real people create, debate, and push ideas forward.
The smarter machines get, the more what’s authentically human matters — and Rumble is the stage for it: the audience, the tools, the distribution, and the freedom to say what’s worth saying. It keeps the conversation human, and it keeps it moving.
Rumble offers multiple formats for humanity to express and explore:
Rumble Video — the true home of free speech, built on authenticity.
Rumble Ads — the promotional engine that puts great ideas front and center.
Rumble Studio — the creativity hub, where livestreaming and content production bring ideas to the masses.
