Which services are HIPAA eligible?
ElevenLabs' HIPAA Attestation can be requested by Enterprise customers. ElevenLabs will enter into a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with eligible Enterprises. The information in the HIPAA Whitepaper is only applicable to such BAA eligible Enterprises.
Covered Services, to the extent that Zero Retention Mode is enabled in connection therewith (HIPAA-eligible services):
Text To Speech API
Speech to Text API
Text to Dialogue API
ElevenAgents
NOTE: If you are a Covered Entity or Business Associate as defined under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (as amended, “HIPAA”), you agree not to use these HIPAA Eligible Services for any purpose or in any manner involving Protected Health Information (as defined under HIPAA) unless and until you have entered into a Business Associate Agreement with ElevenLabs.
NOTE 2: For ElevenAgents, your Business Associate Agreement applies only to conversation content. Agent configuration data is persisted, meaning it is not covered by Zero Retention Mode and should not contain PHI.
