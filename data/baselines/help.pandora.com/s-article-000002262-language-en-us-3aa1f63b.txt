Pandora Premium Family Plan
Pandora Premium Family Plan offers all of the features of Pandora Premium to up to six unique Pandora accounts. This plan costs $19.99/monthly or $219.89/annually. Taxes may also apply depending on local tax laws in your area. Please note you must be at least 21 years of age to purchase a Premium Family subscription and be the primary account holder.

How does it work?
Each of your Premium Family members will get full access to Premium features, but they'll have their own account. (In other words, you won't have to share your music unless you want to.)
How do I upgrade to Family Plan?
To see if you're eligible for a trial or if you're ready to subscribe, visit our upgrade page.
If you're already a subscriber or are currently in a trial subscription select Switch Plans to find your Pandora Premium Family plan options.
Please note: Upgrading to Pandora Premium Family will nullify the remainder of your current Pandora Premium trial and you'll be charged right away.
Manage my Family Plan subscription
To view the current status of your family plan, or to make any additional changes, log into your Pandora account on a computer or mobile device.
Visit the Subscription section of your account, and select Manage Family in Current Plan. This will take you to your family plan management page.
From here you'll be able to send, cancel or resend invitations or add and remove users from your subscription.
Leave a Family Plan subscription
If you're not the primary account holder but would like to remove yourself from a Family Plan, log into Pandora on your computer or mobile device. Then:
Open your Settings and select Subscription.
Select Leave Family Plan, and confirm your choice.
Why can't I upgrade or join a Family Plan subscription?
Joining or upgrading to a Pandora Premium Family subscription is not possible if you are currently subscribed through Apple, Google Play, Roku, or Amazon Appstore. If you're interested in subscribing to Pandora Premium Family or in joining someone else's family plan, you'll need to cancel your current subscription and wait for it to expire. Once you begin to hear ads on your account again, you can sign up for Pandora Premium Family from our website.
Pandora Plus Gift subscribers cannot purchase a Family Plan Subscription, but can join a Premium Family Plan, keeping the following in mind:
If you are invited to a Premium Family Plan, and you are currently subscribed through a Pandora Plus Gift Subscription, joining it will defer your gift subscription and give you access to Premium right away.
That deferred subscription will remain on your account and will resume automatically if you decide to leave the family plan (or if the primary account holder decides to cancel it).
I purchased a Family Plan so I could stream to multiple devices. Why am I receiving this error message?
Upgrading to the Family Plan doesn't allow you to play music from the same Pandora account on multiple devices simultaneously (unless you are routing the audio out of one device into a system with multiple speakers or using a Sonos system).
Each Pandora account on your family plan can only play one stream of ad-free content at a time from your individual accounts.
If you'd like to stream to multiple devices at the same time, you could specifically create and/or add a second account to your family plan for those occasions when you need two devices playing at once. If there's a particular playlist or station you love, you can share it with another Pandora account, including one on your Family Plan.
Otherwise, just make sure you press pause on one device before beginning to listen to the same account on a different device.
