Logging out
You can log out of Kanopy if you're using a public computer or want to log in with a different account.
You can log in to your Kanopy account on any number of devices. Only one Kanopy account can be signed in to a device at a time.
On the Kanopy website
Select the menu button in the top-right corner, then select Log Out.
On the Kanopy mobile app
Tap More (in the bottom-right corner), then tap Log Out.
On the Kanopy TV app
Go to Settings in the top menu, then select Log Out.
