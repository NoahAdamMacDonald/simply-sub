How do I cancel my subscription?
If you cancel your monthly subscription, you'll still have access to Sportsnet+ until the end of your current billing cycle, and you won't be charged for the following month, except in Quebec, where cancellation will be effective right away and you will be refunded for the remaining portion of the month.
Did you know you can pause your subscription instead of cancelling? Learn more here
Need help cancelling your account? Here are the steps you need to take by platform.
If you subscribed through our Sportsnet+ website:
To cancel your subscription, click on the drop-down menu in the top right corner of the desktop version of watch.sportsnet.ca. Once the drop-down menu is open, click on Account and then click Billing to cancel.
If you subscribed through Amazon in-app purchase:
Go to www.amazon.ca
Select My Account in the menu
Select Memberships and Subscriptions
Find Sportsnet+ and select Manage Subscription
Select Actions
Select Turn off Autorenewal
If you subscribed through Google in-app purchase:
Go to the Watch section, tap on the profile icon,
Click on Manage Subscription and select Cancel Subscription
This will take you to your subscription management page in the Google Play store, where you will need to complete the cancellation process.
For additional guidance on how to cancel your Google Subscription, you can also visit this link: Cancel a Subscription on the Google Play
If you subscribed through Apple in-app purchase:
Go to Settings > iTunes & App Store
Tap your Apple ID at the top of the screen
Tap View Apple ID
Under Subscriptions, Tap Manage
Tap on Sportsnet+
Click on Cancel Subscription
Note: For annual subscriptions, if you wish to opt out of auto-renewal, follow the same cancellation process as for monthly subscriptions before your renewal date. If you cancel after renewal or after purchasing the annual subscription, you will retain access until your next renewal date. Please note that there are no refunds.
