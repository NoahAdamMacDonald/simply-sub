Sportsnet+ Citytv+ Bundle FAQ
How do I purchase the Sportsnet+ Citytv+ Bundle?
The Bundle can be purchased via Sportsnet+ and Prime Video. The Bundle costs $36.99+tax/month.
What content can I watch with the Bundle?
The Bundle allows you to stream Sportsnet+ Standard and Citytv+ content.
Sportsnet+ Standard
NHL - Monday Night Hockey – Over 50 NHL matchups featuring a Canadian team
Saturday Night Hockey
Over 100 NHL games throughout the season featuring matchups between two U.S.-based teams
MLB - Over 150 Blue Jays Games, Over 150 US MLB Games + Every Game of the MLB Postseason & World Series
NBA - 39 Raptors Games, Over 125 NBA Regular Season Games and Select Playoff Games through to the Finals.
UFC Fight Nights, PPV Prelims and Original Programming
Click here to learn more about the differences between Sportsnet+ Standard and Premium content.
Citytv+
Access to Citytv, Bravo, HGTV, Food Network, Discovery, FX, ID, and more via the Citytv+ App.
Stream live channels
Thousands of hours of on-demand content
Stream titles like Law & Order: SVU, the Chicago Franchise, Golden Bachelor, Beat Bobby Flay, The Real Housewives, The Valley, Gold Rush, Expedition X and more.
How do I watch the Bundle plan content?
You will need to download both the Sportsnet and Citytv+ apps to stream content. The login you created upon purchase will be used to access both apps.
Check out information on supported devices for Sportsnet app here and for Citytv+ app here.
How do I manage my Bundle plan subscription?
If you have purchased the Bundle via Sportsnet+, you will manage your subscription via your Sportsnet account page.
If I have an existing Sportsnet+ subscription, can I purchase the Bundle plan?
For existing subscribers who have purchased a subscription plan via In-App Purchase in the Apple, Google or Amazon app stores or third-party:
The Bundle is currently unavailable via in-app purchase. At the end of your subscription bill cycle, you can resubscribe to the Bundle here.
For existing Monthly Standard or Monthly Premium subscribers who purchased directly through Sportsnet+:
You can change your plan to the Bundle via your Sportsnet account page.
Switching to the Bundle plan gives you immediate access to Sportsnet+ Standard and Citytv+ content. Customers who switch to the Bundle plan mid-term will receive a prorated refund for the remainder of their monthly subscription and will be charged in full for the Bundle.
For existing Standard Annual or Premium Annual who purchased directly through Sportsnet+:
The Bundle is only available for monthly subscribers. Once you reach the end of your annual bill cycle, you will have the opportunity to purchase the Bundle.
Can I cancel one subscription but keep the other?
No, both Sportsnet+ and Citytv+ are tied together in this Bundle and cancelation will result in ending your subscription to both services. You can always still subscribe to either Sportsnet+ or Citytv+ services individually.
If you cancel your Bundle subscription mid-term, you will continue to receive access to both services until your term end date.
Can I apply a promo code to this Bundle?
Yes, promo codes are supported for this Bundle. Please follow the redemption steps provided with your promo code to successfully activate your subscription.
