FAQs about Sportsnet+ discount with some Rogers plans
The Sportsnet+ Standard offer is available to Rogers wireless customers who:
Get a 5G+ Rogers Popular, Ultimate or Ultimate VIP plan on a new activation or hardware upgrade
Get a 5G+ Rogers Preferred Program Popular, Ultimate or Ultimate VIP plan on a new activation or hardware upgrade
Don’t have the account holder subscribed to Sportsnet+ or have access to Sportsnet through Rogers Xfinity
Have a valid email address on file associated with your mobile account
Which Sportsnet+ plan does this offer apply to?
This offer only applies to Sportsnet+ Standard monthly plan. Promo period starts when you activate your Sportsnet+ account. The monthly plan will be discounted for 12 months for customers on a Popular plan or 24 months for customers on an Ultimate or Ultimate VIP plan.
Where and how do I redeem my Sportsnet+ promo code?
You can only be redeem promo codes via https://www.sportsnetplus.ca/. They can’t be redeemed via third-party providers (Apple, Google, Amazon, or Amazon Prime Video).
Allow up to 5 days after confirmation of your mobile order to get the email with your promo code. It’ll be sent to the email address associated with your mobile account.
To redeem your promo code:
In the email with the promo code, select Activate Now

What happens after the promo period? Will I be automatically renewed into a Sportsnet+ Standard subscription?
If you don’t cancel your subscription before the promo period ends, your Sportsnet+ Standard Monthly Plan will auto-renew every month. The monthly price CAD $34.99/mo + taxes will be charged to your credit card. You’ll be notified via email at least 7 days before your promo period ends. You may cancel your subscription anytime.
What happens if I already have an active Sportsnet+ subscription or Sportsnet through my Rogers Xfinity package?
If your wireless account holder already has Sportsnet access through Sportsnet+ or Rogers Xfinity, you won’t receive a Sportsnet+ promo code after signing up for an eligible wireless plan.
However, if the mobile account holder doesn’t have Sportsnet access either through Sportsnet+ or Rogers Xfinity, you’ll receive a Sportsnet+ promo code by email after signing up for an eligible mobile plan. Follow the instructions in the email to activate your discount on Sportsnet+ Standard.
If I activate multiple lines on the eligible plans, will I get multiple promo codes (i.e. one for each line)?
You’ll get a promo code for each line you activate as long as the account holder:
Haven’t redeemed their promo code OR
Doesn’t have Sportsnet access through Sportsnet+ or Rogers Xfinity.
How long do I have to redeem my promo code?
You must redeem your promo code within 7 days of receiving it.
What happens if my promo code doesn’t work?
Reach out to the Sportsnet+ Customer Care via Live Chat or check out the Submit A Request for help. The Customer Care team is available Monday-Sunday 1:30 p.m. – 1:30 a.m. ET.
I lost my promo code. Can I get another one?
Contact us at 1-888-Rogers1 or *611. We’ll give you a new promo code within 48-72 hours once we confirm your eligibility.
I haven’t received my code yet. When can I get it?
It may take up to 5 days for your code to arrive via email. If you still haven’t received it after this time, contact 1-888-Rogers1 or dial *611.
Can I upgrade to Sportsnet+ Premium during the promo period?
No, you can’t upgrade to Sportsnet+ Premium during the promo period.
What if I change my mobile plan during the promo period? Will I lose my Sportsnet+ access?
No. If you’ve redeemed your promo code, you’ll have access to the Sportsnet+ discount until the end of the promo period.
Will I see Sportsnet+ Standard on my Rogers bill?
No, Sportsnet+ Standard won’t be on your Rogers bill.
How many devices can stream Sportsnet+ at the same time?
You can stream on two different devices at the same time with one Sportsnet+ account.
