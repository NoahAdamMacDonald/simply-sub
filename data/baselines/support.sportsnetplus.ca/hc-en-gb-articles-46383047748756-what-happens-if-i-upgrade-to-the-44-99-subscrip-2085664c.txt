What happens if I upgrade to the $44.99 subscription?
Your existing subscription will be cancelled and a new subscription at the higher tier price will be purchased. You will be immediately billed for a full month subscription of the higher plan, minus the remaining portion of the lower plan.
You will also get a new billing anniversary date, one month from the upgrade action.
