or identifiable individual (“Personal Information”), as

Information. Midwest Tape, the owner and operator of
https://www.hoopladigital.com
(the “Website”) and applications used to access the
Hoopla services and its content (the “Apps”), and all

limited liability company formed under the laws of the State of Ohio.
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Notice, our

handling of Personal Information, you may contact the Midwest Tape privacy
team at any time by emailing
privacy@hoopladigital.com.
1. What Information Do We Collect?
We collect the following categories of Personal Information about you:
Account Information, such as username, password, and
your member/library card number, along with all other information we
collect when you register for a User Account not listed in any other
category. Your username, along with any descriptive, biographical, or
other information shared in your user profile, may be made publicly
available when you use certain features of the Website and Apps,
including when you elect to display media from the Website or Apps on a
screen or monitor that is visible to others. Please exercise caution
when determining how you wish to be identified and the personal details
you choose to share when using the Services.
Please note that the Services may link or integrate with third-party
sites, services or apps. We are not responsible for protecting any
information that you choose to share with third parties through the
Services, nor are we responsible for the privacy or security policies or
practices or the content of such third parties. Accordingly, we
encourage you to review the privacy and security policies and terms of
service of those third parties so that you understand how they collect,
use, share and protect your information. Please exercise discretion when
sharing Personal Information.
Identifying Information, such as first and last name,
email address, or other unique identifiers (including digital
identifiers and device identifiers).
Contact Information, such as your postal address, home
and/or mobile phone number, or other communication details. This also
includes social media account and profile information if you contact us
through a social media platform, access your social media profiles
through our Website or Apps, or mention Hoopla Digital on a publicly
shared social media post.
Location Information, including IP-based geolocation
data and, with your consent and based on your device permissions, Global
Positioning System (GPS) data. When you access the Website or use the
Apps or our Services, we automatically collect and process information
about your general or precise location. We use various technologies to
determine location, including IP address, GPS, Wi-Fi access points, and
mobile towers.
Communications with us, such as when you contact our
customer support by phone, email, or online chat, or when you respond to
surveys or feedback requests. We collect the contents of all such
communications, including any information you provide in communications
with us, including via social media, e-mail, or our customer call
center. We also collect details about the communications we send to you
(e.g., emails, text messages, push notifications, messages within the
Website or Apps) and data concerning your interactions with these
communications.
Transaction Information, including details and activity
regarding your User Account through which you access our Website, Apps,
or Services, including your selection and usage of videos, eBooks, audio
recordings, and other media.
Interests and Preferences, including information
inferred from your Transaction Information, ratings and/or feedback you
provide, and other interaction information.
Other Personal Details about you, including your date
of birth, age, or gender. If you are a vendor, service provider, or
employee of one of our partner institutions, we may also collect
employment-related information such as your job title, department name,
and business contact information.
Inferences about you and your use of the Website, Apps,
and/or Services based on Personal Information we collect about you
(e.g., preferred content based on your watch history) and on information
provided by third parties.
Device Information about the equipment and network you
use to access the Website, the Apps and/or the Services, such as your
operating system version, device type, and the name of the mobile
network you are using. Our system logs may record certain information
about your visits to our Websites or use of our Apps, including the web
request, Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, device and
mobile ad identifiers, browser information, and other similar technical
information. We may also collect device information from emails you send
to or receive from us, which helps us track which emails are opened and
which links are clicked by recipients. If you use your login information
while connected to a Virtual Private Network, we may also collect that
information.
Client and Log data. Technical details, including the
IP address of your device, time zone, and operating system. We will also
store your login information (registration date, date of last password
change, date of last successful login), type, and version of your
browser.
Usage Information about your activity on our Website
and Apps, which includes a date and time stamp of each visit, banners or
content that you clicked, your interaction with such banners,
clickstream information, duration of your visit, and the order in which
you engage with our content. Based on your interactions and usage of the
Website and Apps, additional information that is generated as part of
such usage, such as transaction information and time and location of
transactions may also be collected.
Verification Information, including data we are
required or entitled by applicable law to collect and process for
authentication or identification, or for the verification of data we
collect.
Sensitive Personal Information: We do not ask for, and

restrictions set forth in this Privacy Notice. We do not require you to

our records and unsubscribe the child from any marketing lists.
2. Where Do We Collect Your Personal Information From?
We collect the Personal Information described in Section 1 in the

From You: We collect Personal Information from

contact us, register for access to our Website, Apps, or Services,

our newsletter, or use our Services. We collect the following categories
of Personal Information directly from you: account information,
identifying information, contact information, transaction information,
interests and preferences, and other personal details. In the event we
need to verify your age or identity, pursuant to a Hoopla Digital
internal policy or a legal or contractual obligation, we will collect
limited verification information from you solely for that purpose.
Automatically when You use Hoopla Digital:
When you interact with the Website or Apps, we automatically collect the
following categories of information: account information, transaction
information, location information, communications, device information,
client and log data, and usage information.
We use cookies, local browser storage, and similar technologies,
including “web beacons,” “pixels,” or “tags” as part of our process for
collecting data automatically. A “cookie” is a small amount of data a
website operator, or a third party whose content is embedded in that
website, may store in your web browser and that the website operator or,
as applicable, the third party, can access when you visit the website. A
web beacon, pixel or tag is a small, usually-transparent image placed on
a web page that allows the operator of that image, which may be the
operator of the website you visit or a third party, to read or write a
cookie.
Most web browsers are set to accept cookies by default. You can usually
choose to set your browser to remove or reject browser cookies. Where
required by applicable law, we obtain your consent for the use of
certain non-essential cookies and similar technologies through our
consent banner. You can also adjust your privacy settings by clicking on
the “Change Preferences” button in the consent banner on our Website.
You may decline optional cookies through the consent banner without
foregoing access to the Website, though certain features may be limited.
Please note that if you choose to remove or reject cookies, this could
affect the availability and functionality of our Services. Please note
that certain tracking technologies may continue to operate (for example,
where functionality is necessary to provide the Services) even if you
delete cookies. For more information, please see our Cookie Notice.
From other sources: We may receive information
from third parties, such as our customers (including public libraries),
business partners, advertising and marketing service providers, public
databases, and social media platforms. We may collect the following
categories of Personal Information about you from these other sources:
account information, identifying information, contact information,
location information, transaction information, interests and
preferences, inferences, other personal details, and device information,
in each case as permitted by applicable law.
