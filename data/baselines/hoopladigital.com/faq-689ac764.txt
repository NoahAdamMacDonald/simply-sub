What is Hoopla?
Hoopla is a free service offered by your public library that allows you to borrow eBooks, audiobooks, comics, movies, and TV on a web browser, phone, tablet, or TV.
Libraries across the United States, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand offer Hoopla. If your library doesn't yet, let them know that you'd like it!
How do I know if my library offers Hoopla?
Does Hoopla's content ever change?
Yes! We are constantly receiving new titles from various vendors on a weekly basis. Please note that some titles may only be available for a limited time. If you see something you like, be sure to check it out while it's available!
