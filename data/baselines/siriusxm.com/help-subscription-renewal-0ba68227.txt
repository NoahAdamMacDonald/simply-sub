Does my subscription automatically renew? Will you notify me?
SiriusXM is a continuing service that is billed at the frequency stated at the time of your initial subscription purchase. For example, some plans let you elect to be billed on a monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or longer basis; and your service continues without interruption, for your convenience. These billing terms are explained during every subscription activation and detailed in your Customer Agreement. To find the exact renewal date for a subscription purchased directly with SiriusXM, log in to the Online Account Center and look for your active subscriptions.
