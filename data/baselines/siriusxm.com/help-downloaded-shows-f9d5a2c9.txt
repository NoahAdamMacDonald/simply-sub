How long can I keep downloaded shows on my device?
Some shows have expiring content – those downloads will be removed from your device as they expire. You’ll see an “expiring soon” badge when it’s close to expiring.NOTE: If you have the Remove Played Downloads setting On, we will automatically removed downloads off your device for played episodes.
