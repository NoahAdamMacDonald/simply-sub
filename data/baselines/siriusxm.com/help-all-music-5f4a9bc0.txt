Have questions about our All Music or All Access plan? We’re here to help.
1. What is the All Music plan?
The All Music plan is a new SiriusXM plan offering that includes all of SiriusXM’s music channels, including channels curated by your favorite artists, as well as over 100 ad-free Xtra channels. The All Music plan is currently offered at a rate of $11.99 per month (plus applicable tax).The All Music plan includes access to SiriusXM in your vehicle, as well as through our app so you can listen to SiriusXM at home or on the go. The SiriusXM app is available on smart devices such as Amazon Alexa and Google Home, on most Smart TVs, the App Store, and Google Play store.To see a full list of channels available, click here.
2. What are add-ons?Customers on an All Music plan may choose to customize their listening experience by purchasing add-ons, allowing them to access additional content. Add-ons are currently available for the following content types: Add Sports for $8 per month; includes access to play-by-play coverage from the NFL, MLB, NBA, NHL, NCAA, and NASCAR as well as content from the biggest names in sports talk
Add Talk for $5 per month; includes podcasts and shows from your favorite A-list hosts, such as Radio Andy, Netflix is a Joke Radio, and Kevin Hart’s Laugh Out Loud Radio
Add News for $5 per month; includes access to coverage of Local, International, U.S., Health, and Cultural news from every angle
You may contact us to customize your All Music plan.Add-ons are not sold without the All Music plan. Applicable taxes apply to all add-ons.To see a full list of channels included with each add-on, click here.
3. What is the All Access Plan?The All Access plan includes all of SiriusXM’s content, including all SiriusXM Music, News, Sports, and Talk. The All Access plan is currently offered at a rate of $25.99 per month (plus applicable tax).The All Access plan includes access to SiriusXM in your vehicle, as well as through our app so you can listen to SiriusXM at home or on the go. The SiriusXM app is available on smart devices such as Amazon Alexa and Google Home, on most Smart TVs, the App Store, and Google Play store.To see a full list of channels included with All Access, click here.
4. How can I purchase the All Music plan or All Access plan?You can sign up by contacting us at 1-800-689-6881.
5. I have multiple vehicles. Can I purchase more than one All Music plan or All Access plan?Absolutely! Please contact us to discuss your options and we’ll check to see if your vehicle’s radio is compatible with these plans. At this time, not all radios can support these plans. We expect to support all in-car radios later this year. In the meantime, provided your vehicle is eligible, you may certainly purchase multiple subscriptions for your radios.
6. I want the All Music plan or All Access plan, but I was informed that my car’s radio is ineligible. What are my options?Currently, not every vehicle can deliver these plans. Please visit siriusxm.com/plans to see plans that are available for purchase for your radio or contact us at 1-800-689-6881.
