How to manage your Proton subscription
Reading
6 mins
Category
Premium plans
You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your Proton subscription anytime.
When you downgrade to another paid plan, any unused time is converted into prorated account credits that are applied automatically to future payments.
If you cancel your subscription and switch to a Proton Free plan, your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period and does not renew.
When you cancel a subscription, the downgrade to the free plan happens after the current billing period ends. Switching to a different plan takes effect immediately. We’re working to move all users to this behavior.
In this guide, we explain:
How to upgrade your Proton Free plan
How to switch to another paid plan
How to cancel a paid Proton subscription
What to keep in mind when downgrading or canceling
How to upgrade your Proton Free plan
Go to account.proton.me, sign in, and select a Proton app.
Click Upgrade at the top right.
On the Select a plan page, you can:
Select your preferred currency to display prices
Browse plans under For Individuals, For Families, or For Business
Toggle between 1 month and 12 months pricing
Review what each plan includes
When you’re ready, click Select [plan] to continue.
Review your subscription and enter your payment details. Click Pay… or Confirm to complete your purchase.
To check your subscription renewal date, log into your account and go to Settings → Subscription → Your Subscriptions, where the renewal date will be listed.
How to switch to another paid plan
Go to account.proton.me, sign in, and select a Proton app.
Navigate to ⚙ (Settings) → All settings → Subscription and select Compare all plans.
On the Select a plan page, you can:
Select your preferred currency to display prices
Browse plans under For Individuals, For Families, or For Business
Review what each plan includes
When you’re ready, click Select [plan name] to switch to a different plan, or click Edit subscription to keep your current plan and change the billing period.
Review your subscription and enter your payment details. Click Pay… or Confirm to complete your purchase.
Note: You can’t change plans while there’s an unpaid invoice on the account. You’ll need to pay the outstanding invoice first before switching plans.
How to cancel a paid Proton subscription
Go to account.proton.me, sign in, and select any Proton service.
Navigate to ⚙ (Settings) → All settings → Subscription, scroll down to Cancel subscription, and click Continue.
If you’re sure you want to cancel, click Cancel subscription again to confirm.
What to keep in mind when downgrading or canceling
If your account exceeds the limits of the plan you’re downgraded to, some features may stop working. For example, if you downgraded to the Proton Free plan, and your Mail storage is over the 1 GB limit, you won’t be able to send or receive new emails until you free up space or upgrade again. You can also learn what happens to your data when you downgrade your account to the Free plan. This information is available in Section 9 of our Terms of Service.
Learn more about the free plan limits.
If you don’t want to use Proton apps anymore, find out how to delete your Proton Account.
