Payments and invoices
You can pay for your Proton subscription with payment card, PayPal, and other popular payment methods. In this article, we explain how to check out using each of our accepted payment methods.

How to pay for a Proton subscription
Payment card
We accept all major credit and debit cards.
At checkout, select Credit/debit card from the Payment details menu.
If you already have saved cards on your account, you can select one from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your card number, expiry date, and CVV (security code). You’ll also need to select a billing country. Select your country of tax residency.
3. Review the payment summary, then click Pay now to confirm. You may be asked to complete 3DS (3D Secure) verification with your bank.
PayPal
At checkout, select PayPal from the Payment details menu.
If your PayPal is already linked to your account, select it from the dropdown menu.
Select a billing country (your country of tax residency).
Review the payment summary, then click the PayPal button (under Amount due) to confirm.
4. PayPal will open in a new window. Follow the steps to sign in and set up a billing agreement for recurring payments.
Apple Pay
Apple Pay is available on:
macOS (Safari only). Make sure you’re on the latest version of Safari.
iOS and iPadOS (all browsers).
At checkout, select Apple Pay under the Select your payment method menu.
Select your billing country (your country of tax residency).
Review the payment summary, then click the Apple Pay button (under Billing Country) to confirm.
Apple Pay will open in a new window. Follow the steps to complete your payment.
Google Pay
Select Google Pay from the payment details menu.
Select your billing country (your country of tax residency).
Review the payment summary, then click the GPay button (under Amount due) to confirm.
Google Pay will open in a new window. Follow the steps to complete your payment.
Bitcoin
You can pay for any Proton subscription with Bitcoin — but it won’t appear as a payment option when you first create your account.
To use Bitcoin, create a free Proton Account, then upgrade to a paid subscription in your account settings.
More details: How to pay for Proton with Bitcoin

Check you’re sending the correct amount for your plan. View plans and pricing
Once we receive your payment, we’ll credit your account with the amount you sent. You can then use those credits to pay for your subscription. Learn how to check your credit balance
Important information about cash payments
We can’t issue refunds for subscriptions purchased with cash.
We don’t accept checks, money orders, or other forms of non-cash payments.
We are not responsible for lost shipments. We strongly recommend using a tracked or recorded delivery service. You can also let us know that you’re sending us cash in the mail, so we know to expect it.

Our core currencies are:

These currencies are available worldwide, and are compatible with all payment methods.
If you’re paying with Payment card, PayPal, Apple Pay, or Google Pay, you may also be able to use the local currency in these countries:

If you can’t see your preferred payment method at checkout
Apple Pay
Apple Pay is only available on macOS (Safari) and iOS/iPadOS (any browser).
If you don’t see it as an option on these platforms, check that:
Biometrics are enabled and available on your device.
You have at least one valid debit or credit card saved in Apple Wallet.
Safari is updated to its latest version.
Other payment methods
Some payment methods won’t be available if you upgrade while creating your account. Here’s how to get around this and view all available payment methods as a new Proton user:
Create a free Proton Account
Open your account settings (Settings ⚙️ → All settings)
Select Upgrade plan from the sidebar (or click Compare all plans on the homepage)
Select a subscription from our individual, group, and business plans.
At checkout, scroll to payment details. All payment options will appear (aside from bank transfer, which works a bit differently).
Making payments from Russia
Due to the ongoing conflict in Ukraine, Proton community members in Russia may experience difficulties paying for their subscription.
If you’re having trouble, there are some workarounds which will hopefully allow you to pay. Please visit our dedicated guide for more information.
