Manage your Devices
Managing your devices on F1 is quick and easy. Just follow the steps below to add or remove a device from your account.
Add a device
Log in to your F1 TV account using the device you want to add.
Your device will be automatically registered to your account.
Remove a device
Go to F1.com or F1 TV.
Log in with your email address and password
Select the person icon in the top right-hand corner of the screen
Select Manage Account
Scroll down to Devices and select Edit
Choose Remove Device
Streaming limit
Streaming limits depend on your subscription:
F1 TV Pro subscription, you can stream on one device at a time
F1 TV Premium subscription, you can stream on up to six devices at the same time
