Accepted Payment Methods
When it comes to watching F1 TV, we know you want to get straight to the action. That's why we have a wide range of ways to pay. Here are all the current payment methods you can use.
You can sign up for F1 TV using:
Visa
Mastercard
American Express (Amex)
Google Pay (selected countries)
Apple Pay (selected countries)
Debit cards
Prepaid cards
PayPal is not currently supported.
Note:
American Express is not available for users signing up from France
Google Pay only works in supported countries and when using the Chrome browser
Check Google Pay availability
Apple Pay only works in supported countries and when using the Safari browser
Check Apple Pay availability
