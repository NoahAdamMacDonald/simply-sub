Buy F1 TV through the web
Purchasing an F1 TV subscription via our website is easy. Just follow the steps below to get it sorted.
Before you start
You'll need an F1 account to purchase a subscription. If you already have one, you can use your existing email address and password
You must be over 18
Your billing address needs to match your actual location, and your billing details must match the address
Using a VPN may cause payment failures or account restrictions
Steps to purchasing a subscription
Go to the F1 TV website and select Subscribe
Select your country of residence, if it's not automatically selected
Select the subscription you'd like to purchase. Toggle between monthly and annual if F1 TV Access, F1 TV Pro and F1 TV Premium are shown
When prompted, follow the F1 login or registration screens to sign in with your existing account or create a new one. Once completed, return to the purchase journey and confirm your subscription again
Enter your billing details and address to complete your order. Your bank may request additional verification, such as an authorisation code or confirmation via a banking app
Your order is successful when you see the order confirmation screen and receive the confirmation email
Note: You must verify your email address using the link in your verification email within 10 days of creating your F1 account.
