AgileBits Cookie Policy
AgileBits Inc. its affiliates and our affiliated companies unless otherwise noted (collectively, “1Password” “our,” “we,” or “us”) maintain www.1password.com, www.1Password.eu, www.1Password.ca, and related 1Password emailing services (collectively, our “Website”). This 1Password Cookie Policy (“Cookie Policy”) describes how we and our business partners deploy cookies and similar technologies. Please review our Privacy Policy to learn how we process and use your personal information we collect through our Website more generally.
What are cookies and similar technologies?
Cookies are small text files that are stored in your web browser that allow us or a third party to recognize you. Cookies are used to store and receive identifiers and other information on computers, phones, and other devices. Cookies can be used to collect, store, and share pieces of information, such as IP addresses, about your activities across websites, including on our Website. However, there are other similar technologies, such as pixels, that store and access information on your device in a similar manner to cookies. Therefore, the single term “cookies” for this Cookie Policy, refers to cookies and similar technologies.
We do not use third-party trackers in our web application (my.1password.com), or our client applications for macOS, Windows, Linux, Android or iOS.
What types of cookies do we use and why?
The following information lists the different types of cookies implemented on our Website, who serves those cookies, the cookie name, a brief description of the cookie, and the expiration of the cookie.,.
We use both session cookies and persistent cookies.
Session cookies
Help us recognize a single visit to our Website. They let us identify you while you browse, then expire when you close your browser or after a short period of inactivity.
Persistent cookies
Stay on your device for a set amount of time, which is described in this Cookie Policy. They continue to work even after you close your browser. For example, if you choose to stay signed in to 1Password, we use a persistent cookie to remember that preference, so you don't have to sign in again the next time you visit.
Categories of cookies
Cookies can either be first-party, where 1Password sets and uses those directly, or third-party which are set by third parties, such as our analytics provider, through our Website or server.
The types of cookies used by use and our providers in connection with the Website can be classified into one of the following purposes. To see what cookies we use, refer to the Cookies Addendum set out at the end of this Cookie Policy that lists out the provider, cookie name, description, purpose, and expiration period of each cookie.
Essential Cookies
Purpose: These cookies are essential for the Website to be provided to you, to prevent fraudulent activity, to maintain security, or to enable basic features of the Website and online services, such as to allow you to login to your account. Since these cookies are essential for the Website and the features you expect to work when visiting our Website, these can not be rejected within our consent preference management offerings.
Functional Cookies
Purpose: These cookies enable us or our providers to provide certain functions of our Website to you, such as a video player or language preference for our Website. Please note that if you disable these cookies, we may not be able to provide all functions of our Website to you.
Analytics & Performance Cookies
Purpose: These cookies help us and our providers understand how visitors interact with our Website, enabling us to improve user experience and optimize our content. If you do not allow these cookies, we will not be able to monitor our Website performance.
Marketing Cookies
Purpose: These cookies are used to help us and our providers personalize your experience, reach you and more people like you to spread the word about 1Password, as well as. analyze the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns.
Sale or Share of Personal Information Cookies
Purpose: These cookies may disclose personal information to third parties or support cross-context behavioral advertising in a manner that may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under certain privacy laws. They help us and our advertising partners deliver more relevant advertisements, measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns, and personalize advertising based on your interactions across websites and online services. You can choose whether to allow these cookies through our Cookie Preference Center, subject to the consent choices available in your region.
Managing Cookies
You can manage your cookie preferences at any time, including withdrawing your consent to non-essential cookies, by selecting "Your Privacy Choices" in the footer of our Website. Any changes you make will apply to the future use of non-essential cookies on our Website. You may need to refresh the page before your updated preferences take effect.
Your cookie preferences are stored on the browser and device you use to access our Website. If you clear your browser cookies or cache, use a different browser or device, or otherwise remove the information used to remember your preferences, you may be asked to make your cookie choices again the next time you visit our Website.
Some third-party cookies cannot be disabled through our cookie management tool once they have already been set. If you want to withdraw your consent to optional cookies, including third-party cookies, you can delete them through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to control how cookies are used, block certain types of cookies, and clear existing cookies and browsing data.
Some applications you use to access your 1Password account, including your web browser, may store information on your device to make future sign-ins easier and keep certain information available when you're signed out. You can remove this information from your device at any time. If you do, you'll need to enter your account details, Account Password, and Secret Key the next time you sign in to your 1Password account.
You can also opt out of the sharing of your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising by selecting "Your Privacy Choices" in the footer of our Website and unchecking the box titled "Sharing or sale of personal information".
We also recognize Global Privacy Control ("GPC") opt-out preference signals. GPC is a browser-based setting that lets you tell websites you do not want your personal information sold or shared for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising. If you enable GPC, we'll treat the browser and device you're using as opted out of those activities wherever they apply on our Website.
Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking, deleting, or clearing existing cookies and browsing data. For browser-specific instructions, please refer to your browser manufacturer's documentation:

Industry Advertising Opt-Out Tools
Some of our advertising partners participate in industry self-regulatory programs that provide information about interest-based advertising and offer tools to help you manage your advertising preferences. Depending on where you live, you can learn more or opt out by visiting:
United States: Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) AdChoices Tool
European Economic Area (EEA) and United Kingdom: European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA)
Canada: Digital Advertising Alliance of Canada (DAAC)
If you interact with social networking services through our Website, or if those providers set cookies on our Website, you may also be able to manage your advertising preferences through your account settings with those providers. For more information, please refer to the privacy and advertising settings made available by providers such as Google, Meta, and Reddit.
These opt-out tools and settings are provided by third parties, and we do not control their operation, availability, or accuracy. If your browser is configured to reject cookies when you visit these opt-out pages, if you later clear your browser cookies, or if you use a different browser or device, your opt-out preferences may no longer apply. You may need to update your preferences for each browser and device that you use.
Changes to this Cookie Policy
We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our Website, our use of cookies and similar technologies, our business practices, or applicable laws.
When we make changes, we'll update the “Last Updated” date at the bottom of this Cookie Policy. If the changes are material, we'll let you know where required by law, such as by posting a prominent notice on our Website or requesting your consent before new cookies are used.
We encourage you to review this Cookie Policy and your cookie preferences periodically so you stay informed about how and why we use cookies.
