Does Surfshark offer a free trial?
Updated:
September 11, 2026 12:28
Surfshark offers a 7-day VPN free trial to test the waters. Currently, the free trial for new users is available through Surfshark’s website for 12- and 24-month plans purchased with PayPal, Apple Pay, or a credit card.
Once you get your trial subscription, you can use Surfshark VPN on any device — Windows, iOS, Android, smart TVs, Chrome, etc. — with up to 3 devices allowed during the trial. After the trial, you’ll be able to use Surfshark on an unlimited number of devices.
NOTE: We also offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. Once the trial ends, your payment is charged, and you can still request a full refund within 30 days.
How to sign up for a free trial
Navigate to Surfshark VPN free trial page and click on Start free trial.
Select any 12 or 24-month duration plan and click Start free trial.
Enter the email address for your Surfshark account, continue with Google/Apple, or log in to an already existing account, and, most importantly, choose PayPal or Credit card as the payment method.
How to cancel my free VPN trial?
You can cancel your free trial anytime in your Surfshark account: log in to your account on the website, select your profile icon in the top right corner, choose Subscription from the menu, and disable auto-renewal to stop future payments. To get a refund after the 7-day trial, contact Surfshark customer support via live chat and request your refund, stating that you are still within the 30-day money-back guarantee period.
