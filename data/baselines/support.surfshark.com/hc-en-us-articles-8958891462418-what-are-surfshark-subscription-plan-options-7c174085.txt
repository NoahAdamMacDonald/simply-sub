What are Surfshark subscription plan options?
Updated:
June 15, 2026 09:38
In this article, you will learn about Surfshark subscription plan options.
You can buy a subscription directly from the Surfshark plans & pricing page. Surfshark offers the following plans:
Surfshark Starter
Surfshark One
Surfshark One+
Surfshark VPN
Alternative ID
Surfshark VPN
Surfshark Alert
Surfshark Search
Surfshark Antivirus
Alternative ID
Surfshark VPN
Surfshark Alert
Surfshark Search
Surfshark Antivirus
Alternative ID
Incogni
We have 1-month, 12-month, and 24-month subscriptions with discounts that apply to all of the above-mentioned Surfshark bundles. Learn more about each Surfshark plan to choose the one that suits you best.
You can upgrade any subscription plan with Alternative Number to get a virtual phone number service.
Check out how Surfshark compares to other VPNs.
You can buy plans directly on the App Store or Google Play Store if you are using an Android or an iOS device.
If you have already purchased a subscription but it is not showing up as an active one, try logging out of your account. Once you do, log back in. If that does not help, see the expired subscription error article for more information.
Lastly, for information about any potential fees or taxes, visit Surfshark Terms Of Service, section 4.
