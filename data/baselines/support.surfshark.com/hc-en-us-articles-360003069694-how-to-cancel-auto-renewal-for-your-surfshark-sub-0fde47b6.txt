How to cancel auto-renewal for your Surfshark subscription
Updated:
September 11, 2026 12:28
Auto‑renewal can be canceled at any time to prevent future charges. Canceling auto‑renewal does not end access immediately — the subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period.
The cancellation steps depend on where the subscription was purchased.
Cancel a subscription purchased on the Surfshark website
Auto‑renewal can be canceled directly from the Surfshark account. Here's how:
Log in to the Surfshark account page.
Click the email address in the top‑right corner and select Subscription:
Open the Payments section:
Click Cancel auto‑renewal, then confirm:
Once completed, automatic payments will stop. The subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period.
NOTE: If the option to cancel auto‑renewal is not visible, the subscription may have been purchased through an app store.
Alternatively, you can cancel your subscription by reaching out to our Customer Support team via the live chat.
Subscription purchased through an app store
Subscriptions purchased through app stores are managed by the platform used for payment. Auto‑renewal must be canceled directly through that platform.
Google Play Store
Follow Google’s instructions for canceling a subscription.
Apple App Store
Subscriptions can be managed and canceled in the Apple account settings.
Amazon Appstore
Subscriptions can be canceled through the Amazon account used for purchase.
NOTE: After auto‑renewal is canceled, access to the service remains available until the subscription expires.
What happens after auto‑renewal is canceled
The subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period
No further payments are charged unless auto‑renewal is turned back on
Refunds are not provided for the unused part of an active subscription period.
NOTE: Cancelling your subscription's auto-renewal does not automatically grant a refund – it stops your subscription's auto-renewal and recurring payments. If you purchased our services within the last 30 days and wish to receive a refund, please refer to our article on Surfshark refund policy.
