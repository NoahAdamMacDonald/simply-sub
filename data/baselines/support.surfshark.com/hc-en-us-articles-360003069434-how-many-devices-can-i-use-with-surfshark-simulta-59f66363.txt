How many devices can I use with Surfshark simultaneously?
Updated:
July 08, 2025 07:46
You can download the Surfshark VPN app on an unlimited number of devices. Since we do not have any bandwidth limitations, the number of devices used with one subscription does not affect the connection speed.
Can I share Surfshark with my family?
Yes, you can share a Surfshark subscription with your family. We offer a single subscription that allows unlimited simultaneous connections on different devices, making it suitable for sharing within a household. You can share your account details with family members so they can also log in and use the VPN.
Is there a Surfshark device limit based on a subscription plan?
Surfshark Starter, One, and One+ allow an unlimited number of devices to connect simultaneously under a single subscription. This means you can protect all your devices, such as phones, laptops, and smart TVs, with a single subscription.
What devices can I use Surfshark on?
Surfshark VPN can be used on a wide range of devices, including computers (Windows, macOS, Linux), mobile devices (Android, iOS), smart TVs (Apple TV, Fire TV, Android TV), and routers. Additionally, browser extensions are available for Chrome, Firefox, and Edge. Surfshark Antivirus is currently available on Android, Windows, and macOS devices.
DISCLAIMER: Although one of the core premises of Surfshark is an unlimited number of simultaneous connections (except for Antivirus service), some users might exploit it. Their actions might harm the quality of our services and the experience for the rest of our users. Such users can include, but are not limited to, unauthorized resellers, organizers of illicit activities involving a very large number of devices, and others.
