Nugs.net Enterprises, Inc. Cookie Policy
Information for All Users
Nugs.net Enterprises, Inc. and its affiliates (“Nugs.net Enterprises, Inc.,” “we”) value the privacy of individuals who use our websites, app, and related services (collectively, our “Services”). This Cookie Policy explains how we and our third party partners use cookies, pixel tags, and similar technologies (collectively, “cookies”) to collect information from the users of our Services (“Users” or “you”). When you use our Services, we may automatically collect certain information via cookies, such as your IP address, web browser, device type, details of other websites you visit just before or after using our website, and information about your interactions with our Services (e.g., the date and time of your visit to our website and where you clicked while you were there).
By using our Services, you agree to the collection, use, disclosure, and processing of your information as described by this Cookie Policy. Beyond this Cookie Policy, your use of our Services is also subject to our Nugs.net Terms of Service (https://www.nugs.net/terms.html) and any additional agreements you may enter into with us. This Cookie Policy details our privacy practices surrounding our use of cookies, but it does not address our collection and use of information in other contexts.
How We Use Cookies
Cookies are small text files containing a string of alphanumeric characters that are placed on your device when you use our Services. We (or our third-party partners) may use cookies to collect information as described below. We may use both session cookies and persistent cookies. A session cookie disappears after you close your browser. A persistent cookie remains after you close your browser and may be used by your browser on subsequent visits to our Services. We may use the following types of cookies:
Functional Cookies: Functional Cookies, also referred to as “Essential” or “Strictly Necessary” Cookies, are cookies that we have to use in order for our Services to function. We cannot provide you with the Services without this type of cookie.
Analytical Cookies: Analytical Cookies, also referred to as “Performance Cookies,” capture statistical data that shows us how our Users navigate and interact with our Services. We then use that information to maintain and improve upon the Services.
Advertising Cookies: Advertising Cookies are cookies that show you ads we think may interest you. For example, we may use Advertising Cookies to show you ads for our products and services on other websites, or to show you ads for third party products or services on our website. Advertising Cookies may collect personal data over time and across different websites.
Our Partners
We use analytics services to collect and process certain analytics data, as detailed below. We also work with third-party services to conduct advertising via cookies. These third parties may also collect information about your use of other websites, apps, and online resources. The services we use include:
Google - We may use Google’s services to collect and process analytics data about how our Users use our Services and to place ads that we think may interest our Users and potential users.
For more information, see Google’s Privacy & Terms page.
Adobe - We may use Adobe’s services to collect and process analytics data about how our Users use our Services.
For more information, see Adobe’s Privacy Policy and Analytics Privacy Overview.
Meta - We may use Meta’s services to place ads that we think may interest our users and potential users across Meta’s various websites, such as Facebook and Instagram.
For more information, see Meta’s Data Policy and Privacy Center.
LinkedIn - We use LinkedIn’s services to place ads that we think may interest our users and potential users, as well as to advertise openings to potential employees.
For more information, see LinkedIn’s Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.
Microsoft - We use Microsoft’s services to place ads that we think may interest our users and potential users.
For more information, see Microsoft’s Advertising Policies.
Please note that this list may be updated from time to time to provide you with the latest information.
Your Choices
Cookies. Most web browsers allow you to manage cookies through the browser settings. To find out more about cookies, you can visit www.aboutcookies.org or www.allaboutcookies.org.
Our Partners. You can learn more about Google’s privacy practices and your options about how they use your information on Google’s website. You can also install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Meta, the parent company of Facebook, provides information about how it uses the information it collects through our Services in its Data Policy. You can also learn specifically about Facebook’s advertising practices on its website. Some of our advertising partners are members of the Network Advertising Initiative or the Digital Advertising Alliance. You can visit those organizations’ websites to learn about how you may opt out of receiving web-based personalized ads from their member companies. You can also access any settings offered by your mobile operating system to limit ad tracking. To inquire about your choices regarding our business partners generally, contact us at privacy@nugs.net.
