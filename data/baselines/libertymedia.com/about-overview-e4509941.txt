The Pinnacle of Motorsport
Liberty Media Corporation consists of our wholly-owned subsidiaries Formula 1®, MotoGP™, and other minority investments.
The Series A and Series C Liberty Formula One common stock trade on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the stock symbols FWONA and FWONK, respectively. The Series B Liberty Formula One common stock is quoted on the OTC Markets under the symbol FWONB.
About Formula 1®
Formula 1 holds exclusive commercial rights to the FIA Formula One World Championship, an annual, approximately nine-month-long, motor racing competition in which teams compete for the Constructors’ Championship and drivers compete for the Drivers’ Championship. Formula 1 racing began in 1950 and is the world's premier motor racing competition. The 2025 FIA Formula One World Championship™ marks Formula 1's 75th anniversary and runs from March to December, spanning 24 races across 21 countries and five continents.

About MotoGP™
MotoGP Sports Entertainment Group, S.L. holds exclusive commercial rights to the FIM MotoGP™ World Championship as well as MotoGP feeder series Moto2™ and Moto3™, electric series MotoE™, the FIM Superbike World Championship and the FIM Women’s Circuit Racing World Championship. MotoGP is the world’s leading motorcycle racing championship, featuring top speeds above 360 kilometers per hour (223 miles per hour) and lean angles of over 60 degrees. The 2025 FIM MotoGP World Championship spans across 18 countries and five continents.
