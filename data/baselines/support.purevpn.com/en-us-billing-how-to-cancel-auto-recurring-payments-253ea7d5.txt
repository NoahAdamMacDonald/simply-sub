How to cancel Auto-Recurring payments
For the User's convenience, PureVPN offers various Auto-recurring payment options. Follow this guide to cancel your auto-recurring payments.
Written By Lorenzo Vincent
Updated at January 13th, 2026
feedbacks#update" data-helpjuice-element="Article Content View" data-helpjuice-attributes="visibility,background"> At PureVPN, we understand that different users have different preferences when it comes to paying for our services. That's why we offer a variety of payment methods, including PayPal and credit card, and allow users to set their preferences for auto-recurring payments. Many of our users find this feature convenient because it allows them to keep using our services uninterrupted. However, we also give users the option to opt out of auto-recurring payments at any time by following these simple steps:
Log in to your Member Area account with your email and password.
Click Subscriptions section.
Click the drop-down arrow. and click Cancel Subscription.
Delete Note: If you're using a trial account with PureVPN and cannot locate the cancellation button on your members area page, please contact our 24/7 Customer Support team for help.
If you cancel your subscription, your connection will be left unprotected and vulnerable to security threats. We strongly recommend against canceling your subscription if you value your online security.
You will be asked for the reason and then click continue cancellation.
