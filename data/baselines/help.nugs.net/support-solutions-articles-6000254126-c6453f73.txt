Can I gift a subscription?
Modified on Tue, 17 Feb at 11:03 AM
Unfortunately, it is not possible to "gift" streaming subscriptions directly at this time. A valid credit card is needed to start a new subscription.
The only way to gift a subscription is to purchase a gift card for Apple or Google Play or a Prepaid Credit Card and have your loved ones pay for their subscription using that.
Promotional subscriptions are only available directly on our website, not in app using Apple or Google.
