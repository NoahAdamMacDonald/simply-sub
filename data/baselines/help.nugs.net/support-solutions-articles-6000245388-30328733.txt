How do I delete my account?
Modified on Tue, 20 Sep, 2022 at 11:01 AM
Once your account has been deleted it can no longer be restored - and all of the items in your Library will no longer be available to stream.
To delete your free account entirely follow these steps:
-Click the Settings button in the top right corner of the nugs.net mobile app
-Click Account
-Click Delete Account
-Follow the prompts to complete your account deletion.
If you have any problems please Open a Support Ticket to request account deletion.
We will check your account to ensure that you do not have any active subscriptions or streaming audio that you are not aware of, and delete your account entirely upon your confirmation.
