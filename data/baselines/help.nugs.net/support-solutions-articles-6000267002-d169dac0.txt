How do I create a free account?
Modified on Tue, 3 Dec, 2024 at 7:12 PM
With a free nugs.net account you can purchase CDs, audio downloads, Pay-Per-View Livestreams and/or subscriptions.
A nugs.net subscription is not required to access the shows in your Library, Free Audio Livestreams, Featured shows or nugs radio.
Go to nugs.net/register
Enter your first and last name, a valid email address and select your password - or you can login using Facebook or Google.
*If you use one of the social login options an account will be created automatically with your email address and a password will be auto-generated.
