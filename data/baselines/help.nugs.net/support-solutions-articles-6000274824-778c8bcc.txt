How many devices can I stream on?
Modified on Sat, 7 Sep, 2024 at 6:57 PM
You can access streaming content on any single device at a time.
You may switch from one device to another by starting streaming playback on a new device. This will stop the stream on your first device. Streaming on multiple devices simultaneously is not permitted
If you want to stream on multiple devices simultaneously you need to have multiple subscriptions. We do not offer multi-user subscriptions at this time.
Your subscription is intended for personal use. Sharing your login credentials is prohibited as stated in our Terms of Use (section 2.4 http://nugs.net/terms.aspx).
