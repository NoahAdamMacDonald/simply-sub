How do I get the discounted nugs + Play Dead bundle?
Modified on Wed, 22 Jul at 8:50 PM
If you're a current nugs subscriber*, you can add Play Dead to your subscription for just $4.99/month or $49.99/year - normally $9.99/month or $99.99/year. (*limited time discounted nugs subscriptions are not eligible for the bundle discount)
To access this discounted bundle, you’ll need to subscribe directly through nugs.net (not via Apple, Google, or Play Dead).
New to nugs & Play Dead: Get the bundle HERE. New nugs users also save on their first year of streaming.
For current nugs web subscribers: You can add-on Play Dead anytime in your account settings.
