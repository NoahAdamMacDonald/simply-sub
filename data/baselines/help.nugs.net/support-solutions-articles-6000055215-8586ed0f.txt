What is the nugs.net app?
Modified on Tue, 17 Feb at 11:06 AM
With the nugs.net mobile app for Android or iOS nugs subscribers get on-demand access to thousands of live recordings from the nugs streaming catalog. Listen to full shows on demand, make playlists of your favorite live tracks, read and post reviews, save shows for offline playback, and watch a selection of archival video. New shows are added daily and we are always working on adding new artists to the catalog.
With a nugs Hi-Res subscription you can enable lossless (16-bit 44.1 kHz) playback or MQA, and listen to Sony 360 audio on compatible devices.
Non-subscribers can listen to nugs.net radio for free, and stream cherry-picked full length concerts on demand. Featured shows rotate weekly.
