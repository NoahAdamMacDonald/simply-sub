What is nugs and how much does it cost?
Modified on Tue, 3 Nov at 9:44 AM
nugs subscriptions are $14.99/month or $149.99/year for MP3 quality audio streaming and HD video.
nugs Hi-Res subscriptions are also available for $24.99/month or $249.99/year with 4K video (when available), lossless 16-bit 44.1 kHz streaming audio, MQA & 360 Reality Audio on select shows.
---
A subscription to nugs gives you access - through our mobile app on iOS or Android devices, from a Mac or PC using a browser, Sonos or BluOS - to streaming audio recordings of soundboard masters from 30,000+ live shows, with new shows added daily. Subscribers can create custom playlists and/or save audio for offline streaming on your mobile device.
You also get access to Subscriber Exclusive Livestreams (in HD quality) and 1500+ exclusive archived videos (see a list of compatible devices HERE)
nugs subscriptions also include a 15% discount on download or CD purchases and Pay-Per-View Livestreams (must be purchased on nugs.net - purchases made in-app will NOT be discounted). *Subscriber discounts can not be combined with any other promotional discounts.
With a nugs Hi-Res subscription, you get access to all of the above + 4K video (when available), Lossless Hi-Res streaming audio and 20% off download or CD purchases, and Pay-Per-View Livestreams.
---
nugs subscriptions do not give you access to Pay-Per-View Livestreams. Pay-per-views can be purchased separately at nugs.net or directly through the nugs app. Purchased Livestreams can be accessed using your nugs.net account on any supported device.
New users can try a 7-day free trial on get.nugs.net
