Alibaba Group Will Announce September Quarter 2026 Results on November 19, 2026
November 6, 2026
Hong Kong, China, November 6, 2026 – Alibaba Group Holding Limited (NYSE: BABA and HKEX: 9988 (HKD Counter) and 89988 (RMB Counter), “Alibaba” or “Alibaba Group”) today announced that it will report its unaudited financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 before the U.S. market opens on Thursday, November 19, 2026, and will hold a conference call to discuss the financial results at 7:00 a.m. U.S. Eastern Time (8:00 p.m. Hong Kong Time) the same day.
All participants must pre-register to join this conference call using the Participant Registration link below:
English: https://s1.c-conf.com/diamondpass/10057884-hef5ez.html
Chinese: https://s1.c-conf.com/diamondpass/10057886-qbjxzc.html
Upon registration, each participant will receive details for the conference call, including dial-in numbers, conference call passcode and a unique access PIN. To join the conference, please dial the number provided, enter the passcode followed by your PIN, and you will join the conference.
A live webcast of the earnings conference call can be accessed at https://www.alibabagroup.com/en/ir/earnings. An archived webcast will be available through the same link following the call. A replay of the conference call will be available for one week from the date of the conference (Dial-in number: +1 855 883 1031; English conference PIN 10057884; Chinese conference PIN 10057886).
Please visit Alibaba Group’s Investor Relations website at https://www.alibabagroup.com/en/ir/home on November 19, 2026to view the earnings release and accompanying slides prior to the conference call.
