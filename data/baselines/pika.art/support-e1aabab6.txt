Billing and Credits
Credits vary by feature, resolution and length — and your invoices are under Subscription → Payment methods → Manage in Stripe.
Pika emails you a copy of the invoice for your subscription as soon as you subscribe. If it isn't in your inbox, check your spam folder.
If you need to change details on an invoice, use the Update Information option — we can't edit invoices that have already been issued.
Refunds: subscription payments and credit packs are non-refundable, and there is no refund for the unused part of a billing period.
Credits: unused plan credits don't carry over; they refresh every month, on annual plans too. Top-up credits you buy separately carry over and expire 365 days after purchase.
1Go to pika.art/subscription.
2Find the Payment methods section.
3Click Manage in Stripe to open the billing portal.
4Scroll to Invoice History and click the payment you want to download.
People usually ask these next
It says I have no active subscription.
Nearly always this means you are signed in to a different account from the one you subscribed with — a Google sign-in and an Apple sign-in on the same address are two separate accounts. Check which one you are in before writing to us.
Can I get a refund?
No. Subscription payments and credit packs are non-refundable. If you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for.
Will my unused credits carry over?
Unused plan credits refresh every month; they don't carry over. Top-up credits you buy separately carry over and expire 365 days after purchase.

Cancel plan
Subscription → Adjust plan → Downgrade to free → Cancel plan. You keep what you have already paid for until the period ends.
To cancel a subscription purchased on the web, go to pika.art/subscription, select Adjust plan, choose Downgrade to free, then follow the steps and confirm with Cancel plan.
If you subscribed on old.pika.art, your plan carried over. Standard now appears as Starter and Pro as Creator, and you cancel it the same way.
If you can't find these controls, check that you're signed in to the account with the subscription. If you're still unsure, contact us below so we can help confirm your subscription status.
If you subscribed through our iOS app, your subscription page shows Billed through the App Store. Manage or cancel it in the App Store instead: Manage in App Store on that page takes you there.
After you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the current payment cycle, then it won't renew automatically.
If what you actually want is to delete your entire account, write to us again saying so — and make sure you're reaching us from the account in question.
People usually ask these next
Will I lose access right away?
No. Cancelling stops the next renewal; your plan stays usable until the end of the cycle you've already paid for.
What happens to my credits when I change plan?
Your allowance becomes the new plan's allowance. Top-up credits you bought separately stay with you until they expire, 365 days after purchase.

Stuck generation
Reload and check your library — most stuck generations have actually finished. If credits went missing, tell us.
At busy times a generation can wait in the queue for a while. Refreshing the page or restarting the app won't speed a queued job up.
If a generation seems stuck, give it a little time and check your library — it often finishes there. We'll flag persistent problems to the team, but we can't promise to chase down every slow generation.
If credits were taken for a generation you never received, that one we can look into — tell us roughly when and what you were making.

Tell us where it happened and send a screenshot. Those two things identify almost every bug straight away.
Sorry you hit a snag. To investigate, we mainly need two things: any error message you're seeing, and a screenshot of it.
It also helps to know where it happened and what you were doing just before — there's a field for that below, and the message box for the rest.

Delete account
A person handles deletion and confirms with you first. If you only want the charges to stop, cancelling does that and keeps your videos.
If what you really want is to stop being charged, you don't need to delete your account — just cancel the plan (that's the Cancel plan topic) and keep your videos.
Deleting an account is permanent and can't be undone. Everything created under it is lost, and it can't be recovered afterwards.
Because it's permanent, a person handles it. To go ahead, write to us from the account you want deleted — we can only act on a request that comes from the account's own email.
People usually ask these next
Can I get my videos out first?
We can't export your assets for you, so download anything you want to keep before you ask us to delete — once it's gone it's gone.
What if I only want to stop paying?
Then cancel the plan instead (see Cancel plan). Your account and videos stay, and you just won't be charged again.

Watermark
Every current plan, including Free, exports without a watermark. Upgrading doesn't change videos you already made.
Every current plan, including Free, has no watermark on your outputs.
The Starter, Creator and Fancy plans include a commercial license. The Free plan doesn't grant commercial rights.
Watermark removal and commercial terms only apply to videos created under a plan that includes those benefits. Anything you generated earlier with a watermark keeps it — upgrading doesn't reach back and change files you already made.
People usually ask these next
I upgraded but an old video still has a watermark.
That's expected — upgrading doesn't remove watermarks from videos you already made. Generate it again for a version without a watermark.
Can I use my videos commercially?
Commercial use is included on the Starter, Creator and Fancy plans. The Free plan doesn't grant commercial rights.

API
dev.pika.art has the docs and access. For volume or commercial terms, ask us below.
Pika has an API for developers. Everything — documentation, keys and access — lives at dev.pika.art, so start there; it answers most integration questions directly.
If your question isn't covered there — volume, rate limits or commercial terms — tick the box below and tell us what you're building, and we'll get it to the right people.
