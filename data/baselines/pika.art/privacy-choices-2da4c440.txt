Your Privacy Choices
Pika shares information about your visits with Google and Meta to measure its ads. Privacy laws can treat this as selling or sharing personal information. Turn off ad measurement to opt out. Your choice is saved in this browser only. Read more in our Privacy Policy.
Ad measurement
Lets Pika use Google Analytics, Google Ads and the Meta Pixel to learn which ads bring people to Pika and which visits end in a purchase. Turning it off stops the Google and Meta tags and stops sending your checkouts to Google and Meta from this browser.
