How to pay for Netflix
Netflix membership plans are billed monthly on the date you sign up. We do not offer annual, multi-month, or lifetime memberships.
​
There are multiple options to pay for Netflix. We may offer different payment methods depending on the country you’re in.
Credit and Debit Cards
We accept the following cards, which must be enabled for recurring e-commerce transactions.
NOTE: You may need to enable your card for international transactions.
Visa
MasterCard
American Express
Virtual Cards
We accept virtual cards in select markets. If your virtual card is declined, please choose a different payment method.
Prepaid Cards
We accept prepaid cards with the following logos.
Visa
MasterCard
American Express
Partner Payments
The following partners allow you to add Netflix to their bill or offer a package with Netflix included.
Spark Package
