Netflix payment options
A country must be selected to view content in this article.
Netflix membership plans are billed monthly on the date you sign up. We do not offer annual, multi-month, or lifetime memberships.
​
There are multiple options to pay for Netflix. We may offer different payment methods depending on the country you’re in.
​ Credit & Debit Cards
We accept credit, debit, and virtual cards, as well as co-branded cards issued by local banks with the following logos:
Visa
MasterCard
American Express
Discover
​
Your card must be enabled for recurring automatic e-commerce payments. If your card was issued outside your current country, you may need to enable international or cross-border transactions through your bank.
​
​ Cash Options & Gift Cards
While we don't accept physical cash, you can pay with cash by purchasing a Netflix gift card or a reloadable prepaid card, or by using local over-the-counter (OTC) cash outlets in select regions. Available options are:
Netflix gift cards: Buy a Netflix gift card at a participating retailer, then redeem the code on your account page to apply the balance to your monthly bill. You can redeem multiple gift cards on your account.
Prepaid cards: We accept prepaid cards featuring Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover logos (must support recurring e-commerce billing).
​
Digital & Bank Payments
Depending on your region, you can link these supported digital wallets or direct bank payment options for seamless monthly billing:
PayPal
