PRIVACY ARCHITECTURE
The Venice proxy is at the core of every privacy mode
Every AI interaction on Venice passes through the same proxy architecture. Your conversation history stays on your device. Requests are relayed through the proxy without being stored. The four privacy modes build on top of this foundation, adding progressively stronger protections at the GPU layer.
VENICE PROXY
FOUR PRIVACY MODES
Every model on Venice falls into one of four privacy categories. Each comes with different protections and trade-offs.
Anonymous
Venice proxies your request to leading frontier model providers. Your identity is obscured from the provider, but you should assume the provider is storing your content.
+
Access to leading frontier models (GPT, Claude, Gemini)
−
Provider sees and likely saves your prompt
Private
DEFAULT
Inference runs on Venice-controlled GPUs or zero-data-retention partner infrastructure. Privacy is enforced by contractual commitments — no prompt or response is stored.
+
No data retention, wide model selection (including Grok)
−
Trust-based — relies on Venice and partners honoring contracts
TEE
PRO
Trusted Execution Environment. Inference runs inside a hardware-isolated enclave operated by external TEE partners (NEAR AI Cloud and Phala Network). GPU providers cannot access your prompts. Verified by remote attestation.
+
Hardware-verified privacy, GPU operator cannot see prompts
−
Responses may be slower; fewer models available
E2EE
PRO
End-to-End Encrypted. Your prompt is encrypted on your device, remains encrypted through Venice, and is only decrypted inside a verified TEE. Verifiably and entirely private from all parties.
+
Strongest privacy — no one (including Venice) can see your data
−
No web search or memory; responses may be slower
