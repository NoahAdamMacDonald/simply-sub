Frequently asked questions
Is LastPass free?
Yes, you can get LastPass Free and use LastPass on a single device type to store an unlimited number of passwords in your encrypted, secure vault.
What does LastPass Premium have that the Free version does not?
LastPass Premium goes beyond storing passwords and provides complete protection of your digital life. LastPass Premium includes advanced features like unlimited device type access, one-to-many sharing, unlimited file storage, emergency access, advanced multifactor options, and personal support managed by the LastPass customer care team.
How long can I use LastPass for free?
You can use LastPass Free for as long as you’d like. As a new user, when you sign up you also get a 30-day trial of LastPass Premium and can upgrade to Premium anytime.
Does LastPass have a password manager for families?
Yes, LastPass Families includes all of the features in Premium plus access for up to 6 users, unlimited shared folders, and a family manager dashboard.
