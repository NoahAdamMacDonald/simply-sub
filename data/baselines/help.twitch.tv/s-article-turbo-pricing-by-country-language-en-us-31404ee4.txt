Frequently Asked Questions
Why do monthly prices vary by country?
We have adjusted Turbo prices globally as part of our efforts to help creators build and grow their communities worldwide.
My country is not listed. Have Turbo prices changed for me?
If your country is not listed in the tables above, the monthly price for Turbo in your region is $11.99 USD.
If your Turbo price went up, and you wish to keep your Turbo sub:
Visit twitch.tv/turbo
Opt into the updated price
If your Turbo price went up, and you don't want to keep your Turbo sub:
Cancel your Turbo subscription at twitch.tv/subscriptions -> Turbo Subscriptions
Or (existing subscribers) wait for your locked-in price to expire August 31st.
If your Turbo price went down, and you wish to keep your Turbo sub:
Cancel your existing Turbo subscription at twitch.tv/subscriptions -> Turbo Subscriptions
Visit twitch.tv/turbo
Opt into the updated price
If your Turbo price went down, and you don't want to keep your Turbo sub:
Cancel your existing Turbo subscription at twitch.tv/subscriptions -> Turbo Subscriptions
Or wait for your locked-in price to expire August 31st.
