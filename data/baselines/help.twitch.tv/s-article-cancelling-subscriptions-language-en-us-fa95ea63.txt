Cancelling Subscriptions on Twitch
Paid subscriptions are monthly charges which, unless specified, will continue to renew. You may cancel a paid subscription at any time by navigating to your Subscriptions Management Page, locating the subscription you wish to cancel, and clicking the cog on the upper-right. From there, select Don't Renew Subscription, and confirming your cancellation on the next page.
You must select Don’t Renew Subscription again on this page in order to complete the cancellation.
To help Creators plan or make improvements to their channel, Twitch may share the reasons you cancel your subscription with the Creator in an aggregated and anonymous manner. Learn more about your Privacy Choices on Twitch here.
After successful cancellation, you will notice that your subscription now states a “Sub End Date.” You will continue to enjoy your current subscription benefits (including emote use and channel badge) until this date, after which point your subscription will expire, and your benefits will end.
Change your mind? As long as you resubscribe within 7 days of your subscription benefits ending, you will not notice a break in your sub streak anniversary.
Android & iOS Devices
If you started your Subscription through a mobile device (iOS & Android) the process is different. You will need to cancel the Subscription either in the Twitch Mobile App, or the platform-specific app store or website depending on your device.
iOS Device owners may use the Apple website or the Apple App store.
Android Device owners may the Google Play website while signed into their Google account, or the Google Play subscription center on your Android device.
More information on how to do this can be found here here.
Turbo
If you are attempting to cancel a Turbo subscription, you will find this under Other Subscriptions on your Subscriptions Management page.
Twitch Prime
Twitch Prime channel subscriptions can’t be cancelled once the Subscription is spent. You may redeem your Twitch Prime subscription (if applicable) again the following month. More information on Twitch Prime Subscriptions check out our Twitch Prime Subscription guide.
About Bulk Cancellations
It is not possible to mass-cancel all your subscriptions at once, through Twitch's subscription management interfaces.
