Spending Twitch Gift Cards
A Twitch Gift Card is a prepaid digital code or pin-on-receipt of value, redeemable only on Twitch.tv. Redeeming a Twitch Gift Card to your account adds value to your gift card balance, located in your Twitch Wallet. Spend gift card balance on Subs, Bits, or Turbo. You do not need a Twitch account to buy a Gift Card for someone else, but you need a Twitch account to redeem its balance.
For help with spending gift card balance, visit the troubleshooting section below.
Need a gift card? Learn how to purchase here, or if you have already received a gift card, learn how to redeem the balance to your account.

Spending Twitch Gift Cards
To spend Twitch gift card balance, you must add the value of a gift card to your Wallet. For questions on how to redeem a gift card, visit our guide here.
What can Twitch gift balance can be used towards?
Gift card balance is available for use only on twitch.tv as per our gift card legal policy. Once you redeem your Twitch gift card, you can purchase channel subscriptions or gift subscriptions for your favorite streamers. Bits can also be purchased with Twitch gift cards.

Where can Twitch gift cards be used?
Twitch gift cards can only be redeemed in the country they were purchased from. In other words, the currency of your gift card must match your country of residence. Buying a gift card in Euro while living in the UK means you won't be able to redeem the balance. If you’re buying a gift card for someone else, make sure you’re purchasing them a gift card in a currency they can use! Customers will be asked to enter their country of residence at time of redemption.
Gift cards in US dollars can currently be redeemed by U.S. residents.
Gift cards in British pounds can only be redeemed by UK residents.
Gift cards in Canadian Dollars can only be redeemed by residents of Canada
Gift cards in Australian Dollars can only be redeemed by residents of Australia
Gift cards in Euro can only be redeemed by residents in these 25 European countries: Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia and Spain.
How do I apply my gift card balance to a purchase after redeeming a Twitch Gift Card?
If you have a gift card balance in your Wallet, we automatically apply those funds to purchases you make on twitch.tv. If the cost of your purchase exceeds your gift card balance, we will charge the additional amount to your saved payment method. If you do not have a saved payment method then you will be prompted to add one before you can proceed with your purchase.
Do I need to have a stored payment method (like a credit or debit card) to use a gift card?
Using an additional payment method is only required if the cost of your purchase exceeds your gift card balance. You don’t need to have a stored payment method associated with your account to use the balance of a redeemed Twitch gift card.
Will my Gift Card ever expire?
No! These funds are yours for as long as your Twitch account is active. Occasionally, Twitch may award you promotional credit, separate from gift card balance, as part of a promotion. This separate promotional credit may expire, but does not impact your existing gift card balance.
Twitch Gift Cards & Mobile Devices
Can I spend my gift card balance on the Twitch mobile apps?
Currently your gift card balance cannot be used for making a purchase on Twitch mobile apps. However, if you use your balance to purchase a product on the twitch.tv website, those purchases and benefits (e.g. subscription emotes, subscription benefits, Bits balance) will be available on the mobile apps as well.
I only have a mobile device. How can I redeem and spend my gift card on mobile?
You can redeem gift cards purchased on Amazon by clicking on the gift card code in the email that you would have received (or by visiting twitch.tv/redeem via your mobile browser). These instructions are not applicable for physical gift cards.
Unfortunately, gift card balance is not available for usage in Twitch Mobile Apps. You can access and use your gift card balance by visiting the following pages in your mobile browser (you may be asked to log-in to your Twitch account):
bits.twitch.tv
subs.twitch.tv
If you use your gift card balance to purchase a product on the mobile web then those benefits (e.g. subscription emotes, subscription benefits, Bits balance) will be available on the mobile apps and on twitch.tv.
Troubleshooting Gift Card Spending
I redeemed a gift card, but do not see my balance.
Check that the code was redeemed to the correct account. Review your gift card balance at twitch.tv/wallet or during checkout on any channel. Someone else with access to your computer, account, or device may have logged into another account.
I redeemed a gift card, but can’t spend the balance.
You can only apply your gift card balance to purchases denominated in the same currency. IE, gift card balance in Canadian Dollars can only be applied towards CAD purchases. Ensure you are redeeming gift card balance for purchases in the same currency.
Do you have a saved payment method associated with your account that is in a currency different from your gift card balance? If so, you will not be able to apply that gift card balance until you remove that saved payment method. Xsolla users, check your stored payment methods and more at twitch.tv/payments (payment history)
Remember, gift card balance is currently unavailable for in-app purchase in the Twitch Mobile App. Therefore, you will not be able to apply your gift card balance for purchasing subscriptions or Bits on Apple or Android devices.
If the total amount or your purchase exceeds your gift card balance, you may only use the following payment methods for the remainder: Amazon Pay (US only), Credit Card, PayPal. Your gift card balance will not be applied to a purchase if you try to use a different payment method.
For your security, we may “freeze” your gift card balance in your Wallet. If you think that this was done in error then please contact our Customer Support team from help.twitch.tv. If use of your gift card balance is frozen, your ability to redeem gift cards is not impeded.
If you have more than 1 currency in your gift card balance, we will apply the applicable balance based on your local currency determined using your location and payment method details. If you have permanently moved to a new country and are not able to use your gift card balance then please reach out to our support team through help.twitch.tv.
How do I check my gift card balance?
When you’re logged in, you can find your gift card balance, Payment history, and more in Account Dropdown Menu > Wallet.
How do I convert my existing subscriptions to use my gift card balance (managed through my Wallet) instead of my saved payment method?
This is done automatically! Once you have a gift card balance those funds will automatically be used the next time your subscription renews. If the cost of your subscription(s) exceeds your gift card balance then we will charge the additional amount to your saved payment method.
Currently, we support this capability for subscriptions that have been purchased using the following payment instruments: Amazon Pay (US only), Credit Card, PayPal.
If you used a different payment instrument to purchase your subscription then that subscription will continue to be charged to that payment instrument and your gift card balance will not be applied.
