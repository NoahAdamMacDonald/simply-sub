Delete My Twitch Account
There are many reasons why you may wish to disable or delete your Twitch account. Deleting and disabling your Twitch account are separate actions.
Disabling your account is a temporary solution if you need a break. This prevents your account from being accessed. You can recover a disabled account.
Deleting your account is a permanent action that wipes your data in accordance with Your Privacy Rights. A deleted account cannot be recovered.
Ready to delete your account? Delete your account now or continue reading to learn more.

Disabling Your Twitch Account
Disabling your Twitch account temporarily deactivates your channel and content from anyone trying to view it. If you change your mind at a later time, you can reactivate your account by simply logging back into your account. Learn more about Account Reactivation.
If you choose to reactivate your account, your user information remains intact.
To disable your Twitch account, click on your Profile icon in the up right corner to open the dropdown menu, and then click on your Profile icon a second time.
In your Profile Settings, scroll down to Disabling Your Twitch Account and click on Disable Account
Make sure you are disabling the correct account, enter a reason for disabling, and then click on Disable Account.
Explaining why you are disabling your account is optional and not required to disable your account.
Deleting Your Twitch Account
There is no reversing Twitch account deletion. If you need a break from Twitch, we recommend you disable your account. Disabling your account is temporary, and can be reversed.
If you no longer wish to maintain your Twitch account, you can permanently delete it. This will delete all your content and profile information including clips, highlights, user rewards, and settings. Within 90 days of your request, Twitch will permanently erase all your content and close your account. The deletion process will terminate if you log back into your account while we still are processing your deletion request.
Deleting your account also stops the processing of new personal data, deletes your profile and other identifying data such as usage data, and user generated content, in accordance with Your Privacy Rights.
Unless required by law or in the case of certain legal claims, Twitch will not retain your content, your contact information, or your username. In some cases, Twitch may retain limited personal data for legal, security and other reasons permitted or required under applicable law.
To delete your Twitch account, visit the Delete Account page.
Make sure you are deleting the correct account, enter a reason for deletion, then click Delete Account.
Providing a reason as to why you’re deleting your account is optional.
Partners and Affiliates
Before a Partner or Affiliate account can be deleted or disabled it must first be offboarded. Please contact Customer Support here for assistance with offboarding.
Frequently Asked Questions
I disabled my account a while ago, and it's not deleted. Why?
Disabled accounts may be purged after a period of time.
What happens to my subscriptions (and other community rewards) when I delete or disable my account?
If you temporarily deactivate your Twitch account, your subscriptions are set to cancel, and Twitch will not attempt to collect payment. You may receive updates about your subscription status by email. If you reactivate your account before your subscriptions expire, you will need to continue those subscriptions to maintain tenure streak and sub benefits.
If you permanently delete your Twitch account, your subscriptions are canceled. After the account deletion process is complete, all data is wiped and you cannot restore your subscriptions.
I have a multi-month subscription towards a creator. What happens to their revenue if I delete my account?
If your deletion request is processed before the final month of your multi-month subscription, the creator will not receive that revenue.
Why don't I have the option to delete/disable my account?
Partners and Affiliates must complete the creator offboarding process before deleting or disabling their account. Please contact Twitch Support here for assistance with offboarding. If you are still having issues, contact us for help.
What happens to my username when I delete my account?
In most cases, if you close one account you will not be able to immediately reuse or transfer that username to a different account. After you delete your account, your old username will be recycled. It will take a minimum of 6 months for this process to complete. Eventually, that username will be added to the pool of available usernames. In most cases this means you cannot rename one account to the name of another account you own.
For more information about our username rename and recycle policy please check out our Username Rename and Recycling Policies article.
How do I restore my Twitch account after I disable it?
If you have chosen to temporarily disable your Twitch account, all you need to do is log back into it to restore your account. Please give us 24-36 hours to restore your account to its prior state. If you have questions about your account after this period, please contact Twitch Support here. Learn more about Account Reactivation.
