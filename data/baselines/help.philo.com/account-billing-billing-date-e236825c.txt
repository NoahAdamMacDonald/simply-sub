How to view and change your billing date
You can view your next billing date under the Your Philo Subscription section of your (Link opens in a new tab)Account page on (Link opens in a new tab)philo.com.
How your billing date is determined
Your billing date corresponds to the day your Philo subscription begins (or the first day of your subscription after your 7-day free trial). From then on, you’ll be charged that same day of each month. For example, if you start your subscription on June 17th, you’ll continue to be charged on the 17th of every month.
If your billing date is scheduled for a date that doesn’t occur every month (like the 31st), you’ll be billed on the last day of that month instead.
How to change your billing date
If you’re currently billed by Philo, reach out to our (Link opens in a new tab)Support team for assistance changing your billing date.
If you're not currently billed by Philo, you'll need to (Link opens in a new tab)switch to Philo billing before we can change your billing date.
