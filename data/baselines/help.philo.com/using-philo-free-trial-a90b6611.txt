7-day Essential free trial
Our 7-day free trial of our (Link opens in a new tab)Essential subscription plan provides access to 70+ live channels, along with thousands of on-demand shows and movies. Plus, you can explore our features and learn how Philo works before committing to a paid subscription!
Our 7-day free trial is available for all new Philo customers, with a limit of one per household. If you’ve already had a free trial or are a current or previous Philo subscriber, you won’t be eligible.
You can cancel anytime before your trial ends for absolutely no charge. If you don’t cancel before the end of your trial, you’ll be charged $25/month (+ taxes where applicable) for our Essential subscription plan. Pricing may vary through third-party billers.
