How to sign up for Philo
Sign up for our (Link opens in a new tab)Essential plan (with a 7-day free trial for new Philo customers!) or our (Link opens in a new tab)Bundle+ plan.
Amazon Fire Tablet (Link opens in a new tab)Download and open the Philo app.
Tap Get started.
Enter your email address and then tap Continue.
Open the link emailed to you and click the Confirm sign-up button.
Select your preferred plan.
Confirm your payment method, and then tap Subscribe.
Amazon Fire TV (Link opens in a new tab)Download and open the Philo app.
Click Get started.
Enter your email address and then Submit.
Open the link emailed to you and click the Confirm sign-up button.
Select your preferred subscription plan.
Follow the on-screen prompts to complete your payment with Amazon, and you're in!

Apple TV (Link opens in a new tab)Download and open the Philo app on your Apple TV.
Click Get started.
Enter your email address and then tap Continue.
Open the link emailed to you and click the Confirm sign-up button.
Select your preferred subscription plan.
Follow the on-screen prompts to complete your payment with Apple.
Click Start subscription, and you’re in!
Computer (philo.com) Visit (Link opens in a new tab)philo.com/pricing and select your preferred subscription plan.
Enter your email address.
Check the Terms of Service & Privacy Policy checkbox and then click Continue.
Click the Sign up for Philo link sent to your email address (or enter the code).
Enter your payment information.
Check the Terms of Service checkbox.
Click Subscribe now and you're in!
Digital wallet
If you’re paying with Apple Pay, make sure you’re using Safari and have Apple Pay enabled on your device.
If you’re paying with Google Pay, make sure you’re using Chrome and have Google Pay enabled on your device. Google Pay won’t appear if you’re on Chrome on iOS 16 with Apple Pay enabled, or in Incognito mode.

LG TV (Link opens in a new tab)Download and open the Philo app.
Click Get started.
Visit (Link opens in a new tab)philo.com/go/activate on your mobile device or computer, and enter the code displayed on your TV.
Click Activate.
Enter your email address, and then click Continue.
Enter the code sent to your email address, and then click Verify.
Select your preferred plan, enter your payment information, and you’re in!

Select your preferred subscription plan.
Follow the on-screen prompts to complete your payment with Roku.
Click Start subscription, and you’re in!
