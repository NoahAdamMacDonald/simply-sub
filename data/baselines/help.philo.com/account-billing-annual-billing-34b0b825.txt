Annual Billing
Customers who are billed directly through us, have the option of paying for Philo once annually, rather than monthly.
If you’re currently billed through a third party (like Apple or Roku), you’ll need to (Link opens in a new tab)switch to Philo billing, and then switch to Annual Billing.
(Link opens in a new tab)Essential is $275.88/year (+ taxes where applicable) with Annual Billing — a yearly savings of $24, if you’re currently billed by Philo, or $72 if you’re currently billed by a third party.
(Link opens in a new tab)Bundle+ is $359.88/year (+ taxes where applicable) with Annual Billing — a yearly savings of $36, if you’re currently billed by Philo, or $96 if you’re currently billed by a third party.
How to switch to Annual Billing
If you’re already subscribed to Philo with monthly billing and would like to switch to Annual Billing, follow the steps below.
Visit your (Link opens in a new tab)Account page on (Link opens in a new tab)philo.com.
Click Manage Plan.
Select the Annual tab at the top of the page.
Select your preferred plan (including any add-on(s)), and then click Review changes.
Review your payment information, check the Terms of Service checkbox, and then click Submit.
Annual Billing is not available for our any of our (Link opens in a new tab)add-ons. If you have an annually billed Essential or Bundle+ plan, your add-on(s) will continue to be billed monthly.
