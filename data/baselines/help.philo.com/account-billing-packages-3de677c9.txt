Subscription plans
TV is personal—your subscription should be, too. Mix and match our base plans and add-ons for the ultimate customized viewing experience!
Check out our complete list of offerings.
Essential
Essential is our $25 base plan and includes 70+ (Link opens in a new tab)channels, 100+ ad-supported (Link opens in a new tab)Free Channels, plus a 7-day free trial for new Philo customers.
You’ll have the option to save and record content for up to 1 year (which means you can skip ads!).
Learn more about Essential (Link opens in a new tab)here.
Bundle+
Bundle+ is our $33 base plan and includes 70+ (Link opens in a new tab)channels, 100+ ad-supported (Link opens in a new tab)Free Channels, tons of additional ad-supported content from AMC+, plus access to the (Link opens in a new tab)HBO Max Basic with Ads plan and (Link opens in a new tab)ad-supported discovery+.
You’ll have the option to save and record content for up to 1 year (which means you can skip ads!).
Learn more about Bundle+ (Link opens in a new tab)here.
Free Channels
Our Free Channels experience includes our full array of 100+ ad-supported (Link opens in a new tab)Free Channels at no cost, with no credit card required.
You’ll have the option to save and record select content for up to 30 days (which means you can skip ads!).
Learn more about our Free Channels experience (Link opens in a new tab)here.
