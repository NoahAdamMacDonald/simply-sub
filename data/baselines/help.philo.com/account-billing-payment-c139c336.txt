How to pay for Philo
You’ll select a payment method when you (Link opens in a new tab)sign up for Philo, and can (Link opens in a new tab)update your payment information at any time.
You can either pay directly through us on (Link opens in a new tab)philo.com, or through one of our third-party billing partners (Amazon, Apple, Google, Roku, Samsung, and Vizio).
If you'd like to subscribe to Philo through an existing account with one of those billers, click your preferred biller below to learn more.
Direct billing payment methods
You can pay for Philo directly through us on (Link opens in a new tab)philo.com, using any of the payment methods below.
Credit and debit cards
We accept credit cards from Visa, Mastercard, Discover, and American Express, and debit cards from Visa, Mastercard, and American Express.
Bank account
We accept direct bank account payments through Stripe’s Link system. This will require a Link account, which you can create when adding your bank information on your (Link opens in a new tab)Account page.
Digital wallet
We accept digital wallet payments through Apple Pay, Cash App, and Google Pay.
To use Apple Pay, you’ll need to access (Link opens in a new tab)philo.com on Safari and have Apple Pay enabled on your device
To use Google Pay, you’ll need to access (Link opens in a new tab)philo.com on Chrome and have Google Pay enabled on your device. Google Pay won’t appear if you’re on Chrome on iOS 16 with Apple Pay enabled, or in Incognito mode.
We aren't currently running any promotions, but we offer a (Link opens in a new tab)7-day free trial for all new Philo subscribers.
Amazon billing If you subscribe directly on your Fire device, you’ll be billed by Amazon using the payment information linked to your Amazon account.
Amazon accepts credit and debit cards from Visa, Mastercard, Discover, and American Express, as well as Amazon Prime Store Cards.
Apple billing If you subscribe directly on your Apple device, you’ll be billed by Apple using the payment information linked to your iCloud account.
Apple accepts most credit and debit cards, as well as Apple Pay.
Google billing If you subscribe directly on your Android device, you’ll be billed by Google using the payment information linked to your Google account.
Google accepts most credit and debit cards, as well as Google Pay.
Roku billing If you subscribe directly on your Roku, you’ll be billed by Roku using the payment information linked to your Roku account.
Roku accepts credit and debit cards from Visa, Mastercard, Discover, and American Express, as well as Paypal.
Samsung billing If you subscribe directly on your Samsung TV, you’ll be billed by Samsung using the payment information linked to your Samsung account.
Samsung accepts most credit and debit cards, as well as Paypal.
Vizio billing If you subscribe directly on your Vizio TV, you’ll be billed by Vizio using the payment information linked to your Walmart account.
Vizio accepts credit and debit cards from Visa, Mastercard, Discover, and American Express.
