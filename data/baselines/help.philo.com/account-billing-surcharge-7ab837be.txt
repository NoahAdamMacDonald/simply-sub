Third-party biller surcharge
You’ll always get Philo for the best price when you sign up with us directly on (Link opens in a new tab)philo.com.
If you subscribe to Philo through certain third-party billers (like Amazon, Apple, Google, Roku, Samsung, or Vizio), you may be subject to a surcharge.
If you’re paying through a third-party biller—no biggie! Follow the steps below to switch to being billed directly through Philo and avoid the surcharge.
Visit your (Link opens in a new tab)Account page online.
Click Switch to Philo billing.
Click I understand to confirm.
Enter and submit your payment information.
From there, you’ll just need to cancel your old, third-party billed subscription by following the steps (Link opens in a new tab)here.
The changes will take effect after your current billing cycle completes.
