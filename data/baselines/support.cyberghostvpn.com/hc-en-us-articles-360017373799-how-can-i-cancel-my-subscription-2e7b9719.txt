How Can I Cancel My Subscription?
This page will show you how you can cancel your CyberGhost VPN subscription. This process will cancel your active subscription but won’t delete your account. If you want to delete your account and data, please follow this guide to deleting your CyberGhost VPN account.
Note: Canceling your subscription doesn't automatically refund your subscription. If you are eligible for a refund per our Refund Policy, please contact our support team.

Cancel a Subscription Purchased from the CyberGhost VPN Website
Follow these steps:
Go to your CyberGhost account and select the profile logo in the top-right side.
Select Subscriptions.
Select Cancel Subscription.
Select Continue To Cancel.
You will see a screen saying “The automatic billing for your CyberGhost VPN subscription has been turned OFF.” This means you have successfully cancelled your CyberGhost VPN subscription and can keep using it until the end of your paid subscription period.
Cancel a Subscription Purchased via the Apple App Store
Follow these steps on Mac:
In the App Store app, select your name.
Tap on Account Settings.
Select Subscriptions and tap on the CyberGhost subscription.
Tap Cancel Subscription.
On iPhone or iPad, follow this process:
Go to Settings and tap your name.
Tap Subscriptions.
Select your CyberGhost VPN subscription and choose Cancel Subscription.
You’ll receive a pop-up message to confirm that you wish to cancel the subscription.
Note: Subscriptions purchased as an in-app purchase via the App Store are excluded from our CyberGhost VPN’s money-back guarantee. Instead, you will need to request a refund through the App Store, which is issued solely at the discretion of Apple Support.
Cancel a Subscription Purchased via the Google Play Store
Follow these steps:
Go to the Google Play Store.
Tap on your profile in the upper right corner.
Tap Payments & subscriptions.
Tap Subscriptions and select the CyberGhost VPN subscription.
Choose Manage.
Tap Cancel Subscription.
Follow the on-screen instructions.
Note: Subscriptions purchased through Google Play as an in-app purchase are not covered by CyberGhost VPN’s money-back guarantee. You can request a refund through Google Play, which is issued solely at the discretion of Google Play Support.
Cancel a Subscription Purchased via the Amazon Appstore
Follow these steps to cancel your subscription:
Via your Fire Stick or Fire TV settings
Go to Settings.
Select Applications.
Select Appstore.
Select Manage my subscriptions.
Find the CyberGhost VPN subscription and cancel it.
Via the website
Go to your Amazon Memberships and Subscriptions page and log into your Amazon account.
Locate CyberGhost VPN.
Click Manage Subscription.
Select Cancel Subscription under Advanced Controls.
Note: Subscriptions purchased as an in-app purchase via the Amazon Appstore are excluded from our CyberGhost VPN’s money-back guarantee. Instead, refunds are issued solely at the discretion of Amazon Appstore Support.
