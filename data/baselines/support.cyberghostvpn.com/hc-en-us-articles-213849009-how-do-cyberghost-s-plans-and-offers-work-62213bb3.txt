How do CyberGhost’s Plans and Offers Work?
CyberGhost is available as a recurring subscription. Your plan renews automatically at the end of each billing period. To stop being charged, you'll need to cancel before midnight on the renewal date.
Pricing, Renewal, and Refunds
Longer-term offers are priced at an introductory rate for the initial period and renew annually at the standard rate thereafter. The exact renewal price and terms are displayed at checkout before you complete your purchase.
For current pricing, visit our pricing page. We also run occasional promotions. You can check our homepage or subscribe to our newsletter to stay updated.
CyberGhost VPN comes with a money-back guarantee: 14 days on 1-month subscriptions and 45 days on offers of 6 months or longer. If you aren’t 100% satisfied with CyberGhost VPN, you can request a full refund.
Note: CyberGhost VPN's money-back guarantee does not apply to Google Play, Apple App Store, or Amazon Appstore purchases. Instead, refunds are managed solely at the discretion of Google Play Support, Apple Support, or Amazon Support, respectively. Learn more in our Refund Policy.
What's Included in Your Plan
All CyberGhost plans include access to our full VPN server network and core privacy features. This includes:
VPN servers in 100+ countries
Multiple VPN protocols, including WireGuard®
Servers optimized for streaming, P2P traffic, and gaming
Content Blocker, which blocks ads, trackers, and malware at DNS level
Startup and Wi-Fi rules for automatic protection
Identity Guard that monitors your email address for data breach exposure
Mac Cleaner that scans and removes junk files to free up storage on macOS
7 simultaneous device connections
The following are available as optional add-ons at an additional cost:
Dedicated IP: A private IP address assigned exclusively to you
Security Suite (Windows only): Antivirus and security updater
