Rumble Bug Bounty Program
Rumble is committed to maintaining the highest security standards. Through our Bug Bounty Program, we collaborate with security researchers who share this commitment and help us keep Rumble safe and secure.
How to Report
Please send your vulnerability reports to this email address . To ensure timely triage, include all the details listed in the Report Quality section below. One email address must represent only a single person. Each participant may submit up to four (4) reports per month per email address. We trust participants will not create multiple addresses to bypass this limit — our goal is to encourage higher-quality submissions. Once a report is submitted, it becomes the property of Rumble and may not be disclosed publicly. Any threats of exposure will result in immediate removal from the program.
Scope
The following domains are in scope for this program:
*.rumble.com
*.quake.ai
*.locals.com
Out of Scope
The following activities are strictly out of scope and will not be rewarded:
Social engineering (phishing, vishing, etc.)
Denial of Service (DoS / DDoS) attacks
Automated vulnerability scanning or brute force attacks without a clear PoC exploit
Rewards
Rewards are based on severity, impact, and report quality
Typical maximum bounty: $1,000 USD
Exceptionally critical issues may be rewarded above this amount
Low-quality, incomplete, or duplicate reports may not qualify for rewards
Report Quality
To maximize eligibility and reward, reports must include:
Vulnerability Description – what the bug is
Severity – e.g., critical, high, medium, low
Impact – what an attacker could achieve
Steps to Reproduce – detailed instructions with URLs, commands, screenshots, or video
Recommendation – how you suggest fixing the issue
Payments
Rewards are paid to your Rumble account, after which you can initiate a transfer to PayPal.
Approximate Review Timelines
Initial Response: Within 5 business days
Triage: Within 10 business days after initial response
Business Day Definition: Monday–Friday, 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. Eastern Time (excluding holidays)
Timelines are approximate and may shift with or without notice
Responsible Research
We ask all researchers to:
Respect user privacy and do not access, modify, or delete customer data
Avoid service disruption during testing
Use test accounts where possible
Give us a reasonable time to fix issues before public disclosure
Triage Process Includes
Validation & Reproduction: Confirm that the reported issue is real, reproducible, and within program scope
Duplicate Detection: Determine if the issue has already been reported to avoid duplicate payouts
Severity Assessment: Collaborate with Development and Operations to assess risk and impact
Resolution Planning: Work with Product Management to prioritize fixes or accept risk, and determine the payout value. Please note that fixes may take time to appear in production
Communication: Updates will be provided through the bug bounty program’s official email or another authorized communication channel. Using unapproved channels may result in removal from the program
Thank you for helping secure Rumble!
We note that participation in the program has been and remains voluntary. Rumble has never guaranteed, and continues not to guarantee, payouts or action on any report. As always, Rumble may cancel the program at any time with or without notice.
