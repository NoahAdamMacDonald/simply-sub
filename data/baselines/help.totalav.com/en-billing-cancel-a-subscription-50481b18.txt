If you purchased a subscription through the Apple App Store®, you will need to manage your subscription through your Apple account.
You can manage this easily from your iOS® device by following these steps:
Open the settings app
Tap the iCloud® Subscription at the top of the screen
Tap 'Subscriptions', this may take a few seconds to load
Tap the subscription you wish to manage
Tap 'Cancel Subscription'
Alternatively, use this link to open your the Subscriptions section on your Apple account.
If you purchased a subscription through the Google Play Store, you will need to manage your subscription through your Google account.
You can manage this easily from your Android device by following these steps:
Open the Google Play Store app on your device.
Tap your profile icon (top right).
Select Payments & subscriptions.
Tap Subscriptions.
Choose the subscription you'd like to manage.
Tap Cancel Subscription and follow the instructions
Alternatively, use this link to open your Subscriptions section of your Google account.
