Exporting Passwords From Total Password
Total Password supports exporting your stored passwords.
To do this follow these steps:
Open the Total Password Extension or App
Open Settings
Click/Tap 'Export data'
Select either CSV or Password-protected File
Enter your Master Password
Click/Tap 'Export data'
