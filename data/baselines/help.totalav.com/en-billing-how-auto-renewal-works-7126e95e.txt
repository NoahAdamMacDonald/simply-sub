How Auto-Renewal Works
Never Miss a Beat. We've Got You Covered
To help ensure uninterrupted protection and access to your subscription features, all subscriptions automatically renew at the end of each billing term, whether monthly, quarterly, biannually, or annually. You'll continue to enjoy your service without lifting a finger.
You'll always see your next billing date clearly in your Online Portal, and if you ever want to make a change, cancellation is just a few clicks away.
For annual plans, we'll also send you a reminder email up to 30 days before your renewal date.
NOTE: In certain regions, customers will receive renewal reminder emails annually, regardless of subscription length, to comply with local regulations.
