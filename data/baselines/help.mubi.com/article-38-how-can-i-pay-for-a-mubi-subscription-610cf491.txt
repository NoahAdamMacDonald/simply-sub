How can I pay for a MUBI subscription?
MUBI currently supports payment via PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and the following credit and debit cards: American Express, Master Card, Maestro and VISA. We also accept VISA debit cards except for VISA Electron.
Please note that some payment methods may not be supported in some countries.
