When will my subscription fee be charged?
If you are on our monthly plan, you will be charged every 30 days, and if you are on our yearly plan, you will be charged every 360 days. Your billing date may change if you miss a payment or if you pause your subscription. You can always find your next billing date in your subscription settings.
